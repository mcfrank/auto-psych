Refinement of the incumbent `round_number_averaging_exemplar` (partly averaged similarity to the examples on magnitude, final digit, parity and the set's common divisor, sharpened by suspicious coincidences, plus distance-free multiples, factor-kin and round-number routes). The one change is that "all even" and "all odd" are not equally salient shared properties: how much a parity mismatch cuts a target's similarity depends on whether the set is mostly even or mostly odd, with the strength and direction of this asymmetry inferred from the data. So an odd target after an all-even set such as "12, 14, 16" and an even target after an all-odd set such as "63, 27, 99, 33" are penalized by different amounts, where the incumbent penalizes both by the same amount.
