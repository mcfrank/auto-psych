Refinement of the incumbent `coincidence_rule_violation_route`. People still judge a target by its partly averaged similarity to the examples, with distance-free arithmetic and round-number routes. Their decision is still more all-or-none after suspicious coincidences, and a target that breaks the set's most specific shared rule (same final digit, else all multiples of 5, else same parity) still loses a fixed share of its support. The one change adds a concept to that list. When two or more examples share no such rule, people fall back on the concept "numbers in the range the examples span", and a target outside that range loses the same fixed share of support, however near it is. So after incoherent sets such as "24, 35", "59, 14" or "48, 63, 53", targets below or above the examples (2, 4, 7, 96, 16) are rejected flatly, while targets between them stay graded by similarity. Single examples and coherent sets are unchanged.
