Refinement of `coincidence_violation_exclusion` (partly averaged similarity to the examples on magnitude, final digit, parity and the set's common divisor, with distance-free factor-kin, whole-set round and parity-preserving multiples routes, a decision that grows more all-or-none after suspicious coincidences, and a fixed similarity loss for targets that break the set's shared property). The one change: once people see that a target breaks the set's rule, its closeness to the examples stops counting, and the more convincing the rule (the more suspicious the coincidence), the more completely closeness is ignored. So after a strong digit rule ("15, 85, 45", "78, 48, 88") wrong-digit targets are rejected about equally whether they are 3 or 40 away, which the critique's flat digit-violation contrast shows, while after a weak parity rule ("12, 14") near rule-breakers still get some credit for being close.
