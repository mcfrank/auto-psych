Refinement of `whole_set_round_category`: people judge a target by its similarity to the examples (magnitude, final digit, parity, the set's common divisor, sharpened by suspicious coincidences, with distance-free multiples, factor-kin and whole-set round routes, partly averaged over examples). The one change is the shape of the magnitude gradient: how different a target feels from an example grows with the logarithm of their numeric gap, not in proportion to it, so similarity falls off steeply over the first few steps away from an example and then levels off. The sharpest disagreement with the current best model is at the two ends of the gradient: near neighbours that do not share an example's final digit (e.g. 12 after "15", 52 after "48, 63, 53") lose more than a linear gradient allows, while targets 30 versus 60 away from the examples (e.g. 4 or 94 after "24, 35", 1 or 97 after "53, 47, 41") are rejected about equally.
