Refinement of the incumbent `coincidence_decisive_exemplar` (partly averaged similarity to the examples on magnitude, final digit, asymmetrically salient parity and the set's common divisor, plus distance-free multiples, factor-kin and round-number routes, with suspicious coincidences making the yes/no decision more decisive), with one component grafted from `parity_preserving_multiple_route`: a multiple of an example only feels like the example's kin if it keeps the example's odd-or-even character, so the distance-free multiples route is sharply weakened for even multiples of an odd example. This targets the critique that single-example and incoherent sets get more all-or-none answers than the incumbent predicts: far multiples it leaves at a coin-flip are in fact split by parity, with 34 after "17", 86 after "43", 98 after "49" or 78 after "13" mostly rejected, while 99 after "33", 64 after "16" and 44 after "22" are mostly accepted.
