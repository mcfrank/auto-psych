Refinement of the incumbent `parity_preserving_decisive_route` (partly averaged similarity to the examples on magnitude, final digit, parity and the set's common divisor, with distance-free factor-kin, whole-set round and parity-preserving multiples routes, and a decision that grows more all-or-none after suspicious coincidences). The one change: when two or more examples share a coincidence (all the same final digit, else all multiples of 5, else all the same parity), people take that shared property as a requirement of the program, so a target that breaks it (47 after "78, 48, 88", 52 after "15, 85, 45", 33 after "12, 14, 16") loses similarity to every example by one fixed factor, however near or far it is. This is a categorical exclusion on top of graded similarity: it rejects far rule-breakers after coherent sets more firmly than the incumbent does (the critique's over-accepted far violations), while single examples and incoherent sets are unaffected.
