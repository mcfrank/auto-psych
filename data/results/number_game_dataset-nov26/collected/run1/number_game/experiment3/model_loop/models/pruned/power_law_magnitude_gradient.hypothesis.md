Refinement of `parity_preserving_decisive_route` (partly averaged similarity to the examples on magnitude, final digit, parity and the set's common divisor, plus distance-free multiples, factor-kin and whole-set round routes, with suspicious coincidences making the yes/no decision more decisive). The one change is the shape of the magnitude gradient: how different a target feels from an example grows as a power of their numeric gap, with the exponent inferred from the data, rather than in direct proportion to it. With an exponent below one, the first few steps away from an example cost much of its similarity while further steps cost progressively less (so 12 after "15" is already doubted, and 60 versus 90 away are rejected about equally); above one, similarity would plateau near the examples and then collapse.
