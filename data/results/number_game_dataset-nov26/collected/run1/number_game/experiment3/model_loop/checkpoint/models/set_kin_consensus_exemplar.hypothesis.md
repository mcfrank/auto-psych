Refinement of `parity_preserving_decisive_exemplar` (partly averaged similarity to the examples on magnitude, final digit, asymmetrically salient parity and the set's common divisor, plus distance-free routes for parity-preserving multiples, factor-kin and round-number kinship, with suspicious coincidences making the yes/no decision more decisive). The one change: people credit an arithmetic kinship only to the extent that the target is kin to the set as a whole, so every distance-free route is weakened by the share of examples the target bears no multiple, shared-factor or round-number relation to. After a lone example nothing changes (14 after "7", 64 after "16"), but a far multiple of just one example in a mixed set (72 or 96 after "24, 35") is mostly rejected, while a target related to every example (60 after "3, 30", 30 after "15, 45") keeps the full route.
