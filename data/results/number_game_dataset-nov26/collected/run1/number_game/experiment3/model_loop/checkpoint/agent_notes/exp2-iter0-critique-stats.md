experiment2 iter_0 critique (2026-11-02) of `coincidence_sharpened_exemplar` uses: far (>=15) digit-match rate in same-digit sets (obs 0.78, n=216), near non-matching rate in same-digit sets (0.115), distance-correlation for parity-matching targets in same-parity mixed-digit sets (obs ~0, i.e. flat; e.g. 4,66,78,96), set-level yes-rate SD (0.22), same-decade vs other-decade contrast at distance 1-5, exact-example target yes-rate (0.82, n=180), trial_index drift, and gcd>=3 divisible vs not contrast among targets matching no example digit.
**Why:** experiment2 data shows rule-like flat endorsement (multiples of 10 → 10,20 at ~0.8; all-even set flat ~0.6) that distance-decaying similarity can't produce.
