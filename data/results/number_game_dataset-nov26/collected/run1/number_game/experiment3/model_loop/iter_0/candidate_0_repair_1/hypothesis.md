People judge each target by its similarity to the examples exactly as in the current best account, but the decision rule has a lapse: on a fixed fraction of trials they do not consult the examples at all and simply answer from a standing bias toward yes or no. So targets that similarity marks as clear members (an example itself, a close same-digit neighbour) are still sometimes rejected, and targets it marks as clear non-members are still sometimes accepted, at a floor and ceiling set by the lapse rate and its yes-bias rather than by the examples.
