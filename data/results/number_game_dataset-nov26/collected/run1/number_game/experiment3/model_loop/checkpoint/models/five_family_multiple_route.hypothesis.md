Refinement of the incumbent `coincidence_rule_violation_route` (partly averaged similarity to the examples on magnitude, final digit, parity and the set's common divisor, plus distance-free factor-kin, whole-set round and multiples routes, a fixed penalty for breaking the set's coincidence rule, and a decision that grows more decisive after suspicious coincidences). The one change concerns when a multiple feels like it has lost its example's character. People see a number ending in 5 as a "five-times-table" number rather than an odd one, and every multiple of it stays in that family. So the multiples route is cut for a parity switch only when the example is odd and does not end in 5: 34 after "17" and 86 after "43" are still rejected, but 20, 70 or 100 after "5", 50 after "25" and 90 after "15" keep the full route and are accepted almost all-or-none.
