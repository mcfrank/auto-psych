Refinement of the incumbent `whole_set_round_category`: people still judge a target by its partly averaged similarity to the examples (magnitude, final digit, parity, the set's common divisor, sharpened by suspicious coincidences, plus distance-free multiples, factor-kin and whole-set round-number routes). The one change: a final digit only looks like part of the rule once the examples can be compared with one another. When there is just a single example, people cannot tell whether its final digit matters, so a digit mismatch costs much less similarity than it does after two or more examples. After a lone "43" or "9", near targets with a different digit (40, 47; 6, 12) are therefore endorsed more, and far targets that only share the final digit (93; 49, 89) less, than the incumbent predicts, while multi-example sets are unchanged.
