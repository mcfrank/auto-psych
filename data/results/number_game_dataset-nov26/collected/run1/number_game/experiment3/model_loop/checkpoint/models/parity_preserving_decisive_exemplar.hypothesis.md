Refinement of the incumbent `coincidence_decisive_exemplar` (people judge a target by its partly averaged similarity to the examples on magnitude, final digit, asymmetrically salient parity and the set's common divisor, plus distance-free multiples, factor-kin and round-number routes, and a suspicious coincidence among the examples makes the yes/no decision more decisive). The one change is grafted from `parity_preserving_multiple_route`: a multiple of an example only feels like the example's kin if it keeps the example's odd-or-even character, so the distance-free multiples route is sharply cut when the multiple switches parity. After a lone odd example, its even multiples (34 after "17", 86 after "43", 92 after "23", 98 after "49") are therefore answered with a clear no rather than a graded maybe. Odd multiples of odd examples (99 after "33") and all multiples of even examples (64 after "16", 44 after "22") keep the full route and draw a clear yes. This makes single-example cells more all-or-none than the incumbent predicts, which is the polarization the critique reports.
