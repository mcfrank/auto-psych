People also entertain an arithmetic-progression concept: they read the examples' common spacing (the largest step, between 2 and 20, that divides every gap between them) and take the program to produce numbers reached from the examples in whole steps of it. A target on that step lattice gets a fixed boost toward "yes" at any distance from the examples (e.g. 50 after "80, 70, 90, 100", 35 after "53, 47, 41"). Off-lattice targets are judged only by the incumbent's similarity and arithmetic routes, which are partly averaged across examples.
