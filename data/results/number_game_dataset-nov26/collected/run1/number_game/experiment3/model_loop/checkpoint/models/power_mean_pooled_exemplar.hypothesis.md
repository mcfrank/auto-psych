Refinement of the incumbent `whole_set_round_category` (similarity to the examples on magnitude, final digit, parity and the set's common divisor, sharpened by suspicious coincidences, plus distance-free multiples, factor-kin and whole-set round-number routes, pooled across examples). The one change is how support is pooled across the examples: people do not take a simple (partly normalised) sum but a power mean whose exponent is inferred from the data, and when that exponent is below one the pooled support is dragged toward the example the target fits worst, so a target has to fit the set as a whole. A near target that is close to one example of a 3-4-example set but unlike the others (e.g. 20 after "16, 52, 88") therefore gets much less support than it would from a single example ("16"). Single-example sets are judged exactly as before, which addresses the critique that larger sets dilute near-target support more than the incumbent predicts.
