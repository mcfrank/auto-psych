Refinement of `multiple_route_exemplar`. People still judge a target by its summed similarity to the examples (magnitude, final digit, parity and divisibility by the set's common divisor, sharpened when the examples share a suspicious coincidence), and being a multiple of an example is still a separate route to similarity that works at any distance. The one change is that this route is something people take mostly from a lone example: each further example shown weakens it by a constant factor, because with more examples people judge from what the examples have in common rather than from one example's multiples. So after the single example "7" or "5", far multiples (14, 49, 98; 30, 100) are often endorsed, but after "50, 2" or "55, 3" far multiples of the small example are seldom endorsed, and in four-example sets the route hardly matters.
