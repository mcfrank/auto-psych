Refinement of the incumbent `common_divisor_sharpened_exemplar`. People still judge a target by its summed similarity to the examples (magnitude, final digit, parity, and a set-level factor dimension), sharpened on every dimension by how suspicious the examples' shared coincidence is. The one change is to the factor test. People do not demand that the target be an exact multiple of the set's common divisor. They ask only whether the target shares a factor of 3 or more with it, and a lone example counts as its own common divisor. So after "7" people endorse 14, 49 and 98 above other far targets, after "90, 45" they accept 30 and 60 (which share 15 with 45), and a target that shares no such factor loses similarity to every example by a constant amount.
