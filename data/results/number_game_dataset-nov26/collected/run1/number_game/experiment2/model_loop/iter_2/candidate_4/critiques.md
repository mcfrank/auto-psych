# Critique of `multiple_route_exemplar`

4 of 8 evaluated test statistics show a significant discrepancy (p ≤ 0.05), over 1000 posterior-predictive replicates.

## Significant discrepancies (a better model should address these)

Raw two-sided p shown with a Benjamini-Hochberg FDR-adjusted q across this round's statistics. Prioritise discrepancies that survive the FDR (`q ≤ alpha`); a raw-only hit may be one of several screened at once.
- **single_shared_factor_nonmultiple_contrast** — In single-example sets, yes-rate for targets that share a common factor >=3 with the example but are neither its multiple nor its divisor (e.g. 12 after "15", 9 after "6", 27 after "30"), minus yes-rate for targets with gcd(target, example) < 3 that are not multiples/divisors; observed > null means people generalize to factor-kin of a lone example beyond pure multiples, which the model (multiples route only) under-produces. (observed -0.137 vs null mean -0.361, z=2.68, p=0.00999, q=0.04) [survives FDR]
- **multiple_route_example_size_contrast** — Among targets >=10 from every example and sharing no example's final digit, yes-rate for targets that are multiples of a two-digit example (>=10, e.g. 94 after 47, 62 after 31) minus yes-rate for targets that are multiples only of a one-digit example (2-9, e.g. 49 after 7); observed > null means multiples of large examples are endorsed relatively more than the model's size-independent route predicts, observed < null means the route is really driven by small examples (times-table knowledge). (observed -0.224 vs null mean -0.0586, z=-2.50, p=0.00799, q=0.04) [survives FDR]
- **interior_vs_exterior_contrast** — In sets with >=2 examples, among targets 4-20 from the nearest example that share no example's final digit and are not multiples of any example, yes-rate for targets strictly inside the examples' range minus targets outside it; observed > null means people fill in the interval spanned by the examples (range/hull generalization) more than summed exemplar similarity predicts, observed < null means less. (observed 0.0244 vs null mean -0.0303, z=2.26, p=0.032, q=0.0639)
- **item_rate_sd** — Standard deviation, across distinct (set, target) items, of each item's yes-rate; observed > null means items are more polarized (people agree more strongly on clear yes / clear no items) than the model's item probabilities produce, i.e. the model under-discriminates between items; observed < null means it over-discriminates. (observed 0.283 vs null mean 0.269, z=2.20, p=0.026, q=0.0639)
