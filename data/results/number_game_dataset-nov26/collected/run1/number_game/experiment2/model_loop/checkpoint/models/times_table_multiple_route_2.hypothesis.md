Refinement of the incumbent `multiple_route_exemplar`: people still judge a target by its summed similarity to the examples (magnitude, final digit, parity, the set's common divisor, sharpened by suspicious coincidences), and being a multiple of an example is still a separate route to a yes that works at any distance. The one change is that this route comes from memorised times tables. So it is strong for one-digit examples (14, 49 or 98 after "7"; 64 after "4") and much weaker for two-digit examples, whose multiples are not familiar facts (94 after "47", 62 after "31"). Far multiples of a two-digit example are therefore endorsed far less than far multiples of a one-digit one.
