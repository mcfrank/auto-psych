Refinement of `multiple_route_exemplar`. People still judge a target by its summed similarity to the examples (magnitude, final digit, parity and divisibility by the set's common divisor, sharpened when the examples share a suspicious coincidence), and being a multiple of an example is still a separate route to similarity that works at any distance. The one change is where that route comes from: it is retrieved times-table knowledge, so it exists only for examples that are themselves times-table numbers (2 to 12). After "7" people endorse 14, 49 and 98, but after "47" or "31" the far multiples 94 and 62 get no extra support and are judged by ordinary similarity alone.
