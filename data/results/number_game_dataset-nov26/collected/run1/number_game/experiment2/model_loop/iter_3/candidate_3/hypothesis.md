Refinement of the incumbent `factor_kin_multiple_route`. People still judge a target by its summed similarity to the examples: magnitude, final digit, parity and divisibility by the set's common divisor, all sharpened when the examples share a suspicious coincidence. A target that shares a factor of 3 or more with an example still gets a small similarity boost at any distance. The one change is to the multiples route. It is retrieved one-digit times-table knowledge, so only one-digit examples (2 to 9) give their multiples a distance-free boost (14, 49 or 98 after "7"; 64 after "4"). Far multiples of two-digit examples (94 after "47", 62 after "31", 75 after "25") get no multiples boost and are judged by ordinary similarity and factor-kinship alone.
