Refinement of `set_size_diluted_multiple_route`. People still judge a target by its summed similarity to the examples (magnitude, final digit, parity and the set's common divisor, sharpened when the examples share a suspicious coincidence). Being a multiple of an example is still a separate route to similarity that works at any distance, and it still weakens with each further example. The one change is where the route comes from: it is retrieved from memorized times tables, so it works only for examples from 2 to 10 (49 or 98 after "7", 30 after "5", 64 after "4"). Multiples of larger examples, such as 94 after "47", 62 after "31" or 75 after "25", get no route and are judged on ordinary similarity alone.
