People judge a target by its summed similarity to the examples (magnitude, final digit, parity, divisibility by the set's common divisor, sharpened when the examples share a suspicious coincidence), but the distance-free arithmetic route is not "being a multiple of the example": it is belonging to the same familiar times table, i.e. sharing a factor of 3 or more with an example, with the route's strength falling off as the smallest such shared factor gets larger (3, 4, 5, 7 tables are well known; a 47 table is not). This disagrees most sharply with the current best model on lone examples: it predicts factor-kin that are not multiples are often endorsed (12 or 27 after "15" or "30", 9 after "6"), while far multiples of large two-digit examples (94 after "47", 62 after "31") get little of the boost that multiples of small examples (49 after "7") get.
