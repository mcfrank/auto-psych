People judge a target by its summed similarity to the examples (magnitude, final digit, parity, the set's common divisor, sharpened when the examples share a suspicious coincidence), but the distance-free arithmetic routes (being a multiple or a factor-kin of an example, and round-number kinship) need the example to be actively held in mind while the target is checked, and examples fade from working memory as later ones arrive. So an example's arithmetic support falls off steadily with how many examples were shown after it: the multiples of the last-shown example (21 after "3, 7", 36 after "5, 9") are endorsed much more than equally far multiples of an earlier example (99 after "3, 33" is not carried by the 3), while ordinary magnitude and digit similarity is unaffected by order.
