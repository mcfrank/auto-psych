Refinement of `shared_factor_parity_exemplar`: people still judge a target by its summed similarity to the remembered examples in a space of magnitude, final digit, parity and shared factors, with attention tuned to how variable the examples are. The one change is that when three or more examples all end in the same digit, people take the final digit to be the program's rule and attend to it much more sharply, so a target with a different final digit loses far more similarity to every example than graded digit variability alone allows. Targets that match the shared digit are unaffected. After "46, 76, 66" or "55, 15, 75, 45", a target close in magnitude but with a different final digit (e.g. 50 or 47) is rejected much more often than the incumbent predicts.
