When three or more examples are shown as an orderly, sorted sequence (ascending or descending), people perceive them as a progression along the number line, and this perceptual grouping brings magnitude-range interpretations ("numbers between 22 and 62") to mind, whereas a scrambled list leaves property rules such as "ends in 2" dominant. People otherwise infer the hidden concept by tempered Bayesian concept learning over rules, intervals and their odd/even versions, but an orderly presentation raises their prior belief in a magnitude-range concept by an inferred amount. So for the same digit-sharing examples, in-range targets lacking the shared digit are endorsed more when the list is sorted than when it is shuffled.
