Refinement of `sampled_hypothesis_majority_vote`: people do the same tempered Bayesian concept learning over the 45 individually weighted rules and all intervals, and answer by bringing a few candidate concepts to mind by sampling them from their posterior — but they say "yes" only if at least a criterion share of the sampled concepts contains the target, and that criterion is inferred rather than fixed at a simple majority. The one change is this free decision criterion (a criterion of one half recovers majority vote, one sample recovers the incumbent); a demanding criterion predicts that targets supported only by a minority of concepts — such as numbers far outside the examples' range that fit no small rule — are rejected more firmly than majority vote allows, while targets in the dominant concept are still endorsed.
