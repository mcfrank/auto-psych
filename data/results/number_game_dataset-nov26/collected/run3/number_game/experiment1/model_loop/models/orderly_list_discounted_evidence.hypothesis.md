Refinement of `parity_restricted_concepts`: people do the same tempered-evidence Bayesian concept learning over the 45 weighted rules, all intervals, and their odd-only and even-only versions. But the size principle rests on believing the examples were drawn at random from the concept, and a list shown in sorted order (ascending or descending, three or more examples) looks deliberately arranged rather than randomly sampled. So people trust such orderly examples less as evidence. The one change is an inferred reduction of evidence strength for orderly lists (zero recovers the incumbent). It predicts that the same examples shown in sorted order (e.g. 62,32,22) support the tight "ends in 2" rule less, and non-members are endorsed more, than when shown in a scrambled order (e.g. 63,23,43).
