People do not infer a rule; they judge a target by its summed similarity to the stored examples, as in the context (exemplar) model of classification, where each number is represented by its place-value digits — its tens digit and its units digit — together with its parity and its magnitude, and similarity to an example shrinks multiplicatively with each mismatching dimension, with an inferred attention weight per dimension. "Yes" grows with the target's total similarity to the examples relative to an internal criterion, so a target that shares its tens digit with one example (e.g. 25 after 62, 32, 22) gets partial endorsement even when it breaks the examples' shared final digit, whereas the same target is rejected when no example lies in its decade.
