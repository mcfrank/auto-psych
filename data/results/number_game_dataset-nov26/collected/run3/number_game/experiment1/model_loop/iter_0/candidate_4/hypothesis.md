Refinement of bigelow_piantadosi_mixture: the same Bayesian concept learner over 45 rules and all intervals of 1-100 (rules chosen with probability lambda_0 and equally likely, each interval (1 - lambda_0)/100^2, examples sampled from the concept with 10% noise), except that people under-weight the evidence of the examples — the likelihood of the examples is raised to a single inferred exponent (tempered sampling evidence) before being combined with the prior. With an exponent below 1 the size principle is weakened, so people are less confident in the smallest consistent rule and spread belief onto broader rules and intervals, endorsing minimal-rule members less and nearby non-members more.
