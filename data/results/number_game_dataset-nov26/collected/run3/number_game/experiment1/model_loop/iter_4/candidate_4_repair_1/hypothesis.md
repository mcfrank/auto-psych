Refinement of the incumbent `monotone_order_range_prior_shift`: people do the same tempered, serial-position-weighted Bayesian concept learning over the 45 weighted rules and all intervals of 1-100, with sorted lists of three or more examples shifting prior belief from rules toward ranges. The one change, a component taken from `bounded_range_rule_learning`, is that people can also think of a rule as holding only over a stretch of the number line that covers the examples ("multiples of 3 between 9 and 70"), and these range-bounded rules take an inferred share of the rule prior (share 0 recovers the incumbent). Because a bounded rule is smaller than the full rule, the size principle favours it, so members of the winning arithmetic rule that lie outside the examples' min-max range are endorsed less than members inside it — the out-of-range drop the incumbent underpredicts — while order effects are kept.
