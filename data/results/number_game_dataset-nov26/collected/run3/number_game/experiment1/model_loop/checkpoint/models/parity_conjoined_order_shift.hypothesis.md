Refinement of the incumbent `monotone_order_range_prior_shift`: people do the same tempered, serial-position-weighted Bayesian concept learning over the 45 weighted rules and all intervals of 1-100, with sorted lists of three or more examples shifting prior belief from rules toward magnitude ranges. The one change, taken from `parity_restricted_concepts`, is that parity is a basic feature people conjoin with any concept, so they also entertain the odd-only and even-only version of every rule and range (e.g. "odd multiples of 3", "even numbers between 4 and 96"), which share an inferred part of prior belief (share 0 recovers the incumbent). Because these concepts are half the size, the size principle favours them whenever all examples share a parity, so people reject opposite-parity targets — even inside the examples' range and even for members of the winning arithmetic rule — more firmly than the incumbent predicts, while order effects are kept.
