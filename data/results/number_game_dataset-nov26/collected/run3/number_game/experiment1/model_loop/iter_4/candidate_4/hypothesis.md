Refinement of `monotone_order_range_prior_shift`: people do the same tempered, serial-position-weighted Bayesian concept learning over the 45 weighted rules and all intervals of 1-100, with the same sorted-order shift of prior belief toward ranges, but parity is a basic feature they conjoin with any concept, so they also entertain the odd-only and even-only version of each rule and range ("odd multiples of 3", "even numbers between 4 and 96"), which share an inferred part of the prior (share 0 recovers the incumbent). The one change is adding these parity-restricted concepts, taken from `parity_restricted_concepts`. Because such a concept is half the size, the size principle favours it whenever all examples share a parity, so rule members of the opposite parity are rejected — e.g. after 69, 9, 39, 21 the even multiples of 3 above the examples (72, 84, 96) are endorsed much less than the odd ones inside the range, which is the out-of-range drop the incumbent underpredicts.
