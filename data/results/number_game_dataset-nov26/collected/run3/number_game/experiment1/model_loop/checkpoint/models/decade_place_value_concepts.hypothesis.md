Refinement of `parity_restricted_concepts`: people do the same tempered-evidence Bayesian concept learning over the 45 weighted rules, all intervals of 1-100, and their odd-only and even-only versions. The one change is that people also read numbers by place value, so they entertain decade concepts: "numbers whose tens digit is one of these", e.g. "numbers in the 20s, 30s or 60s". These concepts get an inferred share of prior belief (share 0 recovers the incumbent), and the size principle favours the smallest one, the decades the examples occupy. This predicts that a target with a different final digit is endorsed more when an example sits in its decade (62,32,22 → 25; 73,33,3 → 36) than when none does (63,23,43 → 35), whatever order the examples are shown in.
