I refine the incumbent `magnitude_threshold_concepts`, keeping its tempered, sample-size-neglecting Bayesian concept learning over weighted rules, size-weighted ranges with their own evidence strength and one-sided "above x"/"below x" thresholds as concepts of their own, odd/even versions, fully consistent bounded rules, the lone-example discount and the cumulative explaining away of implied rules. The one change is a component taken from `range_evidence_with_guessing_lapse`: on some trials people do not consult their inferred concept at all and guess, saying "yes" at an inferred rate whatever the target is. Because guessing, not the inferred concept, then supplies the floor of yeses to numbers no plausible concept contains and the occasional "no" to the examples themselves, the concept people infer, thresholds included, should be sharper, while every target keeps a guessing floor and ceiling.
