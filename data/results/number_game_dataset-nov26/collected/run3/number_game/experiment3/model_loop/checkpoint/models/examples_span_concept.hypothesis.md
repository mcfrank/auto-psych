Refinement of `range_specific_evidence_strength` (tempered, sample-size-neglecting Bayesian concept learning over weighted rules, size-weighted ranges with their own evidence strength, odd/even versions and bounded rules that every example fits, with comparison-based rules discounted for a lone example). The one change: when two or more examples are shown, people also explicitly entertain "numbers between the smallest and the largest example", the span the examples mark out, as one salient concept with an inferred share of prior belief (no share recovers the incumbent). Because this concept is small and fits every example, belief that otherwise spreads onto wider ranges settles on the exact span. So for sets that no rule fits, people should reject targets beyond the smallest and largest example more firmly than the incumbent predicts while still endorsing targets inside the span.
