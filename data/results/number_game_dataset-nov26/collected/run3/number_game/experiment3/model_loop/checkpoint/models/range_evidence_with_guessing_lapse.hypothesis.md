I refine `range_specific_evidence_strength`, keeping its tempered, sample-size-neglecting Bayesian concept learning over weighted rules, size-weighted ranges that have their own evidence strength, odd/even versions and fully consistent bounded rules, with comparison-based rules discounted for a lone example. The one change is a component taken from `guessing_lapse_consistent_concepts`: on some trials people do not consult their inferred concept at all and guess instead, saying "yes" at an inferred rate whatever the target is. Then the occasional "yes" to numbers that no plausible concept contains comes from guessing, not from a prior that favours very wide ranges. So the ranges people imagine, which already have their own evidence strength, should stay closer to the examples, and every target, even the examples themselves, keeps a floor and a ceiling set by guessing.
