People's space of candidate concepts includes arithmetic progressions with any starting point, not just multiples: "every fourth number starting from 3" (3, 7, 11, ...), or "every fifth number starting from 2", which is the same as "ends in 2 or 7". As a family, these progressions get an inferred share of prior belief, split equally among them, and because a step can only be seen by comparing examples, they are discounted when a single example is shown. Otherwise people infer the concept exactly as in `magnitude_threshold_concepts`. So when the examples fit such a progression (15, 39, 35; 99, 59, 31, 67; 17, 11, 65, 53), people should endorse its members more than other numbers of the same parity in the same region.
