Refinement of `consistent_examples_bounded_rules` (the same tempered, sample-size-neglecting Bayesian concept learning over the 45 weighted rules, size-weighted intervals, their odd/even versions and bounded rules that every example fits, with digit and bounded rules discounted when only one example is shown). The one change is in how often people think a shown example is a stray that the program did not produce from its concept. That model fixes this rate at one in ten; here it is inferred, and the claim is that people think strays are rarer. An example that breaks a rule then counts strongly against that rule, so for sets that no rule fits fully, belief moves from rules that most examples fit to the magnitude range the examples span. In-range numbers should then be endorsed more for such rule-less sets, and for pairs of examples, than the incumbent predicts.
