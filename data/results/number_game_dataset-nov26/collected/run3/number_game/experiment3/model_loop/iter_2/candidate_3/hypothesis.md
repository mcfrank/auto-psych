I refine `range_specific_evidence_strength`, keeping its tempered, sample-size-neglecting Bayesian concept learning over weighted rules, size-weighted ranges with their own evidence strength, odd/even versions and fully consistent bounded rules. The one change is graded explaining away: when people compare several examples and see that all of them share a specific property (they all end in 3), they treat a broader property this implies (being odd, containing a 3) as a by-product, not as a separate candidate rule, and the more the broader rule reaches beyond the specific one, the less plausible it seems. So after 63,23,43 or 93,43,83,53, odd numbers with another final digit (31, 35) should be endorsed less than the incumbent predicts, numbers containing a 3 only slightly less, and sets with no nested rules should be judged as before.
