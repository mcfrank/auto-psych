I refine `guessing_lapse_consistent_concepts` (tempered, sample-size-neglecting Bayesian concept learning over weighted rules, size-weighted ranges, their odd/even versions and bounded rules that every example must fit, with a guessing lapse on some trials). The one change, taken from `specific_rule_preempts_supersets`: when people compare two or more examples and see that they all share a specific property (all end in 3), they explain away any broader property it implies (being odd, containing a 3) as a by-product rather than a candidate rule, so each rule that strictly contains another rule every example fits keeps only an inferred fraction of its plausibility. After sets such as 63,23,43 or 93,43,83,53, people should then rarely endorse same-parity numbers with a different final digit (31, 35, 39), less often than either parent predicts, while targets with the specific property, lone examples, and sets without nested rules stay as the guessing-lapse model predicts.
