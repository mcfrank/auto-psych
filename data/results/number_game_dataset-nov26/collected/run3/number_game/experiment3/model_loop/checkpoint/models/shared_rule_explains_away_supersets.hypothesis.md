Refinement of `range_specific_evidence_strength` (the same tempered, sample-size-neglecting Bayesian concept learning over weighted rules, size-weighted ranges with their own evidence strength, odd/even versions and fully consistent bounded rules, with comparison-based rules discounted for a lone example). The one change is explaining away: when people compare two or more examples and see that all of them share a specific property (all end in 3), they treat any broader property it implies (being odd, containing a 3) as a by-product of that specific property, not as a separate candidate rule, so every rule that strictly contains another rule all the examples fit loses prior plausibility by an inferred amount (no loss recovers the incumbent). After sets such as 63,23,43 or 93,43,83,53, odd targets with a different final digit (31, 35, 39) should then be endorsed much less than the incumbent predicts, while targets with the specific property and sets without nested rules barely change.
