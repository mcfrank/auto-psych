I refine the incumbent `iter2_candidate1` (cumulative explaining away). It keeps the incumbent's tempered, sample-size-neglecting Bayesian concept learning, in which a broader rule implied by a specific rule that all the examples share (odd or contains-3, given ends-in-3) is explained away more with each further example. The one change is a component taken from `range_evidence_with_guessing_lapse`: on some trials people do not consult their inferred concept at all and guess, saying "yes" at an inferred rate whatever the target is. Because guessing, and not wide ranges or broad stray-tolerant rules, now accounts for the occasional "yes" to numbers that no plausible concept contains, the inferred concept should be sharper: the ranges people imagine stay tighter around the examples, and every target, examples included, keeps a guessing floor and ceiling.
