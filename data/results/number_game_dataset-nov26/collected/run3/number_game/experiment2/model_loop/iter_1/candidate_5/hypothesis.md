Refinement of `sample_size_neglect_evidence`: people do the same tempered Bayesian concept learning with sample-size neglect (45 weighted rules, all intervals, their odd/even versions and range-bounded rules, with each extra example adding less evidence), but they do not consult that concept on every trial. On some trials, at an inferred lapse rate, they skip the inference and answer by a biased guess, saying "yes" at an inferred guessing rate whatever the target is. The one change is this guessing lapse (a lapse rate of 0 recovers the original model). It predicts that even targets that are themselves examples, or that clearly belong to the inferred concept, are sometimes rejected (examples get about 0.7–0.9 yes, not 1), and that clear non-members still get a floor of yeses.
