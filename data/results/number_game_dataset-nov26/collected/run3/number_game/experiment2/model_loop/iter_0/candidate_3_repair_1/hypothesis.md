Refinement of `range_bounded_parity_concepts`: people do the same tempered-evidence Bayesian concept learning over the 45 weighted rules, all intervals of 1-100, their odd-only and even-only versions, and rules restricted to a range covering the examples. The one change is that a range bound is something people read off the spread of the examples, so they restrict a rule to a range only when at least two different examples mark out that range: a single example suggests no range bound and is generalized through the unbounded rules it belongs to (no new parameters). This predicts broader rule-based generalization from one example than the incumbent allows (10 → 60, 70, 80, 90; 11 → 55, 66, 99), while generalization from two or more examples still falls off for rule members outside the examples' span.
