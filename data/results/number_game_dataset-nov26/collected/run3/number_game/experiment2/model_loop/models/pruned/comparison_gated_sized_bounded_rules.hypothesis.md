Refinement of `digit_neglect_sized_bounded_rules`. People do tempered, sample-size-neglecting Bayesian concept learning over the 45 weighted rules, size-weighted intervals, their odd/even versions, and range-bounded rules whose prior grows with how many members they keep. In that model, digit rules are already discounted when only one example is shown. The one change, grafted from `comparison_dependent_concepts`: a rule confined to the span the examples cover also needs a comparison between examples, so with a lone example range-bounded rules lose prior plausibility by the same inferred discount as digit rules. No parameter is added. People should therefore generalize a lone example to the whole of its arithmetic rule, including far members (6 → 18, 24; 25 → 100; 11 → 99), more than either parent allows. For sets of two or more examples, a sized range-bounded reading is kept.
