Refinement of `range_sized_bounded_rules` (tempered, sample-size-neglecting Bayesian concept learning over the 45 weighted rules, size-weighted intervals, their odd/even versions, and range-bounded rules whose prior grows with how many members they keep). The one change, grafted from `digit_rules_need_comparison`: a digit pattern ("ends in 6", "contains a 1") is noticed only by comparing examples, so when a single example is shown the digit-based rules hardly come to mind and their prior is discounted by an inferred amount (zero recovers `range_sized_bounded_rules`). The sized range prior already makes tiny bounded rules around a lone example implausible, so the belief taken from digit rules moves to the example's whole arithmetic rules. People should then generalize a lone example to its multiples (6 → 12, 18; 11 → 55, 66; 25 → 50, 100) more than either parent model allows, and endorse its digit neighbours (14 → 74) less, while judgments about sets of two or more examples are unchanged.
