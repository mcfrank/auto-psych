Refinement of `range_size_prior_concepts`. People do the same tempered-evidence Bayesian concept learning as that model: the 45 weighted rules, all intervals with a size-dependent prior, the odd-only and even-only versions, and range-bounded rules. The one change: on an inferred fraction of trials people do not use the examples and answer from prior belief alone, saying "yes" with the prior probability that the program's concept contains the target (a fraction of 0 recovers the incumbent). Prior belief favours broad concepts, so these evidence-neglect lapses raise endorsement of non-example targets the size principle would reject (most of all after a single example, where people generalize more than the incumbent allows) and lower endorsement of the examples themselves, which people accept only about 70% of the time.
