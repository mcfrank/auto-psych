People do the same tempered-evidence Bayesian concept learning as `range_size_prior_concepts` (45 weighted rules, size-weighted intervals of 1-100, their odd-only and even-only versions, and range-bounded rules), but their space of concepts also includes an open-ended family of "multiples of k" concepts for any k from 2 to 50 (e.g. multiples of 25, of 33, of 14), not just the few small multiples in the fixed rule list. This family gets an inferred share of prior belief, and within it a multiples concept's prior falls off as an inferred power of k (smaller step numbers come to mind more easily). Because the size principle strongly favours such a small concept when it contains the examples, people generalize a lone example to its own multiples (25 → 50, 100; 66 → 99; 10 → 80; 6 → 18) much more than the fixed rule list allows.
