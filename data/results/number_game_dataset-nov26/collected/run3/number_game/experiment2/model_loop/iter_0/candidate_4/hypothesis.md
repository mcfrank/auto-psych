Refinement of `range_bounded_parity_concepts`: people do the same tempered-evidence Bayesian concept learning over the 45 weighted rules, all intervals of 1-100, their odd-only and even-only versions, and range-bounded rules, but how strongly they apply the size principle to each example is not fixed — it grows with the number of examples shown, by an inferred power of the set size (power 0 recovers the incumbent). A single example raises little sense of a "suspicious coincidence", so people treat it as weak evidence and generalize broadly from it (e.g. many multiples of 10 after just "10", well above what the incumbent allows), and only commit to the tightest concept covering the examples once several examples agree.
