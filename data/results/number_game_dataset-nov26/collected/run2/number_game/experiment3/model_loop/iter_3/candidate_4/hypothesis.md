Refinement of the incumbent `parity_bounded_ambiguity_doubt` by one grafted component, taken from `working_memory_three_example_limit`: people can hold only three examples in mind at once, so when four examples are shown, on an inferred share of trials one of them has dropped out of memory and the rule-based judgment (the salience-weighted share of the shared profile with the interval similarity) is made against the richer, more coincidental profile the remaining three share, averaged over which example was lost. Everything else is the incumbent unchanged — rule-ambiguity doubt at every set size whose fallback "yes" keeps the examples' shared parity — so on four-example sets targets that fit the full set's profile are endorsed less than a reasoner using all four examples would endorse them, while sets of one to three examples are judged exactly as before.
