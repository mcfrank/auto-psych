Refinement of `working_memory_three_example_limit` by one grafted component, taken from `rule_ambiguity_doubt_all_sizes`: the doubt that makes people give up on rule-based judgment and answer at a flat rate is not limited to lone examples but operates at every set size, more often the more candidate rules all the examples fit together (its own base rate for multi-example sets). Everything else is the working-memory model unchanged: people hold at most three examples in mind, so on an inferred share of four-example trials one example has dropped out and the target is judged against the richer profile the remaining three share. So rule-ambiguous sets (64, 4) are endorsed broadly and flatly at any size, while four-example sets are generalized more narrowly than their full shared profile implies.
