Refinement of `exception_tolerant_parity_doubt` by one change to its rule-ambiguity doubt: when several examples fit so many rules that people give up on rule-based judgment, their fallback is not "any number of the right parity" but "numbers of the right parity around where the examples fall" — the loosely patterned program they then imagine keeps the examples' magnitude neighbourhood as well as their shared parity, so the fallback "yes" fades smoothly (with its own inferred width) with the target's distance below the smallest or above the largest example. Everything else (exception-tolerant salience-weighted share of the shared profile with interval similarity, and the lone-example doubt that keeps only parity) is unchanged, so rule-ambiguous multi-example sets such as 64, 4 or 96, 48 are endorsed broadly for same-parity targets near and between the examples but much less for same-parity targets far beyond them.
