Refinement of the incumbent `parity_doubt_three_example_memory` by one grafted component, taken from `exception_tolerant_parity_doubt` (originally `single_exception_tolerance`): people allow that one of the examples they hold in mind may be an odd one out, so whenever three or more examples are judged together, a property shared by all of them but one (three of 12, 48, 36, 7 are even) is still entertained as a possible rule, at an inferred fraction of the weight it would carry if every example shared it. This applies to the examples actually in memory: the full three- or four-example set, and, when one of four examples has dropped out of memory, the three that remain. Everything else in the incumbent is unchanged: the salience-weighted share with the interval similarity, the parity-kept rule-ambiguity doubt, and the working-memory drop. So on three- and four-example sets, targets with an almost-shared property are endorsed more than the strictly shared profile of the remembered examples implies.
