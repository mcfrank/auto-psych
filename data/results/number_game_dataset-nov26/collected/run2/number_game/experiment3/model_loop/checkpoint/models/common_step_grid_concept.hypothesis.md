People's space of candidate concepts includes a "common grid" concept the current models lack: when several examples are shown, they notice when every gap between the examples is a multiple of one step of 3 or more (7, 22, 37 all sit on a grid of step 15 starting at 7; 12, 48, 36 on a grid of step 12), even when the examples are unevenly spaced, and entertain "numbers on that grid, in either direction" as a candidate property. Its prior salience is an inferred multiple of the arithmetic-progression concept's salience, and like other non-digit properties it is overshadowed by a visible shared final digit. Otherwise the judgment is that of the current best account, so targets lying on the examples' common grid, inside or outside their range, are endorsed more than the strictly shared profile implies.
