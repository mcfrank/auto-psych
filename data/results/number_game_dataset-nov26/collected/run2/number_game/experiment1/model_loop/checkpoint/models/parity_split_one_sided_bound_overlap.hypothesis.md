Refinement of `parity_direction_salience_overlap`: people note every salient mathematical property all the examples share (each property family with its own salience, "all odd" and "all even" with separate saliences, multiples of 5/10 their own family, no size principle) and say "yes" in proportion to the salience-weighted share of that profile the target also has. The one change, grafted from `one_sided_magnitude_bound_overlap`, replaces the two-sided "within the examples' span" property with two one-sided magnitude bounds, "at least as large as the smallest example" and "no larger than the largest example". Each bound has its own salience, so going past the examples upward can cost a target a different amount than going below them, on top of the directional parity asymmetry.
