Refinement of `seed_overshadowing_last_digit_recency` by one change: when only a single example is shown, people know they cannot yet tell which of its properties the program cares about, so they adopt a more lenient criterion and lean toward "yes" for any target, beyond what the overlap with that example's property profile alone implies. Everything else is unchanged (salience-weighted share of the examples' shared profile, multiples of the seed, a visibly shared final digit overshadowing other properties, and the last example's final digit lingering), so the change predicts higher endorsement of non-example targets for one-example sets than for sets of two to four examples, and no difference among the multi-example set sizes.
