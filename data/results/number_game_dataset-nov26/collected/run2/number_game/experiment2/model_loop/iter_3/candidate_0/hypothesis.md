People weigh each property the examples share by how much of a coincidence that sharing would be: a property that covers few numbers (powers of 3, multiples of 11, squares) carries more weight than a broad one (even, contains a 4), and this tilt toward the rarer properties grows with the number of examples that share them. Otherwise "yes" follows the share of that weighted profile the target has, as in the current best account. So the more examples share a narrow property, the more firmly people reject targets that lack it, even when those targets keep the broad shared properties.
