Refinement of the incumbent `progression_graded_magnitude_band` by one grafted component, taken from `progression_single_example_leniency`: when only a single example is shown, people know they cannot yet tell which of its properties the program cares about, so they adopt a more lenient criterion and lean toward "yes" for every target by an inferred amount, beyond what the overlap with that example's profile implies. Everything else is the incumbent unchanged (salience-weighted share of the examples' shared profile, with magnitude credit falling off smoothly outside the examples' range, the "start at the smallest and add the gap" progression concept, seed multiples, final-digit overshadowing and the last example's lingering digit), so the graded band shapes which targets a lone example generalizes to while the leniency raises how often people say yes to them at all — non-example targets of one-example sets are endorsed more than the band alone predicts, with no change for sets of two to four examples.
