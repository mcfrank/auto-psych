People judge magnitude separately from the examples' mathematical properties: whether the target falls within the range the examples span (with credit fading smoothly with its distance below the smallest or above the largest example) is a dimension of its own that pushes the decision directly, not one property diluted among however many properties the examples happen to share. So a target inside the examples' range that shares none of their parity, digit or multiple properties is still endorsed clearly more than an equally unrelated target outside the range, even for sets with rich shared profiles, while the rest of the judgment (the salience-weighted share of the shared profile, with progression, seed multiples, final-digit overshadowing and the first and last examples' lingering digits) is that of the best model.
