Refinement of the incumbent `heading_extrapolation_weber_width`. People do Bayesian concept learning over the same arithmetic, digit and digit-sum rules and every numeric range of 1-100. They read the examples as distinct draws with some strays and sometimes guess, judge a range's breadth Weber-like (relative to the magnitude of the numbers it covers), and let the direction of the last step stretch the ranges toward where the sequence is heading.

The one change, the component taken from `margin_beyond_examples_range_prior`, is a two-sided margin expectation. People do not expect the few numbers they were shown to sit right at the edges of the program's range, so they credit a candidate range by how far it reaches past the examples on both sides, below the smallest as well as above the largest, whatever order the examples came in (strength inferred). This predicts more "yes" a little beyond either end of a cluster (e.g. both 57 and 67 after 60, 62, 64) than the incumbent gives, tilted by the heading. It also predicts more "yes" just below a cluster than an upward-only counting habit gives.
