People infer the program by Bayesian concept learning over arithmetic, digit and digit-sum rules and numeric ranges, as in the best model, but they are averse to ambiguity about what kind of program it is: when the examples leave them torn between a symbolic rule (e.g. "even numbers") and a stretch of the number line (e.g. "10 to 88"), they withhold "yes" in proportion to how evenly split that belief is (strength inferred). This predicts fewer "yes" answers, even to targets both readings would accept, for sets that fit a rule and a range about equally well (e.g. 70, 10, 88, 40), while sets clearly explained by one kind of concept are judged as in the best model.
