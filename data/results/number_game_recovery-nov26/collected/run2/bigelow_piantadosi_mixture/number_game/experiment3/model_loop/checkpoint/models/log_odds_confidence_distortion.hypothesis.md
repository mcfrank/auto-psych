People infer the program by Bayesian concept learning exactly as the best model does (arithmetic, digit and digit-sum rules and every numeric range, distinct examples with some strays, Weber-like range breadth, ranges stretched toward the heading and upward past the largest example), but they never answer by random guessing; instead they turn their belief into a yes/no answer through a distorted sense of probability, a linear-in-log-odds transform whose slope (compressed or exaggerated confidence) and yes-bias are inferred. This predicts that no-answers to targets no concept covers are near-certain rather than held at a constant guessing floor, while moderately believed targets are shifted together by the yes-bias.
