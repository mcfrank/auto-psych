Refinement of `heading_direction_range_extrapolation` (the incumbent): people do Bayesian concept learning over the same arithmetic, digit and digit-sum rules and every numeric range, and they take the direction of the last step (up or down from the example before) as where the program is heading. The one change is to how that heading shapes the ranges they consider: it is a pure forward reach, not a trade-off between sides. A range gains credibility by how far it runs past the examples in the heading direction, and the room it leaves on the side the sequence came from is neither credited nor penalised. This predicts more "yes" far beyond the examples in the heading direction (e.g. above 60 after 40, 20, 60) without the incumbent's matching extra loss of "yes" just behind the sequence, so generalization on the trailing side stays as in an order-blind learner.
