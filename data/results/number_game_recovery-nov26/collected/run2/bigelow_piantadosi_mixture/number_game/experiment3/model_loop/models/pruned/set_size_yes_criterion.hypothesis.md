People infer the program by the same simple Bayesian concept learning as the simplest current model, but their criterion for answering "yes" depends on how many examples they were shown: each further example makes them feel more licensed to say "yes" (or more cautious), shifting their odds of a "yes" by a constant step per example, with strength and direction inferred. This predicts that adding a fourth example to a three-example set narrows the "yes" answers less than ideal concept learning implies (e.g. more "yes" to non-examples after 31, 3, 1, 15 than after 31, 3, 1), with the largest effect for targets the concept makes moderately likely.
