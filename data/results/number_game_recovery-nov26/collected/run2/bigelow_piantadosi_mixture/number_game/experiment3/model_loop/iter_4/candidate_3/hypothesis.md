Refinement of `position_decaying_example_evidence`: people read the examples one after another and do the same Bayesian concept learning (arithmetic, digit and digit-sum rules and every numeric range, independent draws with some strays, occasional guessing), with each later example's evidence changing by a constant factor per position. The one change is that how much an example revises their guess also depends on whether it tells them anything new about the extent of the program: an example that falls inside the span of the examples shown before it (e.g. 15 after 31, 3, 1) carries its own inferred evidence weight, either discounted as redundant (so it narrows generalization less than a full-weight learner expects) or taken as extra confirmation that the pattern holds (so it narrows it more). This predicts that the same numbers shown in an order where the last one fills a gap (31, 3, 1, 15) generalize differently from an order where every example extends the span (1, 3, 15, 31), with the difference falling on members of broad rules and wide ranges.
