People learn the program by the same Bayesian concept learning as the best model, but they read the question as one about generation rather than membership: they judge how likely the program is to produce the target as its next output, which is lower for members of broad rules and wide ranges because a broad program spreads its outputs thinly, and they answer "yes" when that likelihood clearly exceeds what a random number generator would give, by a soft criterion with inferred position and sharpness. This predicts fewer "yes" answers to members of broad concepts (e.g. other even numbers after 16, 8, 64, 2 if "even" is believed) than to equally certain members of tight concepts, whereas a membership judgment accepts both alike.
