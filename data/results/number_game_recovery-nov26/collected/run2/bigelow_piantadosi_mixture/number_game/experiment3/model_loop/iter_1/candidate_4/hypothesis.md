Refinement of the incumbent `heading_extrapolation_weber_width`: people do Bayesian concept learning over the same arithmetic, digit and digit-sum rules and every numeric range of 1-100. They read the examples as distinct draws with some strays, sometimes guess, and judge a range's breadth Weber-like, relative to the size of the numbers it covers. The one change, the component taken from `upward_room_range_prior`, replaces the incumbent's order-dependent heading stretch: people expect the program's numbers to carry on upward past the largest example, as counting does, whatever order the examples were shown in, so they credit a candidate range by how far it reaches above the examples (strength inferred). This predicts more "yes" just above a cluster than just below it for the same set in rising or falling order (e.g. 67 versus 57 after 60, 62, 64 and after 64, 62, 60), with the incumbent's magnitude-relative tightening of generalization around large clustered examples kept.
