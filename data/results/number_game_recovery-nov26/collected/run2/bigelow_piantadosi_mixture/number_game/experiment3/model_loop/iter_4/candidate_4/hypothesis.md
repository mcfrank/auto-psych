Refinement of `position_decaying_example_evidence`: people do the same Bayesian concept learning over arithmetic, digit and digit-sum rules and every numeric range of 1-100. Each example's evidence shrinks or grows by a constant factor along the sequence, and the examples are read as independent draws with some strays, with occasional guessing. The one change, the component taken from `upward_room_range_prior`, replaces the flat range prior with a counting habit: people expect the program's numbers to carry on upward past the largest example, so a candidate range's prior plausibility rises with how far it reaches above the examples (strength inferred). This predicts more "yes" to numbers a little above a cluster than to numbers the same distance below it (e.g. 67 versus 57 after 60, 62, 64), whatever order the examples were shown in, while rule and digit-sum generalization and the order-dependent weighting of the examples are kept.
