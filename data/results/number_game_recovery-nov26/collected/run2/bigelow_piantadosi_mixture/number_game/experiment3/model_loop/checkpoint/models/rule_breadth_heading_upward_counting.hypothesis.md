Refinement of the incumbent `heading_weber_with_upward_counting`. People do Bayesian concept learning over the same arithmetic, digit and digit-sum rules and every numeric range. They read the examples as distinct draws with some strays, sometimes guess, judge a range's breadth relative to its magnitude, stretch ranges toward where the sequence is heading, and expect the numbers to carry on upward past the largest example. The one change, the component taken from `rule_breadth_prior_concepts`, is that the arithmetic and digit rules do not share an equal prior: before seeing any examples, people find a rule more (or less) plausible as a power of how many numbers it contains, with the strength and direction inferred (the data suggest a slight preference for broad rules). This predicts that broad rules such as "even numbers" or "contains a 3" take a different share of the "yes" answers to their members than the incumbent gives them, compared with narrow rules such as "powers of 4", while range generalization is unchanged.
