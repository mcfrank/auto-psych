Refinement of `rule_breadth_prior_concepts`: people do Bayesian concept learning over the same 45 arithmetic and digit rules (with the same breadth-graded rule prior) and every interval of 1-100, with the size principle and occasional guessing at their own yes rate — but how often they believe the program emits a stray random example is a property of the learner, inferred from their answers, rather than fixed at one in ten. The one change is freeing this outlier rate (the component taken from `digit_concepts_inferred_outlier_rate`), which claims that part of what the lapse now absorbs is really people discounting a misfitting example as a stray, so tight rules survive an odd-one-out example (e.g. 85, 19, 91 or 14, 47) instead of being abandoned for broad ones.
