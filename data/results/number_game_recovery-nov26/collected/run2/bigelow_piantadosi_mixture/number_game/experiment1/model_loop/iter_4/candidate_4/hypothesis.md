Refinement of `concepts_sampled_without_replacement` (the incumbent): people do Bayesian concept learning over the same 45 arithmetic and digit rules and every interval of 1-100, with the size-graded rule prior, occasional guessing at their own yes rate, and strong sampling without replacement (each further example a draw from the concept's members not yet shown). The one change, a component taken from `breadth_prior_concepts_free_outlier_rate`, is that how often people believe the program emits a stray random example is inferred from their answers rather than fixed at one in ten. The claim is that a without-replacement learner also discounts a misfitting example as a stray, at a rate of its own, so tight rules and short ranges survive an odd-one-out example (e.g. 85, 19, 91 or 14, 47) instead of giving way to broad concepts.
