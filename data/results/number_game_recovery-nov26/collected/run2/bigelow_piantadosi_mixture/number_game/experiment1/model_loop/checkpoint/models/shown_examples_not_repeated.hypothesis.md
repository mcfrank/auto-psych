Refinement of `concepts_sampled_without_replacement` (the incumbent): people do Bayesian concept learning over the same 45 arithmetic and digit rules and every interval of 1-100, with the size-graded rule prior, 10% of examples random, occasional guessing at their own yes rate, and the belief that the program shows distinct numbers, so each further example is a draw from the concept's members not yet shown. The one change follows from that same belief: when the number asked about is one of the examples already shown, people sometimes (at a rate inferred from their answers) read "would the program also produce it?" as asking whether it would come up again as a new example, and since the program does not repeat itself they answer "no". This predicts fewer "yes" answers to targets that are themselves examples than the concept's membership implies (about two-thirds rather than three-quarters), while leaving every other judgment governed by the incumbent's concept learning and making the guessing lapse smaller.
