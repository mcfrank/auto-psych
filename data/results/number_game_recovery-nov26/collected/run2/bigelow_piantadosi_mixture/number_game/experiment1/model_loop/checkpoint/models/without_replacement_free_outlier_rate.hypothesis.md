Refinement of `concepts_sampled_without_replacement`: people do Bayesian concept learning over the same 45 arithmetic and digit rules (with the size-graded rule prior) and every interval of 1-100, reading each further example as a draw from the concept's members not yet shown (strong sampling without replacement), with occasional guessing at their own yes rate. The one change (the component taken from `breadth_prior_concepts_free_outlier_rate`) is that how often they believe the program shows a stray random number is a property of the learner, inferred from their answers, rather than fixed at one in ten — so a misfitting example (e.g. 14, 47 or 85, 19, 91) is discounted as a stray at the learner's own rate, letting tight, nearly exhausted concepts survive it instead of yielding to broad rules and wide ranges.
