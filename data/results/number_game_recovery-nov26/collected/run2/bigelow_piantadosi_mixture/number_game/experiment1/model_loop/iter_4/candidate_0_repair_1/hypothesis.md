People do Bayesian concept learning over the same arithmetic and digit rules as the best model (size-graded rule prior, distinct examples drawn without replacement, 10% strays, occasional guessing), but they think of numeric ranges in round-number terms: the ranges they entertain begin and end at multiples of five ("from 60 to 70", "from 35 to 85"), not at arbitrary numbers. This disagrees most sharply with the best model at the edges of the examples' span: after clustered examples such as 60, 62, 64 or 92, 82, 27, 42, "yes" should stay high right up to the next multiple of five beyond the outermost example and then drop in a step, rather than fall off smoothly from the examples themselves.
