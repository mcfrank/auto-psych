Refinement of the incumbent `margin_beyond_examples_range_prior`: people do Bayesian concept learning over the same arithmetic and digit rules, the 18 digit-sum concepts and every numeric range of 1-100. They read the examples as distinct draws with some strays, sometimes guess, favour narrow ranges, discount open-ended ranges that run to 1 or 100, and credit a range by how far it reaches past the examples on both sides. The one change, the component taken from `open_ended_ranges_heading_direction`, is that people also read the examples as a sequence and take the direction of the last step (up or down from the example before) as where the program is heading, so the room they expect beyond the examples is shifted toward that side and away from the other (strength inferred). This predicts the incumbent's extra "yes" just beyond both ends of a cluster, but tilted: more beyond the end the sequence last moved toward (e.g. above 64 after 60, 62, 64) than beyond the end it moved away from (e.g. below 60), and the reverse for the same numbers shown in falling order. Single-example sets and rule and digit-sum generalization are unchanged.
