Refinement of `recency_weighted_even_odd_family_prior`: people do the same recency-weighted Bayesian concept learning over the same 45 rules (with separate prior plausibilities for even, odd and the other kinds of rule) and all intervals of 1-100 with a tempered size principle, but they do not answer by probability matching to their belief that the target is in the concept — they answer more decisively, following the belief's log-odds scaled by a fitted decisiveness exponent, so targets they think are probably in (or probably out) get yes (or no) more consistently than the belief itself implies. The single change is grafting this sharpened belief-to-response mapping (from `response_sharpened_tempered_rule_prior`) onto the incumbent, predicting fewer yeses for weakly supported targets and more for strongly supported ones, with the ordering of targets unchanged.
