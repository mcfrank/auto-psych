Refinement of `recency_weighted_even_odd_family_prior`: people do the same recency-weighted Bayesian concept learning over the same 45 rules (separate prior plausibilities for even, odd and the other kinds of rule) and all intervals of 1-100 with a tempered size principle, but they do not answer by probability matching to their belief that the target is in the concept — they turn that belief into a yes/no answer more (or less) decisively, following the belief's log-odds scaled by a free decisiveness exponent. The single change is grafting this sharpened belief-to-response mapping (from `response_sharpened_tempered_rule_prior`) onto the incumbent, predicting that targets the learner judges probably in (or probably out of) the concept are answered yes (or no) more consistently across people than the graded posterior itself implies.
