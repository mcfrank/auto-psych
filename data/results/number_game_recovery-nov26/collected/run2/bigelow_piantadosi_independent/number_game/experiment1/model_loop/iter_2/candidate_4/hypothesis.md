Refinement of `rule_family_prior_tempered_mixture`: people do the same Bayesian concept learning over the same 45 rules and all intervals of 1-100, with a tempered size principle and a prior over kinds of rule, but "even numbers" and "odd numbers" are not one kind sharing a single plausibility — each has its own prior plausibility inferred from the data. The single change is splitting the parity family into separate even and odd kinds, addressing the critique that people generalize the parity pattern more from all-even examples than from all-odd ones; it predicts stronger same-parity generalization (more yeses to distant even targets) after even sets than after comparable odd sets.
