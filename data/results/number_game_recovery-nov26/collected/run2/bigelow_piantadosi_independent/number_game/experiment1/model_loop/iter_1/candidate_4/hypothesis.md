Refinement of `learned_rule_prior_mixture`: people do the same Bayesian concept learning over the 45 mathematical rules (each with its own learned prior plausibility) and all intervals of 1-100, but they apply the size principle only partially — the evidence that the examples were sampled from a small concept is tempered by a free "sampling strength" exponent (1 = full strong sampling, 0 = weak sampling). The single change is grafting this tempered likelihood from `tempered_size_principle_mixture` onto the learned rule prior, predicting that broad rules and wide intervals keep more posterior weight after a few examples than an ideal learner allows, so generalization is less sharply confined to the tightest consistent concept.
