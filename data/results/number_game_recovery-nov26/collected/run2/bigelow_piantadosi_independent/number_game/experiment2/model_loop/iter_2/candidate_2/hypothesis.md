People infer the hidden concept as the best model does — capacity-limited Bayesian learning over mathematical rules (separate prior plausibilities for even, odd and the other kinds of rule) and numerical ranges — but they check the target against the inferred concept with a fuzzy sense of its magnitude: the target is encoded noisily on the mental number line, so it is judged a member to the extent that numbers near it are members, with neighbours counting less the farther away they lie. The disagreement with the best model lives at targets that break a well-supported rule but sit right next to its members (e.g. 15 after "16, 8, 64", or odd numbers beside even examples): these get a graded share of yeses that the exact-membership best model denies, while ranges merely acquire soft edges.
