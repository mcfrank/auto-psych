Refinement of `exposure_weakened_size_principle`: people do the same Bayesian concept learning over the same 45 rules (separate prior plausibilities for even, odd and the other kinds of rule) and all intervals of 1-100, with a size principle that weakens as the same set is probed again, but they do not apply the size principle equally to the two kinds of concept — magnitude ranges are read as tightly sampled (a separate, fitted sampling strength for intervals), so the range spanned by the examples is taken far more literally than an arithmetic rule's extension. The single change is splitting the one sampling strength into a rule strength and an interval strength, motivated by the critique that in mixed-parity sets (where ranges dominate) people separate targets inside the examples' range from those outside it more sharply than the model predicts.
