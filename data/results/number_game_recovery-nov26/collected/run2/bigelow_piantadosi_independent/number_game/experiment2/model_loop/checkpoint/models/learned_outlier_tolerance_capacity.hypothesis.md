Refinement of `capacity_limited_evidence_integration`: people do the same capacity-limited Bayesian concept learning over rules (separate prior plausibilities for even, odd and the other kinds of rule) and numerical ranges, but how much they suspect the program of producing stray, random numbers is not fixed at 10% — their belief in such "noise" examples is a free parameter inferred from the data. The single change is learning this outlier tolerance: if people trust every example as a genuine member of the concept, ranges that fail to cover all examples are ruled out, so in sets with no fitting rule (e.g. mixed odd and even numbers) they say yes inside the span of the examples and no outside it more sharply than a learner who tolerates stray examples.
