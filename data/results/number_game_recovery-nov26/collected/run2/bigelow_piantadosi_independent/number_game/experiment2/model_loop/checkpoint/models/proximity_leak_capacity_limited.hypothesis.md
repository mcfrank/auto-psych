Refinement of `capacity_limited_evidence_integration`: people do the same capacity-limited Bayesian learning over rules (separate prior plausibilities for even, odd and the other kinds of rule) and numerical ranges, with each added example contributing less evidence, but when the target falls outside the concept they have inferred, its numerical closeness to one of the examples can still pull them into saying yes — a proximity leak whose strength falls off exponentially with the distance to the nearest example (fitted leak strength and fall-off). The single change is this similarity override of the inferred concept, motivated by the critique that, for sets fitting a small rule, targets that break the rule but lie within a few units of an example get more yeses than the incumbent predicts.
