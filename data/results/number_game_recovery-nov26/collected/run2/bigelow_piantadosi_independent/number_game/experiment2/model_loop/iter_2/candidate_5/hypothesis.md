Refinement of `narrow_range_prior_capacity_limited`: people do the same capacity-limited Bayesian learning over rules (separate prior plausibilities for even, odd and the other kinds of rule) and numerical ranges with a narrow-range prior, but when they ask whether the program would produce a target they treat the inferred concept as having fuzzy edges — a number just next to one the concept contains is judged producible too, with this credit falling off with its distance from the nearest member. The single change is this graded, distance-based extension of every concept (rule or range) at the answer stage, in place of all-or-none membership, predicting that targets breaking a small rule but lying within a few units of the examples (or just past a range's edge) get more yeses than strict membership allows — the similarity-leak the critique reports.
