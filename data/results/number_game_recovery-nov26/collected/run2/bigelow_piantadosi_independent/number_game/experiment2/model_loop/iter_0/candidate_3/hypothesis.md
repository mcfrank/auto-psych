Refinement of `capacity_limited_evidence_integration`: people do the same capacity-limited Bayesian learning over rules (separate prior plausibilities for even, odd and the other kinds of rule) and numerical ranges, with each added example contributing less evidence, but they apply the size principle with different strength to the two kinds of concept — examples are read as much more (or less) diagnostic of a tight numerical range than of a small mathematical rule. The single change is a separate, fitted sampling strength for range hypotheses, motivated by the critique that for sets mixing odd and even numbers (where ranges dominate) people separate targets inside versus outside the examples' range more sharply than the incumbent predicts.
