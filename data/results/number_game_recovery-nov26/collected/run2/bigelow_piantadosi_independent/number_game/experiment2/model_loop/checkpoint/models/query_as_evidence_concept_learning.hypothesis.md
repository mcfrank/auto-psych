People infer the hidden concept by the same capacity-limited Bayesian learning over rules (with multiples of 5 as its own, base-ten-salient kind of rule) and numerical ranges as the best model, but they do not treat the question as neutral: being asked about a particular number is itself read as information about the program, so before answering, the learner updates on the queried number as if it were a weak, extra observation (a pragmatic "why are they asking about this?" inference), with its strength and direction inferred from the data. If the question is read as a hint the target is produced, targets covered only by small concepts gain yeses; if it is read as a lure, fewer yeses go to every target and narrow concepts lose more than broad ones, which also lowers yeses on small sets, where the queried number weighs most against the little evidence the examples give.
