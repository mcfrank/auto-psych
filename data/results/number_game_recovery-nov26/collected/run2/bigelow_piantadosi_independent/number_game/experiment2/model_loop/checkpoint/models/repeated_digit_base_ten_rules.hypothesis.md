Refinement of `base_ten_multiples_capacity_limited`: people do the same capacity-limited Bayesian learning over rules and numerical ranges, with "multiples of 5" as its own base-ten-salient kind of rule, but their space of candidate rules also includes a digit pattern that base-ten notation makes visually obvious and that the incumbent lacks entirely: numbers written with a repeated digit (11, 22, 33, ..., 99), a new kind of rule with its own prior plausibility inferred from the data. The single change is adding this repeated-digit rule, predicting that sets such as 3, 33, 99 are generalized to other repdigits (88, 44) that no multiples, parity or final-digit rule covers, and that a single repdigit example (e.g. 33 or 44) earns fewer yeses for numerically close non-repdigits.
