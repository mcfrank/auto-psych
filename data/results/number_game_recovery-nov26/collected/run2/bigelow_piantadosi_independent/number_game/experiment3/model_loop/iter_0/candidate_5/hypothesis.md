Refinement of `sparse_rule_familiarity_within_kinds`: people do the same capacity-limited Bayesian learning over rules (with sparse familiarity within each kind) and numerical ranges. The single change is that people do not apply the size principle equally to both kinds of explanation. They take the examples as drawn less deliberately from a stretch of the number line than from an arithmetic rule, so the examples count for less as evidence that the range is tight (a fitted fraction of the rules' sampling strength). This predicts that sets no rule fits are generalized somewhat beyond the examples' span, and that as examples accumulate, a fitting rule overtakes the tightest covering range faster than a single shared sampling strength allows.
