People build concepts compositionally: besides a bare arithmetic rule (e.g. even numbers) or a bare stretch of the number line (e.g. 20 to 40), they also entertain a rule that the program applies only within a stretch (e.g. even numbers between 20 and 40), and they weigh all of these by the same Bayesian logic as the best model, with computed rules searched for only on some judgments. This predicts that a set fitting a rule but confined to a narrow part of the line (e.g. 22, 26, 30) is generalized to the rule's members near the examples, but neither to its distant members nor to the non-members inside the span.
