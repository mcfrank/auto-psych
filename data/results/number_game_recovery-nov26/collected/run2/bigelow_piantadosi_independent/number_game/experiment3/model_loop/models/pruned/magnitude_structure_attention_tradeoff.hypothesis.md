Refinement of `individual_range_sampling_looseness`: people do the same capacity-limited Bayesian learning over arithmetic rules (kind plausibilities, sparse familiarities within kinds) and numerical ranges, reading the examples with one sampling strength for rules and a looser one for ranges, and each participant has a stable personal trait. The single change is what that trait is: instead of setting only how literally a person reads the examples as evidence for a tight range, it sets how the person divides attention between the examples' magnitudes and their arithmetic structure, so the same personal factor that strengthens their range evidence weakens their rule evidence by the same proportion (and the reverse). This predicts a within-person trade-off, consistent across every set a person sees: people who confine their yeses to the examples' span also extend fitting rules beyond it less, and people who extend rules fill in the span less, with the split growing as examples accumulate.
