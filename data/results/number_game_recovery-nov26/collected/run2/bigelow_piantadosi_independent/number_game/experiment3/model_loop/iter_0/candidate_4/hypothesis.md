Refinement of `parity_veto_sparse_familiar_rules`: people learn the hidden concept by the same capacity-limited Bayesian weighing of arithmetic rules (sparse rule familiarities within kinds) against numerical ranges, with the same parity-conflict veto, but they differ from one another in how readily they reach for a rule rather than a range — each participant has a stable personal leaning that raises or lowers the prior odds of every rule against the ranges. The single change is this participant-level rule-versus-range leaning (its spread inferred from the data), predicting that the same set is generalized by its rule (yeses to distant rule members) by some participants and by proximity (yeses to nearby non-members) by others, consistently across all the sets each person sees.
