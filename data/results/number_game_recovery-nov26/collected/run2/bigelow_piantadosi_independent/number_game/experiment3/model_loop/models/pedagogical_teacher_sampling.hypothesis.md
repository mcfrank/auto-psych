People treat the examples as chosen on purpose by a helpful teacher rather than drawn at random from the hidden concept: when they ask what concept the program follows, they weigh each concept by how likely a teacher who wanted them to grasp that concept would have been to pick exactly these examples, i.e. examples that single that concept out among the alternatives (pedagogical sampling). Their answer is their resulting belief that the target belongs to the concept, so concepts the examples would be an odd way to teach (e.g. a wide range, when the examples are all powers of 2) lose credit beyond what the size principle alone takes away.
