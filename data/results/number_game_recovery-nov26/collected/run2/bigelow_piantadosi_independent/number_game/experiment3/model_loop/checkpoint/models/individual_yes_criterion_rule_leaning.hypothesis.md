Refinement of `loose_range_individual_rule_leaning`. People learn the hidden concept as that model says: capacity-limited Bayesian weighing of arithmetic rules (sparse familiarities within kinds) against numerical ranges, with looser sampling for ranges, a parity-conflict veto, and a personal leaning toward rules or ranges. The single change is at the answer stage: each participant also has a stable response criterion, a personal readiness to say "yes" at a given strength of belief that the target is in the concept. It shifts the log-odds of every answer by the same personal amount (people differ by a fixed spread; an unidentified person is averaged over it). So participants differ consistently in their overall yes-rate across every set and target, most visibly for targets the concept leaves uncertain and least for near-certain members and non-members, independently of whether they lean toward rules or ranges.
