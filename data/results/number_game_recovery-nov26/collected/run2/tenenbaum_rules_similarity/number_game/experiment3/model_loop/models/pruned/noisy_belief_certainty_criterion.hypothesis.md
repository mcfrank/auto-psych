People do the same Bayesian concept learning as no_digit_containment_span_concepts (arithmetic and final-digit rules plus multiples of 13-50, rule runs confined to a stretch of the number line, ranges of a typical width, an inferred share of random examples, examples drawn without replacement, no "contains digit d" concepts), but they do not answer "yes" as often as they believe the target belongs: they compare that belief against an internal criterion of how sure they must be, with noise in the comparison (the criterion and the noise inferred). Because the comparison is made on the probability itself, beliefs comfortably above the criterion all get similarly high "yes" rates and beliefs below it similarly low ones, so graded beliefs are pushed toward all-or-none answers around the criterion, and even certain beliefs keep a non-zero rate of the opposite answer.
