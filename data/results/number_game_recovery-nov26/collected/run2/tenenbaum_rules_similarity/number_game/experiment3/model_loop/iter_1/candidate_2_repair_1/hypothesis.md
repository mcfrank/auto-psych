People infer arithmetic and final-digit rules (and stretches of them) by the same Bayesian concept learning as the current best model, but when the examples suggest a "region of magnitudes" they do not picture a sharp-edged range: they picture a fuzzy cluster around a central number, from which the program emits numbers more often the closer they are to the centre (with a spread on a scale they have learned to expect), and they judge a target's membership by how typical it is of that cluster, which fades smoothly with its distance from the centre. So, for examples that fit no rule, "yes" is strongest near the middle of the examples, weaker at and just beyond the outermost examples, and fades gradually with no sharp cut-off at the edges of the examples' span.
