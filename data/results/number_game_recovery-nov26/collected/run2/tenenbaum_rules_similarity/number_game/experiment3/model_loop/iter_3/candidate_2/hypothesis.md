People do the same Bayesian concept learning as no_digit_containment_span_concepts (arithmetic and final-digit rules plus multiples of 13-50, rule runs confined to a stretch of the number line, ranges of a typical width, an inferred share of random examples, examples drawn without replacement, no "contains digit d" concepts), but on some inferred share of trials their attention lapses and they answer without consulting that belief at all, saying "yes" at their own fixed guessing rate. This decision-stage lapse predicts a flat, belief-independent floor and ceiling on "yes": targets that are themselves examples are still sometimes refused, and targets that no plausible concept contains are still sometimes accepted, by the same amount whatever the set.
