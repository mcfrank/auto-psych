People do the same normative Bayesian concept learning as no_digit_containment_span_concepts (arithmetic and final-digit rules plus multiples of 13-50, rule runs confined to a stretch of the number line, ranges of a typical width, an inferred share of random examples, examples drawn without replacement, no "contains digit d" concepts), but they distort their belief through a prospect-theory probability-weighting function when answering: small beliefs that the target belongs are overweighted and near-certain beliefs underweighted (an inverse-S Prelec curve whose curvature is inferred, one recovering calibrated answers). Unlike a symmetric confidence scaling of log-odds, this distortion is asymmetric, so near-impossible targets get noticeably more "yes" while near-certain ones lose only a little, and the crossover sits below 50% belief.
