A refinement of the incumbent no_digit_containment_span_concepts: people do the same Bayesian concept learning (arithmetic and final-digit rules plus multiples of 13-50, rule runs confined to a stretch of the number line, ranges of a typical width, an inferred share of random examples, examples drawn without replacement, no "contains digit d" concepts), but they do not answer "yes" exactly as often as they believe the concept contains the target: they carry a general response bias, a single shift of the log-odds of their belief (its size and sign inferred, zero recovering the incumbent). This predicts that targets the learner is unsure about (beliefs near 50%) are pushed systematically toward "no" (or "yes"), while targets almost surely in or out of the concept are barely affected.
