I refined the incumbent new_set_criterion_ratio_ceiling. People do the same Bayesian concept learning (arithmetic and final-digit rules plus multiples of 13-50, rule runs confined to a stretch of the number line, ranges of a typical width, an inferred share of random examples, examples drawn without replacement, no "contains digit d" concepts), with the largest example as a ratio-graded soft ceiling and a criterion shift on the first question about a new set. The one change: participants differ in how attentively they answer. On some share of trials each person does not consult their inference and simply guesses (at a guessing rate everyone shares), and that share is a personal trait drawn from a population distribution, so some people answer "no" even to the examples themselves far more often than others do.
