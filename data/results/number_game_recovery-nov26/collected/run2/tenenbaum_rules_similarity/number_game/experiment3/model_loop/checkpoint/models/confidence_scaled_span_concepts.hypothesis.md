A refinement of no_digit_containment_span_concepts: people do exactly the same Bayesian concept learning (arithmetic and final-digit rules plus multiples of 13-50, rule runs confined to a stretch of the number line, ranges of a typical width, an inferred share of random examples, examples drawn without replacement, no "contains digit d" concepts), but they do not answer at their calibrated belief. The one change, grafted from posterior_confidence_miscalibration, is that the log-odds of saying "yes" are their posterior log-odds that the concept contains the target multiplied by a single inferred confidence factor (one recovers the incumbent): above one they commit more firmly than their belief warrants, turning moderately supported generalisations into near-certain yes or no answers; below one they hedge, giving graded answers to strongly supported or excluded targets.
