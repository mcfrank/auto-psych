A refinement of extended_multiples_concepts: people do the same Bayesian concept learning (45 independently weighted rules plus multiples of 13-50, ranges with a preferred typical width, an inferred share of random examples, examples taken without replacement), but they weigh the examples' evidence for rules about how a number is written (ends in digit d, contains digit d) by its own inferred power, while evidence for arithmetic rules and ranges is used at full strength. The one change is this digit-rule-specific evidence weight: below one, a shared written digit (such as examples all in the 30s, which all contain a 3) is a weaker coincidence to people than the size principle implies, so "yes" to nearby targets depends less on whether they share the examples' decade than the incumbent predicts.
