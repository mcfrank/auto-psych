I chose log_scale_interval_width_prior and refined it. People judge how wide a "range" concept is on a compressed, logarithmic mental number line, so their prior expects a typical range to be narrow among small numbers (e.g. 3-6) and wide among large ones (e.g. 60-100); the one change is the concept learner underneath, which is replaced by the current best one (45 independently weighted rules, an inferred share of random examples, and examples taken as drawn from the concept without replacement) in place of the old fixed-10%-noise, with-replacement learner. This predicts that, for examples that fit no rule, "yes" extends much further around large examples than around small ones, and further below a cluster of large examples than the linear-width prior of the best model allows.
