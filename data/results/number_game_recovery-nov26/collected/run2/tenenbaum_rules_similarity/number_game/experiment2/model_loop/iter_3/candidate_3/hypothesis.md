A refinement of extended_multiples_concepts: people do the same Bayesian concept learning (45 independently weighted rules plus multiples of 13-50, ranges of 1-100 with a preferred typical width, an inferred share of random examples, examples drawn without replacement), but they also form conjunctive "odd multiples of m" concepts (odd multiples of 3: 3, 9, 15, 21, ...; of 7, 9, 11, 15, ...) by combining parity with divisibility. The one change is this added family, each member equally likely a priori and the family taking an inferred share of the rule prior; because these concepts are about half the size of "multiples of m", the size principle makes them win when every example is an odd multiple (e.g. 69, 9, 39, 21 or 63, 27, 99, 33), predicting fewer "yes" answers to even multiples of m (12, 30, 54) than the incumbent does.
