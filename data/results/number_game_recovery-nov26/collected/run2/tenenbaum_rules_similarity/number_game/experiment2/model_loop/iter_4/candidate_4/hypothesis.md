A refinement of the incumbent span_restricted_rule_concepts: people do the same Bayesian concept learning (45 independently weighted rules plus multiples of 13-50, ranges of 1-100 with a preferred typical width, an inferred share of random examples, examples drawn without replacement, and rules confined to an unbroken stretch of the number line such as "even numbers from 42 to 92"), but they treat a span-restricted rule as a rule combined with a range, so the stretch it is confined to obeys the same typical-width expectation as their ranges. The one change is that a run's prior plausibility follows the same preferred range width as plain ranges (no new parameter) instead of every run being equally likely, so a familiar rule is confined to a moderate stretch (e.g. "even numbers in the 40s-60s") rather than to the tightest run hugging the examples or to nearly the whole line, changing how far "yes" to rule members extends beyond clustered examples.
