A refinement of without_replacement_example_sampling: people do the same Bayesian concept learning (45 independently weighted rules, ranges of 1-100 with a preferred typical width, an inferred share of random examples, examples taken as drawn from the concept without replacement), but they do not weigh the evidence of the examples at its normative strength relative to their prior over concepts. The one change is an inferred evidence-weight that scales how much the examples' fit counts against prior plausibility (below one is Edwards-style conservatism, staying nearer the prior and generalising more broadly; above one is over-weighting, locking onto the best-fitting small concept faster than Bayes implies; exactly one recovers the incumbent).
