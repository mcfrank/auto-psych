People do the same Bayesian concept learning as the current best model (45 independently weighted rules, ranges of 1-100 with a preferred typical width, an inferred share of random examples, examples taken as drawn without replacement), but they answer by consulting a small number of concepts sampled from their posterior and saying "yes" if any one of them admits the target (a lenient, disjunctive decision rule). The effective number of concepts consulted is inferred (one recovers probability matching to the posterior; more than one inflates "yes" for targets with moderate support while leaving clearly excluded targets near "no"; fewer than one makes them stricter), so the response stage distorts the posterior asymmetrically rather than sharpening it symmetrically or adding a flat guessing rate.
