A refinement of extended_multiples_concepts: people do the same Bayesian concept learning (45 independently weighted rules plus the multiples-of-13-to-50 family, ranges with a preferred typical width, an inferred share of random examples, examples drawn without replacement), but on some share of trials they skip that inference and guess, and their guesses favour small numbers, which feel like more typical outputs of a number-producing program. The one change is this magnitude-biased guessing lapse (a response-stage lapse as in guessing_lapse_without_replacement, with a guess rate that depends on the target's size), which predicts that stray "yes" answers fall more on small targets below the examples than on large targets above them — the downward-over-upward extension the incumbent fails to reproduce.
