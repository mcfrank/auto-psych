A refinement of extended_multiples_concepts: people do the same Bayesian concept learning (45 independently weighted rules plus the multiples-of-13-to-50 family, ranges of 1-100 with a preferred typical width, an inferred share of random examples, examples drawn without replacement), but besides ordinary ranges they also entertain one-sided "numbers up to N" concepts that start at 1 (e.g. "anything up to 40"), which take an inferred share of the range prior on top of the ordinary ranges. The one change is this family of counting-from-one concepts, which predicts that people extend "yes" below the smallest example (toward 1) more readily than above the largest example, the asymmetry the incumbent's symmetric ranges and rules fail to reproduce.
