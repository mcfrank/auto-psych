A refinement of the incumbent span_restricted_rule_concepts: people do the same Bayesian concept learning (45 independently weighted rules plus multiples of 13-50, ranges of 1-100 with a preferred typical size, an inferred share of random examples, examples drawn without replacement, and rules confined to an unbroken stretch of the number line such as "even numbers from 42 to 92"), but they treat such a rule-confined stretch as a bounded concept just like a range, so they expect it to hold a typical number of members — the same size expectation they apply to ranges — instead of finding every stretch equally likely. The one change is this shared size prior on rule stretches (no new parameter), which makes tiny stretches hugging the examples and near-complete rules a priori less plausible than moderate ones, so "yes" to rule members extends a moderate distance beyond clustered examples rather than stopping at their edge.
