A refinement of log_scale_interval_width_prior: people do Bayesian concept learning over the 45 independently weighted rules and all intervals of 1-100 with the size principle, and they expect a range to have a typical width judged on a compressed, logarithmic mental number line (narrow among small numbers, wide among large ones), but they do not assume a fixed 10% of examples are random. The one change is grafting the inferred example-noise rate from inferred_example_noise_concepts: how often people believe the program emits an example from outside its concept is inferred from the data, governing how sharply the size principle accumulates over examples and how readily a rule or range survives an example it does not cover.
