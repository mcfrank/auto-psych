A refinement of noisy_examples_erlang_intervals: people do the same Bayesian concept learning (45 independently weighted rules, all intervals of 1-100 with an Erlang prior on range width, size principle, inferred share of random examples), but they answer "would the program produce this number?" with the same generative model they used to explain the examples. Since they believe the program emits a random number from 1-100 on some share of its outputs, they say "yes" whenever the target is in the inferred concept or when they think the program might be emitting a random number, so the one change is that the inferred noise rate also sets a floor of "yes" for targets outside every plausible concept (no new free parameter, unlike a free guessing lapse).
