People assume the examples were chosen by a helpful teacher rather than drawn at random from the concept (pedagogical sampling): a teacher picks examples that point to the true concept, so a number counts more as evidence for a concept the fewer other plausible concepts it also belongs to. People do Bayesian concept learning over the same rules and ranges of 1-100, but they interpret each example by how diagnostic it is. Concepts made up mostly of numbers a teacher would happily show (distinctive, rarely shared ones) are penalised when such numbers were not shown, so generalisation narrows to the concepts that the chosen examples single out.
