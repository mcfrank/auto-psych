A refinement of noisy_examples_erlang_intervals: people do the same Bayesian concept learning over the 45 independently weighted rules and all intervals of 1-100, with the size principle and an inferred rate of random examples, and a prior over how wide a "range" concept is, but that width prior is not fixed to the Erlang shape. The one change is that the shape of the width prior is inferred along with its typical width (a gamma-shaped prior whose shape can fall below the Erlang value), so people can give tight ranges hugging the examples high prior weight while still allowing occasional wide ranges. This predicts that people extend "yes" beyond tightly clustered examples less than the incumbent does, while still generalising across widely spread ones.
