Refinement of bigelow_piantadosi_independent: people do the same Bayesian concept learning over the 45 rules and all intervals (rule weights inferred separately, size principle, 10% noisy examples), but on a fraction of trials they do not consult that posterior at all and instead guess, answering "yes" with a fixed bias. The one change is this response-lapse process: it predicts that even targets among the examples, or clearly inside the learned concept, get "no" at a non-trivial rate (and clearly excluded targets get occasional "yes"), which the incumbent, whose answers follow the posterior exactly, cannot produce.
