Refinement of `erlang_interval_simplicity_search`: people still build concepts from mental primitives (a fitted cost per extra primitive, Erlang-weighted intervals, the size principle and near-all-or-none commitment to the best-explaining concept). The one change is that the final digit of a written number is a privileged, perceptually salient feature: rules of the form "ends in d" get a fitted prior discount compared with other compound rules (multiples, powers, primes, contains-a-digit). So after 62, 32, 22 or 92, 82, 27, 42 people commit to "ends in 2" instead of "even numbers" or "contains a 2", and say no to other even numbers and to the rest of the 20s.
