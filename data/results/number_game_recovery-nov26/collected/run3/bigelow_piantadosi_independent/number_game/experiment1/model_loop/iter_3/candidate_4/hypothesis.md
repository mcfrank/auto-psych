Refinement of `erlang_interval_simplicity_search`: people still build concepts from mental primitives (a fitted cost per extra primitive, Erlang-weighted intervals, the size principle and near-all-or-none commitment to the best-explaining concept). The one change is that divisibility is a privileged, readily accessible kind of rule: "multiples of m" concepts receive a fitted prior bonus over other two-primitive rules (powers, ending in a digit, containing a digit), so when all the examples share a divisor people more often commit to a multiple-of-m rule and say yes to other multiples of it — closing the critique that people extend arithmetic rules more than the incumbent predicts.
