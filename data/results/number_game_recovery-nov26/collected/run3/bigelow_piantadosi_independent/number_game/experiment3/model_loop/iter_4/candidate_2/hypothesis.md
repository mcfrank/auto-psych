People do not treat the examples as random draws from the program's output. They assume a helpful teacher picked them to be diagnostic, favouring telling numbers that few candidate stretches of the number line contain, namely those near the ends of 1-100. So a concept that contains many such telling numbers seems unlikely when none of them was shown ("if the program reached the 90s, I would have been shown one"). The concept's effective size is weighted by how diagnostic its members are, at a fitted strength, in place of the plain size principle. Everything else in the current best model is kept, including its round-number veto and upward response lean.
