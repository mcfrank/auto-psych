People infer the hidden program as an ideal concept learner does (simple, familiar rules and ranges weighed by how well they explain the examples), but they add one representativeness bias. Round numbers (multiples of 10) are a salient class of their own, so when none of the examples is round, a round target looks unlike anything the program has produced, and people say yes to it less often than the concept they inferred implies. When some example is round, round targets are judged by the inferred concept alone.
