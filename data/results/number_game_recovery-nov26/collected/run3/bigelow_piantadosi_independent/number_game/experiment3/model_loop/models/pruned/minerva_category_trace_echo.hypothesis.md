People do not infer a concept; they hold in long-term memory traces of familiar number categories (parity, primes, squares, cubes, powers, times tables, final digits, contained digits, repeated digits, and stretches of the number line), and the examples act as a memory probe that activates each stored trace in proportion to a steep power of its overlap with the examples (shared members over members in either, as in MINERVA 2 echo retrieval). They say yes to the target in proportion to how strongly the resulting echo — the activation-weighted blend of the retrieved traces — contains it, so broad categories are only weakly retrieved by a few examples and, unlike the size principle, the sharpness of retrieval does not grow with the number of examples shown.
