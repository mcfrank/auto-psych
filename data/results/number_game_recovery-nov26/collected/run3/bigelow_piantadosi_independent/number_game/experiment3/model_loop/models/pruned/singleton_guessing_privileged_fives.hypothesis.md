Refinement of the incumbent `fives_privileged_decoupled_prime_odd`, with all of its concept inference kept: primitive-built rules with per-primitive costs, the final-digit discount, power and square costs, graded times tables, a separate prime bonus and odd cost, the repeated-digit rule, privileged multiples of 5, hundred-chart ranges, the size principle and near-all-or-none commitment. The one change is taken from `single_example_guessing` and concerns the response stage: a lone example is too little evidence to work out a program, so on a fitted share of single-example trials people give up on inference and guess yes or no at even odds, while with two or more examples they answer from the inferred concept as before. After a lone example (e.g. 16 or 81) far-off targets therefore get more yeses, and near ones fewer, than the inferred concept implies, unlike the incumbent, which treats every set size alike.
