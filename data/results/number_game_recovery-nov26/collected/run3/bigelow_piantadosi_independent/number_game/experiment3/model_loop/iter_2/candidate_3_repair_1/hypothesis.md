Refinement of the incumbent `set_size_evidence_privileged_fives`, with all of its concept inference kept (primitive-built rules with per-primitive costs, final-digit discount, power and square costs, graded times tables, prime bonus and odd cost, repdigits, privileged multiples of 5, hundred-chart ranges, the size principle, set-size-graded evidence and near-all-or-none commitment). The one change is that people notice, example by example, that the program seems to skip round numbers (10, 20, ..., 100): each example that is not round is a separate chance to notice this, and once they have noticed it they reject a round target even if it fits the rule they inferred. So rejection of round targets grows with the number of non-round examples: it is weak after a lone 16 and strong after 32, 4, 16, 8, unlike a fixed round-number veto. Round targets after sets with any round example are judged by the inferred concept alone.
