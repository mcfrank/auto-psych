Refinement of the incumbent `fives_privileged_decoupled_prime_odd`, with everything in it kept (primitive-built rules with per-primitive costs, final-digit discount, power and square costs, times-table-graded multiples, separate prime bonus and odd cost, repdigit rule, privileged multiples of 5, hundred-chart ranges, the size principle and near-all-or-none commitment). The one change, taken from `set_size_graded_evidence_weight`, is how people pool evidence: agreeing examples are more convincing together than one at a time, so the weight each example carries grows with the set's size at a fitted rate. After a lone example people commit only weakly to a tight range or rule and generalise broadly, while three or four agreeing examples make them commit more sharply than an ideal learner would.
