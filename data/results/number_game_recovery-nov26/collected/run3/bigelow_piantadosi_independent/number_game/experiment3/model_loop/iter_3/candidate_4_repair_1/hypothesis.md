I refined the incumbent `round_landmark_veto` and kept all of it: the set-size-graded privileged-fives concept learner, plus the veto of round targets (10, 20, ..., 100) on a fitted share of trials when no example is round. The one change is how often people think an example is a stray number that the program did not really produce. The incumbent fixes this at 10%; here it is a fitted belief, and if people trust each example more than that, they will not read a set as a tight rule plus an outlier. Instead, every example must fit the concept, so after sets such as 92, 82, 27, 42 or 14, 98, 91 people fall back on rules or ranges that cover all the examples, rather than a narrow rule that leaves one example out.
