People can read a set of examples as "a number and its square" (for example 8 and 64, 9 and 81, or 4 and 16), so the pair made up of a number and its square is one more concept they consider for the program. It is priced like the other two-part rules, plus its own fitted prior cost or bonus, and is added to the current best model's concept search, which stays the same in every other way. Because the concept has only two members, the size principle makes it win whenever the examples are exactly such a pair. So after 8, 64 people say no to almost every other number, including other even numbers, multiples of 8 and powers of 2 such as 24, 32, 56 or 72, which the current best model predicts they accept.
