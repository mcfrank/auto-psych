Refinement of `costly_squares_final_digit_concepts`: people infer the hidden concept exactly as in that model (primitive-built rules with a per-primitive cost, a final-digit discount, extra costs on powers/cubes and on squares, Erlang-weighted intervals, the size principle), but their willingness to turn that belief into a "yes" is not constant: they become steadily more reluctant to say yes as the session goes on, as if growing skeptical, so their response bias shifts against "yes" at a fitted rate per trial. Early in the session people answer yes more readily than their belief alone implies, and late in the session less readily, even for the same set and target.
