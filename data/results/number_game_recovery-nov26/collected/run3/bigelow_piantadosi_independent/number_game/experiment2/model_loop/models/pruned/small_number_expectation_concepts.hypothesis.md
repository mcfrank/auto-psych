Refinement of `costly_squares_final_digit_concepts`: people infer the hidden concept exactly as in that model (primitive-built rules with a per-primitive cost, a final-digit discount, extra costs on powers/cubes and on squares, Erlang-weighted intervals, the size principle, near-all-or-none commitment). The one change is a small-number expectation: people believe a computer program is more likely to produce small numbers than large ones, so their readiness to say "yes" falls steadily with the target's magnitude at a fitted rate. For the same concept belief, a target in the 80s or 90s gets fewer yeses than one in the teens or 20s.
