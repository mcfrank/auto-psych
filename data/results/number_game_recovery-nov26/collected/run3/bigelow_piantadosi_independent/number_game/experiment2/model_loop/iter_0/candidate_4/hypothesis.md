Refinement of `costly_squares_final_digit_concepts`: people still build number concepts from mental primitives (per-primitive cost, "ends in d" discount, separate costs for powers/cubes and for squares, Erlang-weighted intervals, size principle, near-all-or-none commitment) and on a fraction of trials answer without consulting the concept. The one change is what those inattentive answers are: instead of a fixed coin flip, the default answer a disengaged person gives drifts with their position in the session, at a fitted rate, so that as fatigue sets in their fallback becomes "no". This predicts that the same (set, target) is answered yes less often late in the session than early, beyond what the stimulus order implies, most visibly for targets the concept leaves uncertain or rejects only weakly.
