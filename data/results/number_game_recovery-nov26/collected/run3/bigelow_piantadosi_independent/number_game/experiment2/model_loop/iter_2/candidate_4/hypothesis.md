Refinement of `hundred_chart_row_ranges` (the incumbent). Everything in it is kept: primitive-built rules with a cost per extra primitive, the final-digit discount, the costs on powers and squares, the times-table-graded multiples costs, Erlang ranges with a bonus for whole hundred-chart rows, the size principle and near-all-or-none commitment. The one change, taken from `prime_salient_concepts`, is that "prime numbers" is a familiar school-math category kept ready to hand, so it gets a fitted prior bonus over other compound rules. After all-prime sets (e.g. 2, 47; 31, 17, 61) people therefore commit to "primes" and say yes to other primes more than to odd composite numbers, which the incumbent fails to reproduce.
