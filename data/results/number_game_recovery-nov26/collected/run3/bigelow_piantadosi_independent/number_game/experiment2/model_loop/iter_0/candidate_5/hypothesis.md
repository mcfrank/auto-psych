Refinement of the pruned `final_digit_multiplicative_concepts` (concepts built from primitives with a per-primitive cost, an extra cost on powers/squares/cubes, a prior discount on "ends in d" rules, Erlang-weighted ranges, the size principle and near-all-or-none commitment). The one change is in the response stage. On a fitted fraction of trials people answer without consulting the concept they inferred, and the default answer they give then is not a coin flip: it drifts steadily from leaning "yes" early in the session toward leaning "no" late in it, at a fitted rate. This addresses the critique that people say yes less and less over the session, beyond what the order of stimuli implies. It predicts that targets the concept leaves uncertain or rejects get more stray yeses early and fewer late.
