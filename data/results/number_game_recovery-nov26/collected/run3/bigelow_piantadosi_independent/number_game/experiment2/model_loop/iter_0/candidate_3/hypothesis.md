Refinement of `costly_squares_final_digit_concepts`: people still build number concepts from mental primitives (a fitted cost per extra primitive, a discount for "ends in d" rules, separate extra costs for powers/cubes and for squares, Erlang-weighted intervals, the size principle and near-all-or-none commitment to the best-explaining concept). The one change is a fatigue-driven "when in doubt, say no" default that grows over the session: on a fraction of trials that rises (at a fitted rate) with how far into the session the trial is, people stop consulting their concept and simply answer no. So late in the session people reject even targets their concept contains (including the examples themselves), and the yes rate falls with trial position beyond what the stimuli explain.
