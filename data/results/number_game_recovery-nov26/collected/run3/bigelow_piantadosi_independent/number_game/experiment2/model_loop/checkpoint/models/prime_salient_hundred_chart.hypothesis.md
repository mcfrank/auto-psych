Refinement of `hundred_chart_row_ranges` (everything kept: primitive-built rules with a per-primitive cost, final-digit discount, costs on powers/cubes and squares, times-table-graded multiples, Erlang ranges with a bonus for whole hundred-chart rows, the size principle and near-all-or-none commitment). The one change, taken from `prime_salient_concepts`, is that "prime numbers" is a familiar school-math category kept ready to hand, so it gets a fitted prior bonus over other compound rules. So after all-prime sets such as 2, 47 or 67, 31, 17, 61, people commit to "primes": they say yes to other primes more than to odd composite numbers, which the current best model gets backwards.
