Refinement of `costly_squares_final_digit_concepts`, grafting the response stage of the pruned `no_default_lapse_simplicity_search`: people still build number concepts from mental primitives (a per-primitive cost, an "ends in d" discount, separate extra costs for powers/cubes and for squares, Erlang-weighted intervals, the size principle and near-all-or-none commitment to the best-explaining concept), and on a fitted fraction of trials they answer without consulting that concept. The one change is what those unconsidered answers are: not a coin flip but a learned default with a fitted base rate of "yes", expected to lean toward "no" ("when in doubt, say no"). So people sometimes reject even the examples themselves, while targets the concept excludes get fewer stray yeses than a coin-flip lapse predicts.
