Refinement of `uncertain_reliability_noise_concepts`: people do the same Bayesian concept learning over the 45 rules and all intervals of 1-100 (size principle, uncertain belief in how often examples are stray draws), but they also entertain digit-arithmetic concepts — "numbers whose digits add up to s", one concept for each digit sum from 1 to 18 — which together hold a small a-priori share of the rule prior (about 4%, inferred around that value) split equally among them. The one change is this enlarged concept space, which predicts that sets whose examples share a digit sum (e.g. 23, 41, 50, 14) draw "yes" for other numbers with that digit sum (32, 5, 68) even though no arithmetic rule, final digit or magnitude range explains them.
