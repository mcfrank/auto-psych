People answer "would the program produce this number?" by imagining the target as the program's next output and asking how expected it would be, not by checking whether it belongs to the concept they have inferred. They learn the concept by size-principle Bayesian inference over the 45 rules and all intervals of 1-100, unsure how reliable the program is. Then they say yes in proportion to how much more likely the target is as the next example than a random number, so members of tight concepts (64 after "16, 8") are endorsed more strongly than members of broad concepts that are just as certainly consistent (any even number after "16, 8"), and endorsement is graded rather than all-or-none.
