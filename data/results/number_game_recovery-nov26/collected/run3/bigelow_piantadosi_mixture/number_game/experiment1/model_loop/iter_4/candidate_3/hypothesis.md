Refinement of `uncertain_reliability_noise_concepts`: people do the same Bayesian concept learning over the 45 rules (each with its own inferred prior weight) and all intervals of 1-100 with the size principle, and they are just as unsure how reliable the program is on each set. The one change is what they think a stray example is: besides a number drawn at random from 1-100, a stray can be a slip, i.e. a member of the concept miswritten (one digit changed, added or dropped, or two digits swapped) so that it lands outside the concept, and how often strays are slips is inferred. So an example that is a slip of a tight concept's member (83 next to 38, 58, 68 under "ends in 8") is easily explained away and the tight concept survives, while an example that no slip of any member could produce counts against the concept as fully as before.
