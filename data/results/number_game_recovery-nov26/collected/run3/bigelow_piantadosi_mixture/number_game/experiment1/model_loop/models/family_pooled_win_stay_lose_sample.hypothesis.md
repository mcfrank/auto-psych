Refinement of `win_stay_lose_sample_concepts`: people learn the concept one example at a time, holding a single working guess (a rule or magnitude range picked by size-principle Bayesian concept learning) that they keep with some probability while it still contains each new example and otherwise re-pick from the posterior given all examples so far — so early examples shape generalisation more than later ones. The one change, taken from `family_pooled_rule_prior`, is the prior from which guesses are picked: rules are a priori plausible by kind (parity, primes, squares and cubes, powers, multiples, final digit, contained digit), each rule's weight drawn around a shared family level with an inferred spread, rather than an independently learned weight for every rule, so rules of the same kind (e.g. "contains a 3" and "contains a 7") are about equally likely to be adopted as the working guess.
