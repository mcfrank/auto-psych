People think of magnitude concepts not as arbitrary ranges of 1-100 but as bands of a characteristic width ("numbers around 60", "the teens"): a range is a priori plausible to the extent that its width is typical of such bands, with the typical width inferred, while mathematical rules (equally plausible, size principle, uncertain stray rate, strays sometimes familiar numbers) are learned as usual. So tightly clustered examples are readily read as a band and the non-members between them are filled in, whereas examples spread over most of 1-100 are not taken as a range at all and people fall back on the rule they share.
