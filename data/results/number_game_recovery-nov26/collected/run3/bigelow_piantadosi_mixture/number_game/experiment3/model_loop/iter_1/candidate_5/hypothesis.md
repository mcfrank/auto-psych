Refinement of `two_source_familiar_glitches`: people think the hidden program may run two sources at once, a main concept learned with the size principle and an uncertain belief about how many examples come from it, and a secondary source behind the off-pattern examples, which is either a second familiar rule they generalise from or glitches (sometimes familiar numbers, in proportion to how many well-known rules they belong to) that license no generalisation. The one change, taken from `range_limited_rule_familiar_strays`, is what the main concept can be: besides a rule over all of 1-100 or a magnitude range, the main program may apply a rule only within a magnitude range ("odd numbers from 9 to 33", "multiples of 4 from 52 to 88"), with an inferred share of the rule prior. So when the main pattern fits a rule and sits in part of the number line, people endorse its members inside the examples' span more than members far outside it, while an off-pattern example that fits a small rule still makes them accept that rule's other members.
