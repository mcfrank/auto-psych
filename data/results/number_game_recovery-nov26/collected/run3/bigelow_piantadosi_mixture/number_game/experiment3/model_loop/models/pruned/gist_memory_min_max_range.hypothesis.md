Refinement of `gist_memory_examples`, with one component taken from `min_max_bounded_range`. People infer the program by Bayesian concept learning over equally plausible rules, rules held within a range, and magnitude ranges (size principle, uncertain stray rate, strays sometimes familiar numbers), while keeping each example verbatim only with some probability and otherwise only its rough place on the number line. The one change is that people treat the smallest and largest example as the natural limits of a range, so "numbers from the lowest to the highest example" comes to mind far more readily than any other range. As a result, clustered examples that are partly remembered only as magnitudes lead people to fill in the rule's non-members between the outermost examples, but not beyond them.
