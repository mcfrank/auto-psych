People do not infer a hidden rule; they judge a target by its similarity to the examples, comparing numbers along a few dimensions (magnitude on the number line, parity, final digit, shared written digits, and sharing a common factor), and they focus their attention on whichever dimensions the examples agree on with one another. So a target is endorsed to the extent that it matches the examples on the dimensions along which the examples are mutually similar (e.g. same final digit after 83, 38, 58, 68; close magnitude after 61, 64, 67), with similarity on magnitude falling off exponentially with numerical distance and the sharpness of attention shift inferred.
