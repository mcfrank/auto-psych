Refinement of `familiar_strays_family_level_prior`: people do the same Bayesian concept learning over mathematical rules (plausible by kind of rule only) and magnitude ranges of 1-100, with the size principle and strays that are sometimes "familiar" numbers from well-known rules rather than random ones. The one change, taken from `first_example_anchored_reliability`, is that the first example shown anchors the concept: it is trusted to come from the concept at its own, inferred reliability, and only the later examples go into the uncertain judgement of how often the program produces strays. So whether a familiar or unremarkable outlier is written off depends on where it appears: shown first, it constrains the concept at the anchor's own trust, and the same set in another order generalises differently.
