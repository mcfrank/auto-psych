Refinement of `family_pooled_uncertain_reliability` (same Bayesian concept learning over the 45 family-pooled rules and all intervals of 1-100, with an uncertain belief in how often examples are strays). The one change is the sampling assumption: people are unsure whether the examples were sampled at random from the concept (so small concepts that fit gain strongly, the size principle) or were merely numbers the program happened to produce that are consistent with it (so a concept's size carries no evidence), and they weigh both possibilities, with how much they trust the first inferred. This predicts broader, size-insensitive generalisation than the incumbent except when the examples fit a tight concept so well that the random-sampling explanation dominates.
