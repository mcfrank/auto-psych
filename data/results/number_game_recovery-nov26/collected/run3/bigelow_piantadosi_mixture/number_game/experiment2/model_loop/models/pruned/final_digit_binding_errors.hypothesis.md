People infer the hidden concept by ordinary Bayesian concept learning over mathematical rules (plausibility set by kind of rule) and magnitude ranges of 1-100, with the size principle and an uncertain belief about how often an example is a stray, but when they check a target against a final-digit rule they make feature-binding errors: they register that the target contains the rule's digit and sometimes lose track of its position. So after examples that all end in the same digit (16, 26, 96), a target that has that digit only in the tens place (67, 34 after 63, 23, 43) is sometimes accepted as if it fitted the rule, with how often this happens inferred, while all other rules and ranges are checked exactly.
