People retrieve number concepts as in `graded_exemplar_concept_retrieval` (stored member lists compared by number-line closeness, rules plausible only by kind, an uncertain belief in how often an example is a stray), but when they answer they must check whether the target belongs to the retrieved rule, and that check is effortful and error-prone at a rate that depends on how hard the kind of rule is to compute. Easy checks (parity, final digit, contained digit) are nearly error-free, while hard ones (primes, multiples of 7 or 11, powers, squares and cubes) sometimes misfire: people reject true members and accept non-members far from any member, so endorsement under hard rules is diluted toward the middle independently of number-line distance.
