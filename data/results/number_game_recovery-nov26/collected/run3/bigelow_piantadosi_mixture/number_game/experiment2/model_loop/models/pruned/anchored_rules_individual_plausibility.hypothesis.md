Refinement of `first_example_anchored_reliability`: people do the same Bayesian concept learning over the 45 rules and all magnitude ranges of 1-100 (size principle, the first example anchoring the concept at its own inferred trust, the later examples pooled into an uncertain belief about how often the program produces strays), but their prior over rules is not flat within a kind of rule. The one change, taken from `family_pooled_uncertain_reliability`, is that each rule has its own plausibility, drawn around its family's level with a stated spread, so that particular rules (e.g. "even" over "odd", "powers of 2" over "powers of 7", "ends in 0" over "ends in 7") can be favoured or disfavoured beyond what their kind implies, which changes which rule an anchored first example points to.
