People form a graded belief that the target belongs to the hidden concept by simple Bayesian concept learning (rules and magnitude ranges, size principle, a fixed chance that an example is a stray), but they do not report that belief directly: they answer "yes" only when the belief clears a decision criterion, and that criterion varies from trial to trial. How strict the criterion is on average, and how much it varies, are inferred; a criterion that is equally likely to sit anywhere corresponds to probability matching, while a tight criterion produces near all-or-none answers around a fixed level of confidence.
