Refinement of `beta_share_width_exception_concepts`, with one component taken from the pruned `decade_concept_family_prior`. People do the same Bayesian concept learning over rules (family-organised prior, exception rules as their own kind) and magnitude ranges judged as a typical share of the 1–100 line, with the size principle. They also hold the decade as a ready-made concept ("single-digit numbers", "numbers in the teens", "numbers in the 60s"), which forms its own kind of rule with its own inferred plausibility. So when the examples share a tens digit (e.g. 63, 67 or 2, 8), people confine "yes" sharply to that decade, accepting 60 and 69 but rejecting 70 and 59. A free-floating range of the same width would give a smooth fall-off around the examples instead.
