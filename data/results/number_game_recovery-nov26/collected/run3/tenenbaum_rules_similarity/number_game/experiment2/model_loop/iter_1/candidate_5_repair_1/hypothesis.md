Refinement of `gamma_width_parity_range_concepts`: people do Bayesian concept learning over rules (family-organised prior, exception rules as their own kind), magnitude ranges and "odd (or even) numbers within a range", with the size principle. The one change, taken from `beta_share_width_exception_concepts`, is how they expect a range (plain or parity-restricted) to be wide: as a typical share of the bounded 1–100 line (a beta prior over that share, mean and sharpness inferred) rather than an unbounded gamma expectation over raw width, so near-total ranges are implausible and widely spread same-parity examples (e.g. 4, 66, 78, 96) point to "even numbers in the upper part of the line" rather than to all even numbers or almost any number.
