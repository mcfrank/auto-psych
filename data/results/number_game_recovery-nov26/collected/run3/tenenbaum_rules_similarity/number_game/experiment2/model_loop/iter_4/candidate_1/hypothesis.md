People read the examples in the order shown as a path along the number line, and when they think the program produces a range of magnitudes they expect that range to carry on in the direction the examples were moving (from the first example toward the last), extending further ahead of the path than behind it, to an inferred degree. Otherwise they do the same Bayesian concept learning as the best model (rules with a family-organised prior and exception rules as their own kind, ranges judged as a typical share of the 1–100 line, mixed strong/weak sampling). So the same examples shown in ascending order lead to more "yes" for numbers above them, and shown in descending order to more "yes" for numbers below them.
