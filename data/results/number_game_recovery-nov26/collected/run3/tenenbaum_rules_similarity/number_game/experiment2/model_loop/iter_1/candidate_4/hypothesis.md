Refinement of `beta_share_width_exception_concepts`: people infer the hidden concept by the same Bayesian concept learning (family-organised rule prior with exception rules as their own kind, magnitude ranges with a beta prior over the share of the 1–100 line a range covers, the size principle). The one change, taken from `prior_leaking_membership_judgment`, is in how they turn that inference into a yes/no answer: the log-odds of saying yes is the posterior log-odds that the concept contains the target, shifted by an inferred share of how plausible the target was as a member before any example was seen. So a priori typical numbers (mid-range, even, multiples) get more "yes" than the examples alone warrant (or less, if the share is negative), now measured against the bounded-share range prior rather than the unbounded gamma one.
