Refinement of `prior_leaking_membership_judgment`: people infer the hidden concept by Bayesian concept learning (rules with a family-organised prior, exception rules as their own kind, magnitude ranges, the size principle), and their yes/no judgment is shifted by an inferred share of how plausible the target was before any examples were seen. The one change, taken from `beta_share_width_exception_concepts`, is their expectation about how wide a range is: a beta prior over the share of the bounded 1–100 line a range covers, in place of an unbounded gamma prior over raw width. Near-total ranges are then implausible both when people infer the concept and in the prior plausibility that leaks into the judgment, so the leakage favours mid-range targets differently from the gamma version and widely spread examples point to a rule.
