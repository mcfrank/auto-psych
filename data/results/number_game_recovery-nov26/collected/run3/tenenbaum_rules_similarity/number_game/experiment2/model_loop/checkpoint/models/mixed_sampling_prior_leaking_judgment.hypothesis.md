Refinement of `mixed_strong_weak_sampling_concepts` with one component taken from `beta_share_width_prior_leaking_judgment`: people infer the hidden concept exactly as the incumbent's learner does (family-organised rules with exception rules as their own kind, magnitude ranges judged as a typical share of the 1–100 line, occasional stray examples, and only an inferred share of examples taken as random draws that drive the size principle). The one change is in the yes/no judgment: it is pulled by how plausible the target was before any examples were shown, so the log-odds of saying "yes" is the posterior log-odds that the concept contains the target, shifted by an inferred share of the target's prior log-odds of membership. A priori typical numbers (mid-range numbers that most moderately wide ranges contain, members of many rules) therefore get more "yes" than the examples warrant, or less if the share is negative, on top of the broad-concept generalisation that mixed sampling produces.
