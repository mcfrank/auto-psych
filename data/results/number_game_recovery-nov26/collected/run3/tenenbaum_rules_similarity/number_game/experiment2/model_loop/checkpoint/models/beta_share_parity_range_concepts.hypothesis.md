Refinement of `beta_share_width_exception_concepts` with one component taken from `parity_within_range_concepts`: people do the same Bayesian concept learning over rules (family-organised prior, exception rules as their own kind) and magnitude ranges judged as a typical share of the 1–100 line, with the size principle, but they can also entertain the conjunctive concept "odd (or even) numbers within a range" (e.g. "even numbers above 60") as its own kind of concept, with its own inferred plausibility and the same share-of-the-line width prior as plain ranges. So after 4, 66, 78, 96 people say "yes" to large even numbers (70, 74) but "no" to small ones and to odd numbers between the examples, and after 23, 35, 95, 83 they reject even numbers between the examples, rather than spreading "yes" over every number in the range or over all even (odd) numbers.
