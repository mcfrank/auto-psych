Refinement of `two_cluster_kind_doubt_criterion`, with one component taken from `mixed_strong_weak_sampling_concepts`. People do Bayesian concept learning over arithmetic rules (organised by kind, with exception rules as their own kind), single magnitude ranges and "two separate groups" concepts, all judged as a typical share of the 1–100 line, and they shift their yes/no criterion the more the examples leave open what kind of program it is (a kind of rule, one stretch of the line, or two separate groups). The one change is their sampling assumption: they take only an inferred share of the examples as random draws from the concept (the size principle) and the rest as mere members of it, so broad rules, wide ranges and wide two-group concepts stay in play after examples that also fit a narrower concept. This broadens "yes" to those broad concepts' other members and raises exactly the kind-level doubt that moves the criterion for those sets.
