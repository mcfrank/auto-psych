Refinement of `kind_uncertainty_shifted_criterion`: people infer the hidden concept exactly as its Bayesian concept learner does and shift their yes/no criterion with how unsure they are about what kind of program they face. The one change is how coarse that kind judgment is: the only question that moves their criterion is whether the program follows an arithmetic rule at all or picks out a stretch of the number line, so examples that leave rule-versus-range open shift answers toward "no" (or "yes"), while doubt among kinds of rule (say multiples versus final digit) does not shift them, unlike in the incumbent.
