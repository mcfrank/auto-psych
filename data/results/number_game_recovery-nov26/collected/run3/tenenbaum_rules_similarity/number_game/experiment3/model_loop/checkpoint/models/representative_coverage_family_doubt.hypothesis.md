Refinement of the live model `representative_coverage_sampling`, with one component taken from the incumbent `rule_family_doubt_criterion`. People infer the hidden concept as a Bayesian concept learner does but assume the examples are representative of the program's output, so they discount (or, if the inferred degree is negative, favour) concepts most of whose members lie outside the stretch the examples cover. The one change is the yes/no criterion: as in the incumbent, whatever doubt remains about which kind of arithmetic rule the program follows (weighted by how much they believe it is a rule at all) shifts their answer toward "no" (or "yes"), so the representativeness judgment both changes which concepts they generalise from and how much rule-kind doubt is left to move the criterion.
