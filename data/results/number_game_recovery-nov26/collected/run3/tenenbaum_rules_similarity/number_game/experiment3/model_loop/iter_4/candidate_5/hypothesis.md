Refinement of `prototype_magnitude_pull`, with one component taken from the incumbent `rule_family_doubt_criterion`. People infer the hidden concept as a Bayesian concept learner does and summarise the examples by a prototype (their average magnitude), so targets numerically close to that centre get extra "yes" even when no example lies near them. The one change is the yes/no criterion: as in the incumbent, doubt about which kind of arithmetic rule the program follows (weighted by how much they believe it is a rule at all) also shifts their answer toward "no" (or "yes"), so central targets of sets that leave several kinds of rule open are both pulled up by the prototype and pushed by the doubt-driven criterion.
