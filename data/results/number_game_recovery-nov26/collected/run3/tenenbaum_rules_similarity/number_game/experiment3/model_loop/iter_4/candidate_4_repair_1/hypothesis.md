Refinement of the incumbent `rule_family_doubt_criterion`, with one component taken from `mixed_strong_weak_sampling_concepts`: people infer the hidden concept as the incumbent's Bayesian learner does and shift their yes/no criterion with their doubt about which kind of arithmetic rule the program follows (weighted by how much they believe it is a rule at all). The one change is their sampling assumption: they take only an inferred share of the examples as random draws from the concept (the size principle) and the rest as mere members of it, so after examples that fit a narrow rule, broad rules of other kinds (e.g. all even numbers alongside powers of 2) stay in play — broadening "yes" to those rules' other members and raising exactly the rule-kind doubt that shifts the criterion for those sets.
