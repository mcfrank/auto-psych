Refinement of `kind_uncertainty_shifted_criterion` with one component taken from `mixed_strong_weak_sampling_concepts`: people infer the hidden concept by the same Bayesian concept learning (family-organised rules with exception rules as their own kind, ranges judged as a typical share of the 1–100 line, occasional stray examples) and shift their yes/no criterion with how unsure they are what kind of program they face (which family of rule, or a magnitude range). The one change is their sampling assumption: they take only an inferred share of the examples as random draws from the concept (the size principle) and the rest as mere members of it, so broad rules and wide ranges of other kinds stay in play after examples that also fit a narrow concept — broadening generalisation to the broad concept's members and raising exactly the kind-level doubt that moves the criterion for those sets.
