Refinement of `mixed_strong_weak_sampling_concepts`, with one component taken from `uncertainty_shifted_criterion`. People infer the hidden concept as the mixed-sampling learner does: rules organised by kind, magnitude ranges judged as a typical share of the 1–100 line, occasional stray examples, and only an inferred share of examples treated as random draws that drive the size principle, so broad rules such as "multiples of 3" stay in play. The one change is the yes/no criterion: the more their belief is spread over competing concepts, the further they shift away from the inferred membership probability, toward "no" (uncertainty aversion) or, if the inferred shift is negative, toward "yes". So two targets with the same membership probability get different answers depending on whether the examples point clearly to one concept or leave many open.
