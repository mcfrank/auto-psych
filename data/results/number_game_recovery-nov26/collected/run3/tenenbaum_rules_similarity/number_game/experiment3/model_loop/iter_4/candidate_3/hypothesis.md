Refinement of `rule_family_doubt_criterion`, with one component taken from `prototype_magnitude_pull`: people infer the hidden concept as the incumbent's Bayesian learner does and shift their yes/no criterion with how unsure they are which kind of arithmetic rule the program follows (weighted by how much they believe it is a rule at all). The one change is that they also summarise the examples by a prototype, their average magnitude, and their answer is pulled toward "yes" (or "no", if the inferred strength is negative) by the target's closeness on the number line to that average, falling off exponentially at an inferred rate — so targets near the centre of the examples (e.g. 50 after 13, 91) get more "yes" than the concept and the doubt-shifted criterion alone predict, even when no example lies near them.
