Refinement of the incumbent `rule_family_doubt_criterion`, with one component taken from `prototype_magnitude_pull`: people infer the hidden concept as the incumbent's Bayesian concept learner does and shift their yes/no criterion with their doubt about which kind of arithmetic rule the program follows (weighted by how much they believe it is a rule at all). The one change is that they also summarise the examples by a prototype, their average magnitude, so their answer is additionally pulled toward "yes" (or "no", if the inferred strength is negative) the closer the target lies on the number line to that prototype, with similarity falling off exponentially with distance — so targets near the centre of the examples (e.g. 50 after 13, 91) get more "yes" than the inferred concept and criterion alone warrant, while targets equally close to one example but far from the centre do not.
