People search for the hidden program in a fixed order of ease: they first ask whether the examples simply fall in some stretch of the number line, and they go on to look for an arithmetic rule (parity, multiples, powers, digits and so on) less thoroughly the better such a stretch already accounts for the examples (satisficing), to an inferred degree — or more thoroughly, if that degree is negative. Otherwise they infer the concept as a Bayesian concept learner does, so for tightly clustered examples rule members outside the cluster get fewer "yes" answers than full search predicts, while for widely scattered examples rules keep their full weight.
