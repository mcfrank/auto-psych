Refinement of `two_cluster_range_concepts`, with one component taken from the incumbent `rule_family_doubt_criterion`. People do Bayesian concept learning over arithmetic rules (organised by kind, with exception rules as their own kind), single magnitude ranges and "two separate groups" concepts (two ranges with a gap between them), all judged as a typical share of the 1–100 line and with the size principle. The one change is the yes/no criterion: only doubt about which kind of arithmetic rule the program follows, weighted by how much they believe it is a rule at all, shifts the answer from the inferred membership probability toward "no" (or "yes"), while doubt about whether the numbers form one stretch, two groups or a rule does not — so for widely split sets the two-group concept both carries generalisation away from the empty middle and, by drawing belief away from rules, weakens the criterion shift there.
