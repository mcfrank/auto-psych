Refinement of the incumbent `rule_family_doubt_criterion`: people infer the hidden concept exactly as its Bayesian concept learner does (exception rules such as "powers of 2 except 32" keep their own low prior plausibility), and doubt about which kind of arithmetic rule the program follows, weighted by belief that it is a rule, shifts their yes/no criterion toward "no" (or "yes"). The one change is what counts as a different kind of rule when judging that doubt: an exception rule is felt as a variant of the rule it amends, not as another kind, so wavering between "powers of 2" and "powers of 2 except 32" (or "plus 37") does not count as doubt about the kind of program. Powers-of-2 sets such as 16, 32, 2, 8 therefore get almost no criterion shift, where the incumbent treats them as leaving the kind of rule open.
