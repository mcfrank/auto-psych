Refinement of `range_first_satisficing_search`, with one component taken from the incumbent `rule_family_doubt_criterion`. People look first for a stretch of the number line that accounts for the examples and search for an arithmetic rule less thoroughly the better that stretch already explains them, otherwise inferring the concept as a Bayesian concept learner does. The one change is their yes/no criterion: the more doubt they are left with about which kind of arithmetic rule the program follows (weighted by how much they believe it is a rule at all), the further they shift their answer from the inferred membership probability toward "no" (or "yes"), so the satisficing search, by deciding how far rules stay in play, also decides how strongly rule-kind doubt moves the answer.
