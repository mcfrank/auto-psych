Refinement of `rule_family_doubt_criterion`, with one component taken from `digit_parity_concepts`. People infer the hidden concept as the incumbent's Bayesian learner does and shift their yes/no criterion with how unsure they are which kind of arithmetic rule the program follows (weighted by how much they believe it is a rule at all); the one change is that their repertoire of rule kinds also includes a digit-based kind — "numbers written only with odd digits" and "numbers written only with even digits" — with its own inferred plausibility. So after examples such as 31, 3, 1, 15 people say "yes" to 71 or 95 but "no" to 63 or 85, and sets that leave this digit kind open alongside parity or other rules carry more kind-of-rule doubt, shifting the criterion where the incumbent's does not.
