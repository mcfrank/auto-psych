Refinement of `uncertainty_shifted_criterion` with one component taken from `mixed_strong_weak_sampling_concepts`: people infer the hidden concept by the same Bayesian concept learning (family-organised rules with exception rules as their own kind, ranges judged as a typical share of the 1–100 line, occasional stray examples) and shift their yes/no criterion toward "no" (or "yes") the more their belief is spread over competing concepts. The one change is their sampling assumption: they take only an inferred share of the examples as random draws from the concept (the size principle) and the rest as mere members of it, so broad rules and wide ranges stay in play after examples that also fit a narrower concept — which both broadens generalisation to the broad concept's other members and raises the uncertainty that drives the criterion shift for exactly those sets.
