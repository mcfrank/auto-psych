People do not weigh every concept that could explain the examples; they answer by comparing two best explanations: the single most plausible concept (rule or magnitude range, judged with the same priors and size principle as the Bayesian learner) that includes the target, against the single most plausible concept that excludes it. They say "yes" with a probability that grows with how much better the best target-including explanation is than the best target-excluding one, so a target is accepted when one good concept contains it, regardless of how many other good concepts do or do not.
