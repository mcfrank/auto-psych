Refinement of the incumbent `parity_within_range_concepts`: people do the same Bayesian concept learning over rules (family-organised prior, exception rules as their own kind), magnitude ranges and "odd (or even) numbers within a range", with the size principle. The one change, taken from `gamma_width_exception_family_concepts`, is the shape of their expectation about how wide a range is: instead of a fixed, loose Erlang preference, the width prior is a gamma distribution whose sharpness is inferred along with its typical width. So people expect ranges (plain or parity-restricted) of a fairly sharply characteristic width, about half the number line, and disfavour both very narrow and near-total ranges more than the incumbent does.
