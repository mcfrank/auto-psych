Refinement of the incumbent `parity_within_range_concepts`: people do the same Bayesian concept learning over rules (family-organised prior, exception rules as their own kind), magnitude ranges and "odd (or even) numbers within a range", with the Erlang width preference and the size principle. The one change is that the conjunctive "rule within a range" idea extends beyond parity: people also entertain "multiples of m within a range" (e.g. "multiples of 3 between 10 and 60", "multiples of 9 from 27 to 81") as a further kind of concept with its own inferred plausibility and the same width preference. So after 39, 60, 12, 45 or 63, 27, 81 they accept multiples near the examples (e.g. 36, 54) but reject multiples far outside the examples' span (e.g. 90, 99, or 3), instead of spreading "yes" over all multiples of 3 or over every number in the range.
