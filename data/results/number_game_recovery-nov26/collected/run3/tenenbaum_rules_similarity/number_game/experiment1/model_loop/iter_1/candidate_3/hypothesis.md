Refinement of `rule_family_prior_concepts`: people do the same Bayesian concept learning over the 45 rules (with the inferred family-level rule prior) and all intervals, with "yes" the posterior probability that the concept contains the target. The one change is how strongly people believe the examples were sampled from the concept: instead of a fixed 90% (10% of examples random noise), the share of examples they treat as genuine samples from the concept is a free parameter inferred from the data, so the strength of the size principle — how sharply generalization narrows as consistent examples accumulate, and how readily an inconsistent example is dismissed as noise — is estimated rather than assumed.
