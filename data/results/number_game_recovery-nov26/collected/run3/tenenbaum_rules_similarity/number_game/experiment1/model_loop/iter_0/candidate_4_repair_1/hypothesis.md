A refinement of `bigelow_piantadosi_independent`: people do Bayesian concept learning over the same 45 independently weighted rules and all intervals of 1-100 with the size principle (10% of examples treated as noise), but their prior over magnitude ranges is not flat over endpoint pairs. Instead people expect a range of a characteristic size: the prior on an interval's width follows an Erlang distribution (as in Tenenbaum's number game) whose scale is inferred from the data, so ranges much narrower or much wider than that typical size are judged implausible a priori, changing how far "yes" extends around examples that suggest a magnitude range.
