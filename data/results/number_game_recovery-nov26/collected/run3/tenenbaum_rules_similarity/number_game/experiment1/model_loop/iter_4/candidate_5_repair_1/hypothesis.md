Refinement of `decade_concept_family_prior` (tied with the best): people do the same Bayesian concept learning over rules (family-organised prior, exception-laden rules as their own kind, and decades such as "numbers in the 60s" as their own kind of rule) and magnitude ranges with a preferred width and the size principle. The one change, taken from `parity_within_range_concepts`, is that people can also entertain "odd (or even) numbers within a range" as a kind of concept with its own inferred plausibility, so examples sharing a tens digit (61, 64, 67) confine "yes" to that decade while examples such as 4, 66, 78, 96 confine it to large even numbers — two ready-made ways of bounding generalisation that the incumbent and the decade model each have only one of.
