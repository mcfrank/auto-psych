People read the question "would the program produce this number?" generatively: instead of asking whether the target belongs to the hidden concept, they judge how likely the program is to produce that particular number as its next output (averaging over the concepts that explain the examples, each of which spreads its output thinly when it contains many numbers), and say "yes" in proportion to how that likelihood compares with the most likely next output, with an inferred sensitivity. So targets that only a large concept contains (any even number, anywhere in a wide range) get far fewer "yes" answers than their membership probability implies, while targets of a small, well-fitting concept are accepted.
