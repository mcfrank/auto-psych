A refinement of `bigelow_piantadosi_independent`: people do Bayesian concept learning over the same 45 rules and all intervals of 1-100 with independently weighted rules and the size principle, but how far they trust each example to come from the concept is not fixed at 90%. Instead the rate at which examples are believed to be random noise drawn uniformly from 1-100 is a free parameter inferred from the data, so that sets containing an "odd one out" (e.g. 8, 24, 48, 63) are explained by a rule plus an outlier to the degree people actually tolerate outliers.
