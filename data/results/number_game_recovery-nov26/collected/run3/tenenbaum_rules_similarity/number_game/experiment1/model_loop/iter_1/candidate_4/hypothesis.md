Refinement of `rule_family_prior_concepts`: people do the same Bayesian concept learning over the 45 rules (with the family-organised rule prior) and all intervals of 1-100, with size-principle sampling, but how strongly they treat the examples as drawn from the concept is not fixed at 90% — the share of examples they believe are genuine samples of the concept (versus random numbers from 1-100) is a free quantity inferred from their judgments. The one change is replacing the fixed 10% noise rate with this inferred sampling reliability, which sets how sharply each extra example narrows generalization to the smallest concept that fits (the critique shows people narrow with more examples differently from what the fixed rate predicts) and how readily a concept that misses one example is still entertained.
