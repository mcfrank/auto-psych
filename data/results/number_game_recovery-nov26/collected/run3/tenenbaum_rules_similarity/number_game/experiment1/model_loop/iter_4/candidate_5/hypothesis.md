Refinement of `gamma_width_exception_family_concepts`: people do the same Bayesian concept learning over rules and magnitude ranges (size principle, family-organised rule prior with exception-laden rules as their own kind, and a gamma prior over range width whose sharpness is inferred along with its typical width). The one change, taken from `parity_within_range_concepts`, is that people can also entertain a conjunctive kind of concept, "odd (or even) numbers within a range" (e.g. "even numbers above 60"), with its own inferred plausibility and the same sharply characteristic width preference as plain ranges. So after 4, 66, 78, 96 people say yes to large even numbers but no to small ones, and the ranges they generalise over — plain or parity-restricted — are of one characteristic width rather than extending loosely beyond the examples.
