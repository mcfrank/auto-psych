Refinement of `rule_family_prior_concepts`: people do the same Bayesian concept learning over the 45 rules, with the inferred family-level rule prior, and over intervals of 1-100, using the size principle (10% of examples random), with "yes" the posterior probability that the concept contains the target. The one change, taken from `erlang_interval_size_concept_learning`, is the prior over intervals: instead of every interval being equally likely, which makes wide ranges far more numerous a priori, people expect a range of a characteristic width (an Erlang prior over interval size with an inferred scale). Narrow, magnitude-based generalisation is then favoured from the first few examples, so adding examples narrows the range less than the flat-interval model predicts.
