A refinement of bigelow_piantadosi_independent: people do Bayesian concept learning over the same 45 rules and all intervals of 1-100, with a prior weight for each rule and a share lambda_0 for rules, and answer "yes" with the posterior probability that the concept contains the target. The one change is that how far people trust the examples to have come from the concept (the strength of the size principle, versus examples being random numbers from 1-100) is inferred from their judgments rather than fixed at 90%, so the data decide how strongly small concepts are favoured and how readily a concept is kept despite an example it does not contain.
