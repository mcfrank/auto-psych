Refinement of `rule_plus_exception_neighbourhood`: people do the same tempered, exception-tolerant Bayesian concept learning over the same 45 rules (final-digit rules salient), moderate-width ranges on a compressed number line, and "rule plus a small fuzzy neighbourhood exception" concepts ("ends in 2, and also numbers around 27", radius zero to three, smaller radii likelier), but when they code the off-pattern example as an exception they can alternatively code it at the place-value level, as the whole decade it falls in ("ends in 2, and also the twenties"), with a fitted share of the exception prior going to these rule-plus-decade concepts (grafted from `rule_plus_decade_exception`). The one change is this second, decade-level format for the exception: when one example breaks the others' pattern, people endorse its immediate neighbours on both sides and also its farther same-decade neighbours, while endorsing numbers a few steps away across a decade boundary less, and still extending the rule to distant numbers.
