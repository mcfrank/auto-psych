People do the same exception-tolerant, tempered Bayesian concept learning over the same 45 rules (final-digit rules salient) and moderate-width ranges on a compressed number line, but when one example breaks the pattern of the others they explain it not as a lone extra number but as the seed of a small numerical region the program also covers — "ends in 2, and also numbers around 27" — with the region's typical half-width fitted (a width of zero gives back the single-exception concept). This disagrees most sharply with the current best model on non-example targets just a few steps from an off-pattern example (e.g. 25-29 for 27 among 92, 82, 42): where the rule-plus-single-exception learner rejects them, this learner endorses them while still extending the rule to distant numbers.
