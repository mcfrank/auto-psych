Refinement of `rule_plus_exception_neighbourhood`: people do the same tempered, exception-tolerant Bayesian concept learning over the same 45 rules, moderate-width ranges on a compressed number line, and "rule plus a small fuzzy exception region" concepts. The one change, grafted from `family_salience_rule_prior`, is that their prior over rules is not just "final-digit rules are salient, all others equal": each family of rules (parity, final digit, contained digit, multiples, powers, special sets such as primes or squares) has its own fitted salience. Rules readable straight off the numeral's digits (parity, final digit, and also a digit the number contains) come to mind far more readily than arithmetic ones, so people extend a shared contained digit to distant numbers more, and extend a less familiar arithmetic regularity (such as "multiples of 7" for 14, 98, 91) less, than the incumbent predicts.
