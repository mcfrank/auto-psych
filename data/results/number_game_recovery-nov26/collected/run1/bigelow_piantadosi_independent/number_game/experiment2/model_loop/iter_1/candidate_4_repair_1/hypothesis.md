Refinement of `final_digit_salient_rules`: people do the same exception-tolerant, tempered Bayesian concept learning over the same 45 rules (final-digit rules salient) and moderate-width ranges on a compressed number line, but they treat the examples of a set as partly redundant evidence rather than independent draws, so each additional example adds less weight to the size principle than the one before (the strength of this discount fitted). The one change is this diminishing per-example evidence: with three or four examples people tighten their generalisation, and shift from broad rules and wide ranges to narrow ones, less than a learner who counts every example in full, while a single example is weighed just as the incumbent weighs it.
