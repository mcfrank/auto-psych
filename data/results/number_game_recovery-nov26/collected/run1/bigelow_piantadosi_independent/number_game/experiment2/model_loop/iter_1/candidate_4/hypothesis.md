Refinement of `final_digit_salient_rules`: people do the same exception-tolerant, tempered Bayesian concept learning over the same 45 rules (final-digit rules made salient) and Erlang-width ranges on a compressed number line, but when the target is one of the examples they were just shown, they also recognise it from memory as a number the program has already produced, and with a fitted probability answer yes on that recognition alone, whatever concept they inferred. The one change is this episodic recognition of shown examples: people endorse the examples themselves almost unanimously, even an off-pattern example their inferred concept treats as an exception, more than the incumbent's concept-membership belief predicts.
