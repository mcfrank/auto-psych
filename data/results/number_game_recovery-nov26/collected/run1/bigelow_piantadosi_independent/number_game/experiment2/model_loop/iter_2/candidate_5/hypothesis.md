Refinement of `final_digit_salient_rules`: people do the same exception-tolerant, tempered Bayesian concept learning over the same 45 rules (final-digit rules salient) and moderate-width ranges on a compressed number line, but their vocabulary also has "rule plus an exception range" concepts. These are a rule together with one short run of two to seven consecutive numbers ("ends in 2, and also the numbers around 27"), and they take a fitted share of each rule's prior, with shorter runs more plausible. The one change is these rule-plus-run concepts: when one example breaks the others' pattern, people treat the stretch around it as part of the program's output, not just the single number, so they endorse that off-pattern example and its close neighbours of either parity while still extending the rule to distant numbers.
