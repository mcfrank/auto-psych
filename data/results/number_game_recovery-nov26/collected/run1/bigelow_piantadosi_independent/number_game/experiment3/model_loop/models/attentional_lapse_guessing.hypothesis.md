People infer the program's concept exactly as the current best learner does (tempered, exception-tolerant Bayesian concept learning over rules, moderate-width ranges and example-anchored rule-plus-neighbourhood concepts), but their answer does not always come from that inference: on some trials attention lapses and they respond by guessing, so even numbers their concept clearly excludes get a residual rate of "yes" (a floor) and numbers it clearly includes get a residual rate of "no" (a ceiling below one), the two rates fitted separately. The decision rule, not the inference, is what changes: endorsement is compressed toward the middle by a constant, belief-independent guessing component, most visibly for the far-off and the clearly-in targets.
