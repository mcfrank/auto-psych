Refinement of `anchored_neighbourhood_side_channel`: people do the same tempered, exception-tolerant Bayesian concept learning (45 rules with salient final-digit rules, moderate-width ranges on a compressed number line, and a rule-plus-exception program whose side channel emits a fuzzy neighbourhood around an off-rule example), but on a fitted fraction of trials their attention lapses and they answer without consulting the inferred concept at all, saying yes at a fitted personal guessing rate. The one change is this concept-independent guessing on lapsed trials: it pulls every set's endorsement toward a common base rate, so sets differ less in overall generalisation breadth (tight sets get some stray yeses, broad sets some stray noes) than pure concept inference predicts, closing the critique that the incumbent exaggerates between-set heterogeneity.
