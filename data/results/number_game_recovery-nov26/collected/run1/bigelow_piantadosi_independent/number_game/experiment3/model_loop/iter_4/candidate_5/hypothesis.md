Refinement of `conservative_belief_reporting`: people infer the program's concept exactly as the best Bayesian concept learner does (tempered, exception-tolerant inference over salient final-digit and contained-digit rules, ranges on a compressed number line with a gamma-shaped width prior, and rule-plus-neighbourhood exceptions anchored on an off-pattern example), and their departure from that learner lies only in how they turn belief into a yes/no answer. The one change is that this report stage is not one conservative compression shared by everyone: each person brings their own stable response criterion, a personal lean toward "yes" or toward "no" that shifts every answer they give, with criteria varying across people by a fitted spread. So the same belief is endorsed consistently more by some people than by others across all their trials, and the population hedges toward fifty-fifty only through this spread of criteria.
