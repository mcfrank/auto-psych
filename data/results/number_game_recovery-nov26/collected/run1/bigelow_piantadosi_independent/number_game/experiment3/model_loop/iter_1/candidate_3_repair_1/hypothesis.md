Refinement of `anchored_neighbourhood_side_channel`: people do the same tempered, exception-tolerant Bayesian concept learning (45 rules with salient final-digit rules, moderate-width ranges on a compressed number line, and a rule-plus-exception program whose side channel emits a fuzzy neighbourhood around an off-rule example), but on some trials they answer without consulting the inferred concept at all — a stray "yes" at one fitted rate and a stray "no" at another. The one change is these concept-independent response slips: they put a floor under every set's endorsement and a ceiling over it, so sets differ less in overall generalisation breadth (tight sets get some stray yeses, broad sets some stray noes) than pure concept inference predicts, closing the critique that the incumbent exaggerates between-set heterogeneity.
