Refinement of example_anchored_exception_neighbourhood: people do the same tempered, exception-tolerant Bayesian concept learning over the same 45 rules (final-digit rules salient), moderate-width ranges on a compressed number line, and "rule plus a small fuzzy neighbourhood" concepts whose exception region is centred only on a shown example the rule fails to cover, but they picture such a program as a main rule with an occasional side-output: most of the time it emits a rule member and, at a fitted rate, a number from the exception neighbourhood, rather than drawing evenly from rule and neighbourhood pooled together (the two-channel sampling grafted from rule_with_exception_side_channel). The one change is this side-channel sampling of the anchored neighbourhood: a wider region around the outlier no longer weakens the evidence the rule-following examples give for the rule, so people credit a broader region around the off-pattern example and endorse its close neighbours even more than the anchored model predicts, while still rejecting off-rule neighbours of the majority examples (closing the critique's outlier-versus-majority neighbour gap).
