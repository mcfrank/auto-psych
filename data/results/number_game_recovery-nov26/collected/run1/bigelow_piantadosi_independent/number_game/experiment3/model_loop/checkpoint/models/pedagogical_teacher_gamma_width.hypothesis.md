Refinement of `pedagogical_teacher_sampling`: people assume the examples were picked by a helpful teacher who chose numbers that point unambiguously to the program (so an ambiguous "special" number such as 16 or 64 is strong evidence for the rule it is special for and weak evidence for a mere range around it), and otherwise do the same tempered, exception-tolerant concept learning over salient final-digit and other rules, ranges, and rule-plus-neighbourhood exceptions anchored on an off-pattern example and emitted through a side channel. The one change, grafted from `gamma_shaped_range_width_prior`, is their prior expectation of how wide a "range of numbers" program is: not the fixed Erlang shape that makes ranges of one or two numbers unlikely, but a gamma shape whose shape is fitted, so that when a helpful teacher shows unremarkable numbers people may read them as pointing to a tight range hugging the examples and generalise steeply to close neighbours, while special numbers still pull them towards the rules they exemplify.
