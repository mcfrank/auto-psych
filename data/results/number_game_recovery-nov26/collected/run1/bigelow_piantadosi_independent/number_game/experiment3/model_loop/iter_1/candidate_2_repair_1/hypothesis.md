Refinement of `anchored_neighbourhood_side_channel`: people do the same tempered, exception-tolerant Bayesian concept learning over the same rules (final-digit rules salient), moderate-width ranges on a compressed number line, and rule-plus-neighbourhood exceptions anchored on an off-pattern example and emitted through a side channel, but they treat the examples of a set as partly redundant evidence rather than independent draws, so the weight each example carries in the size principle changes with how many examples are shown (shrinking or growing at a fitted rate). The one change is this set-size-dependent evidence per example: a single example is weighed exactly as before, while with three or four examples people tighten their generalisation from broad rules and wide ranges to narrow ones less (or more) than a learner who counts every example in full, which changes how much sets of different sizes differ in breadth.
