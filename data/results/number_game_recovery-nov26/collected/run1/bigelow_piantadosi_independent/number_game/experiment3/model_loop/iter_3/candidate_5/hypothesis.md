Refinement of `range_plus_exception_side_channel`: people do the same tempered, exception-tolerant Bayesian concept learning (salient final-digit rules, ranges on a compressed number line, rule-plus-neighbourhood exceptions anchored on an off-pattern example and emitted through a side channel) and also entertain "range plus exception" programs ("numbers from 20 to 35, and now and then something around 87"), but their prior expectation of how wide a range is — for plain ranges and for the main range of a range-plus-exception program alike — is not the fixed Erlang shape that makes ranges of one or two numbers unlikely, but a gamma shape whose shape is fitted (grafted from `gamma_shaped_range_width_prior`). The one change is this free-shaped width prior: if people find tight ranges plausible, they generalise steeply to the close neighbours of a lone example or tight cluster, and when most examples cluster and one lies far away they fill in only a narrow stretch around the cluster, endorse the outlier's close neighbours, and reject the gap between them.
