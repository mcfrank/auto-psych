Refinement of `anchored_neighbourhood_side_channel`: people do the same tempered, exception-tolerant Bayesian concept learning (salient final-digit rules, moderate-width ranges on a compressed number line, and "rule plus exception" programs whose side channel emits a small fuzzy neighbourhood around an off-pattern example), but the main channel of such a "program plus exception" can also be a range of numbers, not only an arithmetic rule: "numbers from 20 to 35, and now and then something around 87". The one change is this range-plus-exception program, which takes the same share of prior belief, the same neighbourhood radii and the same side-channel rate as the rule version. So when most examples cluster on the number line and one lies far away, people fill in the cluster's stretch and also endorse the outlier's close neighbours, while rejecting the empty gap between them, instead of falling back on one wide range or on a rule.
