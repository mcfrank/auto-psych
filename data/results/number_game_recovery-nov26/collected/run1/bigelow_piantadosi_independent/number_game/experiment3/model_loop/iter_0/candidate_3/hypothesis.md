Refinement of `example_anchored_exception_neighbourhood`: people consider the same "rule plus a small fuzzy neighbourhood around an off-pattern example" concepts (anchored only on shown examples the rule misses) alongside the same rules and ranges, learned by the same tempered, exception-tolerant Bayesian inference, but when they weigh how well such a concept explains the examples they charge it in full for the rule and for the off-pattern examples they actually saw, and only partly (by a fitted fraction) for the never-shown numbers in the fuzzy region. The one change is this discounted size-principle charge on the exception region: a wider region around the outlier costs little evidence, so people endorse the outlier's close neighbours, falling off with distance, even more than off-rule neighbours of the majority examples (closing the critique's outlier-versus-majority neighbour gap), while still extending the rule to distant numbers.
