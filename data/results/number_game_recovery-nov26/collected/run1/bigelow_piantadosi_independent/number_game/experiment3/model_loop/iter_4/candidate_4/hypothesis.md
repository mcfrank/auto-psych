Refinement of `salient_contained_digit_rules`: people do the same tempered, exception-tolerant Bayesian concept learning (salient final-digit and contained-digit rules, ranges on a compressed number line with a gamma-shaped width prior, and rule-plus-neighbourhood exceptions emitted through a side channel), but individuals differ stably in how readily they think of the hidden program as an arithmetic or digit rule rather than a range of magnitudes. The one change is this person-specific prior share for rules versus ranges, fitted for each person: some participants consistently extend shared rules to distant numbers, while others consistently stay near the examples' stretch of the number line across every set they judge.
