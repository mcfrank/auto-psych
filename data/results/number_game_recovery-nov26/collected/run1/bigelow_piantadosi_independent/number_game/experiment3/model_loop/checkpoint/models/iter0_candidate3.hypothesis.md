Refinement of `example_anchored_exception_neighbourhood`: people do the same tempered, exception-tolerant Bayesian concept learning over the same rules (final-digit rules salient), moderate-width ranges on a compressed number line, and "rule plus exception" concepts whose exception is built only around a shown example the rule fails to cover, but that exception can be coded in one of two ways: as a small fuzzy neighbourhood around the off-pattern example (as before) or, at a fitted rate, as the whole decade it falls in ("ends in 2, and also the twenties"), the decade-level exception grafted from `rule_plus_decade_exception`. The one change is this place-value coding of the anchored exception: when one example breaks the others' pattern, people endorse its same-decade neighbours, even ones four to nine steps away, far more than off-rule neighbours of the majority examples, while rejecting its neighbours just across a decade boundary and still extending the rule to distant numbers (closing the critique's outlier-versus-majority neighbour gap).
