Refinement of `anchored_neighbourhood_side_channel`: people do the same tempered, exception-tolerant Bayesian concept learning (45 rules with salient final-digit rules, moderate-width ranges on a compressed number line, and "rule plus exception" programs whose side channel emits a fuzzy neighbourhood around an off-rule example), but on a fitted fraction of trials they do not consult the inferred concept at all and simply guess, with a fitted lean toward yes or no. The one change is this concept-independent guessing response: it pulls every set's endorsement toward a common baseline, so example sets differ less in overall generalisation breadth than the concept learner alone predicts (closing the critique that the incumbent exaggerates between-set heterogeneity), and confidently ruled-out targets get an occasional yes while confidently ruled-in ones get an occasional no.
