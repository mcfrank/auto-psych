Refinement of `iter0_candidate3`: people do the same tempered, exception-tolerant Bayesian concept learning over the same rules (final-digit rules salient) and moderate-width ranges on a compressed number line, and they also form "rule plus exception" concepts whose exception region is built only around a shown example the rule fails to cover — a small fuzzy neighbourhood around it, or its whole decade, the decade treated simply as the next-larger exception region, with one graded preference for smaller regions over all of them. The one change, grafted from `anchored_neighbourhood_side_channel`, is that they picture such a program as a main rule with an occasional side-output: at a fitted rate it emits a member of the exception region, and otherwise a rule member, instead of drawing evenly from rule and region pooled together. Because a whole-decade exception then no longer weakens the evidence the rule-following examples give for the rule, people credit the off-pattern example's decade more readily, endorsing its same-decade neighbours (even four to nine steps away) over off-rule neighbours of the majority examples while rejecting its neighbours just across a decade boundary.
