Refinement of `bigelow_piantadosi_mixture`: people infer the concept exactly as in that model (Bayesian concept learning over 45 equally likely rules and all intervals, with the size principle), but they are conservative endorsers — even when the inferred concept contains the target, they commit to "yes" only on a fixed fraction of occasions and otherwise answer "no". The one change is this no-biased response stage, which scales every posterior membership probability down by a single commitment probability (so even targets shown as examples get "yes" only about three times in four, and partial-belief targets near examples or off a parity/last-digit rule are endorsed less).
