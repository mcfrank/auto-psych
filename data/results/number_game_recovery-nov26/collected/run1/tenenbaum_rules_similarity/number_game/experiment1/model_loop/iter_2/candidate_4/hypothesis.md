Refinement of `size_principle_strength_family_prior`: people do the same Bayesian concept learning over rules and intervals, with a prior mass per concept family and a learned strength of the size principle, but they do not treat gerrymandered "exception" rules (powers of 2 without 32, powers of 2 plus 37) as just as natural as plain mathematical sets like powers of 2 or squares. The one change is that these ad-hoc exception rules form their own concept family with their own inferred (expectedly small) prior mass, so after seeing powers of 2, people rarely say the program would produce the lure 37.
