Refinement of `exception_rule_family_prior_2`: people do the same Bayesian concept learning over rules and numeric intervals, with the size principle and a prior mass per concept family (the exception rules in their own family), but their hypothesis space also contains conjunctive "parity within a range" concepts — "even numbers between 20 and 60", "odd numbers from 9 to 69" — as a new family with its own inferred prior mass. The one change is this composed rule-plus-magnitude family: when examples are all even (or all odd) and clustered in magnitude, people generalise to same-parity numbers near the examples while rejecting both nearby numbers of the other parity and same-parity numbers far outside the examples' range, which neither a pure parity rule nor a pure interval predicts.
