People learn the hidden program's concept by Bayesian inference over natural rules and numeric ranges (with the size principle and a separate prior plausibility for each family of concepts), and their space of candidate rules also includes arithmetic progressions that do not start at zero — "every k-th number starting from r", such as 4, 11, 18, 25, … or the odd multiples of 3 — as their own family with its own prior plausibility. When the examples are evenly spaced by a common step, people therefore endorse targets that continue the same progression and reject nearby targets that fall off it.
