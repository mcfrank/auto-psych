People do not infer a hidden rule; they store the shown examples as exemplars and judge a target by its summed similarity to them, as in an exemplar (generalized context) model. Each number is represented on three psychological dimensions — its magnitude, its parity, and its final digit — and similarity to an example decays exponentially with magnitude distance and with each mismatch in parity or final digit; the probability of "yes" is the summed similarity relative to a fixed background response criterion.
