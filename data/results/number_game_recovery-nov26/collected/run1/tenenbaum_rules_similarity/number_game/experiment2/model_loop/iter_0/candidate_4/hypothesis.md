Refinement of `erlang_interval_size_family_prior`: people do the same Bayesian concept learning over rules and numeric intervals, with the size principle, a prior mass per concept family (exception rules in their own family) and an Erlang prior over interval width, but how much they trust the examples to come from the hidden concept is not fixed at 90%; it is an inferred parameter. The one change is this learned example reliability (the component from `family_weighted_learned_reliability`, now added to the Erlang-interval model): the less people trust the examples, the more readily a concept that misses one example survives, so they generalise from the consistent majority of a set and hesitate even on targets that were themselves shown as examples.
