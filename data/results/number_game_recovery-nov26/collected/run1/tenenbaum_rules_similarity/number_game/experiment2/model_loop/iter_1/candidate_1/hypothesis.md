People infer the hidden program exactly as in the best current model (Bayesian concept learning over number rules, bounded ranges and one-sided thresholds), but they answer by recall rather than by reading off their belief: a few candidate programs come to mind, sampled from their beliefs, and they say "yes" if any one of them would produce the target. The one change is this liberal any-of-several decision rule, with the effective number of programs considered inferred from the data. It raises "yes" most for targets that only a minority of plausible programs contain, and leaves targets that every or no plausible program contains unchanged; one program considered recovers probability matching.
