Refinement of `set_size_amplified_size_principle`, adding one component from `digit_sum_concepts_threshold_erlang`. People do the same Bayesian concept learning over number rules, bounded ranges (with an Erlang prior on width) and one-sided thresholds, with a prior mass per concept family and a size principle whose per-example weight grows with how many examples were shown. The one change is that their space of candidate programs also includes digit-sum concepts — "numbers whose digits add up to s" (e.g. 5, 14, 23, 32, 41, 50) — as a new family with its own inferred prior mass shared equally among the 18 sums, so when the examples share a digit sum people endorse other numbers with that sum (and reject neighbours with a different sum) more than the parent predicts, and this reading sharpens with more examples faster than an ideal learner's would.
