Refinement of `set_size_amplified_size_principle`, adding one component from `threshold_concepts_primes_family`. People do the same Bayesian concept learning over number rules, bounded ranges (with an Erlang prior on width) and one-sided thresholds, with a prior mass per concept family and a size principle whose per-example weight grows with how many examples were shown. The one change is that "prime numbers" leaves the shared special-sets family (squares, cubes, powers) and becomes its own family with a separately inferred prior mass, so prime-like sets such as 3, 7 or 53, 2 are read as "primes" (endorsing other primes, rejecting nearby composites) independently of how plausible squares or powers are, and this reading tightens with more prime examples faster than an ideal learner's would.
