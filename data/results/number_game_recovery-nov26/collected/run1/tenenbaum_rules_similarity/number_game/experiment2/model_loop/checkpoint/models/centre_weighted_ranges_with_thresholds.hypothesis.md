Refinement of `threshold_concepts_erlang_interval`, adding one component from `centre_weighted_range_sampling`. People do the same Bayesian concept learning over number rules, bounded ranges (with an Erlang prior on width) and one-sided thresholds (their own family), with the size principle and a prior mass per concept family. The one change is that people expect a bounded-range program ("numbers between 30 and 50") to produce numbers clustered toward the middle of its range, while a threshold program ("numbers above 60") still produces its members evenly. How strongly ranges cluster is inferred from the data, and zero recovers the incumbent. An example at a range's edge is then a suspicious coincidence, so ranges centred on the examples that reach past them gain belief relative to thresholds and rules.
