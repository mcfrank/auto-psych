Refinement of `graded_prior_digit_sum_concepts`, adding one component from `centre_weighted_ranges_primes_family`: people do the same Bayesian concept learning over number rules, digit-sum rules (with a smoothly graded prior over the sum) and centre-weighted numeric ranges with an Erlang width prior, the size principle and a prior mass per concept family. The one change is that "prime numbers" is no longer lumped with squares, cubes and powers in a shared special-sets family but forms its own family with a separately inferred prior mass, so how readily people read prime-like sets as "primes" — endorsing other primes and rejecting nearby composites — no longer depends on how plausible squares or powers are.
