Refinement of `centre_weighted_ranges_with_thresholds`, adding one component from `round_number_range_boundaries`. People do the same Bayesian concept learning over number rules, bounded ranges (Erlang width prior, outputs expected to cluster toward the range's middle) and one-sided thresholds, with the size principle and a prior mass per concept family; the one change is that multiples of ten act as reference points in judging how plausible a bounded range is a priori, so a range whose lower or upper edge falls exactly on a multiple of ten is judged more or less likely than an otherwise identical range, with the direction and strength of this bias inferred from the data. Centre-weighting decides how far past the examples a range is expected to reach, and the round-number bias decides whether "yes" answers stop just before or exactly at a nearby multiple of ten (e.g. whether 40 is endorsed after examples in the low thirties).
