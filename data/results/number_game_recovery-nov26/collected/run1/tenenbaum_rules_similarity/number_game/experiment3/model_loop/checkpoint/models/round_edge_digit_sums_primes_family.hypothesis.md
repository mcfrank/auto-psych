Refinement of `round_edge_ranges_digit_sums`, adding one component from `centre_weighted_ranges_primes_family`. People do the same Bayesian concept learning over number rules, digit-sum rules ("numbers whose digits add up to s") and numeric ranges, with an Erlang width prior, range outputs clustered toward the middle, the size principle, a prior mass per concept family, and multiples of ten acting as preferred or avoided range edges. The one change is that "prime numbers" is no longer lumped with squares, cubes and powers in a shared special-sets family. Primes form their own family with a separately inferred prior mass, so how readily people read prime-like sets (e.g. 3, 7 or 53, 2) as "primes", endorsing other primes and rejecting nearby composites, no longer depends on how plausible squares or powers are.
