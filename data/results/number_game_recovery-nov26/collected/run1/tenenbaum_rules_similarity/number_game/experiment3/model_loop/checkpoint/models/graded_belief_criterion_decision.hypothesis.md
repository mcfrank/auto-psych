People infer the hidden program as an ideal Bayesian concept learner would (number rules, bounded ranges with a typical width, and one-sided thresholds, with the size principle and examples drawn without repetition), but they do not simply say "yes" with the probability they believe the target belongs. Instead they weigh how strongly they believe it belongs against a personal response criterion, with some response noise: the criterion sets a bias toward "yes" or "no", and the noise sets whether answers are more decisive than the belief (a soft threshold) or more hedged than it, so partly-believed targets are pushed toward or away from the extremes in the same way for every set.
