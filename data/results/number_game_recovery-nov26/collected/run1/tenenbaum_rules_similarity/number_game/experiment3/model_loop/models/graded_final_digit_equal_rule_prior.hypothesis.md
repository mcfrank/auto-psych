Refinement of `equal_prior_familiar_rules_digit_sums`, adding one component from `graded_final_digit_digit_sum_concepts`: people do the same Bayesian concept learning over equally plausible familiar number rules, digit-sum rules (prior changing smoothly with the sum) and numeric ranges (Erlang width prior, outputs clustered toward the middle, the size principle). The one change is that the "ends in d" rules are not equally natural to each other. They keep the same total share of belief as before, but within that share a rule's prior plausibility changes smoothly with its final digit, with the direction and strength inferred from the data. So after examples that share a final digit, how readily people go on to endorse other numbers ending in that digit, rather than a range around the examples, depends on which digit it is.
