Refinement of `last_example_recency_weight`. People infer the program as that model says: Bayesian concept learning over equally plausible familiar rules, digit-sum rules and ranges, with the size principle, and the last example given its own evidential weight. The one change is in how they answer. How decisively belief becomes a yes or no depends on how many examples were shown. After more examples, people feel better informed and push their answers toward the extremes of their belief, so confident targets are endorsed or rejected more firmly. After a single example they hedge toward 50/50, so the same strength of belief gives a less extreme yes rate. The direction and strength of this effect are inferred from the data, and no effect recovers the parent.
