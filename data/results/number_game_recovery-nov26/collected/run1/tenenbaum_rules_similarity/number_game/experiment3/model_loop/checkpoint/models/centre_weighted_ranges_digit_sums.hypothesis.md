Refinement of `centre_weighted_range_sampling`, adding one component from `digit_sum_concepts_threshold_erlang`: people do the same Bayesian concept learning over number rules and numeric ranges, with the size principle, a prior mass per concept family (exception rules in their own family), an Erlang prior over range width, and range outputs expected to cluster toward the middle of the range. The one change is that the programs people consider also include digit-sum concepts, "numbers whose digits add up to s" (e.g. 5, 14, 23, 32, 41, 50), as a new family with its own inferred prior mass shared equally among the 18 possible sums. Because these concepts are tiny, examples that happen to share a digit sum are a suspicious coincidence, so people endorse other numbers with that sum, and reject nearby numbers with a different sum, more than the incumbent predicts.
