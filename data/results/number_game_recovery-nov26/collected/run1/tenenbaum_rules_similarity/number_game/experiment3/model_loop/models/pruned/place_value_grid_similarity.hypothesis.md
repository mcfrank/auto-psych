People do not infer a rule; they represent each number by its written place-value form — a tens digit and a units digit, i.e. a point on a 10-by-10 grid — and judge a target by its average similarity to the examples, where similarity decays exponentially with the city-block distance between the target and an example on that grid, with a separate attention weight on the tens and the units dimension. So a target is endorsed when it shares (or nearly shares) the examples' decade and final digit, e.g. 43 after 13, 23 is close on the units axis even though it is far in magnitude, while 44 is a near miss and 47 is far.
