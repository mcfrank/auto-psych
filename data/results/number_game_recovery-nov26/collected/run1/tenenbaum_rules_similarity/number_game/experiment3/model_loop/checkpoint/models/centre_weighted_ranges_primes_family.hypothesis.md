Refinement of `centre_weighted_range_sampling`, adding one component from `without_replacement_primes_family`: people do the same Bayesian concept learning over number rules and numeric ranges, with the size principle, a prior mass per concept family (exception rules in their own family), an Erlang prior over range width and range outputs expected to cluster toward the middle of the range. The one change is that "prime numbers" is no longer lumped with squares, cubes and powers in a shared special-sets family but forms its own family with a separately inferred prior mass, so how readily people read prime-like sets (e.g. 3, 7 or 53, 2) as "primes" — endorsing other primes and rejecting nearby composites — no longer depends on how plausible squares or powers are.
