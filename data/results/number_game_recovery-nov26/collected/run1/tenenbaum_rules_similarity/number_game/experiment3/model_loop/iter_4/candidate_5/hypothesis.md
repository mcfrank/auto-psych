Refinement of `equal_prior_rules_round_edge_ranges`: people do the same Bayesian concept learning over equally plausible familiar number rules, digit-sum rules and numeric ranges (Erlang width prior with round-number edges, the size principle), but they are not all alike in how readily they think of the program as a magnitude range rather than a number rule. The one change is that each person has their own prior odds of ranges versus rules, drawn from a population distribution whose mean and spread are inferred, so the same set is read as "multiples of 7" or "ends in 3" by rule-seekers and as a band of nearby numbers by magnitude-seekers, while how the examples weigh programs within each kind is the same for everyone.
