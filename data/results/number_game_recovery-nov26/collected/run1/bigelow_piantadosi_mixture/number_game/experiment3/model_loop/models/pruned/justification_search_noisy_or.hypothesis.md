People do not weigh competing explanations against one another; they answer by searching memory for any familiar explanation (a rule such as "even" or "ends in 6", or a numerical range) that covers both the examples and the target, and say "yes" as soon as one comes to mind. Each explanation comes to mind with a chance that grows with how well it accounts for the examples on its own (how plausible it is and how surprising the examples would be without it), so a target is endorsed by the absolute evidence for explanations that include it, not by its share relative to better explanations that exclude it — e.g. after 16, 8, 2, 64, "even" can still come to mind and license 10, even though "powers of 2" fits far better.
