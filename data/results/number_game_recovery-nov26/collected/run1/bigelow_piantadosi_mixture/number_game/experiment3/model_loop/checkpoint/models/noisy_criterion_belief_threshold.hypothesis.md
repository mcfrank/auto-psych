People infer the concept as the simplest current sticky-guess learner does, but they do not answer by probability matching: they compare how strongly they believe the target belongs against a fixed internal criterion of how sure they must be, and that comparison is perturbed by trial-to-trial noise in the felt belief, so they say "yes" with a probability that rises smoothly (logistically) with belief strength around the criterion. This noisy criterion produces both a ceiling below 1 for certain members (the examples themselves) and a floor above 0 for clear non-members, and makes moderately believed targets fall clearly on one side of the criterion rather than being endorsed in proportion to belief.
