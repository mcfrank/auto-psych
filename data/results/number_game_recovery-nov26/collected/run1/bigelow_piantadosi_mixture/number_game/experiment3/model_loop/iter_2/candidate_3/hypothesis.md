Refinement of the incumbent gamma_width_range_prior_guess (a single working concept guess — a rule or a numerical range with a fitted-shape gamma width prior — formed from the first example by Bayesian concept learning, kept with fitted stickiness, digit rules discounted and salient only once two examples share the digit, a newly noticed coincidence triggering a rethink, fitted trust, and misfits written off more readily the more examples the guess explains). The one change: when people's working guess is a numerical range, its edges are fuzzy rather than sharp — a target just outside the guessed range is still judged a possible output, with willingness falling off exponentially with its distance beyond the nearer edge at a fitted reach, while rules keep crisp membership. So after clustered examples such as 24 and 35, numbers a few steps past the edges (19, 40) are endorsed more than a sharp-edged range predicts, without raising endorsement of far rule members.
