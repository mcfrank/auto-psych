Refinement of compressed_concept_size_sticky_guess (a single sticky working guess — a rule or a numerical range — formed from the first example by Bayesian concept learning, digit rules discounted and made salient only once two examples share the digit, fitted trust in the examples, and concept sizes perceived on a compressed, numerosity-like scale). The one change, the component taken from coincidence_triggered_guess_rethink: noticing a coincidence is itself a trigger to rethink — when a new example shares a digit with an earlier one for the first time, people drop their current guess with a fitted probability and reconsider the concept from all the examples, even if the guess still fits, and that reconsideration uses the same compressed sense of concept size. So after 46 then 86 a range guess formed from 46 is often replaced by "ends in 6", and because "ends in 6" does not feel as much smaller than a broad range as it really is, the rethought concept is broader than a normative learner's.
