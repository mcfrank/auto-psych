Refinement of the incumbent evidence_graded_misfit_tolerance (a single working concept guess — a rule or a numerical range — formed from the first example by Bayesian concept learning, kept with fitted stickiness while later examples fit it, digit rules discounted and salient only once two examples share the digit, a newly noticed coincidence triggering a rethink, fitted trust, and a contradicting example written off as a stray more readily the more earlier examples the guess explains). The one change: the typical width people expect of a numerical range is not fixed but grows with the magnitude of the examples seen so far, as Weber's law predicts for the mental number line (scaling exponent fitted, expected near proportional). So after 82 and 92 people imagine a broader range and generalise further on the number line than after 2 and 12, and range guesses compete with rules more for large numbers than for small ones.
