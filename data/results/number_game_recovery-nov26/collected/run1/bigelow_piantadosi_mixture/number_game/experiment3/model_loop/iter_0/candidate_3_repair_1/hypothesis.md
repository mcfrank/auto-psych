Refinement of the incumbent misfit_tolerant_coincidence_rethink. The incumbent holds a single working concept guess, a rule or a numerical range, formed from the first example by Bayesian concept learning. The guess is kept with fitted stickiness while later examples fit it; digit rules are discounted and become salient only once two examples share a digit; a newly noticed coincidence triggers a rethink; trust in the examples is fitted; and a misfitting example is sometimes written off as a stray. The one change: how readily a contradicting example is written off depends on how well established the guess is. Each earlier example the guess explains independently gives a fitted chance of dismissing the misfit. A guess supported by one example is then easily abandoned, one supported by two or three examples (77, 17 → "ends in 7") usually survives an outlier, and a guess supported by none is never protected. (The incumbent's weakly constrained preference for ranges at particular places on the number line is dropped as a simplification; ranges keep their preferred-width prior.)
