People infer the program's concept as the simple sequential learner does (a single sticky working guess — a rule or a numerical range — formed from the first example, revised as examples arrive, with misfits written off more readily the more evidence the guess has) and say "yes" whenever the target belongs to that guess; but their decision rule is acquiescent: when the target falls outside their guess they do not reliably say "no", and instead answer "yes" anyway with a fitted probability. This one-sided yes-bias gives every non-member a common floor of endorsement while leaving endorsement of members of the guess untouched.
