Refinement of the incumbent gamma_width_distance_hesitant_guess (a single sticky working concept guess — a rule or a numerical range with a fitted gamma-shaped width prior — formed from the first example by Bayesian concept learning, digit rules salient once two examples share the digit, coincidences triggering a rethink, fitted trust, evidence-graded misfit tolerance, and a smooth reluctance to extend the guess far from the examples). The one change: the reluctance is about extrapolation, not interpolation — people hesitate only to extend their concept beyond the stretch of the number line the examples already cover, so willingness falls off with the target's distance outside the examples' span (smallest to largest example), while members lying between the examples (32 after 16, 8, 64; 50 after 5, 20, 92) are endorsed as fully as membership implies; for a single example this is the same as the incumbent.
