People infer the program's concept as the current best learner does (a single sticky working guess — a rule or a numerical range — formed from the first example, with digit rules salient once two examples share a digit, coincidences triggering a rethink, and misfits written off more readily the more evidence the guess has), but they hesitate to extrapolate: even when the target belongs to their guessed concept, their willingness to say the program would produce it falls off smoothly (Gaussian-like) with how far the target lies on the number line from the nearest example they have seen, at a fitted reach. So far-away members of a rule — 96 after a lone 16, 64 after a lone 16 — are endorsed less than concept membership alone predicts, while members close to an example are endorsed fully.
