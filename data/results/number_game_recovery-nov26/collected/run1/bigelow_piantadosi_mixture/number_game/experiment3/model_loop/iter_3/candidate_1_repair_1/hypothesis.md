People infer which concept (a familiar rule such as "powers of 2" or "ends in 6", or a numerical range) produced the examples by weighing all the examples together, but whether they say the program would produce a target depends on how similar it is to the nearest example, judged by ratio on a logarithmically compressed mental number line rather than by raw difference: willingness falls off smoothly with the log-ratio distance at a fitted reach. So the same numerical gap is a small step among large numbers (90 after 80) but a long leap among small ones (12 after 2), and a doubling or halving (32 or 8 after 16) counts as the same step at any magnitude.
