Refinement of the best model, gamma_width_distance_hesitant_guess. Concept learning is unchanged: a single sticky working guess (a rule, or a range with a gamma-shaped prior on its width) formed from the first example, digit rules salient once two examples share a digit, a newly noticed coincidence triggering a rethink, and misfits written off more readily the more examples the guess explains. People still hesitate to extend their guessed concept to numbers far from the examples. The one change is that this willingness is anchored on the examples in proportion to how recently each was shown, not on whichever example is nearest. The example just shown fully licenses nearby targets, and each earlier example licenses them less, by a fitted fading factor per intervening example. So the same examples in a different order produce different gradients: after 20, 80, a target near 80 is endorsed more than one near 20, and after 80, 20 the opposite holds.
