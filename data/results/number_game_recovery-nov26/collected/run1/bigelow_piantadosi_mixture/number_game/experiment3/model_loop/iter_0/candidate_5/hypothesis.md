Refinement of compressed_concept_size_sticky_guess (a single sticky working guess — a rule or a numerical range — formed from the first example by Bayesian concept learning, digit rules discounted and made salient only once two examples share the digit, fitted trust in the examples, and concept sizes perceived on a compressed, numerosity-like scale). The one change, the component taken from misfit_tolerant_coincidence_rethink: a guess is not automatically abandoned when a new example contradicts it — with a fitted tolerance people write the misfitting example off as a stray number and keep their guess. So the softened size principle and the tolerance of outliers together let a broad-ish early guess survive a later example that breaks it, rather than forcing a rethink from all the examples.
