Refinement of the incumbent span_extrapolation_hesitant_guess_2 (a single sticky working concept guess, either a rule or a numerical range with a fitted gamma-shaped width prior, formed from the first example by Bayesian concept learning; digit rules salient once two examples share the digit; coincidences triggering a rethink; fitted trust; evidence-graded misfit tolerance; and reluctance to extend the concept beyond the span the examples cover). The one change: only rule guesses carry that reluctance to extrapolate. A range guess already states where on the number line the program's numbers lie, so a target inside a guessed range is endorsed as fully as membership implies, even beyond the examples' span. Extending a rule such as "powers of 2" or "ends in 6" to a member past the examples' span (64 after 16 and 8, or 96 after 46 and 86) is a leap, though, and willingness falls off smoothly with how far beyond the span it lies.
