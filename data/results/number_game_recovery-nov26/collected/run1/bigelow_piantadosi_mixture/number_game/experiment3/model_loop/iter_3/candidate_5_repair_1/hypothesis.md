Refinement of distance_hesitant_concept_extension (a single sticky working guess — a rule or a numerical range — formed from the first example by Bayesian concept learning, digit rules salient once two examples share a digit, coincidences triggering a rethink, misfits written off more readily the more evidence the guess has, and a reluctance to extend the guessed concept to targets far from the nearest example). The one change: how far people are willing to extend their concept is not a fixed reach but is calibrated by how spread out the examples are — the examples show how widely the program's numbers range, so people extend beyond the nearest example by a fitted base reach plus as far again as the examples themselves are spread (span added one-for-one, no extra free parameter). A lone example or a tight cluster licenses only nearby members of a guessed rule or range, while widely scattered examples (16, 8, 64) make far members such as 2 or 96 feel like a small step.
