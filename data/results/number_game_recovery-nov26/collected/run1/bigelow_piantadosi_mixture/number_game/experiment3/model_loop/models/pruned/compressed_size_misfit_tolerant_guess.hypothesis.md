The one change, the component taken from compressed_concept_size_sticky_guess: people misjudge how many numbers a candidate concept contains, perceiving its size on a compressed, numerosity-like scale relative to the whole range 1-100 (compression fitted), so "powers of 2" or a narrow range feels less small and "even numbers" less large than they are — tempering the size principle's preference for specific concepts, and so changing which guess people first latch onto and which one a rethink lands on, while a misfitting example still counts against a guess as before.
