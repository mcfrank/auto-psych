## Hypotheses

- **range_reestimating_span_hesitant_guess**: Refinement of span_extrapolation_hesitant_guess (a single working concept guess — a rule or a numerical range with a fitted gamma-shaped width prior — formed from the first example by Bayesian concept learning, kept with fitted stickiness, digit rules salient once two examples share the digit, coincidences triggering a rethink, fitted trust, evidence-graded misfit tolerance, and reluctance to extend the concept beyond the examples' span). The one change: only rule guesses are sticky — a guess about where on the number line the program's numbers lie is never simply kept, but re-estimated from all the examples seen whenever a new example arrives, so a range formed around the first example does not persist and extrapolation beyond the span is no more generous on the first example's side than on the later examples' side (while a rule such as "ends in 6" is still kept with fitted stickiness and survives misfits as before).
- **span_extrapolation_hesitant_guess_2**: Refinement of the incumbent gamma_width_distance_hesitant_guess (a single sticky working concept guess — a rule or a numerical range with a fitted gamma-shaped width prior — formed from the first example by Bayesian concept learning, digit rules salient once two examples share the digit, coincidences triggering a rethink, fitted trust, evidence-graded misfit tolerance, and reluctance to extend the guessed concept far from the examples). The one change: the reluctance is to extrapolate beyond the region the examples span, not to stray from the nearest example — a target lying between the smallest and largest example is endorsed as fully as concept membership implies, and willingness falls off smoothly (Gaussian-like, fitted reach) only with how far the target lies outside that span. So after 46 and 86, a member such as 66 is not discounted at all, while 96 or 6 are discounted by their distance beyond the span; for a single example the prediction is unchanged. (To keep the fit tractable, the width prior's decay is parameterised by a rate rather than a scale, so a near-flat decay is reached smoothly instead of along a ridge to an infinite scale.)
- **rule_only_span_extrapolation_guess**: Refinement of the incumbent span_extrapolation_hesitant_guess_2 (a single sticky working concept guess, either a rule or a numerical range with a fitted gamma-shaped width prior, formed from the first example by Bayesian concept learning; digit rules salient once two examples share the digit; coincidences triggering a rethink; fitted trust; evidence-graded misfit tolerance; and reluctance to extend the concept beyond the span the examples cover). The one change: only rule guesses carry that reluctance to extrapolate. A range guess already states where on the number line the program's numbers lie, so a target inside a guessed range is endorsed as fully as membership implies, even beyond the examples' span. Extending a rule such as "powers of 2" or "ends in 6" to a member past the examples' span (64 after 16 and 8, or 96 after 46 and 86) is a leap, though, and willingness falls off smoothly with how far beyond the span it lies.
- **span_extrapolation_hesitant_guess**: Refinement of the incumbent gamma_width_distance_hesitant_guess (a single sticky working concept guess — a rule or a numerical range with a fitted gamma-shaped width prior — formed from the first example by Bayesian concept learning, digit rules salient once two examples share the digit, coincidences triggering a rethink, fitted trust, evidence-graded misfit tolerance, and a smooth reluctance to extend the guess far from the examples). The one change: the reluctance is about extrapolation, not interpolation — people hesitate only to extend their concept beyond the stretch of the number line the examples already cover, so willingness falls off with the target's distance outside the examples' span (smallest to largest example), while members lying between the examples (32 after 16, 8, 64; 50 after 5, 20, 92) are endorsed as fully as membership implies; for a single example this is the same as the incumbent.
- **gamma_width_range_prior_guess**: Refinement of the incumbent evidence_graded_misfit_tolerance (a single working concept guess — a rule or a numerical range — formed from the first example by Bayesian concept learning, kept with fitted stickiness, digit rules discounted and salient only once two examples share the digit, a newly noticed coincidence triggering a rethink, fitted trust, and a contradicting example written off as a stray more readily the more earlier examples the guess explains). The one change: people's prior sense of how wide the program's range of numbers is does not have the fixed Erlang form the incumbent assumes, but a gamma form whose shape is fitted, so how implausible very narrow ranges hugging the examples are, relative to how fast very broad ranges become implausible, is set by the data. With a shape below the incumbent's, tight ranges around clustered examples are more readily entertained while broad ranges stay plausible longer, so people generalise sharply right around clustered examples but with a longer tail out to distant numbers.
- **distance_hesitant_concept_extension**: People infer the program's concept as the current best learner does (a single sticky working guess — a rule or a numerical range — formed from the first example, with digit rules salient once two examples share a digit, coincidences triggering a rethink, and misfits written off more readily the more evidence the guess has), but they hesitate to extrapolate: even when the target belongs to their guessed concept, their willingness to say the program would produce it falls off smoothly (Gaussian-like) with how far the target lies on the number line from the nearest example they have seen, at a fitted reach. So far-away members of a rule — 96 after a lone 16, 64 after a lone 16 — are endorsed less than concept membership alone predicts, while members close to an example are endorsed fully.
- **rule_only_distance_hesitation**: Refinement of distance_hesitant_concept_extension (a single sticky working guess — a rule or a numerical range — formed from the first example by Bayesian concept learning, digit rules salient once two examples share a digit, coincidences triggering a rethink, misfits written off more readily the more evidence the guess has, and a reluctance to extend the guessed concept to targets far from the nearest example). The one change: that hesitation applies only when the working guess is a rule, not a range. A range guess already says where on the number line the program's numbers lie, so its members are endorsed as fully as membership implies; but extending a rule such as "powers of 2" or "ends in 6" to a member far from every example seen (64 or 96 after a lone 16) feels like a leap, and willingness falls off smoothly with that distance at a fitted reach — so generalisation from range guesses is not doubly shrunk while far rule members still are.
- **evidence_graded_misfit_tolerance**: Refinement of the incumbent misfit_tolerant_coincidence_rethink (a single working concept guess — a rule or a numerical range — formed from the first example by Bayesian concept learning, kept with fitted stickiness while later examples fit it, digit rules discounted and salient only once two examples share the digit, a newly noticed coincidence triggering a rethink, fitted trust, and a contradicting example sometimes written off as a stray). The one change: how readily people write off a contradicting example grows with how much evidence their guess already has — each earlier example the guess explains is an independent reason to keep it, so the chance of dismissing the misfit is one minus the chance that none of those confirmations holds (the same fitted tolerance per confirming example, zero for a guess that explains nothing). So "ends in 7" survives an outlier 8 more often after 77, 17, 37 than after 77, 17, and a misfit late in a long set is shrugged off more than one early in a short set. (To keep the fit tractable, the incumbent's power-law preference for where a range lies on the number line, which earlier rounds found the data barely constrain, is fixed at neutral.)
