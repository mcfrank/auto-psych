Refinement of the incumbent evidence_graded_misfit_tolerance (a single working concept guess — a rule or a numerical range — formed from the first example by Bayesian concept learning, digit rules discounted and salient only once two examples share the digit, a newly noticed coincidence triggering a rethink, fitted trust, and a contradicting example written off as a stray more readily the more earlier examples the guess explains). The one change: the same evidence-grading applies to keeping a guess that the new example fits — rather than a fixed chance of sticking, each earlier example the guess explains is an independent reason not to reconsider, so commitment to a guess grows with its confirmations (one minus the chance that none of them holds, with a fitted per-example stickiness). So a guess that has survived three examples is almost never reconsidered on the fourth, while one formed from a single example is often revised when a second one arrives, making late examples in long sets less able to reshape the concept than early ones.
