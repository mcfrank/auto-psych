People infer the program's concept as the current best learner does (a single sticky working guess — a rule or a numerical range — formed from the first example, with digit rules salient once two examples share a digit, coincidences triggering a rethink, and misfits written off more readily the more evidence the guess has), but when they answer they must check whether the target belongs to their guess, and for rules that require mental arithmetic ("multiples of 7", "primes", "squares", "powers of 2", "odd") that check is error-prone: with a fitted probability they get the membership wrong. Rules read off the written number ("ends in 6", "contains a 3") and numerical ranges are checked without error, so under an arithmetic-rule guess members are sometimes rejected and non-members sometimes endorsed, while digit-rule and range guesses yield crisp answers.
