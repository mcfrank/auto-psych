Refinement of distance_hesitant_concept_extension (a single sticky working guess — a rule or a numerical range — formed from the first example by Bayesian concept learning, digit rules salient once two examples share a digit, coincidences triggering a rethink, misfits written off more readily the more evidence the guess has, and a reluctance to extend the guessed concept to targets far from the nearest example). The one change: that hesitation applies only when the working guess is a rule, not a range. A range guess already says where on the number line the program's numbers lie, so its members are endorsed as fully as membership implies; but extending a rule such as "powers of 2" or "ends in 6" to a member far from every example seen (64 or 96 after a lone 16) feels like a leap, and willingness falls off smoothly with that distance at a fitted reach — so generalisation from range guesses is not doubly shrunk while far rule members still are.
