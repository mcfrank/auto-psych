People do the same Bayesian concept learning over the 45 rules and all intervals of 1-100 as the Erlang-interval model (size principle, 10% noisy examples, a prior weight per rule, an Erlang prior on interval size), but their space of rules also includes "same-step" arithmetic progressions with any starting offset — every class of numbers that are a fixed step apart without being multiples of it (e.g. 2, 7, 12, ... 97 for 82, 67, 72; or 1, 5, 9, ... for 25, 17) — for steps 3 to 12. This family of offset progressions takes a single fitted share of the rule prior, split equally among its members, so examples that share a common spacing are generalised to other numbers on the same step even when no familiar rule (multiples, last digit) fits them.
