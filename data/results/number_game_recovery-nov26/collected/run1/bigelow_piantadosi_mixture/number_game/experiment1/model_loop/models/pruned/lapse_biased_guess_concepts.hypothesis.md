People do the same Bayesian concept learning over the 45 rules and all intervals of 1-100 as the simplest model (size principle, 10% noisy examples, a prior weight per rule), and on most trials they answer by reporting the posterior probability that the concept contains the target; but on a fitted fraction of trials they lapse — they do not consult the inferred concept at all and instead guess, answering "yes" with a fitted guessing bias. This all-or-none attentional lapse with a biased guess is the one change: it puts a floor and a ceiling on yes rates that are the same for every stimulus, rather than graded softening of confident posteriors.
