Refinement of bigelow_piantadosi_independent: people do the same Bayesian concept learning over the 45 rules and all intervals of 1-100 (size principle, 10% noisy examples, a prior weight inferred for each rule), but they do not consider every interval equally likely a priori; instead their prior over intervals follows an Erlang density on the interval's size with a fitted scale, so very narrow intervals hugging the examples are a priori implausible and intervals of a characteristic, moderate breadth are preferred. This one change to the interval prior sets how far beyond and between the examples people extend a magnitude-based concept.
