Refinement of erlang_interval_prior_concepts: people do the same Bayesian concept learning over the 45 rules and all intervals of 1-100 (size principle, 10% noisy examples, a free prior weight per rule, an Erlang prior on interval size), but when they think of the program as producing a numerical range they favour ranges bounded by round numbers — decade boundaries such as "1 to 10", "20 to 29", "between 10 and 50", "51 to 100" — over ranges with arbitrary endpoints. The one change is a fitted prior boost for decade-aligned intervals, so people generalise from examples out to the nearest decade boundaries (e.g. from 24 and 27 to the whole of the twenties) rather than smoothly beyond the examples' span.
