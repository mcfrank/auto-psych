Refinement of sticky_guess_power_law_ranges: people hold a single working guess about the concept (a rule or a numerical range, with power-law and Erlang priors on ranges), formed from the first example by Bayesian concept learning and kept with a fitted stickiness while later examples fit it, reconsidering only when contradicted. The one change: rules are not all equally natural guesses — the ten "ends in digit d" rules share a single fitted prior weight relative to the other rules, so people latch onto and keep last-digit rules less (or more) readily than arithmetic rules like multiples or powers, which governs how much they endorse far-away numbers merely because they share an example's final digit.
