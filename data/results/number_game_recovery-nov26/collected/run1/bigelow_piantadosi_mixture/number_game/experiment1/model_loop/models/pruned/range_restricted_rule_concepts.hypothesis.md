Refinement of power_law_endpoints_erlang_width (same Bayesian concept learning over the 45 rules and all intervals of 1-100, size principle, 10% noisy examples, a prior weight per rule, power-law endpoint and Erlang width priors on ranges). The one change: people can also entertain a rule restricted to a range of the number line — e.g. "numbers ending in 3 between 10 and 40" rather than "all numbers ending in 3" — with a fitted share of each rule's prior given to its range-restricted version (any range equally plausible). Because such a restricted rule is smaller, the size principle favours it when the examples cluster, so people generalise a rule (especially a surface one like a shared last digit) less to numbers far outside the examples' span than the unrestricted rule predicts.
