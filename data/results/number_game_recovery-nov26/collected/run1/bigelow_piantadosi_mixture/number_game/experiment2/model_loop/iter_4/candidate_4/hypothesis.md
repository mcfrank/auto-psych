Refinement of the incumbent coincidence_triggered_guess_rethink (a single sticky working concept guess — a rule or a numerical range — formed from the first example by Bayesian concept learning, digit rules discounted and salient only once a digit is shared by two examples, fitted trust in the examples, and a newly noticed shared digit triggering a rethink of the guess from all examples). The one change: how disruptive a noticed coincidence is depends on its kind — a newly shared final digit (46 then 86: "ends in 6") is a striking, structurally aligned pattern that triggers a rethink with its own fitted probability, whereas a digit that merely appears somewhere in both numbers (16 then 64, or 13 then 31) is a weaker, less noticeable coincidence that triggers a rethink with a separate fitted probability, instead of both sharing one probability.
