Refinement of digit_coincidence_noticing_guess (a single working concept guess — a rule or a numerical range — formed from the first example by Bayesian concept learning and kept with fitted stickiness while later examples fit it, digit rules discounted, fitted trust in the examples, and digit rules made salient only once a digit is noticed as shared by at least two examples). The one change: the same coincidence-noticing gate applies to arithmetic rules too — "even", "multiples of 7" or "squares" becomes a salient candidate only once at least two of the examples seen so far share that property, and until then (always after a single example) its plausibility is reduced by its own fitted availability factor. So a lone example suggests a range of nearby numbers rather than a broad rule, people generalise less to far-away numbers from one example, and the first working guess is range-like until a shared property is noticed.
