Refinement of digit_coincidence_noticing_guess (a single sticky working guess about the concept — a rule or a numerical range — formed from the first example by Bayesian concept learning, digit rules discounted and made salient only once a digit is noticed as shared by two examples, fitted trust in the examples). The one change is in how people imagine the program picks a numerical range: they first decide how wide it is (the same preference for a typical width) and only then place a range of that width anywhere it fits on 1-100, so every width gets its share of plausibility no matter how many placements it has, rather than each individual range being weighted by its width. Because a wide range has few placements, each one becomes individually more plausible, while the width preference itself becomes much narrower; a lone example is therefore generalised along a steeper number-line gradient (and far-away numbers sharing its last digit are endorsed less), whereas widely spread examples still support a broad range.
