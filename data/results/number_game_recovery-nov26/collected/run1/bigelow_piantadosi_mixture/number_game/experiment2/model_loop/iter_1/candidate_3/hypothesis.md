Refinement of sticky_guess_fitted_sampling_trust (a single working guess about the concept — a rule or a numerical range — formed from the first example by Bayesian concept learning, kept while later examples fit it and reconsidered from all examples when contradicted, with digit rules discounted and a fitted trust in the examples). The one change: how firmly people hold their guess depends on its kind — a rule ("powers of 2", "ends in 3") is a categorical commitment kept with one fitted stickiness, whereas a numerical range is only a provisional summary of where the examples lie, kept with its own, separately fitted stickiness and more readily re-estimated from all examples as new ones arrive even when they fit it.
