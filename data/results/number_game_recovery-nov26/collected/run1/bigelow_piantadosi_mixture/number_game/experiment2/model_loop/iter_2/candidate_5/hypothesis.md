Refinement of sticky_guess_fitted_sampling_trust (a single working concept guess — a rule or a numerical range — formed from the first example by Bayesian concept learning, kept with fitted stickiness while later examples fit it, digit rules discounted, fitted trust in the examples). The one change: any rule at all — arithmetic ("even", "powers of 2", "multiples of 4") as well as digit-based ("ends in 6") — becomes a salient candidate only once people notice it as a regularity shared by at least two of the examples seen so far; until then (always after a single example) its plausibility is reduced by one fitted availability factor, so a lone example is generalised mostly as a nearby numerical range. Unlike digit_coincidence_noticing_guess, which delays only digit rules, this predicts that a lone 46 does not readily lead people to say yes to 96 through "even" either, and a lone 16 does not readily suggest 64 through "powers of 2", while two examples sharing the rule do.
