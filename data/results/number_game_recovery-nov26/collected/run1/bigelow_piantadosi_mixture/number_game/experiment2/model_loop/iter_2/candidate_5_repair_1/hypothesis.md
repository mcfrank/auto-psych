Refinement of sticky_guess_fitted_sampling_trust (a single working concept guess — a rule or a numerical range — formed from the first example by Bayesian concept learning and kept with fitted stickiness while later examples fit it, reconsidered from all examples only when contradicted, digit rules discounted, fitted trust in the examples). The one change, the component taken from decade_restricted_rule_concepts: people's working guess can also be a rule confined to a decade-bounded region of the number line ("ends in 6 in the forties", "even numbers between 21 and 50"), with a fitted share of each rule's plausibility going to such restricted versions; because a restricted rule is small, the size principle favours it whenever the examples sit in one region, so people extend a rule — especially a shared last digit from a single example, like 46 to 96 — to far-away numbers much less than a whole-number-line rule predicts, while still generalising it to nearby members.
