Refinement of digit_coincidence_noticing_guess (a single working concept guess — a rule or a numerical range — formed from the first example by Bayesian concept learning, kept with fitted stickiness while later examples fit it, digit rules discounted, fitted trust in the examples, and rules about how numbers are written made salient only by a noticed shared digit). The one change: noticing a shared digit is not all-or-none at two examples but grows with every example that carries it — the reduced plausibility of a written-form rule ("ends in 6", "contains a 3") is divided among the examples that have its digit, so it is fully reduced with one such example, half as reduced with two, a third with three — making "ends in 6" a gradually more salient guess as more examples repeat the digit, rather than fully salient as soon as two do.
