Refinement of sticky_guess_fitted_sampling_trust: people hold a single working guess about the concept (a rule or a numerical range, digit rules discounted, power-law/Erlang priors on ranges, fitted trust in the examples), formed from the first example by Bayesian concept learning and kept with a fitted stickiness while later examples fit it, reconsidering from all examples only when contradicted. The one change (the component taken from lapse_biased_guess_concepts): on a fitted fraction of trials people do not consult their guess at all and simply guess, answering "yes" with a fitted bias — which explains why they reject even the examples themselves about a quarter of the time without forcing the concept learner to distrust its examples, and puts a common floor under "yes" for numbers outside every plausible concept.
