Refinement of digit_coincidence_noticing_guess (a single working concept guess — a rule or a numerical range — formed from the first example by Bayesian concept learning, kept with fitted stickiness while later examples fit it, digit rules discounted, fitted trust in the examples, and rules about how numbers are written made salient only by a noticed shared digit). The one change: noticing a shared digit is not all-or-none at two examples but probabilistic — every pair of examples seen so far that share the digit is a separate chance (with a fitted probability) of spotting the coincidence — so a digit shared by only two examples is often missed, while one shared by three or four examples (three or six pairs) is almost always noticed, and "ends in 6" or "contains a 3" becomes a salient guess gradually as more examples carry the digit.
