Refinement of the incumbent coincidence_triggered_guess_rethink. It keeps everything: a single sticky working guess (a rule or a numerical range) formed from the first example by Bayesian concept learning, digit rules discounted and salient only once two numbers seen share the digit, fitted trust in the examples, and a newly noticed shared digit making people drop a guess that still fits and rethink from all the examples. The one change: the number being asked about can itself reveal a coincidence. When the target shares a digit with exactly one example (being asked about 96 after a lone 46, or after 46 and 13), people notice that coincidence while answering. With the same fitted probability as any other noticed coincidence, they then rethink from all the examples, and the digit rule covering both numbers ("ends in 6") is now salient. This adds no new parameter, so targets that share a digit with a single example are endorsed more than the incumbent predicts, even when no two examples share that digit.
