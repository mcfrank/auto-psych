Refinement of sticky_guess_fitted_sampling_trust (a single working guess about the concept — a rule or a numerical range — formed from the first example by Bayesian concept learning, kept with a fitted stickiness while later examples fit it and reconsidered from all examples when contradicted, with digit rules discounted and a fitted trust in the examples). The one change: when people answer from a range guess they treat its boundaries as fuzzy ("roughly 20 to 40") rather than sharp, so a target just outside the range is still endorsed, with a probability that falls off with its distance beyond the nearer boundary at a fitted rate — rule guesses stay crisp. This predicts graded "yes" for near-misses just outside the examples' span that the incumbent's sharp ranges reject, while far-away numbers fitting only a rule are unaffected.
