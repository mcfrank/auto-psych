Refinement of digit_coincidence_noticing_guess (a single sticky working guess — a rule or a numerical range — formed from the first example by Bayesian concept learning, digit rules discounted, fitted trust in the examples, and rules waiting to be noticed as a coincidence). The one change: it is not only rules about how numbers are written that must be noticed — any rule (odd, even, multiples, powers, squares, primes, as well as "ends in 6") becomes a salient candidate only once at least two of the examples seen so far share it, and until then its plausibility is reduced by one fitted availability factor. So a single example is read mainly as a place on the number line, and people endorse far-away numbers that merely share a property (parity, last digit, a divisor) with one lone example less than the incumbent predicts, while two examples sharing that property make the rule readily available.
