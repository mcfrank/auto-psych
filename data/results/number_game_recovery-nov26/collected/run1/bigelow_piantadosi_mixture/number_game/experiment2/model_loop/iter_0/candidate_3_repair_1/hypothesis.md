Refinement of digit_rule_discount_sticky_guess: people hold a single working guess about the concept (a rule or a numerical range, with the incumbent's power-law/Erlang range prior and discounted digit rules), formed from the first example by Bayesian concept learning and kept with a fitted stickiness while later examples fit it. The one change: a contradicting example does not always overturn the guess — with a fitted tolerance people dismiss an example that does not fit their current guess as an anomaly (noise) and keep the guess anyway, rather than always reconsidering from all examples, so a guess formed early survives an odd-one-out example and the outlier itself is endorsed less.
