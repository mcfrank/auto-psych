Refinement of coincidence_gated_all_rules_guess (a single sticky working guess — a rule or a numerical range — formed from the first example by Bayesian concept learning, with rules made salient only once two examples share them). The one change: noticing a coincidence is itself a trigger for rethinking — when a new example makes a rule shared by two examples for the first time, people abandon their current guess and reconsider the concept from all examples (even if the guess still fits), with a fitted probability scaled by how well the newly noticed rule explains the examples. So a guess formed early, for instance from an unrepresentative first example, does not block a pattern that later examples reveal, and order effects are weaker than pure stickiness predicts.
