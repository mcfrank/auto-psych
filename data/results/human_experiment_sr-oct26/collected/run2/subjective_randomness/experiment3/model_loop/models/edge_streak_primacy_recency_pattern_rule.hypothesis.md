Refinement of the incumbent `terminal_streak_recency_pattern_rule` (tally span judged against a personal expected span, switching by closeness to a personal ideal switching rate, chunk variety and a rule-built penalty scaled by a personal pattern sensitivity, a personal left/right lean, and a shared penalty on every flip of the final run beyond the first). The one change: the edges of a sequence are salient at both ends, not only at its end — the run a sequence opens on is read first and noticed just as the run it finishes on is read last, so a sequence that starts with a streak (HHHTHT…, TTHTHH…) also looks less random, every flip of its opening run beyond the first counting against it by its own shared opening-streak penalty. Of two sequences with the same number of switches and the same ending, people therefore pick the one that starts with a switch, which the incumbent, blind to where the opening switches fall, predicts they do not.
