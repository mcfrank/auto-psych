Refinement of the incumbent `person_pattern_sensitivity_rule_built` (running heads-minus-tails tally judged by closeness of its span to a personal expected span with person-specific, length-scaled sensitivity; switching judged by closeness to a shared ideal rate with a person-specific weight; three-flip chunk variety and a rule-built penalty scaled by a personal pattern sensitivity; a personal left/right lean). The one change: people judge switching as accumulated evidence rather than as a bare rate — a departure of the switching rate from the ideal counts in proportion to how many transitions it rests on (as a fair coin's switch count would be judged in standard-error units), so the same too-streaky or too-alternating rate looks far less random in an eight-flip sequence than in a four-flip one. This predicts that agreement driven by switching grows with sequence length, which the incumbent's length-independent switching term cannot produce, and bears on the critique that consistency differs outside pattern structure.
