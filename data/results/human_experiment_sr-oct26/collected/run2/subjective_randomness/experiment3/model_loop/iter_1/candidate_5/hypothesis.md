Refinement of `iter0_candidate4` (running heads-minus-tails tally judged by closeness of its span to a personal expected span with length-scaled sensitivity; switching judged by closeness to a shared ideal rate with a person-specific weight; a penalty on a visibly repeating unit; a reward for variety of three-flip chunks; a personal left/right lean). The one change is in how decisively each person acts on their whole impression: people differ in a single overall decisiveness that scales every cue of randomness together (tally span, switching, periodicity and chunk variety alike), so some people follow their impression consistently on every pair while others choose close to a coin flip throughout — replacing the earlier person-specific sensitivity that applied to the tally span alone. This addresses the critique (surviving FDR) that participants differ in how consistently they agree with the majority choice more than the current models allow.
