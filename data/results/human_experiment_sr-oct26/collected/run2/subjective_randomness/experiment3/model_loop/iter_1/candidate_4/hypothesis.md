Refinement of the incumbent `rule_built_tally_switch_triplet` (tally span judged against a personal expected span, switching judged against an ideal rate with a person-specific weight, a reward for three-flip chunk variety, a penalty for rule-built sequences, a personal left/right lean). The one change: people differ in how decisively they act on their whole impression of randomness — each person has a single consistency (decisiveness) that scales all of their cues together (span, switching, chunk variety and rule-built penalty), replacing the incumbent's person-specific sensitivity on the tally span alone, so some people follow the consensus on almost every pair while others choose nearly at random. This addresses the critique (surviving FDR) that people differ in how often they agree with the majority more than the incumbent allows.
