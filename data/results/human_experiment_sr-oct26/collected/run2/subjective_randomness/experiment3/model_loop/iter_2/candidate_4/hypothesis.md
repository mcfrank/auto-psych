Refinement of the incumbent `person_pattern_sensitivity_rule_built` (running heads-minus-tails tally judged by closeness of its span to a personal expected span with person-specific, length-scaled sensitivity; switching judged by closeness to a shared ideal rate with a person-specific weight; three-flip chunk variety and a rule-built penalty scaled by a personal pattern sensitivity; a personal left/right lean). The one change: how closely a person attends to the tally and how closely they attend to switching are not independent traits but share a common factor of attention to the sequence's statistics, so a person who is sharply sensitive to the tally span is also sharply sensitive to the switching rate (the correlation between the two person-specific weights is estimated). This addresses the critique that people differ in how consistently they agree with the majority even on pairs where pattern structure cannot act, more than independent cue weights allow: with correlated span and switching weights, some people follow both statistical cues consistently and others neither.
