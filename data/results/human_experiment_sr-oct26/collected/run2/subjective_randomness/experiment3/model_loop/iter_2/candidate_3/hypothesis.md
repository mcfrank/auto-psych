Refinement of the incumbent `person_pattern_sensitivity_rule_built` (running heads-minus-tails tally judged by closeness of its span to a personal expected span with person-specific, length-scaled sensitivity; switching judged by closeness to an ideal switching rate with a person-specific weight; a reward for three-flip chunk variety and a penalty on rule-built sequences, both scaled by a personal pattern sensitivity; a personal left/right lean). The one change: people differ in the switching rate they expect from a fair coin — each person has their own ideal switching rate, drawn from a population, instead of one shared ideal — so on pairs that differ only in how often they switch, some people pick the more-switching sequence and others the less-switching one. This addresses the critique that people differ in how often they agree with the majority even on pairs where pattern structure cannot act, locating that remaining heterogeneity in each person's picture of how often a random coin alternates.
