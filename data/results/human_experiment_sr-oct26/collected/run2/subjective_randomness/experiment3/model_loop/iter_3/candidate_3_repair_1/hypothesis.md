Refinement of the incumbent `person_switch_ideal_pattern_rule_built` (tally span judged against a personal expected span, switching judged by closeness to a personal ideal switching rate with a personal weight, three-flip chunk variety and a rule-built penalty scaled by a personal pattern sensitivity, a personal left/right lean). The one change: people expect a random coin to produce streaks of irregular, mixed lengths — single flips, pairs and triples side by side — so a sequence whose runs come in many different lengths looks more random than one whose runs are all the same length (HHTTHHTT, HTHT..., HHTHHT), by a shared reward on the number of distinct run lengths relative to the most its length allows. Of two sequences with the same number of switches, people therefore pick the one with more varied run lengths more often than the incumbent predicts, addressing the critique that it under-produces exactly that choice.
