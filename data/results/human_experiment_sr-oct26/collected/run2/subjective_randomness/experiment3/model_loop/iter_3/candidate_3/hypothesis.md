Refinement of the incumbent `person_switch_ideal_pattern_rule_built` (tally span judged against a personal expected span, switching judged by closeness to a personal ideal switching rate, chunk variety and a rule-built penalty scaled by a personal pattern sensitivity, a personal left/right lean). The one change: people weigh the end of a sequence more than its middle — the last flips they read leave the strongest impression, and a sequence that ends on a repeat (the last two flips the same) reads as a streak just starting, while one that ends on a switch reads as still random — so a shared reward on ending with a switch is added. Of two sequences with the same number of switches, people pick the one whose final two flips differ more often than the incumbent predicts, addressing the critique that the incumbent is blind to where the switches fall.
