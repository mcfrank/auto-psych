Refinement of `person_switch_ideal_pattern_rule_built` (tally span judged against a personal expected span, switching judged by closeness to a personal ideal switching rate, chunk variety and a rule-built penalty scaled by a personal pattern sensitivity, a personal left/right lean). The one change: people notice a local stretch of strict alternation (HTHT or THTH within any four consecutive flips) as a visible pattern in its own right, and every such alternating four-flip stretch counts against a sequence's randomness through a shared penalty scaled by the person's pattern sensitivity — even when the whole sequence is not rule-built and its overall switching rate is near their ideal. Of two sequences with the same number of switches, people therefore pick the one whose switches are not bunched into extended alternation, addressing the critique that among equal-switch pairs people choose the sequence with fewer alternating four-flip windows more often than the best model predicts.
