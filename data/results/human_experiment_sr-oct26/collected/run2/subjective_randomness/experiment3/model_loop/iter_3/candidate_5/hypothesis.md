Refinement of `streak_excess_pattern_rule_built` (tally span judged against a personal expected span with person-specific, length-scaled sensitivity; switching judged by closeness to an ideal rate with a person-specific weight; chunk variety and a rule-built penalty scaled by a personal pattern sensitivity; a personal left/right lean; and a shared penalty on streaks). The one change is where the streak is felt: people read a sequence left to right and judge it most by how it ends, so the streak that counts against randomness is the run the sequence finishes on — every repeated flip of its final run (…HH, …TTT) makes it look less random, while a sequence ending on a switch looks most random — replacing the penalty on the longest run anywhere. Of two sequences with equal switch counts, people therefore pick the one that ends with a switch, addressing the critique that people choose the sequence ending with a switch more often than the best model, which is blind to switch position, predicts.
