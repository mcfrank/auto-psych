Refinement of the incumbent `iter2_candidate3` (running heads-minus-tails tally span judged against a personal expected span, a person-specific reward for switching, and a personal left/right lean): people still judge a sequence by its tally span and still reward switching, but they also register the variety of its short local patterns, so a sequence whose overlapping three-flip chunks are mostly different from one another looks more random, while one that keeps reusing the same few chunks (strict alternation HTHTHTHT, which contains only HTH and THT, or blocks like HHTTHHTT) looks patterned. The one change is a shared reward on the share of distinct three-flip chunks (grafted from `iter1_candidate4`), addressing the critique (surviving FDR) that people choose the perfect alternator less often, and the sequence with more distinct triplets at equal switch counts more often, than the incumbent's linear switching reward predicts.
