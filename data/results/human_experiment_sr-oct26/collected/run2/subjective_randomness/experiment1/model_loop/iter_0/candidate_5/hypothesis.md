Refinement of `motif_stack`: people judge randomness as the likelihood ratio of a fair coin against Griffiths et al.'s four-motif stack automaton (shared by everyone), but individuals differ in how strongly they additionally treat frequent alternation itself as a sign of randomness. The one change is a person-specific alternation-preference weight (drawn from a population distribution) on the difference in alternation rate between the two sequences, addressing the critique that the data show far more individual variation in alternation preference than a single-population model produces.
