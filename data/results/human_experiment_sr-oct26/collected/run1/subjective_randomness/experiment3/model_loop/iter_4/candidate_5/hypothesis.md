Refinement of `capacity_blur_personal_antisymmetry` (statistically tied with the best): each person still has their own working-memory capacity, so their impression of which sequence looks more random decides the choice sharply for short pairs but is blurred for pairs whose length approaches or exceeds that capacity, and the impression is still judged by a personal ideal switching rate and sensitivity (lenient toward over-alternation), personal weights on imbalance and the longest streak, shared structural cues (final streak, mirror symmetry, complement symmetry, run-length variety, most lopsided stretch) and a personal guessing rate. The one change is replacing its personal weight on antisymmetry alone with the incumbent's personal pattern-detection gain: each person has one trait for noticing designed structure that scales all the structural cues together — so two separate personal sources of consistency (memory capacity, concentrated on long pairs, and general pattern noticing, concentrated on structured sequences) are estimated jointly, addressing the critique (surviving FDR) that people differ in how consistently they agree with the majority more than the incumbent's gain alone produces.
