Refinement of the incumbent `personal_pattern_detection_gain`: each sequence is still judged by its switching rate against a personal ideal (lenient toward over-alternation), personal weights on heads/tails imbalance and the longest streak, the shared structural cues of a designed sequence (final streak, mirror symmetry, complement symmetry, run-length variety, most lopsided stretch) scaled by a personal pattern-detection gain, and a personal guessing rate. The one change is that a person's attentiveness is a single latent trait shared by their consistency parameters: the same attentiveness raises how sharply they judge the switching rate, how strongly they notice structure, and lowers how often they guess (each by a fitted loading), instead of these three varying independently — so engaged people are consistent on every kind of pair and disengaged people on none, predicting a wider spread of people's agreement with the majority than independent traits produce (the critique's surviving discrepancy).
