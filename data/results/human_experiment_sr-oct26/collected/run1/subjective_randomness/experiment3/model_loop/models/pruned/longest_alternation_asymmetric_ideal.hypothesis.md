Refinement of `run_variety_asymmetric_ideal_rate` (rank 1, statistically tied with the best): people still compare a sequence's switching rate with their own ideal (with their own sensitivity, judging switching too often more leniently than too rarely by a shared fraction), still prefer varied run lengths, penalise heads/tails imbalance and the longest streak by personal weights, weigh the final streak and mirror symmetry by shared weights, and guess at a personal lapse rate. The one change is that people also notice the longest unbroken stretch of strict heads/tails alternation (HTHTH…) as a pattern in its own right — the alternation counterpart of the longest streak — and it shifts how random the sequence looks by a shared weight, so two sequences with the same number of switches differ when one gathers its switches into a long rigid alternating stretch (HHTHTHTT) and the other spreads them out (HTHHTTHT).
