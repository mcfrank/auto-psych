## Hypotheses

- **personal_pattern_detection_gain**: Refinement of the incumbent `personal_complement_symmetry_aversion`: each sequence is still judged by its switching rate against a personal ideal (personal sensitivity, lenient toward over-alternation), personal weights on heads/tails imbalance and the longest streak, the structural cues of a designed sequence (the final streak, mirror symmetry, complement symmetry, run-length variety and the most lopsided stretch), and a personal guessing rate. The one change is that noticing structure is a single personal trait rather than a trait specific to antisymmetry: each person has their own pattern-detection gain, drawn from a population, that scales all the structural cues together (now shared in their relative weights), so a person who spots palindromes also spots antisymmetry, rhythmic runs and local excesses, while another sees none of them — predicting that people's agreement with the majority is a stable personal trait that varies more across people than cue-specific weights produce (the critique's excess spread and split-half stability of majority agreement).
- **capacity_blur_pattern_gain**: Refinement of `capacity_blur_personal_antisymmetry` (statistically tied with the best): each person still has their own working-memory capacity, so their impression of which sequence looks more random decides the choice sharply for short pairs but is blurred for pairs whose length approaches or exceeds that capacity, and the impression is still judged by a personal ideal switching rate and sensitivity (lenient toward over-alternation), personal weights on imbalance and the longest streak, shared structural cues (final streak, mirror symmetry, complement symmetry, run-length variety, most lopsided stretch) and a personal guessing rate. The one change is replacing its personal weight on antisymmetry alone with the incumbent's personal pattern-detection gain: each person has one trait for noticing designed structure that scales all the structural cues together — so two separate personal sources of consistency (memory capacity, concentrated on long pairs, and general pattern noticing, concentrated on structured sequences) are estimated jointly, addressing the critique (surviving FDR) that people differ in how consistently they agree with the majority more than the incumbent's gain alone produces.
- **returns_to_balance_expectation**: People keep a running tally of heads versus tails as they read a sequence and expect a fair coin to keep correcting itself: the tally should drift away from balance and then come back to even, again and again. A sequence whose tally returns to an even count often (relative to how many returns its length allows) looks self-correcting and so more random, while one whose tally wanders off and rarely or never comes back to balance looks biased — by a shared weight on the share of possible returns to balance, on top of the current best judgement (personal ideal switching rate and sensitivity with lenience toward over-alternation, personal weights on imbalance and the longest streak, a personal pattern-detection gain scaling the final streak, mirror symmetry, complement symmetry, run-length variety and most-lopsided-stretch cues, and a personal guessing rate). It predicts that two sequences with the same final counts, switching rate and range of the tally differ when one passes through balance more often along the way (HTTHHT, back to even after every second flip, should look more random than HHTTHT, which matches it on switches, counts and range but returns to balance only twice).
- **sensitivity_linked_consistency**: Refinement of the incumbent `personal_pattern_detection_gain`: each sequence is still judged by its switching rate against a personal ideal (personal sensitivity, lenient toward over-alternation), personal weights on heads/tails imbalance and the longest streak, the shared structural cues of a designed sequence (final streak, mirror symmetry, complement symmetry, run-length variety, most lopsided stretch) scaled by a personal pattern-detection gain, and a personal guessing rate. The one change is that a person's consistency is one coherent trait: how sharply a person judges the switching rate also predicts, by fitted signed amounts, how strongly they notice structure and how rarely they guess (correlated rather than independent personal traits). So people who are careful judges of alternation also spot designed patterns and seldom guess, while careless ones do none of these. This predicts a wider spread across people in how often they agree with the majority than independent traits produce, which is the critique's discrepancy that survives FDR.
- **capacity_blur_personal_antisymmetry**: Refinement of `personal_memory_capacity_blur` (statistically tied with the best): each person still has their own working-memory capacity, so their impression of which sequence looks more random decides the choice sharply for short pairs but is blurred for pairs whose length approaches or exceeds that capacity, and the impression is still the current best judgement (personal ideal switching rate and sensitivity with lenience toward over-alternation, personal weights on imbalance and the longest streak, shared weights on the final streak, mirror symmetry, run-length variety and the most lopsided stretch, and a personal guessing rate). The one change is grafting in the incumbent's personal complement-symmetry aversion: whether a sequence that equals its own reverse with heads and tails swapped (HHTT, HTHTHTHT, HHHTHTTT) looks deliberately constructed is each person's own trait rather than shared — so the two personal sources of consistency (memory capacity, concentrated on long pairs, and noticing antisymmetry, concentrated on antisymmetric pairs) are estimated together, addressing the critique (surviving FDR) that people differ in how often they agree with the majority more than the incumbent produces.
- **longest_repeated_chunk_aversion**: People scan a sequence for its single most striking repeated chunk: the longest stretch of flips that appears again, unchanged, somewhere else in the same sequence (HTT in HTTHHTTH, HTHT in HTHTHTHT, HHT in THHTHHTT). One long recurring chunk makes the whole sequence look copied and so less random, by a shared weight on the length of that longest repeat (relative to the longest repeat the sequence's length allows). This repeat cue is added to the current best judgement (personal ideal switching rate and sensitivity with lenience toward over-alternation, personal weights on imbalance and the longest streak, shared weights on the final streak, mirror symmetry, run-length variety and the most lopsided stretch, personal weight on complement symmetry, and a personal guessing rate). It predicts that two sequences matching on switching, balance and streaks differ when one contains a long chunk that recurs and the other does not.
- **personal_first_read_anchoring**: People do not judge the two sequences independently: they read the left sequence first and its impression becomes an anchor, so each person weighs the first-read sequence's impression of randomness by a personal anchoring weight (more, or less, than the second sequence's), drawn from a population. The choice therefore depends not only on which sequence looks more random but on how random the pair looks overall relative to an ideal-looking sequence — when both look clearly non-random, a person who over-weights the first sequence's flaws turns to the right one, and when both look nearly ideal they stay with the left one — so the same sequence is chosen at different rates beside different partners, and people's left-choice rates and agreement with the majority differ in a pair-dependent way. Each sequence's impression is the current best judgement (personal ideal switching rate and sensitivity with lenience toward over-alternation, personal weights on imbalance, the longest streak and complement symmetry, shared weights on the final streak, mirror symmetry, run-length variety and the most lopsided stretch, and a personal guessing rate).
- **side_lean_complement_symmetry**: Refinement of the incumbent `personal_complement_symmetry_aversion`: each person still judges how random each sequence looks exactly as the incumbent does (personal ideal switching rate and sensitivity with lenience toward over-alternation, personal weights on imbalance, the longest streak and complement symmetry, shared weights on the final streak, mirror symmetry, run-length variety and the most lopsided stretch, and a personal guessing rate). The one change, grafted from `side_bias_asymmetric_tolerance_stretch`, is in the decision: each person also has a habitual leaning toward the Left or Right button, drawn from a population that may lean Left overall, which tips every comparison they make toward that side by a fixed amount — so it decides near-ties and makes people's own Left-choice rates differ more than the stimuli alone produce (the critique's excess spread of participants' Left-choice rates), estimated on top of the best current judgement of randomness.
