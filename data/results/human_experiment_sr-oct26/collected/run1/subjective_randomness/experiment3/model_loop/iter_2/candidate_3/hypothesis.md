Refinement of the incumbent `complement_symmetry_aversion`: each sequence's randomness is still judged as there (ideal switching rate with lenience toward over-alternation, personal weights on heads/tails imbalance and the longest streak, shared weights on the final streak, mirror symmetry, complement symmetry, run-length variety and the most lopsided stretch, and a personal guessing rate). The one change is where people differ in sharpness: instead of a personal sensitivity to the switching rate alone, each person has their own overall decisiveness that sharpens or flattens how strongly the whole difference in apparent randomness drives their choice — so some people follow every cue (symmetry, streaks, local excess as well as switching) consistently and others follow all of them weakly, addressing the critique (surviving FDR) that people differ in how often they agree with the majority choice more than the incumbent produces.
