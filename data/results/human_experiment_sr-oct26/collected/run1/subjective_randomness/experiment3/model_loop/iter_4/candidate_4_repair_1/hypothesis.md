Refinement of the incumbent `personal_pattern_detection_gain`: each sequence is still judged by its switching rate against a personal ideal (personal sensitivity, lenient toward over-alternation), personal weights on heads/tails imbalance and the longest streak, the shared structural cues of a designed sequence (final streak, mirror symmetry, complement symmetry, run-length variety, most lopsided stretch) scaled by a personal pattern-detection gain, and a personal guessing rate. The one change is that a person's consistency is one coherent trait: how sharply a person judges the switching rate also predicts, by fitted signed amounts, how strongly they notice structure and how rarely they guess (correlated rather than independent personal traits). So people who are careful judges of alternation also spot designed patterns and seldom guess, while careless ones do none of these. This predicts a wider spread across people in how often they agree with the majority than independent traits produce, which is the critique's discrepancy that survives FDR.
