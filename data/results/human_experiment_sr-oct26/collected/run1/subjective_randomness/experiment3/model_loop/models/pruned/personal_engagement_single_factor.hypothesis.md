Refinement of the incumbent `personal_pattern_detection_gain`: each sequence is still judged by its switching rate against a personal ideal (lenient toward over-alternation), personal weights on heads/tails imbalance and the longest streak, and the shared structural cues of a designed sequence (final streak, mirror symmetry, complement symmetry, run-length variety, most lopsided stretch), with occasional guessing. The one change is that a person's switching sensitivity, pattern-detection gain and guessing rate are not three separate traits but expressions of a single personal level of engagement with the task: a more engaged person is at once more sensitive to switching, notices more structure and guesses less, so people line up on one dimension from following every cue the majority follows to following none — predicting the critique's surviving excess spread of participants' agreement with the majority, with those differences tied together across cue types rather than independent.
