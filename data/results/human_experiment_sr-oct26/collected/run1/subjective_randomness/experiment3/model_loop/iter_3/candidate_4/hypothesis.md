Refinement of the incumbent `personal_complement_symmetry_aversion`: each person still judges how random each sequence looks exactly as the incumbent does (personal ideal switching rate and sensitivity with lenience toward over-alternation, personal weights on imbalance, the longest streak and complement symmetry, shared weights on the final streak, mirror symmetry, run-length variety and the most lopsided stretch). The one change is in how a person acts on that impression: instead of a personal guessing rate that can only pull their choices toward a coin flip, each person has a signed engagement drawn from a population — most people follow their impression (more or less consistently), but a few have reversed the task and systematically choose the sequence that looks *less* random — so the model predicts that some people agree with the majority choice well below one half (the data contain people at 12–33% agreement), widening the spread of majority agreement across people that the critique (surviving FDR) says the incumbent under-produces.
