Refinement of the incumbent `personal_pattern_detection_gain`: each sequence is still judged by its switching rate against a personal ideal (lenient toward over-alternation), personal weights on heads/tails imbalance and the longest streak, and the shared structural cues of a designed sequence (final streak, mirror symmetry, complement symmetry, run-length variety, most lopsided stretch) scaled by a personal pattern-detection gain, with a personal guessing rate. The one change is that a person's consistency traits are not independent: a single personal level of engagement with the task jointly sharpens their sensitivity to switching, raises their pattern-detection gain and lowers their guessing rate (each by a fitted loading, on top of trait-specific variation), so engaged people follow every cue the majority follows while disengaged people follow none — predicting that agreement with the majority varies across people more than independent traits produce (the critique's surviving excess variance of participants' majority agreement).
