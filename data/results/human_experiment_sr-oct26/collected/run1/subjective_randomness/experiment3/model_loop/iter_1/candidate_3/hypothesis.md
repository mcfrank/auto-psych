Refinement of the incumbent `asymmetric_tolerance_lopsided_stretch`: people still find a sequence less random when one stretch of it shows a striking local heads/tails excess, and still judge it by their own ideal switching rate and sensitivity, personal weights on overall imbalance and the longest streak, shared weights on the final streak, mirror symmetry and run-length variety, and a personal guessing rate, with switching more often than one's ideal judged more leniently than switching too rarely. The one change is that this leniency toward over-alternation is each person's own trait (drawn from a population) rather than shared: some people barely mind a sequence that alternates more than they expect while others reject it as firmly as a streaky one — so people differ in how they treat highly alternating sequences beyond where their ideal switching rate lies, and the population's leniency is not forced onto everyone.
