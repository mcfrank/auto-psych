Refinement of `terminal_discounted_streak_lapse` (statistically tied with the incumbent): each person still judges a sequence as more random the closer its alternation rate lies to their own ideal switching rate, still penalises lopsided heads/tails counts and the most striking streak (with the run still in progress at the end weighed by a shared factor) by weights of their own, and still guesses on some trials at a personal lapse rate. The one change is that people notice mirror symmetry: a sequence of four or more flips that reads the same backwards as forwards (a palindrome such as HTTTTTTH or HHHTTHHH) looks designed, and so less random, by a shared weight — addressing the critique that people choose the palindrome of a pair less often than the alternation, balance and streak terms predict.
