People judge each sequence's randomness from how close its switching rate lies to their own ideal, how lopsided its heads/tails mix is and how long its longest streak is (each by weights of their own), and they guess on some trials at a personal lapse rate. The change is in the decision rule: when the two sequences seem about equally random, the difference falls inside an indifference band and the person effectively flips a coin. Only the part of the difference that goes beyond the band drives the choice, so near-ties look like guessing while clear differences are decided as sharply as before.
