Refinement of the incumbent `personal_lapse_ideal_alternation`: people still judge a sequence as more random the closer its proportion of alternations lies to their own personal ideal switching rate, and still guess on some trials at a personal, trait-like lapse rate, but when they do engage the comparison they also notice the single longest streak of identical flips and treat a longer streak as a sign of non-randomness beyond what the overall alternation rate shows. The one change is this shared longest-streak penalty inside the engaged decision (taken from `ideal_alternation_streak_penalty`), because the critique shows that among pairs matched on alternation rate people pick the sequence with the shorter longest run more often than the incumbent predicts.
