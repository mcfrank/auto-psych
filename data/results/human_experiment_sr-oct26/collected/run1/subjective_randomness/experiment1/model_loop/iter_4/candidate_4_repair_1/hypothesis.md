Refinement of the incumbent `lapse_individual_balance_alternation`: each person still judges a sequence as more random the closer its proportion of alternations lies to their own ideal switching rate, still penalises lopsided heads/tails counts by a weight of their own, and still guesses on some trials at a personal lapse rate. The one change is that a streak sitting at the very start or end of a sequence is especially salient (primacy/recency of what is read first and last): the number of flips by which a run of identical flips at either edge exceeds two makes the sequence look less random by a shared weight, while the same streak buried in the middle carries no extra penalty — addressing the critique that among alternation-matched pairs people avoid the sequence with an edge streak more than the incumbent predicts.
