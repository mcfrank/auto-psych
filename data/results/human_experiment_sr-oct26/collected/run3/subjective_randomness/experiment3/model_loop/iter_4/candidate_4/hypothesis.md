Refinement of the incumbent `streak_weary_chance_pseudoflip` (people judge a sequence random to the extent their own second-order, streak-weary picture of a fair coin explains it better than the regular generators they suspect — a switch-biased coin, a heads-favoured trick coin and a repeating motif with slips — with pseudo-flip-diluted per-flip evidence, heavy-tailed signed person sensitivity and a side habit). The single change is in what people imagine a trick coin to be: instead of a fixed uniform spread of rigged heads rates, how lopsided the suspected trick coins are is fitted (a free concentration of the trick coin's prior), so if people imagine only mildly rigged coins a modest heads/tails imbalance is weaker evidence against chance than the incumbent assumes. This addresses the critique that in length-8 pairs where the more-switching sequence is also the more lopsided one, people choose the more-switching sequence more often than the incumbent predicts, i.e. they weigh heads/tails balance less against switching.
