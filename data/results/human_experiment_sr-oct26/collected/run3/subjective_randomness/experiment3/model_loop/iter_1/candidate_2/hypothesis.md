People judge a sequence random to the extent their own picture of a fair coin explains it better than the regular ways it could have been made — and among those regular ways they also suspect a kind no current model considers: a coin swap partway through, where the first stretch came from one trick coin and the rest from another with a different lean (so HHHHHTTT, HHTHHTTT or THTTTHHH look staged even when their overall switch count and heads/tails balance are ordinary). People differ in how readily they suspect such a mid-sequence swap, which makes them disagree with the majority on exactly those pairs; everything else is as in the current best account (a person-specific, second-order, gambler's-fallacy chance coin, a switch-biased coin, a biased coin and a repeating motif with each person's own motif suspicion, evidence weighed per flip, person-specific sensitivity, side habit and lapse rate).
