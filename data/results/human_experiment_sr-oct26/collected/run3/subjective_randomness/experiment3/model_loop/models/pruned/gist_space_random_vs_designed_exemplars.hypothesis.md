People judge randomness by exemplar similarity in a psychological space of sequence gist — how often a sequence switches, how lopsided its heads/tails count is, and how long its longest streak is: they compare a new sequence with remembered examples of what a real coin produces (every sequence of that length, remembered as often as their own over-alternating picture of a coin would produce it) and with a few remembered "designed" sequences (a streak, perfect alternation, and the repeated motifs HHT… and HHTT…, each with its own memorability). Similarity falls off exponentially with the attention-weighted distance in that space, and the sequence whose summed similarity to the remembered random examples most outweighs its similarity to the designed ones is chosen as more random, with people differing in how decisively (even in which direction) they act on it and in a habitual side preference.
