Refinement of the incumbent `heavy_tailed_signed_sensitivity_motif` (people judge a sequence random to the extent their own second-order model of a fair coin explains it better than the regular generators they suspect — a switch-biased coin, a heads-favoured trick coin and a repeating motif with slips — the evidence weighed per flip, with each person's own heavy-tailed, possibly reversed sensitivity and side habit). The single change is that a person's quick switch-count impression (their belief about how often a fair coin switches) is acted on through the same personal, signed sensitivity as the pattern evidence rather than through an unrelated person-level weight: a person who is nearly indifferent to patterns is also nearly indifferent to switching, and a person who reverses the judgment (picking the sequence that looks less random) reverses their preference for switching too, rather than keeping the majority's preference for more switches. Reversed and indifferent participants are thus consistent across all pairs, including the short pairs decided mainly by switch count.
