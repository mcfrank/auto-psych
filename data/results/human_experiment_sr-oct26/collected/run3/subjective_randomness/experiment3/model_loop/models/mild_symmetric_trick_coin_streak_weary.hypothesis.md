Refinement of the incumbent `streak_weary_chance_pseudoflip` (people judge a sequence random to the extent their own second-order, streak-weary picture of a fair coin explains it better than the regular generators they suspect — a switch-biased coin, a trick coin and a repeating motif with slips — with pseudo-flip-diluted per-flip evidence, heavy-tailed signed person sensitivity and a side habit). The single change is to the trick coin people imagine: not one leaning towards heads with any degree of rigging equally likely, but a symmetric coin that is only mildly rigged, with how mildly fitted. A modest heads/tails imbalance is then weak evidence against chance, so people weigh balance less against switching. This addresses the critique that in length-8 pairs where the more-switching sequence is also the more lopsided one, people choose the more-switching sequence more often than the incumbent predicts.
