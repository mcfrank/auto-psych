People do not weigh all the evidence about a sequence together; they compare the two sequences cue by cue in a fixed order of salience and let the first cue that clearly tells them apart decide (a non-compensatory "take-the-best" comparison). They first look at the longest streak — the sequence with the shorter longest run looks more random; only if the streaks look alike do they compare how often each sequence switches between heads and tails; and only if that too looks alike do they compare heads/tails balance — so a clear difference in switching overrides any difference in balance, and when no cue separates the pair they fall back on their own side habit.
