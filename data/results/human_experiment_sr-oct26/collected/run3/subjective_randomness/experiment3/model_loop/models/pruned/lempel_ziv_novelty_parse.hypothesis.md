People judge randomness by how compressible a sequence is when read once from left to right: they parse it into the shortest list of new "phrases", each phrase being the shortest stretch not already seen earlier in the sequence (Lempel-Ziv parsing), and a sequence that keeps producing new phrases looks random while one that keeps repeating what came before (streaks, alternation, repeated motifs) looks designed. The phrase count is judged relative to what is typical for a fair coin at that length, and the sequence with the higher relative phrase count is chosen, with people differing in how decisively they act on it and in a habitual side preference.
