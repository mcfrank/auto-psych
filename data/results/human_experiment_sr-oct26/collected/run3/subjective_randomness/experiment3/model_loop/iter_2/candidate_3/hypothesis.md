Refinement of the incumbent `heavy_tailed_signed_sensitivity_motif` (people judge a sequence random to the extent their own second-order model of a fair coin explains it better than the regular generators they suspect — a switch-biased coin, a heads-favoured trick coin and a repeating motif with slips — the evidence weighed per flip, with each person's own heavy-tailed, possibly reversed sensitivity and side habit). The single change is that a person's departure from the shared judgment is one trait, not two unrelated ones: how strongly (and in which direction) a person acts on the pattern evidence also carries over to their quick switch-count impression, so a person who is nearly indifferent to patterns is also nearly indifferent to switches, and a person who reverses the judgment (picking the sequence that looks less random) reverses their switch preference too, rather than keeping the majority's preference for more switching. This makes reversed and indifferent participants consistent across all pairs, including the short pairs that are decided mainly by switch count.
