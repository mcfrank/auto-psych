People do the same Bayesian concept learning as the best model (arithmetic rules with primes excluding 1, every range of 1-100 and range-restricted rules, with a last-shown largest example marking a range's top and a first-shown smallest marking its bottom), but their space of concepts also includes counting in round steps — "multiples of 15", "multiples of 20", "multiples of 25", "multiples of 50", familiar from clocks, scores and money — as a kind of rule with its own inferred prior share. So after 50 or 100 people say "yes" to 25 or 75 more than to 40 or 60, and after 90,45 they say "yes" to 15, 30 and 60 more than to 18 or 27, than a learner who knows only multiples up to 12.
