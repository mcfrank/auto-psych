Refinement of `top_marking_range_sampling`: people do the same Bayesian concept learning over arithmetic rules (primes excluding 1), every range of 1-100 and range-restricted rules, and believe that, with some inferred odds, the program deliberately shows its range's top number and draws the other examples at random. The one change is that people are unsure where in the sequence the marked top appears: with an inferred probability they believe the program ends on it, so only a largest example shown last can be the mark; otherwise any example could be the mark. So a largest example shown last marks the top strongly but not with certainty, and people still extend a little above it (the critique's finding that the order-only incumbent over-sharpens the top edge in max-last sets), while a largest example shown earlier still marks the top weakly instead of not at all.
