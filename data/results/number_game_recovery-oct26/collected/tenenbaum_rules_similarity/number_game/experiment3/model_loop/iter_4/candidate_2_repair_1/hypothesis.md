People take being asked about a number as a hint that the program might produce it (a suggestion, or positive-test, bias). They do the best model's Bayesian concept learning over arithmetic rules (primes excluding 1), ranges of 1-100 and range-restricted rules, with a last-shown largest example marking a range's top and a first-shown smallest marking its bottom, but before judging they count the queried number as an extra example with an inferred fraction of an example's weight. So "yes" is inflated most for targets that would fit a small, plausible concept together with the examples (e.g. 64 after 16,8, or a number just outside a tight cluster) and hardly at all for targets that only a broad concept would contain.
