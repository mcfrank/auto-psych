Refinement of `last_example_marks_range_top`: people do the same Bayesian concept learning over arithmetic rules (primes excluding 1), every range of 1-100 and range-restricted rules, with the program believed to end its demonstration on its range's top number. The one change: "even numbers" and "odd numbers" are a kind of rule in their own right, with their own inferred prior share, rather than sharing one "special sets" share equally with primes, squares and cubes. So when all the examples share a parity (e.g. 22,36,48), parity stays a live explanation next to the ranges, and people say "yes" to same-parity numbers outside the examples' span (e.g. 80) more than to opposite-parity ones (81), which addresses the critique that the incumbent under-uses parity beyond the range.
