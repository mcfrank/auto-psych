Refinement of `range_restricted_rule_concepts`: people do the same Bayesian concept learning over arithmetic rules, plain ranges of 1-100 and range-restricted rules ("even numbers in the 60s-70s", "multiples of 4 below 20"), with the size principle and "yes" = posterior probability that the concept contains the target. The one change is that the region a rule is restricted to has its own size prior: people expect how wide a rule's region is to be set separately from how wide a plain range is, with its own inferred typical width (and decade-boundary preference), instead of reusing the plain-range prior. So how sharply "yes" to rule members drops off outside the examples' region, such as 12 or 30 after 66,78, is no longer tied to how far plain-range generalization extends.
