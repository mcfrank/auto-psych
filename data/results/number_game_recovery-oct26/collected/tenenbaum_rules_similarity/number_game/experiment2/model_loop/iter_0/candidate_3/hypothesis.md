Refinement of `range_restricted_rule_concepts`: people do the same Bayesian concept learning over arithmetic rules, ranges of 1-100 and range-restricted rules ("even numbers in the 60s-70s"), with the size principle and "yes" = posterior probability the concept contains the target, but the region that restricts a rule has its own width prior, separate from the prior over plain ranges. The one change is a second inferred typical width for rule-restricting regions, so how far people extend an arithmetic pattern beyond the examples' span (e.g. "yes" to 30 or 90 after 66,78) is governed independently of how far they extend a plain numeric range.
