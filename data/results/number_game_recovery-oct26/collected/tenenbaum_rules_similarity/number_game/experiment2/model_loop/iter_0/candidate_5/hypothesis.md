Refinement of `random_example_forgetting`: people do Bayesian concept learning over 42 arithmetic rules and every range of 1-100 (kind-level rule prior, size principle with 10% random examples, Erlang width prior with a preference for decade-bounded ranges, "yes" = posterior probability the concept contains the target) from only the examples they still hold in memory, each shown example being independently forgotten with an inferred probability. The one change, grafted from `range_restricted_rule_concepts`: the concepts people entertain also include range-restricted rules ("even numbers in the 60s", "multiples of 4 below 20") with their own inferred share of prior belief, so the remembered examples can pick out a rule confined to the region they occupy — forgetting loosens generalization on large sets while the conjunctions confine "yes" to rule members near the remembered examples, and the two together predict how far rule-based "yes" extends beyond the examples' span.
