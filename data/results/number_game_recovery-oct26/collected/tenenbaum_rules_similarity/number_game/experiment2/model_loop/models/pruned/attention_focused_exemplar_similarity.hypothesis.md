People judge a target by its summed similarity to the remembered examples on a multidimensional representation of numbers (magnitude, parity, final digit, decade, divisibility by 3, 4 and 5, primality, squareness, power of two), where the metric is an attention-weighted city-block distance and similarity decays exponentially with that distance. The examples steer attention: dimensions on which the examples agree with each other (e.g. all even, all in the 60s, all ending in 3) draw attention in proportion to that agreement, by an inferred degree of focusing, so a target that differs from the examples on a dimension they share is judged far, while differences on dimensions the examples vary on barely matter.
