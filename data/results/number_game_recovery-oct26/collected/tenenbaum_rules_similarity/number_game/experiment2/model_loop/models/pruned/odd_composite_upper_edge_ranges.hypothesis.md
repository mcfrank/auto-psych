Refinement of `odd_composite_concept`: people do Bayesian concept learning over arithmetic rules (primes excluding 1), every range of 1-100 and range-restricted rules, with "odd numbers that are not prime" as a kind of rule of its own, so sets of odd composites (9,15,25) lead to "yes" for other odd non-primes but "no" for odd primes. The one change, grafted from `upper_edge_marking_ranges`: people take the largest example shown as marking where the program's range stops at the top, so a range ending exactly at the largest example gets an inferred evidence bonus while its lower end is unconstrained, and people generalize downward from the examples more readily than upward.
