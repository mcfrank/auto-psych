People infer the program's concept by plain Bayesian concept learning over arithmetic rules and numeric ranges (rules of one kind equally likely, a width prior favouring moderately wide and decade-bounded ranges, the size principle, primes excluding 1), but their answer is not a direct readout of their belief: they carry a constant response bias toward "yes" or toward "no" that shifts their confidence by the same amount on the log-odds scale on every trial. The bias therefore moves answers most for targets the learner is unsure about (posterior near one half) and hardly at all for targets that are clearly in or clearly out of the concept.
