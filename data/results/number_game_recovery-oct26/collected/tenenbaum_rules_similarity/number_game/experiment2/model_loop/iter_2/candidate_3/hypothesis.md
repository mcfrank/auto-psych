Refinement of `upper_edge_marking_ranges`: people do the same Bayesian concept learning over arithmetic rules (primes excluding 1), every range of 1-100 and range-restricted rules, and they take the largest example as marking where the program's range stops at the top — but only approximately. The one change: the inferred evidence bonus is not reserved for a range whose top is exactly the largest example; a range ending one, two or three numbers above it gets a share of the bonus that shrinks by an inferred factor with each step of the gap, so people expect the range to stop at or just above the largest example, accepting the next number or two up more readily than numbers further up, rather than placing a sharp edge exactly at the largest example.
