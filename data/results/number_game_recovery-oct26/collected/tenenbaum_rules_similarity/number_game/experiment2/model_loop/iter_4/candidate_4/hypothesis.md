Refinement of `top_marking_range_sampling`: people do the same Bayesian concept learning over arithmetic rules (primes excluding 1), every range of 1-100 and range-restricted rules, and believe that with inferred odds the program deliberately shows its range's top number and draws the other examples at random — but, the one change grafted from `uncertain_sampling_assumption`, they are unsure whether the remaining examples were drawn from the program's concept (strong sampling, size principle) or are merely numbers it happens to accept (weak sampling), and average over both readings with an inferred probability. So the marked top still blocks upward generalization, while broad concepts ("even numbers", wide ranges ending at the largest example) keep a floor of support however many examples fit a narrower one, and "yes" tightens with more examples less than the incumbent predicts.
