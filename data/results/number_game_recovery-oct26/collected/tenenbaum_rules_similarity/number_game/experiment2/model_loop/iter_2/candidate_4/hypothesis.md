Refinement of `upper_edge_marking_ranges`: people do the same Bayesian concept learning over arithmetic rules (primes excluding 1), every range of 1-100 and range-restricted rules ("even numbers from 60 to 78"), and they take the largest example as marking where the program's range stops at the top. The one change: this edge inference applies to every range people consider, including the range that restricts a rule, not only to plain ranges, so a range-restricted rule whose upper end is exactly the largest example gets the same inferred evidence bonus. The model therefore predicts that people reject rule members above the largest example (after 66,78 they say no to 80 or 90 more than to 60 or 50) as well as plain neighbours above it.
