Refinement of `pedagogical_teacher_sampling`: people assume a helpful teacher chose the examples, so distinctive examples (numbers few plausible concepts would produce) count as stronger evidence for the concepts that contain them, over arithmetic rules (primes excluding 1) and ranges of 1-100. The one change, grafted from `upper_edge_marking_ranges`: people also take the largest example as marking where the program's range stops at the top, so a range ending exactly at the largest example gets an inferred evidence bonus while its lower end is unconstrained — people generalize downward from the examples more readily than upward (after 83,77 they reject 84 but accept 75).
