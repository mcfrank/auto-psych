Refinement of erlang_interval_size_prior: people do the same Bayesian concept learning over 42 rules and all intervals of 1-100 (size principle, Erlang prior over interval width), but on some fraction of trials they do not consult their posterior at all and instead answer by a fixed yes/no guessing bias. The one change is this response lapse, which explains why people say "no" even to targets that are among the examples (about a third of the time here) and "yes" to targets no plausible concept contains, rather than forcing the concept posterior itself to absorb those answers.
