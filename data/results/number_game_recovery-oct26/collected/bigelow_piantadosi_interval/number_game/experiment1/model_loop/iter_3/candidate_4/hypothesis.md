Refinement of tempered_evidence_decision_gain: people do the same Bayesian concept learning over the 42 weighted rules and all intervals of 1-100 (size principle, 10% noisy examples) with tempered evidence, and turn their belief into a yes/no answer through the same inferred decision gain on its log-odds, but they compare that scaled evidence against a shifted decision criterion rather than against even odds. The one change, taken from response_bias_concept_learning, is this inferred criterion shift: a liberal (or conservative) criterion moves every belief's log-odds in one direction, so yeses to weakly supported targets rise (or fall) without the symmetric hedging of the gain forcing the examples' own yes rate down by the same amount.
