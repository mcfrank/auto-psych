Refinement of bigelow_piantadosi_independent: people do the same Bayesian concept learning over the 42 weighted rules and all intervals of 1-100 with the size principle, but how often they believe an example is a stray, random number rather than a true member of the concept is not fixed at 10% — it is a property of the learner, inferred from the data. The one change is this inferred outlier rate: a high rate lets a concept that fits most (but not all) of the examples keep posterior weight and weakens how sharply the examples narrow generalization, whereas a low rate makes a single misfit example rule a concept out.
