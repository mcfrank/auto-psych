Refinement of win_stay_lose_shift_concept_tracking: people still hold a single running guess about the concept (drawn from the tempered Bayesian posterior over weighted rules and intervals after the first example, kept while later examples fit it, redrawn from the current posterior when an example contradicts it), but they do not answer by occasionally guessing at random. The one change, taken from the incumbent, replaces the flat guessing floor with an inferred decision gain on the log-odds of their belief that the tracked concept contains the target: people hedge (or sharpen) symmetrically between belief and answer, so targets with near-zero belief under the order-dependent guess still get near-zero yeses.
