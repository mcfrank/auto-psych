Refinement of tempered_evidence_decision_gain: people do the same Bayesian concept learning over the 42 weighted rules and all intervals of 1-100 (size principle, 10% noisy examples, tempered evidence) and turn their belief into a yes/no answer through the same inferred decision gain on its log-odds, but they treat additional examples as partly redundant rather than as independent draws. The one change, taken from redundant_examples_tempered_concepts, is that the total evidence from a set grows as an inferred power of the number of examples instead of in proportion to it: with sublinear growth, a single example already supports a fairly tight concept while three or four examples sharpen generalization much less than strong sampling predicts (an exponent of one recovers the incumbent).
