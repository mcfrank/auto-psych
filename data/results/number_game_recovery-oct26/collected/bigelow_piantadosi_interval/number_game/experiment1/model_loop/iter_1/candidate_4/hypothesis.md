Refinement of concept_learning_with_guessing: people do the same Bayesian concept learning over 42 weighted rules and all intervals of 1-100 (size principle), with the same guessing on a fraction of trials, but the share of examples they believe are random noise rather than drawn from the concept is not fixed at 10% — it is a property of their reasoning that we infer. The single change is this inferred noise belief: the more examples people treat as possibly random, the more readily a rule survives an example that violates it and the weaker the size principle's pull toward the tightest concept, changing generalization on sets with an odd-one-out example.
