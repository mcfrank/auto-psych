Refinement of tempered_evidence_concept_guessing: people do the same Bayesian concept learning over 42 weighted rules and all intervals of 1-100 with tempered evidence and occasional guessing, but the weight each example's evidence carries depends on how many examples they were shown — evidence accumulates with diminishing (or accelerating) returns rather than at a fixed rate per example. The one change is this inferred set-size dependence of the evidence weight: with diminishing returns, a single example already sharpens the concept strongly while a third or fourth example adds little, so people generalize more narrowly from one example and more broadly from four than a fixed per-example weight predicts; no dependence recovers the incumbent.
