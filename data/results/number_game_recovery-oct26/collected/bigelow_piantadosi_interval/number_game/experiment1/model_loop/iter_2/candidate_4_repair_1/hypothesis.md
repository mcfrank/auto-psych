Refinement of tempered_evidence_concept_guessing: people do the same Bayesian concept learning over 42 weighted rules and all intervals of 1-100, with tempered evidence (each example counts for an inferred fraction or multiple of a full draw) and occasional guessing, but how often they believe an example is a stray random number rather than a member of the concept is inferred from the data instead of fixed at 10%. The one change, taken from example_noise_concept_learning, is this inferred example-noise rate: tempering sets how sharply consistent concepts are favoured by their size, while the noise rate separately sets how readily a concept survives an example that breaks it (e.g. "ends in 2" after 92, 82, 27, 42), so sets with an outlier generalize differently from what either change alone predicts.
