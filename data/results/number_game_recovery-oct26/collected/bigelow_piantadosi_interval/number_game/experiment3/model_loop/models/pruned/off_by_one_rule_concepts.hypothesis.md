People infer the program's concept by the same Bayesian weighing of familiar arithmetic rules and numeric ranges as the best model, but their space of rules also includes near-misses of the sparse rules: every power, square or cube shifted one up or one down ("one more than a power of 2": 3, 5, 9, 17, 33, 65; "one less than a square": 3, 8, 15, 24, 35, ...), which together receive a small, inferred share of their prior belief in rules. So after examples like 33, 17, 5, 9 or 8, 24, 48, 63 people should generalize to the other shifted members (65; 15, 35, 80) rather than only to the examples' range or to broad rules like "odd numbers" or "multiples of 8".
