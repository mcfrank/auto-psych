Refinement of volatile_changepoint_inflated_size (statistically tied with the best model): people still read the examples one at a time, updating a Bayesian belief over 42 weighted rules (with a rule-size prior) and all ranges of 1-100 while allowing that the program may switch concept between outputs, with perceived concept sizes inflated by a constant, tempered evidence, a re-weighted first example, a decision gain and a shifted criterion. The one change is to the concepts people entertain: because they see numbers as written digits, they also consider "numbers ending in any of these digits" (every union of two, three or four final digits, such as "ends in 2 or 8"), which together share an inferred slice of prior belief. After power-of-2 sets such as 2, 8 or 16, 32, 2, 8, such a union is smaller than "even numbers" and is not as exotic as "powers of 2". So people generalize to far even numbers that end in an example's final digit (62, 82, 48) more than to the remaining small powers of 2 (16, 4, 2), and in general a target sharing a final digit with any one example gets extra yeses even outside the examples' range. A zero share recovers volatile_changepoint_inflated_size.
