People do not always work out the arithmetic structure of the examples: on an inferred fraction of judgments they reason only with properties they can read straight off the written numerals and their magnitude (odd or even, the final digit, which digits appear, multiples of 5 or 10, numeric ranges), and only on the rest do they also consider computed rules such as powers, squares, cubes, primes and multiples of 3, 4, 6, 7, 8, 9, 11 or 12. Either way they do the same Bayesian concept learning as the best model within the concepts they have in mind, so however many power-of-two examples are shown, the judgments made without computing still generalize to other even numbers or numbers sharing a final digit (14, 24, 44 after 64, 4; 62 after 2, 8) rather than to the remaining powers, a leak that more examples cannot remove.
