Refinement of conservative_updating_inflated_size (statistically tied with the best model). People still read the examples one at a time and update conservatively: after the first example (Bayesian concept learning over 42 weighted rules with a size-dependent prior and all ranges of 1-100, perceived concept sizes inflated by a constant, one shared evidence weight, a re-weighted first example, a decision gain and a shifted criterion), each later example moves belief only an inferred fraction of the way toward its full Bayesian update. The one change, taken from the incumbent serial_position_ends_inflated_size, is a recency effect: in sets of two or more, the last example shown is still fresh in memory when the target is judged, so its update is driven by evidence weighted by its own inferred multiplier. Belief is therefore shaped most by the first example (anchoring and conservatism) and the last one (recency), and least by the middle ones; a multiplier of one recovers conservative_updating_inflated_size.
