Refinement of inflated_size_familiar_rule_prior: people do the same Bayesian concept learning over 42 weighted rules (whose prior rises as an inferred power of their size) and all ranges of 1-100, with concepts perceived as larger than they are by an inferred constant number of members, evidence tempered separately for rules and ranges, a re-weighted first example, a decision gain and a shifted criterion. The one change, taken from conservative_partial_belief_updating, is that they read the examples one at a time and revise their belief conservatively: each example after the first moves their belief only an inferred fraction of the way from what they believed before toward the full Bayesian update on it. Broad concepts adopted from the first example ("even numbers", "ends in 4", a wide range) therefore keep a share of belief after later examples point to a tiny rule, so the same numbers in a different order generalize differently, and an update fraction of one recovers the incumbent.
