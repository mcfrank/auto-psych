Refinement of inflated_size_familiar_rule_prior: people do the same Bayesian concept learning over 42 weighted rules (with a prior rising as an inferred power of each rule's size) and all ranges of 1-100, with evidence tempered separately for rules and ranges, a stronger- or weaker-weighted first example, every concept perceived as larger than it is by an inferred constant number of members, and a decision gain and shifted criterion. The one change, taken from conservative_partial_belief_updating, is that they read the examples one at a time and revise their belief conservatively: each example after the first moves their belief only an inferred fraction of the way from what they believed before toward the full Bayesian update on that example, so concepts favoured by the early examples (a broad rule or wide range after the first number) keep belief after later examples single out a tiny rule, and the same numbers in a different order generalize differently; an update fraction of one recovers the incumbent.
