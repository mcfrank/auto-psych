Refinement of inflated_size_familiar_rule_prior: people do the same Bayesian concept learning over 42 rules (whose prior rises as a power of their size) and all ranges of 1-100, with tempered evidence, a re-weighted first example, perceived concept sizes inflated by a constant, a decision gain and a shifted criterion. The one change, taken from inferred_example_noise_belief, is that how often people believe an example is a stray number picked at random rather than produced by the concept is a property of the learner, inferred from the data, rather than fixed at 10%. This belief sets how costly it is for a concept to leave one example unexplained, so it decides whether a rule shared by most examples survives an odd one out (92, 82, 27, 42 → "ends in 2"; 8, 24, 48, 63 → "multiples of 8") or loses to a wide range that covers every example; a 10% belief recovers the incumbent.
