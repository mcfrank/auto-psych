Refinement of inflated_size_familiar_rule_prior: people do the same Bayesian concept learning over 42 rules (with a size-dependent familiarity prior) and all ranges of 1-100, with perceived concept sizes inflated by a constant, evidence tempered separately for rules and ranges, the first example weighed as a stronger or weaker draw, and a decision gain and shifted criterion. The one change is a recency effect alongside that primacy effect: in sets of two or more, the last example shown, still fresh in memory when the target is judged, is also weighed as a stronger (or weaker) draw from the concept by its own inferred multiplier, so concepts that contain both the first and the last example gain belief over those that fit only the middle examples, and a concept must explain the last number more than the middle ones; a multiplier of one recovers the incumbent.
