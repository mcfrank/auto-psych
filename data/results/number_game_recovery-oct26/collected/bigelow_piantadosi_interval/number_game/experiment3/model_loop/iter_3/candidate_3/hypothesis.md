Refinement of serial_position_ends_inflated_size. People do the same Bayesian concept learning as that model: 42 rules and all ranges of 1-100, perceived concept sizes inflated by a constant, tempered evidence, first and last examples re-weighted, a rule prior that rises with rule size, and a decision gain with a shifted criterion. The one change is how familiar each rule is before any examples. Instead of 42 unrelated familiarity weights, people's familiarity with a rule is inherited from its schema (powers of n, multiples of n, "ends in d", "contains digit d", and the special rules primes, even, odd, squares and cubes), plus a rule-specific deviation whose spread is inferred. So rules of one schema are similarly familiar, and a rule the examples say little about (e.g. "powers of 7", "multiples of 11") takes its schema's familiarity rather than an arbitrary one; a very large spread recovers independent rule weights.
