Refinement of inflated_concept_size_principle: people do the same Bayesian concept learning over 42 weighted rules and all ranges of 1-100 (perceived concept sizes inflated by a constant, evidence tempered separately for rules and ranges, a re-weighted first example, a decision gain and a shifted criterion). The one change is to the concepts people entertain: because they see numbers as written digits, they also consider "numbers ending in any of these digits" — every union of two, three or four final digits, such as "ends in 2 or 8" — which together take an inferred slice of the prior belief given to rules. After power-of-2 sets such as 2, 8 or 64, 4 such a union is smaller than "even numbers" yet not as exotic as "powers of 2", so people endorse far even numbers sharing an example's final digit (62, 88, 14, 94) more than the remaining powers of 2 (16, 32), generalizing to the nested tiny rule less than the incumbent predicts; a zero share recovers inflated_concept_size_principle.
