People only go looking for an arithmetic rule once there is enough of a pattern to notice: the more examples they are shown, the more of their consideration shifts from magnitude ranges to rules such as "powers of 2", "multiples of 10" or "ends in 4", with the share of belief they give rules a priori changing by an inferred amount with each extra example (everything else as in inflated_size_familiar_rule_prior's Bayesian concept learning). So a single example is judged mostly by nearness on the number line, while three or four examples are judged mostly by the rule they share, beyond what the extra evidence alone would produce; no change with set size recovers inflated_size_familiar_rule_prior.
