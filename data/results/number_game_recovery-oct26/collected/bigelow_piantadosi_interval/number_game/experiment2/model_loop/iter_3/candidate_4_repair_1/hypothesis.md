Refinement of rule_size_prior_anchored_evidence: people do the same Bayesian concept learning (42 weighted rules with a size-dependent prior and all ranges of 1-100, evidence tempered separately for rules and ranges, a stronger- or weaker-weighted first example, a decision gain and shifted criterion), but how often they believe the program emits a stray number that does not belong to its concept is a property of the learner, inferred from the data, rather than fixed at 10%. The one change is this inferred example-noise belief: a high believed noise rate lets a tight rule survive an example that breaks it (e.g. "ends in 2" after 92, 82, 27, 42, so 52 and 62 get yeses) and weakens how much a lone mismatching example rules a concept out, while a low rate forces a concept that covers every example (here a wide range or "contains a 2"); a rate of 10% recovers the refined model.
