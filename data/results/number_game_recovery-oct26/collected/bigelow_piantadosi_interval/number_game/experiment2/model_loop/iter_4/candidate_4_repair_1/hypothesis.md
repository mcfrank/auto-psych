Refinement of volatile_concept_changepoint: people still read the examples one at a time, updating a Bayesian belief over weighted rules and ranges while allowing, with an inferred switch probability, that the program re-draws its concept from their prior between outputs (everything else as in that model). The one change is that they apply the same belief in a volatile generator to the number they are asked about: the target is itself a next output, so with the same switch probability they judge it under a freshly drawn concept (their prior belief that a number like it belongs) rather than under the concept the examples point to. This blends a set-independent, prior-based judgment into every answer, so yeses to targets the examples' concept rules out (other even numbers after powers of 2, numbers outside the examples' range) rise and the sharpness of rule-specific generalization falls, by an amount tied to the same switch probability that sets the recency of updating; ignoring the switch before the target recovers the incumbent.
