Refinement of range_bounded_rule_concepts: people do the same Bayesian concept learning over 42 weighted rules, their range-bounded versions (any unbroken run of a rule's members, e.g. "multiples of 10 from 70 to 100", taking an inferred share of each rule's prior) and all ranges of 1-100, and answer through the same decision gain and shifted criterion. The one change, taken from split_rule_range_tempering, is that the examples' evidence is tempered by separate inferred weights for arithmetic concepts (rules and their bounded runs) and for plain magnitude ranges, so people can lock onto a tight, possibly range-bounded rule from a few examples while still treating the same examples as weak evidence about where a plain range stops (equal weights recover range_bounded_rule_concepts).
