Refinement of rule_size_prior_anchored_evidence: people do the same Bayesian concept learning (42 rules with a size-dependent prior and all ranges of 1-100, evidence tempered separately for rules and ranges, a stronger-weighted first example, a decision gain and shifted criterion), but their yes/no criterion is not fixed across the session — it is pulled toward the answer they just gave. The one change is response perseveration: after answering "yes" the criterion shifts toward "yes" on the next trial, and after "no" toward "no", by an inferred amount on the log-odds, so the trial-independent incumbent's under-production of yes-after-yes runs is closed and targets with middling belief are judged differently depending on the previous answer; a zero shift recovers the incumbent.
