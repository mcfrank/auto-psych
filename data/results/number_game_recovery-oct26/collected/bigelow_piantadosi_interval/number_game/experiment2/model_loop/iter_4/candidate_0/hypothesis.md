People infer the program's concept as the best model does (sequential Bayesian learning over weighted rules and numeric ranges with a volatile generator), but checking whether a new target belongs to a believed arithmetic rule is itself fallible: verifying membership in a sparse, exotic rule ("is 64 a power of 2?", "is 27 a cube?") fails more often than in a dense, familiar one ("is 20 even?", "does 34 end in 4?"), with the verification failure rate rising smoothly as the rule gets smaller by an inferred gradient. So even when people believe "powers of 2", they give fewer yeses to its unseen members than to other even numbers, while targets they saw as examples and range membership are judged without error.
