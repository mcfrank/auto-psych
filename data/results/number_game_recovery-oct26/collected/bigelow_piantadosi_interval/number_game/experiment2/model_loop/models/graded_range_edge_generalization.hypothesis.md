People infer which numeric range or arithmetic rule the program uses just as in rule_size_prior_anchored_evidence (weighted rules with a size-dependent prior and all ranges of 1-100, evidence tempered separately for rules and ranges, a stronger-weighted first example, a decision gain and shifted criterion), but they generalize from a believed range by similarity on the number line rather than by crisp membership: a target inside the range counts fully, and one outside it counts partially, with its similarity falling off geometrically with its distance to the range's nearest edge at an inferred rate per step. So targets just past the examples' span (e.g. 51 or 59 after 52, 57, 55) get yeses that fade smoothly with distance instead of dropping to the rule-free floor, while membership in arithmetic rules is still judged exactly; a zero rate recovers the refined model.
