Refinement of split_rule_range_tempering: people do the same Bayesian concept learning over the 42 weighted rules and all intervals of 1-100 (size principle, 10% noisy examples), weigh the examples' evidence by separate inferred strengths for rules and for ranges, and answer through the same decision gain and shifted criterion. The one change, taken from round_landmark_interval_prior, is that the ranges people entertain are not equally natural a priori: ranges whose boundaries fall on round decade landmarks (e.g. 1-50, 20-29, 61-100, "the 80s") get extra prior belief by an inferred factor, so the broad range-based generalization that weak range evidence allows tends to stop at, or extend out to, the nearest decade boundary rather than fading evenly with distance from the examples.
