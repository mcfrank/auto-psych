Refinement of first_example_anchored_concepts: people do the same first-example-anchored Bayesian concept learning over weighted rules and numeric ranges (concepts containing the first example shown get extra belief; evidence tempered separately for rules and ranges) and answer through the same decision gain and shifted criterion. The one change, taken from range_bounded_rules_split_tempering, is that their rules can also be bounded to a range — besides "multiples of 10" they entertain "multiples of 10 from 70 to 100", any unbroken run of a rule's members, taking an inferred share of each rule's prior — so when the examples cluster, people generalize a rule only near the examples and give far fewer yeses to rule-consistent targets far outside the examples' span, while the first example still anchors which concepts are believed.
