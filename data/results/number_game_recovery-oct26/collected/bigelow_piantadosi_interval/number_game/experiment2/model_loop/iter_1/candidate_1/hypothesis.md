People infer the program's concept exactly as the simplest current model does (Bayesian weighing of weighted mathematical rules and numeric ranges, tempered evidence, a decision gain and response bias), but their yes/no decision is sticky: the answer they just gave on the previous trial shifts their criterion toward giving the same answer again. This response perseveration makes consecutive answers in a session more alike than the stimuli warrant — a weakly supported target gets more yeses right after a "yes" and fewer right after a "no" — without changing what concept they believe in.
