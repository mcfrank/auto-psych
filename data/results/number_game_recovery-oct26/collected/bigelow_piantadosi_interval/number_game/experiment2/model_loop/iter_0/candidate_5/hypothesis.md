Refinement of round_landmark_interval_prior: people do the same Bayesian concept learning over 42 weighted mathematical rules and all ranges of 1-100, with extra prior belief (by an inferred factor) for ranges whose boundaries fall on round decade landmarks, and answer through the same decision gain and shifted criterion. The one change, taken from split_rule_range_tempering, is that the examples' evidence (the size principle) is tempered by a separate inferred weight for rules and for ranges: people can lock onto a tight rule from a few examples while treating the same examples as only weak evidence about where a range stops, so range-based generalization stays broad and extends out to the nearest round decade boundary rather than hugging the examples' span (equal weights recover round_landmark_interval_prior).
