Refinement of first_example_anchored_concepts: people do the same first-example-anchored Bayesian concept learning over weighted rules and numeric ranges and answer through the same decision gain and shifted criterion, but their yes/no criterion is not fixed across trials — it carries over from their own previous answer. The one change is response perseveration: having just said "yes" makes the next "yes" more likely (and having just said "no" makes the next "no" more likely) by an inferred shift of the criterion on the log-odds, independent of the stimulus, which produces the sequential yes/no carryover the trial-independent incumbent fails to reproduce; zero carryover recovers the incumbent.
