Refinement of rule_size_prior_anchored_evidence: people do the same Bayesian concept learning (42 weighted rules with a size-dependent prior and all ranges of 1-100, evidence tempered separately for rules and ranges, a stronger-weighted first example, a decision gain and shifted criterion), but their yes/no criterion is not fixed across the session — it is pulled toward the answer they gave on the immediately preceding trial. The one change is this response perseveration: after a "yes" the criterion becomes more liberal and after a "no" more conservative, by an inferred amount on the log-odds scale, so answers carry over from one trial to the next independently of the stimulus (zero stickiness recovers the refined model).
