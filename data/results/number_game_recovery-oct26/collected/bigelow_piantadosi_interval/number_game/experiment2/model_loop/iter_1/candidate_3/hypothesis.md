Refinement of first_example_anchored_concepts: people do the same first-example-anchored Bayesian concept learning over weighted rules and numeric ranges (tempered evidence for rules and ranges, decision gain and shifted criterion), but their yes/no criterion is not reset between trials — having just answered "yes" makes them more inclined to answer "yes" again (and a "no" to answer "no"), a response perseveration whose strength is inferred. The one change is this carryover of the immediately preceding answer onto the decision criterion, which makes consecutive answers in a session more alike than the stimuli warrant (the lag-1 autocorrelation the incumbent under-produces); zero carryover recovers the incumbent.
