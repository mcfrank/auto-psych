Refinement of span_filling_size_exemplar: people still judge a target by its recency-weighted exponential similarity to the remembered examples (final digit, parity, graded shared factor, coherence-stretched attention), still consult the "multiples of g" and "ends in d" rules as separate evidence, still relax their criterion over the session, and still treat the span between the smallest and largest example as filled in. The single change is that the span is only partly filled in: a target's size mismatch to each remembered example blends how far it falls outside the span with its own distance from that example, so inside a wide span targets near an example still feel closer to it — and because the most recently shown example is remembered best, size generalization favours targets near the last example over targets near the first (the critique's recency_last_vs_first_example gap, which a purely span-based mismatch cannot produce).
