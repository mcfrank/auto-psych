Refinement of drifting_criterion_dual_route_exemplar: people still judge a target by its recency-weighted exponential similarity to the remembered examples (size, final digit, parity, graded shared factor, attention stretched by how much the examples resemble one another), still weigh the abstracted "multiples of g" and "ends in d" rules as separate evidence, and still relax their yes-criterion over the session. The single change is that closeness in size is judged on a compressed, logarithmic mental number line (by ratio, not by difference), both when comparing the target with each example and when judging how alike the examples are in size. So people generalize narrowly around small examples and broadly around large ones, and further above an example than below it, which predicts the upward-vs-downward generalization gap and the larger set-to-set differences in breadth that the critique reports.
