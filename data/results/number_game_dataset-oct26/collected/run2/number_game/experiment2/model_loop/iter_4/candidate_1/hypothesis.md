The single change is that people read the examples in the order shown as a sequence of jumps and take the typical jump between successive examples as the program's stride: the bigger the steps it has been taking from one example to the next, the further beyond the examples they expect its next number could land, so their size gradient is broad after big successive jumps and narrow after small ones.
