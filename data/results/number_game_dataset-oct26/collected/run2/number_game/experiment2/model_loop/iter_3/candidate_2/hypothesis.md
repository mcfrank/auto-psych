Refinement of span_filling_size_exemplar: people still infer that the program fills in the stretch of the number line the examples mark out (size mismatch = how far the target falls outside that stretch), still judge final digit, parity and shared factor by recency-weighted similarity to the examples, consult the "multiples of g" and "ends in d" rules, and relax their criterion over the session. The single change is a memory limit on that span inference: the stretch is marked out only by the examples people still remember, and the same fading memory that down-weights older examples makes them more likely to be forgotten altogether, so the inferred stretch often shrinks toward the most recently shown example. Targets near the last example are therefore endorsed more than equally placed targets near the first, which the full-span model cannot produce (the critique's last-versus-first recency gap).
