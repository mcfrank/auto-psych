Refinement of drifting_criterion_dual_route_exemplar: people still judge a target by its recency-weighted exponential similarity to the remembered examples (size, final digit, parity, graded shared factor, coherence-stretched attention), still consult the "multiples of g" and "ends in d" rules as separate evidence, and still relax their criterion over the session. The single change is that size similarity is directional: people imagine the program as producing numbers that continue upward from what it has shown, so a target a given distance above an example feels closer to it than one the same distance below. The disagreement with the best model lives just outside the examples' range: it predicts more "yes" for targets 1-20 above the largest example than for targets 1-20 below the smallest (the upward-vs-downward generalization gap the critique reports), where the best model's symmetric size distance predicts equal rates.
