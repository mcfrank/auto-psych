Refinement of coherence_decisive_dual_route_exemplar: people still judge a target by its recency-weighted exponential similarity to the remembered examples (size, final digit, parity, graded shared factor, each dimension's attention stretched by how much the examples resemble one another), still consult the abstracted "multiples of g" rule as separate evidence at the decision, and still let set coherence set how decisively the combined evidence is turned into a yes or no.
