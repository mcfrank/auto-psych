Refinement of span_filling_size_exemplar: people still judge a target by its recency-weighted exponential similarity to the remembered examples (final digit, parity, graded shared factor, coherence-stretched attention), still consult the "multiples of g" and "ends in d" rules as separate evidence, and still relax their criterion over the session. The single change is that the span between the examples is only partly filled in: a target's size mismatch with each example is a blend of how far it falls outside the span of the examples and its own distance from that example, with the filling-in share inferred. Because the per-example part of size closeness is weighted by memory, targets inside the span that lie nearer the last-shown example are endorsed more than those nearer the first — the recency-in-size-generalization the critique reports, which a pure span mismatch (identical for every example) cannot produce.
