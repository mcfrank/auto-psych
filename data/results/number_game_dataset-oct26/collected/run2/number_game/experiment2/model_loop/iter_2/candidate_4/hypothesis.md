Refinement of drifting_criterion_dual_route_exemplar: people still judge a target by its recency-weighted exponential similarity to the remembered examples (size, final digit, parity, graded shared factor, attention stretched by how much the examples resemble one another), still weigh the abstracted "multiples of g" and "ends in d" rules as separate evidence, and still relax their yes-criterion over the session. The single change is that psychological distance in size is asymmetric: people treat the program's numbers as more likely to continue upward, so a target a given amount larger than an example feels closer to it than a target the same amount smaller. They therefore generalize further to numbers above the examples than below them, which closes the upward-vs-downward generalization gap the critique reports and which a symmetric size distance cannot produce.
