Refinement of dual_route_factor_and_digit_rule_exemplar: people still judge a target by its recency-weighted exponential similarity to the remembered examples (size, final digit, parity, graded shared factor, attention stretched by how much the examples resemble one another) and still consult an abstracted explicit rule alongside similarity at the decision, but the rules they can abstract are not just "multiples of g" and "ends in d". The single change is the rule space: people entertain every counting-by-steps rule the examples fit ("start from an example and step by s", i.e. numbers leaving the same remainder as the examples when divided by s, for any step s that carries every example to every other), each favoured a priori the smaller its step, with steps of 10 extra natural because of base-10 notation, and weigh them by how unlikely it is that the examples would line up on that step by chance. So after 5, 9 people endorse 13 and 17 and after 83, 77 they endorse 71 and 89, regardless of size, which neither the factor nor the final-digit rule predicts, while "ends in d" (step 10) and "multiples of g" (remainder 0) remain special cases.
