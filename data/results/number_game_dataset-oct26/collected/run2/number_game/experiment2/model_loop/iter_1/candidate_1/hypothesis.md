People judge a target by its plain recency-weighted similarity to the remembered examples (closeness in size, shared final digit, shared parity, graded shared factor), but the criterion they compare that familiarity against is not fixed: as the session wears on their response criterion relaxes steadily, so the same familiarity earns "yes" more often late in the session than early. The single claim is a decision-level, gradually drifting yes-bias over trials (a criterion shift), with the inference itself left as simple exemplar similarity.
