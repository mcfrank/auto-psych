Refinement of iter1_candidate4 (dual-route exemplar with progressive disengagement): people still judge a target by its recency-weighted exponential similarity to the remembered examples (size, final digit, parity, graded shared factor, attention stretched by how much the examples resemble one another), still consult the abstracted "multiples of g" and "ends in d" rules as separate evidence, and still disengage and guess on a growing share of trials over the session. The single change is that size similarity is directional: people treat a number larger than a remembered example as psychologically closer to it than an equally distant smaller number (as if the program is expected to keep counting upward), so they generalise more to targets above the examples than below them — closing the critique's upward-versus-downward generalisation gap, which symmetric size distance cannot produce.
