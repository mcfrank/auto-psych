Refinement of drifting_criterion_dual_route_exemplar: people still judge a target by its recency-weighted exponential similarity to the remembered examples (size, final digit, parity, graded shared factor, attention stretched by how much the examples resemble one another), still weigh the abstracted "multiples of g" and "ends in d" rules as separate evidence, and still relax their yes-criterion over the session. The single change is that size similarity is directionally asymmetric: people treat the program as more likely to go on to larger numbers than back to smaller ones, so a target a given distance above an example is judged closer to it than one the same distance below. This predicts more "yes" answers to targets just above the largest example than just below the smallest, closing the critique's upward-vs-downward generalization gap that symmetric size distance cannot produce.
