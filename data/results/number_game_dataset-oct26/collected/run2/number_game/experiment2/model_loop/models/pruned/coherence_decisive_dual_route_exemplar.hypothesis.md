Refinement of dual_route_shared_factor_rule_exemplar: people still judge a target by its recency-weighted exponential similarity to the remembered examples (size, final digit, parity, graded shared factor, each dimension's attention stretched by how much the examples resemble one another) and still consult the abstracted "multiples of g" rule as separate evidence at the decision when the examples share a factor of 3 or more. The single change, taken from coherence_sharpened_decision_exemplar, is that how much the examples resemble one another also sets how decisively people answer: after a coherent set the combined evidence (similarity plus rule, relative to their criterion) is turned into a near-deterministic yes or no, whereas after an incoherent set or a single example answers stay noisy — so responses to the same item are more consistent across people than the incumbent predicts (the cell_response_dispersion gap) and the "ends in d" contrast in coherent same-digit sets is sharper.
