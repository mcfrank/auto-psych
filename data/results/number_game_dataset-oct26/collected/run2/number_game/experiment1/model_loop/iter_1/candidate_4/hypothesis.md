Refinement of recency_weighted_exemplar_similarity: people judge a target by its recency-weighted similarity to the remembered examples, but when several examples share a common factor of 3 or more they also abstract that shared factor as a property of the whole set, and a target that is not a multiple of it is judged psychologically farther from every example. The single change is this set-level "shared common factor" mismatch added to the exemplar distance (an inferred weight; 0 recovers the incumbent), which predicts more "yes" answers to multiples of the examples' greatest common divisor than pairwise divisibility, digit and parity similarity alone.
