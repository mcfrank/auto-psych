A refinement of bigelow_piantadosi_independent: people still do Bayesian concept learning over the same 42 rules and all intervals of 1-100 with the size principle, but they weigh the evidence of each example by how recently it was shown — the last example counts fully and each earlier one is discounted by a further inferred factor — so the concepts favoured, and hence "yes", lean toward the most recent examples. The single change is this recency discount on the examples' likelihood (the incumbent is the special case of no discount).
