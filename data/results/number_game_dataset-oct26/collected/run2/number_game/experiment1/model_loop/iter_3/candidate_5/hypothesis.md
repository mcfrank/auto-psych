Refinement of coherence_tuned_attention_exemplar: people still judge a target by its recency-weighted similarity to the remembered examples, with attention stretched toward the dimensions (size, final digit, parity, shared factor) on which the examples resemble one another, but how readily a shared property captures attention differs by kind of property: a run of examples all ending in the same digit is far more conspicuous than shared parity or closeness in size. The single change is that each dimension has its own coherence sensitivity instead of one common stretch rate, so sets whose examples share a final digit (or a factor) make people generalize sharply to targets matching that property, closing the "ends in d" and "multiples of g" rule gaps, while a single example still leaves attention at its baseline.
