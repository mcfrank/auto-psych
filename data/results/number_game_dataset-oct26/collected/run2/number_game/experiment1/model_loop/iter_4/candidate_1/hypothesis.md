Refinement of coherence_sharpened_decision_exemplar: people still judge a target by its recency-weighted similarity to the remembered examples (size, final digit, parity, graded shared factor), with attention stretched toward the respects in which the examples resemble one another and set coherence sharpening the decision, but they judge closeness in size on a compressed, Weber-like mental number line rather than a linear one. The single change is that size differences among small numbers loom larger than equal differences among large numbers (e.g. 4 and 8 feel farther apart than 84 and 88), with the degree of compression inferred, so generalization from small examples is narrow and from large examples broad.
