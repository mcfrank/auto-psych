The single change, taken from leading_digit_string_similarity, is that people also compare numbers as written digit strings by their leading digit: a target whose first written digit matches an example's (34 after 31 or 3) feels more similar to that example, all-or-none, with attention to this match stretched when the examples share a leading digit. So inside the range-inference account, targets sharing the examples' leading digit are endorsed more, and those that do not are endorsed less, than range-relative distance alone predicts — e.g. after 3, 8 a two-digit 35 can feel close while 20 does not.
