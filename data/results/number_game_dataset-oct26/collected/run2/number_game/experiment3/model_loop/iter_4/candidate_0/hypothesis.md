Refinement of leading_digit_string_similarity (all else unchanged). The single change is to the "multiples of g" concepts people consider: the family now runs from g = 2 ("even numbers") upward, and the concepts are not equally plausible in advance. Skip-counting bases that are round in base ten or reached by doubling (2, 4, 5, 8, 10, 20, 25, 50 and the powers of two) have a higher prior plausibility than other bases (3, 6, 7, 9, 14 ...). So after examples that share a round base (e.g. 15, 85, 45 or 16, 8), multiples of that base are endorsed and non-multiples rejected more than coincidence evidence alone predicts. After examples that share a less natural base (e.g. 3, 33, 99 or 36, 45, 54, 81), that rule is applied more weakly.
