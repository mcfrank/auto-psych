People reason about the examples relationally, by analogy, as well as by surface similarity. They notice how the examples relate to each other (the difference between two examples, or the ratio between them: 20 then 30 is "add 10", 8 then 16 is "double"). They then ask whether the target relates to some example in the same way, so a target reached by applying an observed relation to another example (40 after 20, 30; 32 after 8, 16) gets extra support for "yes" beyond its feature similarity. Everything else is as in leading_digit_string_similarity.
