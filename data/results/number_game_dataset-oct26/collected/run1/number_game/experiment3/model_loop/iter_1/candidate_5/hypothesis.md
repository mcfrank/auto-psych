A refinement of `contains_digit_concept` (final-digit similarity judged against the examples' mutual similarity, rule-pre-empted parity credit, a common-factor boost, rule-gated decade-band familiarity and "contains the digit d" support, all kept). The one change is that people's response criterion is not fixed across the session: as they work through the trials their threshold for saying "yes" shifts steadily with how far into the session they are (with direction and rate inferred), so the same set and target is accepted more (or less) often late in the session than early on. This addresses the critique that people drift toward "yes" later in the session, which a stationary model cannot produce.
