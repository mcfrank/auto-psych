A refinement of `shortfall_from_mutual_similarity` (final-digit similarity with only the target's shortfall below the examples' mutual similarity counting against "yes", rule-pre-empted parity credit and rule-gated decade-band familiarity, all kept). The one change is to the common-factor rule: when the examples' common factor is a multiple of 5 (counting by 5s, 10s or 25s, the round base-ten structure of the number system), people find "multiples of that" far more compelling than a common factor such as 3, 4 or 8, so round targets that fit it (e.g. 100, 80, 50 after 5, 65, or 100 after 25) get a larger, separately inferred boost toward "yes" than multiples of a non-decimal factor (e.g. 64 after 8, 80, 48).
