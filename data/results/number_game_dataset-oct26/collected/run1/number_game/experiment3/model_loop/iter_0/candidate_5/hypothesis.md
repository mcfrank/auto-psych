A refinement of `rule_gated_decade_familiarity` (final-digit similarity averaged over the examples with "ends in that digit" rule pre-empting parity credit, a common-factor boost, and decade-band familiarity that an explicit rule pre-empts, all kept). The one change is to the final-digit similarity metric itself: besides parity, people treat the "round" digits 0 and 5 as kin, so a target ending in 0 is judged partly similar to an example ending in 5 (and vice versa) even though their parities differ, with that kinship credit inferred. So after examples like 5, 65 or 90, 100, 5, round targets such as 80, 50 or 100 are accepted far more than parity-based similarity predicts.
