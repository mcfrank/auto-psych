A refinement of `contains_digit_concept` (final-digit similarity judged against the examples' mutual similarity, rule-pre-empted parity credit, a common-factor boost, rule-gated decade-band familiarity and "contains the digit d" support, all kept). The one change is to how decisive the judgment is: comparing the target with each example is a separate mental comparison, and the more examples there are to weigh, the noisier the combined judgment becomes (or, if the inferred direction is reversed, the more examples, the more confident). So with a lone example people answer nearly all-or-none, and each further example blurs the decision toward a graded response, all by an inferred amount. This addresses the critique that people's judgments are more unanimous per item than the incumbent's lukewarm probabilities, which in the data is concentrated in single-example sets.
