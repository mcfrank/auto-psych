A refinement of the incumbent `digit_rule_size_principle_counting` (shortfall from the examples' mutual final-digit similarity, rule-pre-empted parity credit, a privileged round common-factor rule, rule-gated decade-band familiarity, step-costed counting up from single examples, and the size principle on a shared final-digit rule, all kept). The one change, a component taken from `privileged_named_number_concepts`, is that "even numbers" is a privileged, named concept while "odd numbers" is not: when every example is even, people readily take "even numbers" as the program's rule and reject odd targets by an extra, inferred amount (e.g. 37 after 16, 8, 64 or after 68, 58), whereas an all-odd set evokes no "odd numbers" rule and its even targets pay nothing extra.
