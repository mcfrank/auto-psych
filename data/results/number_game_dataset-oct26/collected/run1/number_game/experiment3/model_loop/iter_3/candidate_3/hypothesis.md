A refinement of the incumbent `privileged_named_number_concepts` (shortfall from the examples' mutual final-digit similarity, rule-pre-empted parity credit, the privileged "even numbers" rule, separate round and non-round common-factor boosts, and rule-gated decade-band familiarity, all kept). The one change is a size principle on the mutual-similarity standard: each further example that conforms to the examples' shared pattern is more evidence that the pattern is the program's rule and not a coincidence, so the penalty for a target falling short of that standard grows, by an inferred amount, with every example beyond the second. So after three or four examples all ending in the same digit, targets with a different final digit are rejected much more firmly than after two such examples.
