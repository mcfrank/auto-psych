A refinement of `diminishing_similarity_decade_band`: people still judge the target by its final-digit similarity to the examples (same final digit most similar, same parity partly, opposite parity least), with diminishing returns from each further resembling example, a boost for multiples of the examples' common factor, and full leading-digit familiarity inside the band of decades the examples span, fading outside it. The one change is that when every example ends in the same final digit, people take that shared digit as the program's rule and, by a size-principle-like sharpening, give a different digit of the same parity less and less partial credit with each additional example that confirms the shared digit. This addresses the critique that, in sets whose examples all share one final digit, people accept parity-only matches far less than the model predicts.
