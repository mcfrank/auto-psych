A refinement of `decade_band_digit_common_factor`: people still say "yes" in proportion to the target's average final-digit similarity to the examples, with a boost for multiples of the examples' common factor and full decade-band familiarity fading outside the band. The one change is rule abstraction on the final digit: when two or more examples all end in the same digit, people abstract the explicit rule "ends in that digit", and this rule pre-empts the graded parity similarity, so a target of the same parity but a different final digit loses (by an inferred share) the partial credit it would otherwise get. The sharpest disagreement with the incumbent lives in uniform-final-digit sets (e.g. 13, 43, 73): the incumbent accepts same-parity, different-digit targets (e.g. 15) far more than opposite-parity ones, whereas this model predicts they are rejected about as often.
