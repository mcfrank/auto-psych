People judge the target by the simple final-digit similarity used in the simplest current model (same final digit most similar, same parity partly, opposite parity least), but their decision criterion is not fixed over the session: as the session goes on and they see more and more of the program's output without feedback, their bias toward answering "yes" shifts steadily, so the same (set, target) pair is accepted more (or less) often the later in the session it is asked.
