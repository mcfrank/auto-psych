A refinement of `nearest_decade_digit_common_factor`: people still say "yes" in proportion to the target's average final-digit similarity to the examples (same final digit most similar, same parity partly, opposite parity least), with a boost for multiples of the examples' common factor and a familiarity boost from the example in the nearest decade. The one change is that, when several examples are shown, people also take the stretch of the number line between the smallest and largest example as ground the program covers, so a target lying strictly inside the examples' range gets an extra, inferred boost toward "yes", whatever its digits or decade. This addresses the critique that the incumbent under-predicts "yes" for targets inside the examples' range compared with targets outside it (range interpolation).
