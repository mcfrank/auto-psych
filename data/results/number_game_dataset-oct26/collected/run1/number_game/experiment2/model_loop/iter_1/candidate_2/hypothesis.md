A refinement of `nearest_decade_digit_common_factor` that changes its space of arithmetic concepts: instead of entertaining only one rule, "multiples of the examples' greatest common factor (3 or more)", people entertain every "multiples of d" concept that all the examples obey (d = 2 and up, every common divisor, e.g. multiples of 2, 4 and 8 after 16, 8, 64), and weigh them by the size principle, so smaller, more specific concepts are favoured more strongly the more examples there are, with an inferred strength. A target's arithmetic boost toward "yes" is the resulting graded belief that it lies in the inferred multiples concept, alongside the unchanged final-digit similarity and nearest-decade familiarity.
