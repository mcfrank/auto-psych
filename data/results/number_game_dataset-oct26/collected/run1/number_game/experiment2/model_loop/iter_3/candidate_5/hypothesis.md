A refinement of `shared_digit_rule_preempts_parity` (final-digit similarity averaged over the examples, with an abstracted "ends in that digit" rule pre-empting parity credit when the examples share a final digit, a common-factor boost and decade-band familiarity all kept). The one change is that people's response criterion is not fixed over the session: as the session goes on they grow steadily more lenient, so their bias toward answering "yes" drifts by an inferred amount with how far into the session the trial comes, applied equally to every set and target. This addresses the critique (surviving FDR) that people say "yes" increasingly often over the session, which a stationary model cannot produce.
