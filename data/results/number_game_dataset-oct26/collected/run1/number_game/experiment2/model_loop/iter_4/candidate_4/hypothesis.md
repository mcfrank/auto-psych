A refinement of `rule_gated_decade_familiarity` (final-digit similarity with rule-pre-empted parity credit, common-factor boost, and decade-band familiarity cut back when an explicit rule is detected, all kept). The one change: people's response criterion is not fixed over the session but relaxes with experience — as they answer more questions about the hidden program they come to believe it produces more numbers than they first thought, so their readiness to say "yes" to any given (set, target) rises over the early part of the session and then levels off, at an inferred rate and size. This addresses the critique that people drift toward "yes" over the session (the same stimulus is accepted more when asked late than early), which a stationary model cannot produce.
