A refinement of `nearest_decade_digit_common_factor`: people still say "yes" in proportion to the target's average final-digit similarity to the examples (same final digit most similar, same parity partly, opposite parity least), with a boost for multiples of the examples' common factor and a leading-digit (decade) familiarity boost that decays with each decade of separation. The one change is that people fill in the decades lying between the smallest and largest example, treating that whole band of decades as familiar territory, so the decade boost is full for any target whose decade lies within the examples' decade span and decays only with how many decades the target lies beyond that span, rather than with its distance to the nearest single example's decade. This addresses the critique that the incumbent under-predicts "yes" for targets inside the examples' range relative to targets outside it.
