A refinement of the incumbent `final_digit_rule_suppresses_parity` (final-digit similarity with rule-suppressed parity credit, common-factor boost and decade-band familiarity all kept). The one change: how much people lean on decade-band (magnitude-neighbourhood) familiarity depends on how many examples they have seen — with a single example there is no shared pattern to abstract, so people generalize mostly to its numerical neighbourhood (its decade), and each additional example shifts reliance away from the neighbourhood toward the digit and factor patterns the examples share, with an inferred rate of decline. This addresses the critique that after a single example people accept same-decade targets with a different final digit far more than the incumbent predicts.
