A refinement of `final_digit_rule_suppresses_parity` (final-digit similarity with parity credit suppressed by a shared final digit, common-factor boost and decade-band familiarity all kept). The one change: people's response criterion is not fixed over the session but loosens steadily as they answer more questions, so with every judgment made their bias toward saying "yes" grows by an inferred amount, whatever the stimulus. This addresses the critique that people drift toward "yes" over the session, which the stationary model cannot produce.
