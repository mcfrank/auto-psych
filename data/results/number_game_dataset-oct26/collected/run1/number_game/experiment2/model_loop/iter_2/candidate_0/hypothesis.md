People judge the target by its similarity to the stored examples, and the breadth of their generalization around those exemplars is set by how variable the exemplars are (the category-variability effect): when the examples all end in the same digit, the similarity gradient on final digit is tight, so a target that merely shares their parity gets almost no credit, whereas when the examples' final digits vary, the gradient is broad and same-parity targets are accepted almost as readily as digit matches. Apart from this variability-tuned digit gradient, the comparison is the current best model's (final-digit similarity averaged over examples, full leading-digit familiarity within the examples' band of decades fading outside it, and a boost for multiples of their common factor).
