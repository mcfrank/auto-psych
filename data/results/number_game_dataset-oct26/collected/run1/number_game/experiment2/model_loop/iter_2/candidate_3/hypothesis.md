A refinement of `decade_band_digit_common_factor`: people still say "yes" in proportion to the target's final-digit similarity to the examples (same final digit most similar, same parity partly, opposite parity least), with a boost for multiples of the examples' common factor and full leading-digit familiarity anywhere in the band of decades the examples span, fading outside it. The one change is that when two or more examples all end in the same digit, people read that shared final digit as the program's rule, so a target that merely shares the examples' parity with a different final digit loses some or all of its partial parity credit (an inferred amount of suppression). This addresses the critique that, in sets whose examples share one final digit, people accept parity-only matches far less than the incumbent predicts.
