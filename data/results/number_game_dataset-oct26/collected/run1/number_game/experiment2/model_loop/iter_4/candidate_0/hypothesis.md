A refinement of `rule_gated_decade_familiarity` (final-digit similarity with an "ends in that digit" rule pre-empting parity credit, a common-factor boost, and decade-band familiarity that detected rules pre-empt, all kept). The one change: when two or more examples share no explicit rule (mixed final digits and no common factor of 3 or more), people sometimes conclude that the program just produces arbitrary numbers, and on those occasions they stop comparing the target with the examples and say "yes" at a flat rate that does not depend on the target. So rule-less sets flatten toward uniform acceptance: far, unmatched targets get accepted much more than similarity predicts, and sets differ more in how broadly people generalize.
