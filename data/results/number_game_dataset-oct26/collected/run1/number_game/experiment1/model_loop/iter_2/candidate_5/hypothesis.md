A refinement of `nearest_exemplar_luce_similarity`: people still judge the target by its similarity to the most similar example on arithmetic features, combined with magnitude similarity and compared against a Luce criterion, but the one change is that on the magnitude dimension they fill in the gaps between the examples, treating the whole span from the smallest to the largest example as already "seen", so magnitude dissimilarity is the target's distance to that span (zero anywhere inside it) rather than to the nearest single example. Targets inside the examples' range are therefore accepted more readily than equally feature-similar targets outside it, addressing the critique that the incumbent misses range-based generalization.
