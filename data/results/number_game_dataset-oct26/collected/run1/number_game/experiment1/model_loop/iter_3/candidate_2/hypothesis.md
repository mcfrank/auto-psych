People do not look for shared properties or similarity; they infer the generating operation from the relations among the examples themselves (relational, analogical extrapolation). They notice the differences and whole-number ratios linking pairs of examples (e.g. 16 is 4 times 4, 83 is 15 below 98) and say "yes" to a target that the same step produces from one of the examples (64 after 4,16,12; 68 after 98,83), and "no" otherwise, so a lone example, which carries no relation, gives no basis for extrapolation.
