# Critique of `final_digit_similarity_gradient`

6 of 8 evaluated test statistics show a significant discrepancy (p ≤ 0.05), over 1000 posterior-predictive replicates.

## Significant discrepancies (a better model should address these)

Raw two-sided p shown with a Benjamini-Hochberg FDR-adjusted q across this round's statistics. Prioritise discrepancies that survive the FDR (`q ≤ alpha`); a raw-only hit may be one of several screened at once.
- **set_mean_variance** — Variance across number sets (with >=18 responses) of the per-set mean yes rate; observed above null means sets differ in overall generalization breadth more than the digit-similarity model with a single intercept allows. (observed 0.0309 vs null mean 0.0176, z=4.07, p=0.002, q=0.00533) [survives FDR]
- **item_mean_variance** — Variance across (set, target) items of the item mean yes rate; observed above null means human judgments differ across items more sharply than the digit-similarity model predicts (model too flat / missing item-relevant features), below means the model over-discriminates. (observed 0.0551 vs null mean 0.0409, z=3.93, p=0.002, q=0.00533) [survives FDR]
- **inside_minus_outside_range** — On 2-4 example sets, yes rate for targets strictly inside the examples' min-max range minus targets outside it; observed above null means people use interval/range-based generalization that the magnitude-blind digit model lacks, below null means they favour out-of-range targets more than predicted. (observed 0.0075 vs null mean -0.0797, z=3.89, p=0.002, q=0.00533) [survives FDR]
- **opposite_parity_yes_rate** — Yes rate for targets whose parity differs from every example (zero final-digit/parity similarity, the model's floor) on sets with >=2 examples; observed above null means people accept parity-violating targets more than the digit model's floor allows (parity/digit is not the operative dimension), below null means they reject them more sharply. (observed 0.199 vs null mean 0.263, z=-2.46, p=0.00799, q=0.016) [survives FDR]
- **trial_index_drift** — Pearson correlation between trial_index and rating (yes); the model has no time dependence so the null is ~0, observed above null means participants say yes more as the session goes on, below means they become more conservative over trials. (observed 0.0425 vs null mean -0.00256, z=2.35, p=0.018, q=0.0288) [survives FDR]
- **math_rule_without_digit_match** — Among targets sharing no final digit with any example on 2-4 example sets, yes rate for targets that share every non-parity arithmetic property common to all examples (mult of 3/4/5/10, power of 2, perfect square; set must have >=1 such property) minus yes rate for targets lacking all of them; observed above null means people generalize by mathematical rules that the final-digit model cannot see. (observed 0.154 vs null mean 0.0549, z=2.06, p=0.04, q=0.0533)
