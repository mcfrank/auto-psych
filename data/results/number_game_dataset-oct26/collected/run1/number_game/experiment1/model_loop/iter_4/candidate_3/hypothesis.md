A refinement of `final_digit_similarity_gradient`: people still say "yes" in proportion to the target's average final-digit similarity to the examples (same final digit most similar, same parity partly, opposite parity least), but the one change is that each example's digit resemblance counts for more when that example is numerically close to the target, so an example's contribution is discounted (by an inferred amount and over an inferred distance) the farther it lies from the target. Proximity thus matters only through the examples the target resembles in its final digit — a near digit-matched target is accepted more readily than a far one — rather than as a separate range or span boost, addressing the critique that numerical closeness boosts digit-matched targets.
