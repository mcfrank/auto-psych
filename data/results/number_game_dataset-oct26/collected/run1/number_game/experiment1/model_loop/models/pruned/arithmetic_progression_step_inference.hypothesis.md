People infer that the program counts in fixed steps: the concepts they consider are arithmetic progressions through 1-100 (all numbers with the same remainder after dividing by some step size, e.g. counting by 2s, 5s, 10s or 7s from the examples), preferring small step sizes a priori but, by the size principle, favouring the largest step that all the examples fit. They say "yes" with the posterior probability that the target lies on the inferred progression, so targets congruent with the examples are accepted however far away they are, and acceptance is all-or-none per step rather than graded by final-digit resemblance.
