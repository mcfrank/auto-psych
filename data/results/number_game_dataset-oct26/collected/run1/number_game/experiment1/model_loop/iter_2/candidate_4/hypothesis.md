A refinement of `shared_property_rule_matching`: people still say "yes" to the degree that the target has the arithmetic properties all the examples share, regardless of its distance from them, but they also treat the span between the smallest and largest example as territory the program covers, so a target lying inside that range gets an extra, inferred boost toward "yes" (the one change, a range-membership component taken from `range_extension_by_spacing`). So, as the critique found, in-range targets are accepted more often than rule matching alone predicts, while rule-consistent targets far outside the range are still accepted.
