Refinement of `erlang_sized_range_guessing_concepts`: people do the same conservative Bayesian concept learning over the 42 arithmetic rules and midpoint-weighted, decade-favoured intervals of 1-100 with a preference for ranges of intermediate width (Erlang size prior), evidence weight growing with set size, and guessing that becomes rarer as more examples are shown — but how readily they accept that an example could be a stray number produced outside the program's concept is their own inferred belief rather than a fixed one-in-ten chance (the exception tolerance grafted from `outlier_tolerant_rule_concepts`). The one change is this inferred tolerance for exceptions, which predicts that when all examples but one fit a rule or a moderately wide range, people keep that concept and endorse far targets that fit it, instead of retreating to a range spanning every example.
