Refinement of `outlier_tolerant_rule_concepts`: people do the same conservative Bayesian concept learning (42 arithmetic rules, midpoint-weighted decade-favoured ranges, evidence weight growing with set size, inferred tolerance for stray examples, guessing that becomes rarer with more examples), but their concept space also holds final-digit-restricted ranges — "numbers ending in 3 from 13 to 53" — as a family with its own inferred share of prior belief, split equally among them. The one change is this family: because such a range is ten times smaller than the plain range and smaller than the whole final-digit rule, the size principle favours it when examples share a final digit, so people reject both in-range numbers with a different final digit (e.g. 25 after 13, 33, 43) and same-digit numbers far beyond the examples (e.g. 93 after 13, 23, 33) more than the incumbent predicts — the two final-digit discrepancies its critique flags.
