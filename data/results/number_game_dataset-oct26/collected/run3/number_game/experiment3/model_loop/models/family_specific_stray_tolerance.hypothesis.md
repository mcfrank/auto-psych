Refinement of `late_session_acquiescence_concepts`: people do the same conservative Bayesian concept learning over the 42 arithmetic rules and midpoint-weighted, decade-favoured number ranges (evidence weight growing with set size, guessing that becomes rarer as examples accumulate, blind "yes" growing over the session), but their readiness to write off an example as a stray is not one belief shared by every concept: they allow an arithmetic rule its exceptions at one inferred rate and a number range at its own separately inferred rate. The one change is this concept-family-specific tolerance for strays; if people treat a range as defined by the span of the examples (so a range that leaves an example outside is implausible) while still letting a near-fitting rule keep its odd example, they fill the whole span of patternless sets (e.g. "yes" to 40 or 55 after 67, 31, 17, 61) more than a learner who lets a narrower range discard an example — the excess in-range "yes" for rule-less sets that the incumbent's critique flags.
