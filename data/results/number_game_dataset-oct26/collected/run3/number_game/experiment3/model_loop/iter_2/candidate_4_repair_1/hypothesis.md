Refinement of `outlier_tolerant_rule_concepts`: people do the same conservative Bayesian concept learning (42 arithmetic rules, midpoint-weighted decade-favoured intervals, evidence weight growing with set size, inferred tolerance for stray examples, guessing that becomes rarer with more examples), but they can also hold a rule as confined to a round stretch of the number line — "primes in the 20s to 40s", "multiples of 3 between 10 and 39" — a family of decade-bounded rule bands with its own inferred share of the rules' prior belief, split equally among the bands. The one change is this family of decade-bounded rules: because a band is smaller than the whole rule, the size principle favours it when the examples cluster, so people extend the shared property to targets near the examples but much less to far targets that have it (e.g. fewer "yes" to 89 after 23, 29, 31) — the over-extension of the prime rule beyond the examples' range that the critique of the incumbent flags.
