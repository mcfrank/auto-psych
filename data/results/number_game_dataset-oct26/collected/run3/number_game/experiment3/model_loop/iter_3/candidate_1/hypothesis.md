People do not infer a rule; they judge the target by how close it is on a compressed (logarithmic, Weber-like) mental number line to the nearest example, so a gap of 5 feels large between 3 and 8 but small between 83 and 88. Endorsement falls off exponentially with this compressed distance, and the generalization gradient narrows as more examples are shown; apart from occasional blind guesses, "yes" follows this ratio-scale similarity to the closest example.
