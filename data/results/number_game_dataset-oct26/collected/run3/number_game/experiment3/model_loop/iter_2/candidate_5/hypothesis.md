Refinement of `parity_banded_range_concepts`: people do the same conservative Bayesian concept learning over arithmetic rules, decade-favoured number ranges and parity-restricted ranges ("the odd numbers from 51 to 67"), with evidence weight growing with set size, an inferred tolerance for stray examples and guessing that becomes rarer as examples accumulate — but as the long session wears on they increasingly answer "yes" without consulting their concept at all, a blind-"yes" acquiescence whose rate rises with how far into the session the trial falls. The one change is this session-growing acquiescence (the component of `late_session_acquiescence_concepts`, grafted onto the parity-banded learner), which predicts that targets the concept excludes — including opposite-parity neighbours that parity-banded ranges reject sharply — are endorsed more late in the session than early for the same stimulus, the within-stimulus late-session "yes" drift the incumbent's critique flags.
