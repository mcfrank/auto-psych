Refinement of `outlier_tolerant_rule_concepts`: people do the same conservative Bayesian concept learning over the 42 arithmetic rules and midpoint-weighted, decade-favoured intervals of 1-100 (evidence weight growing with set size, an inferred tolerance for stray examples, guessing that becomes rarer as more examples are shown), but their space of concepts also contains final-digit rules restricted to a stretch of the number line — "numbers ending in 2 from 22 to 62" — held as a family with its own inferred share of prior belief, split equally among them. The one change is this family of digit-banded ranges: because such a concept is smaller than the full final-digit rule, the size principle favours it when the examples share a last digit, so people reject both in-range numbers with a different last digit and same-digit numbers far beyond the examples' span — the two over-generalizations the critique of the incumbent flags for same-last-digit sets.
