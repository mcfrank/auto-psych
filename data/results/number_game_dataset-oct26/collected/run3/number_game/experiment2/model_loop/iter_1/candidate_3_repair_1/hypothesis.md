Refinement of `evidence_scarce_guessing_concepts`: people do the same conservative Bayesian concept learning over the 42 arithmetic rules and midpoint-weighted, decade-favoured intervals of 1-100 (evidence weight growing with set size, guessing that becomes rarer as more examples are shown), but they do not weigh the examples' evidence equally for the two kinds of concept: how strongly a tight fit counts in a concept's favour (the size principle) differs between arithmetic rules and number ranges, by an inferred ratio. The one change is this family-specific evidence weight, which, if people discount the coincidence that examples cluster in a narrow range relative to the coincidence that they all fit a rule, predicts that sets whose examples share a property (e.g. all even, or all ending in 4) are explained by that rule rather than by a nearby range, so nearby targets lacking the property (e.g. 25 after 22, 26, 30) are rejected more sharply — the excess opposite-parity "yes" rate the critique of the incumbent flags.
