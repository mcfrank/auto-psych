Refinement of `family_weighted_evidence_concepts`: people do the same conservative Bayesian concept learning over the 42 arithmetic rules and midpoint-weighted, decade-favoured intervals of 1-100 (evidence weight growing with set size, a family-specific evidence weight for ranges versus rules, guessing that becomes rarer as more examples are shown), but when they do consult their concept they do not report their belief by probability matching: they say "yes" when their confidence that the target belongs clears a criterion, applied with inferred sharpness (the decision stage grafted from `criterion_decision_decade_concepts`). The one change is this criterion-based decision on the concept-consulting trials, which predicts that, after several examples, answers to a given item are more all-or-none and consensual than the graded posterior implies — moderately believed targets (e.g. nearby rule violators) are pushed toward a consistent answer — the excess within-item gradedness the incumbent's critique flags.
