Refinement of `session_growing_acquiescence_concepts`: people do the same conservative Bayesian concept learning over the 42 arithmetic rules and midpoint-weighted, decade-favoured ranges of 1-100 (evidence weight growing with set size, blind "yes" growing over the session), but they also entertain parity-restricted ranges — "the even numbers from 20 to 40", "the odd numbers in the 60s" — as concepts in their own right, with an inferred prior share. The one change is this family of conjunctive range-plus-parity concepts: because such a concept is half the size of the plain range, the size principle makes it win whenever two or more examples are close together and share a parity, so people reject nearby targets of the opposite parity more sharply than a plain range would predict — the too-lenient parity-violator "yes" rate the critique of the best model flags — while a single example still generalizes to both parities.
