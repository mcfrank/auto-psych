Refinement of `evidence_scarce_guessing_concepts`: people do the same conservative Bayesian concept learning over the 42 arithmetic rules and midpoint-weighted, decade-favoured intervals of 1-100 (evidence weight growing with set size, guessing that becomes rarer as more examples are shown), but their repertoire of ranges also includes parity-restricted ranges — "even numbers between 20 and 40", "odd numbers from 31 to 67" — held with their own inferred prior share. The one change is this family of parity-restricted ranges, which the size principle favours whenever several examples all share one parity, so people reject a nearby target of the opposite parity (e.g. 31 after 24, 28, 36) more sharply than a plain-range learner — the excess opposite-parity "yes" rate the critique of the incumbent flags.
