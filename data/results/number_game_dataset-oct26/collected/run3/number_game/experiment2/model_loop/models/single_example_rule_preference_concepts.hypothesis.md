Refinement of `session_growing_acquiescence_concepts`: people do the same conservative Bayesian concept learning over the 42 arithmetic rules and midpoint-weighted, decade-favouring intervals of 1-100 (evidence weight growing with set size, a blind-"yes" rate that grows over the session), but a lone example does not suggest a number range to them — with nothing to span, they look for an arithmetic property of that number instead, so when the set has a single example the rules receive an inferred extra share of prior belief at the expense of ranges. The one change is this single-example shift of prior belief from ranges toward rules, which predicts broader generalization from one example to far-away numbers sharing its property (e.g. 29 after 89, 35 after 5) while its immediate neighbours (88 after 89) stay rarely endorsed — the single-example non-member yes-rate the incumbent's critique flags as too low.
