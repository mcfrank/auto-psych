Refinement of `evidence_scarce_guessing_concepts`: people do the same conservative Bayesian concept learning over the 42 arithmetic rules and midpoint-weighted, decade-favoured intervals of 1-100 (evidence weight growing with set size), and on some trials they skip the concept and simply guess with their own leaning toward "yes", guessing less when more examples are shown — but they also disengage as the long session wears on, so the chance of guessing instead of consulting the concept rises with how far into the session the trial falls (an inferred fatigue rate, grafted as the session-growth component of `session_growing_acquiescence_concepts`). The one change is this session-growing disengagement of the guessing process, which predicts that late in the session excluded targets get more "yes" (and included ones slightly fewer), most strongly after few examples — the upward drift in "yes" over trials the critique of the incumbent flags.
