Refinement of `decade_concept_bayesian_learning`: people do the same conservative Bayesian concept learning over arithmetic rules, midpoint-weighted intervals and decade concepts (evidence weight growing with set size, blind "yes"/"no" lapses), but as the long session wears on they increasingly answer "yes" without consulting their concept, so the chance of a blind "yes" drifts with how far into the session the trial falls, at an inferred rate. The one change is this session-drifting yes-lapse rate, which predicts that targets the concept excludes are endorsed more often late in the session than early — the growing liberality the critique of the best model flags.
