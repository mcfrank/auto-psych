Refinement of bigelow_piantadosi_interval: people judge by the same Bayesian concept learning over rules and midpoint-weighted intervals, but on some trials they do not consult the concept posterior and instead guess, and the yes-rate of that guessing drifts over the session as time on task grows. The one change is this session-drifting guess process mixed into the response, which addresses the critique that yes responses become more frequent later in the session.
