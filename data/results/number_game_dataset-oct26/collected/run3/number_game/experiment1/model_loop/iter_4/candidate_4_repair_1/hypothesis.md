Refinement of `decade_aligned_range_concepts`: people do the same conservative Bayesian concept learning over the 42 arithmetic rules and midpoint-weighted, decade-favoured intervals of 1-100, with evidence weight growing with set size, but they do not report their belief by probability matching — they say "yes" when their confidence that the target belongs clears a personal criterion, applied noisily (the decision rule grafted from `soft_criterion_concept_decision`). The one change is this criterion-based decision stage (an inferred criterion and decision sharpness), which predicts that targets the learner believes moderately are pushed toward a consistent answer, and that near-certain inclusions and exclusions are softened, rather than "yes" rates equal to the posterior probability everywhere.
