Refinement of `decade_aligned_range_concepts`: people do the same conservative Bayesian concept learning over the 42 arithmetic rules and midpoint-weighted intervals of 1-100, with evidence weight growing with set size, and they think about number ranges in round decades — but they anchor each end of a range at a decade boundary independently, so a range earns the same inferred prior bonus once for a lower edge on a decade start and again for an upper edge on a decade end, instead of a bonus only when both ends are decade-aligned. The one change is this per-edge decade anchoring, which predicts that generalization stretches to the decade boundary on one side even when the range's other end is not decade-aligned (e.g. after 23, 25, 34 people endorse 38-39 more than the incumbent predicts, while 21-22 gets no matching boost).
