Refinement of `bigelow_piantadosi_interval`: people answer by the same Bayesian concept learning over 42 arithmetic rules and all intervals of 1-100 (rule weights inferred, interval prior a Beta density over the midpoint, size principle), but on some trials they stop consulting the concept and answer yes or no at random, and these random lapses become more frequent as the session wears on (fatigue). The single change is this time-dependent lapse, which both softens the model's near-certain predictions and produces the observed drift toward more yeses late in the session.
