Depth-2 RSA with the salience prior as common knowledge: listeners reason two levels deep (inverting a speaker who simulates the pragmatic listener) while treating object salience, shaped by feature count and familiarization base rates, as mutually shared common knowledge across all levels of reasoning. Relative to rsa_l2, this retains depth-2 reasoning (choice_probs calls L2) but adds the shared salience prior component from rsa_l1_shared_prior, introducing parameters w_features and w_familiar. This weights object referents by the salience prior in L0, L1, and L2, and predicts choice driven by the prior rather than uniform guessing on uninformative prior trials, improving fit on base-rate and prior-elicitation tasks while preserving depth-2 pragmatic resolution.
