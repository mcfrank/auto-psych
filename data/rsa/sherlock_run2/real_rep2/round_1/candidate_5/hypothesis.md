Listeners reason at depth 2 (inverting a speaker who simulates the depth-1 pragmatic listener) with object salience based on feature count and familiarization base rate treated as common knowledge across all levels of the reasoning hierarchy, rather than assuming a uniform prior. This improves on depth-2 RSA by capturing base-rate sensitivity and non-uniform prior expectations on prior-elicitation trials while preserving recursive pragmatic implicature resolution. Differences from rsa_l2: recursion depth remains 2 (choice_probs calls L2); parameters w_features and w_familiar are added (no parameters removed); the salience prior is included in L0, L1, and L2 as common knowledge; and choice_probs returns the salience prior on prior trials instead of uniform guessing.
