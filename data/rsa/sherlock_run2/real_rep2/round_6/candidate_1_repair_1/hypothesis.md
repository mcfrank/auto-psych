Listeners operate under limited visual attention across the display: hearing a referring expression directs their attentional spotlight to candidate referents matching the uttered word, while non-candidate distractors enter communicative modeling with attention that decays exponentially with their perceptual distance from the candidates. Distractors that share visual features with candidate items intrude into focal attention and demand communicative differentiation, whereas visually distant distractors are preattentively filtered out of the visual scene. On uninformative prior trials without an informative word, visual attention is distributed uniformly across all objects.
