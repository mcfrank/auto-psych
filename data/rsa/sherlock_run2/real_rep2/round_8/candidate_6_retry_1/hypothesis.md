Listeners interpret referring expressions through depth-3 pragmatic reasoning that jointly accounts for perceptual isolation, contextual feature surprisal, graded semantic applicability, utterance extension costs, and prior familiarization experience. When resolving an ambiguous referring expression, listeners invert a speaker who anticipates a depth-2 pragmatic listener's interpretation, expecting speakers to refer preferentially to objects that exhibit nearest-neighbor visual contrast, contain rare distinctive features, and have higher prior familiarization base rates, while penalizing feature dilution and overextended descriptions. On uninformative prior trials, listeners' expectations directly follow this integrated prior of perceptual isolation, feature surprisal, and familiarization history.

This model refines `surprisal_isolated_graded_costly_l3` by incorporating familiarization base-rate sensitivity from `familiar_isolated_graded_costly_l2` into the shared referent prior. While depth-3 recursive reasoning, nearest-neighbor isolation, feature surprisal, graded semantics, and utterance extension costs successfully resolve complex pragmatic implicatures and visual pop-out, participants also rely on exposure frequency when evaluating referents and predicting choices a priori.

Differences from `surprisal_isolated_graded_costly_l3`:
- Recursion depth: Remains 3 (`choice_probs` calls `L3`).
- Parameters added: `w_familiar` with prior `dist.Normal(0.0, 2.0)`. Parameters removed: None (retains `alpha`, `w_distinct`, `w_surprisal`, `gamma`, `cost_weight`, and `lapse`).
- Other terms: In `choice_probs`, the object prior integrates familiarization base rates alongside perceptual isolation and feature surprisal: `prior = softmax_prior(params["w_distinct"] * dist_vec + params["w_surprisal"] * surp_vec + params["w_familiar"] * ctx.familiarization)`. All other components (`L0`, `L1`, `L2`, `L3`, `object_isolation`, `object_feature_surprisal`, `compute_graded_lex`, utterance extension costs, and the lapse mixture) remain identical.
