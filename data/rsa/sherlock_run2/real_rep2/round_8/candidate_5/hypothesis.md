Listeners interpret referring expressions through depth-4 pragmatic reasoning under perceptual isolation, contextual feature surprisal, graded semantics, and utterance extension costs, converting posterior beliefs into choices via a softmax decision rule rather than strict probability matching. By applying choice determinism over posterior communicative beliefs, boundedly rational listeners calibrate the sharpness of their referent selection independently of the speaker's communicative rationality, softening over-confident higher-order implicatures while preserving recursive pragmatic coordination. On uninformative prior trials without an informative word, listeners' expectations directly follow the integrated prior of perceptual isolation and feature surprisal.

This model refines `surprisal_isolated_graded_costly_l4` by incorporating the softmax decision rule over posterior beliefs from `softmax_belief_listener`. While depth-4 recursive reasoning, nearest-neighbor isolation, feature surprisal, graded semantics, and utterance extension costs successfully resolve complex pragmatic implicatures and singleton pop-out, standard RSA assumes strict probability matching where action probabilities equal communicative beliefs; decoupling choice determinism from speaker rationality allows boundedly rational listeners to modulate response determinism against high-order recursive beliefs, preventing over-penalization of competing referents on multi-object displays.

Differences from `surprisal_isolated_graded_costly_l4`:
- Recursion depth: Remains 4 (`choice_probs` calls `L4`).
- Parameters added: `beta` with prior `dist.LogNormal(0.0, 1.0)`. Parameters removed: None (retains `alpha`, `w_distinct`, `w_surprisal`, `gamma`, `cost_weight`, and `lapse`).
- Other terms: In `choice_probs`, posterior beliefs `heard` from `L4` are transformed by choice determinism `beta` via a normalized softmax transformation over non-zero belief logits (`logits = params["beta"] * jnp.log(safe_heard)`), producing choice probabilities `choice` before the uniform lapse mixture. All other components (`L0`, `L1`, `L2`, `L3`, `L4`, `object_isolation`, `object_feature_surprisal`, `compute_graded_lex`, utterance extension costs, and the prior over objects) remain identical.
