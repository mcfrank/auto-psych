Listeners operate under visual attentional constraints, allocating primary attention to referents that match the heard utterance while discounting non-matching distractors in the display. When simulating the speaker's communicative choices, this attentional bottleneck attenuates the mental representation of distractors, leading listeners to underestimate the ambiguity of competing alternative features that overlap with unattended objects. On uninformative prior trials without a focal utterance, attention is distributed uniformly across all objects in the display.
