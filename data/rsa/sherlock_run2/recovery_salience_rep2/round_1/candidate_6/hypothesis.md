We refine rsa_l1 by replacing its uniform object prior with a feature-complexity prior where candidate referents are weighted by their total number of visual features. Rather than assuming all objects are equally likely a priori, listeners assume the speaker has a prior over referents modulated by feature count, favoring simpler (or more salient) entities, and rely on this same prior when no informative word is provided. Differences from rsa_l1: recursion depth remains depth 1 (choice_probs calls L1); one parameter w_features is added (Normal(0, 1) prior); and the uniform referent prior is replaced by softmax_prior(w_features * ctx.feature_count) in the speaker's referent distribution and on prior trials.
