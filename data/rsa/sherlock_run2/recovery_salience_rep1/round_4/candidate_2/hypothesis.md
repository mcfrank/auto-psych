The participant population is cognitively heterogeneous, composed of a mixture of literal and pragmatic listener types. Literal listeners interpret messages semantically by choosing uniformly among all objects matching the heard word, whereas pragmatic listeners invert an informative speaker to infer intended referents via pragmatic implicature. Observed choices in the reference game reflect the mixture of these two cognitive sub-types across the population, falling back to uniform guessing when no informative word is provided.
