We refine rsa_l1 by introducing a feature-simplicity prior over referents, positing that communicators expect referents with fewer defining features to be targeted more readily than complex referents. In choice_probs, recursion depth remains unchanged at depth 1 (calling L1), one parameter is added (simplicity with a Normal(0, 1) prior), and no parameters are removed. In L1, the speaker samples referents according to a softmax prior over object feature counts rather than uniformly, and uninformative prior trials predict this prior rather than a uniform distribution.
