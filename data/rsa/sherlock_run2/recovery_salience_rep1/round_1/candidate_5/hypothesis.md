Listeners reason at depth 2 while integrating familiarization base rates into their prior expectations about which referent the speaker intends to convey, choosing by base rate when no informative word is spoken. Compared to rsa_l2, this refines the object prior from uniform to familiarization base rates (defaulting to uniform when absent), explaining human sensitivity to exposure frequency in reference games. Differences from rsa_l2: recursion depth is unchanged (choice_probs calls L2); no parameters are added or removed (retaining alpha and lapse); L0, L1, and L2 take an object prior vector derived from familiarization base rates, and choice_probs returns this prior on uninformative prior trials.
