Listeners integrate pragmatic speaker reasoning with empirical base rates established during prior familiarization, weighting referents by their baseline exposure frequency rather than assuming an equiprobable prior over objects. On conflict displays such as the low-baserate target condition of experiment E5, where a rare implicature target (base rate 1/9) competes with a highly familiar two-feature object (base rate 7/9), depth-2 RSA predicts near-exclusive choice of the implicature target (>94%) because it assumes uniform priors, whereas base-rate tracking predicts that the familiar competitor will be chosen frequently (~40%), matching human choices.
