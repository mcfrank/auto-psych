We refine depth-2 RSA with singleton salience, contextual distinctiveness, and literal L0 (rsa_l2_singleton_distinct_literal_l0) by incorporating the familiarization base-rate component from rsa_l2_singleton_distinct_familiar into the shared perceptual and experiential salience prior across communicative reasoning levels. When interpreting referring expressions or choosing referents a priori, speakers and listeners reason recursively at depth 2 with a shared prior favoring visually unique singletons and contextually distinctive items while additionally assigning higher prior prominence to objects encountered more frequently during experiential familiarization, while simulated foundational literal listeners interpret words purely according to truth-conditional semantics without perceptual or experiential bias. This fits better by maintaining depth-2 recursive reasoning, discrete singleton salience, continuous feature distinctiveness, and uncompounded literal semantics, while correcting the incumbent's inability to integrate experiential exposure frequency on base-rate trials. Differences from rsa_l2_singleton_distinct_literal_l0: recursion depth remains depth 2 (choice_probs calls L2); parameter w_familiar (Normal(0.0, 2.0)) is added; no parameters are removed; and the shared softmax salience prior computed in choice_probs adds w_familiar times each object's familiarization base rate (ctx.familiarization) to the existing singleton and distinctiveness terms.
