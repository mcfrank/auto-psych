We refine depth-2 RSA with singleton salience, contextual distinctiveness, and grammatical exhaustivity (rsa_l2_singleton_distinct_exhaustivity) by decoupling truth-conditional literal semantics from perceptual salience at the base level of recursive mental simulation. When interpreting referring expressions or evaluating referents a priori, listeners reason recursively at depth 2 about speakers whose communicative choices are guided by perceptual singleton and contextual feature distinctiveness priors while penalizing referents with superfluous unmentioned features, but those speakers simulate a foundational literal listener who interprets words purely according to conventional truth-conditional semantics without perceptual bias. This fits better by preventing the redundant compounding of visual salience priors across recursive reasoning tiers while preserving grammatical exhaustification and visual distinctiveness during pragmatic reference resolution. Differences from rsa_l2_singleton_distinct_exhaustivity: recursion depth remains depth 2 (choice_probs calls L2); no parameters are added; no parameters are removed; in L0, the listener chooses referents weighted purely by truth-conditional semantics (wpp=at(lex, u, r)) rather than prior-weighted semantics (wpp=vec(prior, r) * at(lex, u, r)); and in L1, the speaker simulates L0[u, r](lex) without passing the salience prior.
