We refine depth-2 RSA with singleton and distinctiveness salience and literal L0 (rsa_l2_singleton_distinct_literal_l0) by incorporating the familiarization base-rate component from rsa_l2_singleton_distinct_familiar into the shared perceptual salience prior. When interpreting referring expressions or choosing referents a priori, speakers and listeners reason recursively at depth 2 under an object prior combining discrete visual singleton salience, continuous contextual distinctiveness, and experiential exposure frequency, while the simulated foundational literal listener L0 interprets words purely according to conventional truth-conditional semantics without prior bias. This fits better by maintaining uncompounded literal semantics and recursive pragmatic competence on complex reference displays while capturing empirical human preferences for frequently exposed objects on familiarization trials where the incumbent assumes uniform prior expectations. Differences from rsa_l2_singleton_distinct_literal_l0: recursion depth remains depth 2 (choice_probs calls L2); parameter w_familiar (Normal(0.0, 2.0)) is added; no parameters are removed; and the shared softmax salience prior computed in choice_probs adds w_familiar times each object's familiarization base rate to the existing singleton and distinctiveness terms.
