Listeners assume the speaker is egocentric rather than communicative: when referring to an object, the speaker simply samples uniformly from among that object's true features, and the listener inverts this generative model to favor referents with fewer total features. This mechanism disagrees most sharply with depth-2 RSA on complex reference displays where one referent has an alternative feature shared with a distractor while another referent has a unique alternative feature: recursive RSA predicts a strong pragmatic preference for the former, whereas the egocentric speaker listener predicts exact 50/50 indifference because both referents possess the same number of features.
