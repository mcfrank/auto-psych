We refine depth-2 RSA (rsa_l2) by incorporating the listener salience prior component from rsa_l1_salience into the depth-2 pragmatic listener. Listeners reason at depth 2 (inverting a speaker who simulates a depth-1 pragmatic listener), but rather than assuming uniform prior expectations over referents, the listener combines speaker informativeness with an object prior shaped by perceptual feature counts and familiarization base rates, which also directly guides choices on uninformative prior trials. This fits better by retaining depth-2 recursive reasoning for complex visual displays while capturing empirical base-rate and familiarization preferences that the uniform incumbent ignores. Differences from rsa_l2: recursion depth remains depth 2 (choice_probs calls L2); parameters w_features (Normal(0.0, 1.0)) and w_familiar (Normal(0.0, 2.0)) are added; no parameters are removed; in L2, the listener assigns objects prior weight vec(prior, r) when inverting the speaker; and choice_probs predicts this salience prior rather than uniform guessing on prior trials.
