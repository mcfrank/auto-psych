We refine depth-2 RSA (rsa_l2) by incorporating the shared salience prior component from rsa_l1_shared_prior as common knowledge across all reasoning levels. Listeners reason at depth 2 (inverting a speaker who simulates a depth-1 pragmatic listener), but rather than assuming uniform prior expectations over referents, all agents share a prior shaped by perceptual feature counts and familiarization base rates, which also governs choices on uninformative prior trials. This fits better by maintaining depth-2 recursive competence for complex reference games while capturing non-uniform baseline preferences and familiarization effects where the incumbent suffered. Differences from rsa_l2: recursion depth remains depth 2 (choice_probs calls L2); parameters w_features (Normal(0, 1)) and w_familiar (Normal(0, 2)) are added; no parameters are removed; and a shared softmax salience prior weights referents at L0, S1, and S2, and replaces uniform guessing on prior trials.
