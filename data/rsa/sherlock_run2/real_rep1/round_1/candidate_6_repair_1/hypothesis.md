We refine rsa_l2_shared_prior by incorporating visual color contrast into the shared perceptual salience prior across all reasoning levels. When interpreting referring expressions or choosing referents a priori, speakers and listeners reason recursively at depth 2 while treating fully colored objects as perceptually more prominent than desaturated grayscale distractors alongside visual feature count and familiarization frequency. Differences from rsa_l2_shared_prior: recursion depth remains depth 2 (choice_probs calls L2); parameter w_color (Normal(0.0, 1.0)) is added; no parameters are removed; and the shared salience prior includes a color contrast term weighting colored over grayscale referents.
