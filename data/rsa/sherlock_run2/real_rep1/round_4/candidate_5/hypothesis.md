We refine depth-2 RSA with singleton salience and contextual distinctiveness (rsa_l2_singleton_distinctiveness) by incorporating the familiarization base-rate component from rsa_l2_shared_prior into the shared perceptual salience prior across all reasoning levels. When interpreting referring expressions or choosing referents a priori, speakers and listeners reason recursively at depth 2 under a shared prior that favors visually unique singletons and contextually distinctive items, while additionally assigning higher prior prominence to objects encountered more frequently during familiarization. This fits better by maintaining depth-2 recursive reasoning, discrete singleton salience, and continuous feature distinctiveness, while resolving the incumbent's inability to integrate experiential exposure frequency on base-rate trials. Differences from rsa_l2_singleton_distinctiveness: recursion depth remains depth 2 (choice_probs calls L2); parameter w_familiar (Normal(0.0, 2.0)) is added; no parameters are removed; and the shared softmax salience prior computed in choice_probs adds w_familiar times each object's familiarization base rate to the existing singleton and distinctiveness terms.
