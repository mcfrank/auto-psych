We refine rsa_l2_singleton_prior by incorporating the bounded-rational softmax decision rule from softmax_decision_listener into the depth-2 pragmatic listener. When interpreting referring expressions, listeners reason recursively at depth 2 with a shared visual singleton prior favoring unique objects over duplicated distractors, but select behavioral choices via an exponentiated softmax decision rule over inferred beliefs rather than passive probability matching. This fits better by retaining depth-2 recursive competence and singleton salience for complex displays and prior trials, while resolving the severe deficit of standard probability matching on unambiguous or highly salient targets where human participants exhibit much sharper decisional confidence and natural baseline decision noise. Differences from rsa_l2_singleton_prior: recursion depth remains depth 2 (choice_probs calls L2); parameter beta (LogNormal(1.0, 1.0)) is added; no parameters are removed; in L2, the listener chooses referents weighted by exp(beta * Pr[speaker.r == r]) rather than Pr[speaker.r == r]; and choice_probs passes beta to L2.
