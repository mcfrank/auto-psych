We refine depth-2 RSA with singleton and distinctiveness salience (rsa_l2_singleton_distinctiveness) by decoupling truth-conditional literal semantics from perceptual salience at the base level of recursive mental simulation. When interpreting referring expressions or evaluating referents a priori, listeners reason recursively at depth 2 about speakers whose communicative choices are guided by perceptual singleton and contextual feature distinctiveness priors, but those speakers simulate a foundational literal listener who interprets words purely according to conventional truth-conditional semantics without perceptual bias. This fits better by preventing the incumbent's redundant compounding of visual salience priors across recursive reasoning tiers, properly preserving the balance between semantic informativeness and perceptual prominence during pragmatic reference resolution. Differences from rsa_l2_singleton_distinctiveness: recursion depth remains depth 2 (choice_probs calls L2); no parameters are added; no parameters are removed; in L0, the listener chooses referents weighted purely by truth-conditional semantics (wpp=at(lex, u, r)) rather than prior-weighted semantics (wpp=vec(prior, r) * at(lex, u, r)); and in L1, the speaker simulates L0[u, r](lex) without passing the salience prior.
