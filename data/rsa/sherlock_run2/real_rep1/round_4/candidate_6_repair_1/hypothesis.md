We refine rsa_l2_singleton_exhaustivity by incorporating the contextual feature distinctiveness component from distinctiveness_attention_listener into the shared perceptual salience prior across all reasoning levels. When interpreting referring expressions or evaluating referents a priori, speakers and listeners reason recursively at depth 2 under a shared prior that favors unique singletons lacking identical counterparts as well as objects with greater continuous visual feature contrast relative to the rest of the display, while the depth-2 pragmatic listener additionally applies an exhaustification penalty to referents possessing superfluous unmentioned features. Differences from rsa_l2_singleton_exhaustivity: recursion depth remains depth 2 (choice_probs calls L2); parameter w_distinct (Normal(0.0, 1.0)) is added; no parameters are removed; and the shared softmax salience prior computed in choice_probs adds w_distinct times each object's contextual feature distinctiveness (total Hamming distance from all other objects) to w_singleton times the singleton indicator.
