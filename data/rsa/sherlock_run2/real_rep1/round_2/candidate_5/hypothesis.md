We refine depth-2 RSA (rsa_l2) by incorporating a shared visual singleton salience prior across all reasoning levels. When interpreting referring expressions or choosing referents a priori in visual displays containing duplicated distractors, speakers and listeners reason recursively at depth 2 while treating unique singleton objects that have no identical counterparts as perceptually prominent targets. This fits better by maintaining depth-2 recursive pragmatic reasoning for complex reference games while correctly capturing the strong empirical human preference for unique singleton referents over duplicated items on ambiguous descriptions and uninformative prior trials, where standard uniform RSA severely underperforms. Differences from rsa_l2: recursion depth remains depth 2 (choice_probs calls L2); parameter w_singleton (Normal(0.0, 1.0)) is added; no parameters are removed; a shared softmax singleton salience prior weights referents at L0, S1, and S2; and choice_probs predicts this singleton salience prior rather than uniform guessing on prior trials.
