We refine depth-2 RSA (rsa_l2) by incorporating the grammatical exhaustivity component from exhaustivity_listener into the depth-2 pragmatic listener. Listeners reason recursively at depth 2 (inverting a speaker who simulates a depth-1 pragmatic listener), but rather than relying solely on the speaker's communicative likelihood, the listener applies an exhaustification penalty to any referent possessing additional unmentioned features in the context. This fits better by combining depth-2 recursive reasoning for complex visual displays with grammatical exhaustification that disfavors referents with superfluous competing features, resolving the incumbent's primary deficit on displays with varying feature density. Differences from rsa_l2: recursion depth remains depth 2 (choice_probs calls L2); parameter lambda_exh (LogNormal(0.0, 1.0)) is added; no parameters are removed; in L2, the listener chooses referents weighted by Pr[speaker.r == r] * exp(-lambda_exh * (vec(feature_count, r) - at(lex, u, r))) rather than Pr[speaker.r == r]; and listener: knows(u) is added to L2 so the listener evaluates feature unmentionedness for the heard utterance.
