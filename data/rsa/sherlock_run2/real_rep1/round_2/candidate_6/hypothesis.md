We refine rsa_l2_salience by incorporating visual color contrast into the listener salience prior. When interpreting referring expressions or choosing referents a priori, depth-2 pragmatic listeners combine communicative likelihood with an unshared perceptual salience prior that favors fully colored objects over desaturated grayscale distractors alongside visual feature count and familiarization frequency, while simulated lower-level communicative agents reason with uniform expectations. Differences from rsa_l2_salience: recursion depth remains depth 2 (choice_probs calls L2); parameter w_color (Normal(0.0, 1.0)) is added; no parameters are removed; and the listener salience prior includes a visual color contrast term weighting colored over grayscale referents.
