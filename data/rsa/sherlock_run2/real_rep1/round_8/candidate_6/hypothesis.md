We refine depth-2 RSA with singleton and distinctiveness salience, visual feature complexity, visual color contrast, literal L0, and grammatical exhaustivity (rsa_l2_singleton_distinct_feat_color_exh) by incorporating the communicative valence framing component from valence_salience_listener into the shared perceptual and evaluative salience prior across reasoning levels. When interpreting referring expressions or choosing referents a priori, speakers and listeners reason recursively at depth 2 under a shared prior where communicative framing determines the evaluative appeal of visual features—favoring feature-rich objects under positive framing or default neutral communication, but inverting to favor minimal or featureless objects when describing a least favorite item—while also accounting for visual singleton salience, contextual distinctiveness, chromatic color contrast, foundational truth-conditional literal semantics at L0, and grammatical exhaustification penalizing superfluous unmentioned features. Differences from rsa_l2_singleton_distinct_feat_color_exh: recursion depth remains depth 2 (choice_probs calls L2); parameter w_valence (Normal(0.0, 1.0)) is added; no parameters are removed; and the shared softmax salience prior computed in choice_probs modulates the visual feature complexity term by communicative framing valence, adding w_valence times framing valence times feature count to the existing singleton, distinctiveness, feature count, and color terms.
