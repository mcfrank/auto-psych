We refine depth-2 RSA with singleton and distinctiveness salience, visual feature complexity, visual color contrast, and literal L0 (rsa_l2_singleton_distinct_feat_color_l0) by incorporating the perceptual load component from perceptual_load_listener into the communicative rationality mechanism. When interpreting referring expressions or choosing referents a priori, speakers and listeners reason recursively at depth 2 under an object prior combining discrete visual singleton salience, continuous contextual distinctiveness, visual feature complexity, and visual color contrast with an uncompounded literal L0 listener, but communicative rationality is diluted by the total perceptual load of all visual features in the display. This fits better by maintaining recursive pragmatic competence and perceptual salience priors while capturing human bounded rationality under visual clutter, allowing sharp pragmatic inferences on simple displays and more entropy on complex scenes where fixed-rationality models overpredict confidence. Differences from rsa_l2_singleton_distinct_feat_color_l0: recursion depth remains depth 2 (choice_probs calls L2); parameter w_load (LogNormal(-1.0, 1.0)) is added; no parameters are removed; in choice_probs, effective rationality is scaled to alpha_eff = alpha / (1.0 + w_load * total_features) where total_features is the sum of ctx.feature_count across the display; and choice_probs passes alpha_eff to L2 instead of alpha.
