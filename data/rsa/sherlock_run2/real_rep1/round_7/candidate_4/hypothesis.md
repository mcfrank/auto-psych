We refine depth-2 RSA with singleton and distinctiveness salience, visual feature complexity, visual color contrast, and literal L0 (rsa_l2_singleton_distinct_feat_color_l0) by incorporating the familiarization base-rate component from rsa_l2_singleton_features_familiar_l0 into the shared perceptual and experiential salience prior across communicative reasoning levels. When interpreting referring expressions or choosing referents a priori, speakers and listeners reason recursively at depth 2 under an object prior combining discrete visual singleton salience, continuous contextual distinctiveness, visual feature complexity, visual color contrast favoring fully colored objects over desaturated grayscale distractors, and experiential exposure frequency favoring frequently encountered objects, while the simulated foundational literal listener L0 interprets words purely according to conventional truth-conditional semantics without perceptual or experiential bias. This fits better by maintaining uncompounded literal semantics, recursive pragmatic competence, visual feature complexity, and chromatic color contrast across complex reference games while capturing empirical human preferences for frequently exposed objects on familiarization trials where the incumbent assumes uniform prior expectations across exposure frequencies.
