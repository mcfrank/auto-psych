When interpreting referring expressions, listeners rely on a distractor divergence penalty heuristic rather than recursive Theory-of-Mind mental simulation. The listener restricts candidate referents to objects literally matching the uttered word and penalizes candidates in proportion to their total visual feature distance from the non-matching distractors in the scene, favoring referents that differ from the ruled-out distractors along minimal feature dimensions. On uninformative trials with no descriptive word, listeners choose uniformly among the objects.
