We refine depth-2 RSA with singleton salience, contextual distinctiveness, grammatical exhaustivity, and literal L0 (rsa_l2_singleton_distinct_literal_exh) by incorporating the visual feature count salience component from rsa_l2_singleton_distinct_features_l0 into the shared perceptual salience prior across communicative reasoning levels. When interpreting referring expressions or choosing referents a priori, speakers and listeners reason recursively at depth 2 under an object prior combining discrete visual singleton salience, continuous contextual distinctiveness, and visual feature complexity, while the depth-2 pragmatic listener additionally applies an exhaustification penalty to referents possessing superfluous unmentioned features and the simulated foundational literal listener L0 interprets words purely according to conventional truth-conditional semantics without perceptual bias. Differences from rsa_l2_singleton_distinct_literal_exh: recursion depth remains depth 2 (choice_probs calls L2); parameter w_features (Normal(0.0, 1.0)) is added; no parameters are removed; and the shared softmax salience prior computed in choice_probs adds w_features times each object's visual feature count (ctx.feature_count) to the existing singleton and distinctiveness terms.
