We refine depth-2 RSA with singleton and distinctiveness salience, visual feature complexity, and literal L0 (rsa_l2_singleton_distinct_features_l0) by incorporating the familiarization base-rate component from rsa_l2_singleton_distinct_familiar_l0 into the shared perceptual and experiential salience prior across communicative reasoning levels. When interpreting referring expressions or choosing referents a priori, speakers and listeners reason recursively at depth 2 under an object prior combining discrete visual singleton salience, continuous contextual distinctiveness, visual feature complexity, and experiential exposure frequency, while the simulated foundational literal listener L0 interprets words purely according to conventional truth-conditional semantics without prior bias. This fits better by maintaining uncompounded literal semantics, recursive pragmatic competence, and visual feature complexity on multi-feature reference displays while capturing empirical human preferences for frequently exposed objects on familiarization trials where the incumbent assumes uniform prior expectations across exposure frequencies. Differences from rsa_l2_singleton_distinct_features_l0: recursion depth remains depth 2 (choice_probs calls L2); parameter w_familiar (Normal(0.0, 2.0)) is added; no parameters are removed; and the shared softmax salience prior computed in choice_probs adds w_familiar times each object's familiarization base rate (ctx.familiarization) to the existing singleton, distinctiveness, and feature count terms.
