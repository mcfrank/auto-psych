We refine depth-2 RSA with singleton salience (rsa_l2_singleton_prior) by incorporating the contextual feature distinctiveness component from distinctiveness_attention_listener into the shared perceptual salience prior across all reasoning levels. When interpreting referring expressions or evaluating referents a priori, speakers and listeners reason recursively at depth 2 under a shared prior that assigns greater prominence not only to unique singleton objects lacking identical duplicates, but continuously to objects with greater visual feature contrast relative to the rest of the display. This fits better by maintaining depth-2 recursive reasoning and discrete singleton salience on displays with duplicated distractors, while resolving the incumbent's primary deficit on displays without duplicates where its prior degenerates to uniform, properly capturing visual distinctiveness biases on complex multi-feature reference games and prior trials. Differences from rsa_l2_singleton_prior: recursion depth remains depth 2 (choice_probs calls L2); parameter w_distinct (Normal(0.0, 1.0)) is added; no parameters are removed; and the shared softmax salience prior computed in choice_probs adds w_distinct times each object's contextual feature distinctiveness (total Hamming distance from all other objects) to w_singleton times the singleton indicator.
