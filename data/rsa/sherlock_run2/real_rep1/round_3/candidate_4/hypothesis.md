We refine rsa_l2_singleton_prior by incorporating the grammatical exhaustivity component from rsa_l2_exhaustivity into the depth-2 pragmatic listener. When interpreting referring expressions, listeners reason recursively at depth 2 with a shared visual singleton prior favoring unique objects over duplicated distractors, while additionally penalizing candidate referents that possess superfluous unmentioned features in the communicative display. This fits better by maintaining the incumbent's depth-2 recursive reasoning and singleton prior for displays with duplicated distractors and prior trials, while correcting its deficit on displays where candidate referents carry additional unmentioned features that listeners expect an informative speaker to avoid. Differences from rsa_l2_singleton_prior: recursion depth remains depth 2 (choice_probs calls L2); parameter lambda_exh (LogNormal(0.0, 1.0)) is added; no parameters are removed; in L2, the listener adds listener: knows(u), receives ctx.feature_count, and chooses referents weighted by Pr[speaker.r == r] * exp(-lambda_exh * (vec(feature_count, r) - at(lex, u, r))) rather than Pr[speaker.r == r]; and choice_probs passes lambda_exh and ctx.feature_count to L2.
