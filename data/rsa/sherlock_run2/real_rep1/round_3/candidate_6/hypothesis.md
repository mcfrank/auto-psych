We refine distinctiveness_attention_listener by increasing listener recursion depth from level 1 to level 2. Visual attention across a multi-object display is capacity-limited and driven by contextual distinctiveness: objects that differ most in visual features from the other items in the scene capture visual attention, while visually redundant items suffer attentional suppression. Pragmatic listeners reason recursively at depth 2 (inverting a speaker who simulates a depth-1 pragmatic listener), with speakers at each level operating over this display-relative attentional prior, which also directly guides referent choice when no informative description is heard. Differences from distinctiveness_attention_listener: recursion depth increases from depth 1 to depth 2 (choice_probs calls L2 instead of L1); no parameters are added; no parameters are removed; and L2 is added where the listener inverts a speaker who simulates L1 with the distinctiveness prior.
