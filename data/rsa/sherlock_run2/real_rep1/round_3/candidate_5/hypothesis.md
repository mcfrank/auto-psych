We refine depth-2 RSA with singleton salience (rsa_l2_singleton_prior) by incorporating the grammatical exhaustivity component from rsa_l2_exhaustivity into the depth-2 pragmatic listener. When interpreting referring expressions or evaluating referents a priori, speakers and listeners reason recursively at depth 2 under a shared visual singleton prior that highlights unique non-duplicated objects, but the depth-2 pragmatic listener additionally applies an exhaustification penalty to candidate referents that possess superfluous unmentioned features in the display. This fits better by retaining the incumbent's critical singleton salience advantage on displays with duplicated distractors while penalizing referents with extraneous unmentioned features, resolving the incumbent's primary deficit across complex displays with varying feature density. Differences from rsa_l2_singleton_prior: recursion depth remains depth 2 (choice_probs calls L2); parameter lambda_exh (LogNormal(0.0, 1.0)) is added; no parameters are removed; in L2, listener: knows(u) is added and the listener chooses referents weighted by Pr[speaker.r == r] * exp(-lambda_exh * (vec(feature_count, r) - at(lex, u, r))) rather than Pr[speaker.r == r]; and choice_probs passes ctx.feature_count to L2.
