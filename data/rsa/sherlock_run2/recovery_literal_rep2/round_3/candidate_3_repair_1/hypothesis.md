Listeners select candidate referents by maximizing expected task accuracy under a softmax choice rule over posterior beliefs rather than power-law probability matching. When resolving a referring expression under depth-1 pragmatic reasoning, listeners restrict their choice to semantically true candidates and choose among them with probabilities that scale exponentially with their posterior probability of being the intended target. On unprompted prior-elicitation trials where no communicative word is spoken, listeners fall back to uniform guessing across available objects.
