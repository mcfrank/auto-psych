We refine crowding_singleton_chromatic_l2 by incorporating a communicative penalty on ambiguous utterances when distinguishing alternatives exist (taking the discriminative alternative cost component from discriminative_alternative_speaker) into the simulated speakers' utility: speakers incur a production cost when choosing an ambiguous word for their intended referent if a uniquely distinguishing word was available in the visual context. Pragmatic listeners invert this cost-sensitive speaker across recursive reasoning levels, recognizing that using an ambiguous word signals that the speaker lacked an informative distinguishing alternative, combining Gricean quantity reasoning with shared visual crowding avoidance, singleton isolation, and chromatic prominence. Compared to crowding_singleton_chromatic_l2, recursion depth remains at L2 (choice_probs calls L2), one parameter (cost) is added, no parameters are removed (retaining alpha, w_singleton, w_crowd, w_color, and lapse), the shared referent prior is unchanged, and the speaker choice utility subtracts the communicative penalty for ambiguous words when a distinguishing alternative exists.
