We refine crowding_singleton_chromatic_l2 by incorporating a feature parsimony preference (taking the feature parsimony component from attentional_crowding_parsimony_l2) into the shared referent prior: listeners and the speakers they model combine perceptual isolation for contextually unique singletons, attentional crowding penalties for shared feature overlap, and chromatic prominence for colorful items with an intrinsic preference for feature-sparse minimal referents over cluttered objects with superfluous unmentioned features. This joint prior grounds depth-2 recursive pragmatic reasoning in visual simplicity, capturing human preferences for minimal referents on uninformative trials and reinforcing scalar implicatures across complex displays while preserving singleton isolation, visual crowding avoidance, and chromatic prominence. Compared to crowding_singleton_chromatic_l2, recursion depth remains at L2 (choice_probs calls L2), one parameter (w_features) is added, no parameters are removed (retaining alpha, w_singleton, w_crowd, w_color, and lapse), and the shared prior adds the feature count term alongside the singleton isolation indicator, attentional crowding penalty, and chromatic prominence term.
