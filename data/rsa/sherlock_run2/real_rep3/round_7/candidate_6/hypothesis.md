We refine attentional_crowding_singleton_l2 by incorporating competitor confusion aversion (taking the competitor confusion component from competitor_confusion_speaker) into the speaker's communicative utility: speakers actively penalize referring expressions that are shared with visually confusable distractors in the context, in addition to reasoning at depth 2 with a shared referent prior grounded in singleton isolation and visual crowding avoidance.
