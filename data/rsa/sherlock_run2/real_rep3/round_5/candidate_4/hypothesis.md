We refine attentional_crowding_singleton_l2 by incorporating an episodic familiarization base-rate preference (taking the familiarization prior component from singleton_familiarization_listener) into the shared referent prior: listeners and the speakers they model combine perceptual isolation for contextually unique singletons and attentional crowding penalties for shared feature overlap with prior exposure frequency, expecting previously familiarized referents to be discussed more frequently. This joint prior grounds depth-2 recursive pragmatic reasoning in empirical base rates while retaining perceptual crowding avoidance and singleton isolation across complex displays. Compared to attentional_crowding_singleton_l2, recursion depth remains at L2 (choice_probs calls L2), one parameter (w_familiar) is added, no parameters are removed (retaining alpha, w_singleton, w_crowd, and lapse), and the shared prior adds the familiarization base-rate term alongside the singleton isolation indicator and attentional crowding penalty.
