We refine rsa_l2_singleton_shared_prior by incorporating chromatic prominence (taking the color salience component from valence_salience_listener) into the shared referent prior: listeners and the speakers they model treat colorful objects as perceptually salient referents over desaturated grayscale items, alongside unique singletons, familiarized referents, and feature-sparse objects. This grounds depth-2 recursive pragmatic reasoning in visual chromatic prominence as well as perceptual isolation, accounting for color-based prior expectations while preserving second-order Theory of Mind reasoning over scalar implicatures. Differences from rsa_l2_singleton_shared_prior: recursion depth remains at L2 (choice_probs calls L2); one parameter (w_color) is added; no parameters are removed; and the shared prior adds the chromatic prominence term alongside the singleton isolation indicator, feature count, and familiarization base-rate terms.
