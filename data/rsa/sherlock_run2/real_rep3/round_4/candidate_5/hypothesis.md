We refine singleton_isolation_l2 by incorporating a feature parsimony preference (taking the feature parsimony component from feature_parsimony_heuristic) into the shared referent prior: listeners and the speakers they model treat contextually unique singletons lacking duplicates as salient referents while preferring feature-sparse, minimal referents over cluttered objects with superfluous unmentioned features. This shared prior grounds depth-2 recursive pragmatic reasoning in visual simplicity, capturing human preferences for minimal referents on uninformative trials and reinforcing scalar implicatures on referring expression trials while preserving second-order Theory of Mind. Compared to singleton_isolation_l2, recursion depth remains at L2 (choice_probs calls L2), one parameter (w_features) is added, no parameters are removed (retaining alpha, w_singleton, and lapse), and the shared prior adds the feature count term alongside the singleton isolation indicator.
