We refine singleton_isolation_listener by increasing listener recursive reasoning from depth 1 to depth 2: listeners invert a speaker who simulates a depth-1 pragmatic listener rather than a literal listener, while maintaining the contextual singleton isolation preference as common knowledge across all levels of recursion. This allows listeners to resolve complex scalar implicatures and informativeness asymmetries that depth-1 reasoning leaves ambiguous, while preserving the perceptual isolation bias that favours unique singletons over identical duplicate objects. Compared to singleton_isolation_listener, recursion depth increases from L1 to L2 (choice_probs calls L2 instead of L1), introducing an intermediate pragmatic speaker S2 and depth-2 listener L2 with the singleton prior shared across all levels, with no parameters added or removed (retaining alpha, w_singleton, and lapse).
