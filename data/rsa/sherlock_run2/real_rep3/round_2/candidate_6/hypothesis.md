We refine rsa_l1_singleton_shared_prior by incorporating a communicative penalty on ambiguous utterances when distinguishing alternatives exist (taking the discriminative alternative cost component from discriminative_alternative_speaker): speakers incur a production cost when choosing an ambiguous word for their intended referent if a uniquely distinguishing word was available in the visual context. Pragmatic listeners invert this speaker, recognizing that the use of an ambiguous expression signals that the speaker lacked an informative distinguishing alternative, combining Gricean quantity reasoning with shared perceptual singleton and salience grounding. Compared to rsa_l1_singleton_shared_prior, recursion depth remains at L1, one parameter (cost) is added, no parameters are removed, and the speaker choice utility subtracts this communicative penalty for ambiguous words when a distinguishing alternative exists.
