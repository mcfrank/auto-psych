We refine singleton_isolation_listener by incorporating an episodic familiarization base-rate preference (taking the familiarization prior component from rsa_l1_shared_prior) into the shared referent prior: listeners and the speakers they model combine perceptual isolation for contextually unique singletons with prior exposure frequency, expecting previously familiarized referents to be discussed more often. This joint prior accounts for empirical base-rate effects across trials while preserving the isolation advantage for unique singletons over identical duplicate objects when resolving ambiguous expressions or uninformative prior trials. Compared to singleton_isolation_listener, recursion depth remains at L1 (choice_probs calls L1), one parameter (w_familiar) is added, no parameters are removed, and the shared prior adds the familiarization base-rate term alongside the singleton isolation indicator.
