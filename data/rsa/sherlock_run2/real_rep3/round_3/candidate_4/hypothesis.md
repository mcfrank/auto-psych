We refine singleton_isolation_l2 by incorporating an episodic familiarization base-rate preference (taking the familiarization prior component from singleton_familiarization_listener) into the shared referent prior: listeners and the speakers they model combine perceptual isolation for contextually unique singletons with prior exposure frequency, expecting previously familiarized referents to be discussed more frequently. This joint prior grounds depth-2 recursive pragmatic reasoning in contextual exposure frequencies, accounting for empirical base-rate effects across trials while preserving the ability of depth-2 reasoning to resolve complex scalar implicatures and singletons over duplicate objects. Compared to singleton_isolation_l2, recursion depth remains at L2 (choice_probs calls L2), one parameter (w_familiar) is added, no parameters are removed, and the shared prior adds the familiarization base-rate term alongside the singleton isolation indicator.
