We refine singleton_isolation_l2 by incorporating the shared perceptual and familiarization salience prior (taking the feature parsimony and familiarization components from rsa_l1_singleton_shared_prior) into the common-knowledge referent prior: listeners and the speakers they model treat unique singletons lacking identical duplicates as salient referents alongside familiarized items and feature-sparse objects. This grounds depth-2 recursive pragmatic reasoning in mutual contextual exposure and perceptual simplicity, accounting for empirical base-rate and feature complexity asymmetries across trials while preserving the ability of second-order Theory of Mind to resolve complex scalar implicatures and singletons over duplicate objects. Differences from singleton_isolation_l2: recursion depth remains at L2 (choice_probs calls L2); two parameters (w_features and w_familiar) are added; no parameters are removed; and the shared prior adds the feature count and familiarization base-rate terms alongside the singleton isolation indicator.
