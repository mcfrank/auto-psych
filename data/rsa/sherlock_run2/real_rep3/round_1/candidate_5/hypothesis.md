We refine rsa_l2 by incorporating an asymmetric object salience prior (taking the salience prior component from rsa_l1_salience) at the depth-2 pragmatic listener level: listeners combine depth-2 recursive pragmatic reasoning with a subjective salience prior over referents based on feature counts and familiarization base rates, while modeling simulated lower-level agents as reasoning with uniform object priors. This allows listeners to capture contextual exposure and perceptual biases—and predict non-uniform choices on uninformative prior trials—without assuming that lower-level literal and pragmatic reasoning is biased by referent salience. Compared to rsa_l2, recursion depth remains at L2, two parameters (w_features and w_familiar) are added to construct the salience prior, the prior enters the top-level speaker simulation S2 in L2 as a prior over referents (while L0 and S1 retain uniform object priors), and choices on prior trials evaluate to this salience prior rather than uniform guessing.
