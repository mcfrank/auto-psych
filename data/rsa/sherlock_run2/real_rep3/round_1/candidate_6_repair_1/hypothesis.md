We refine rsa_l1_shared_prior by incorporating an isolation preference for contextually unique singletons (taking the singleton isolation component from singleton_isolation_listener) into the shared object prior: listeners and the speakers they model treat unique singletons lacking identical duplicates in the visual display as salient referents alongside familiar and feature-sparse objects. This perceptual isolation bias elevates singletons over identical duplicate objects when resolving ambiguous expressions or selecting objects on uninformative prior trials, while preserving mutual salience grounding and pragmatic implicature. Compared to rsa_l1_shared_prior, recursion depth remains at L1, one parameter (w_singleton) is added, no parameters are removed, and the shared prior adds an indicator term for contextually unique singletons lacking duplicates.
