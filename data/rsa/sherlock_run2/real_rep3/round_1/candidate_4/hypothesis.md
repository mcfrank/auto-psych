Listeners reason at depth 2 (inverting a speaker who simulates a pragmatic listener) while treating object salience—driven by feature count and familiarization base rates—as common knowledge across all levels of recursion. By sharing the salience prior among the literal listener, both simulated speakers, and the pragmatic listener (and defaulting to this prior on prior trials), the model accounts for contextual object preferences and prior elicitation while preserving depth-2 pragmatic implicature. Compared to rsa_l2, recursion depth remains at L2, two parameters (w_features and w_familiar) are added to form the shared salience prior via softmax, the object prior at every level (L0, S1, S2, and L2) changes from uniform to this salience prior, and choices on prior trials evaluate to this prior rather than uniform guessing.
