Speakers formulate referring expressions by prioritizing dedicated exclusive descriptors that uniquely single out the target, penalizing ambiguous descriptors whenever the intended referent possesses a foolproof exclusive alternative in the display. Pragmatic listeners invert this speaker by directing ambiguous expressions toward candidate referents that lack any exclusive descriptor in the context. On a display where one candidate possesses an exclusive unique descriptor alongside the heard word while another candidate has multiple cluttered background features that are never exclusive, this exclusive-alternative listener selects the cluttered candidate because it lacked an exclusive descriptor, whereas the current best model selects the candidate with the exclusive descriptor due to accumulated omission and confusion penalties.
