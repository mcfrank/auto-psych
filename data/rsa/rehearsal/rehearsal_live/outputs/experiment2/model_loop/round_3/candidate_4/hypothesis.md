Attenuated solitary perceptual oddity listener with twin contrast, visual color contrast, and attenuated evaluative valence framing. Listeners interpret referring expressions by integrating visual color contrast and twin-contrast solitary oddity pop-out with evaluative valence framing whose behavioral influence is strong during spontaneous ungrounded expectations but attenuated during linguistic utterance comprehension. ### Refinement details relative to twin_contrast_valence_color_listener
- **Single stated change**: Decoupled the evaluative valence framing parameter in twin_contrast_valence_color_listener into separate ungrounded prior (`w_valence_prior`) and linguistic utterance comprehension (`w_valence_utt`) weights.
- **Why it should fit better**: In visual reference games with evaluative framing (such as experiment E6_valence), speakers use positive framing ("my favorite") or negative framing ("my least favorite") to express an emotional attitude toward candidate objects. On ungrounded prior trials without an informative word, listeners follow this evaluative attitude unconstrained, exhibiting a strong preference for feature-rich objects under positive framing and sparse objects under negative framing; however, when an informative referring expression is spoken, selective attention shifts toward semantic applicability and referential denotation, dampening the influence of the speaker's emotional attitude among matching referents. The source model enforced an identical valence weighting across both prior expectations and utterance comprehension, systematically overestimating valence distortion during linguistic comprehension and underestimating it in ungrounded prior choices; decoupling the valence weights allows listeners to attend strongly to speaker attitude a priori while attenuating emotional bias during utterance interpretation, while preserving twin-contrast solitary oddity pop-out and visual color contrast weighting.
- **Recursion depth**: Unchanged at depth 0 (`choice_probs` evaluates `L_heur` directly without recursive speaker simulation).
- **Parameters added or removed**: Removed parameter `w_valence`; added parameters `w_valence_prior` (prior Normal(0.0, 2.0)) and `w_valence_utt` (prior Normal(0.0, 2.0)); retained parameter `beta_prior` (prior Normal(0.0, 2.0)), parameter `beta_utt` (prior Normal(0.0, 2.0)), parameter `w_color` (prior Normal(0.0, 2.0)), and parameter `lapse` (prior Beta(1.0, 9.0)).
