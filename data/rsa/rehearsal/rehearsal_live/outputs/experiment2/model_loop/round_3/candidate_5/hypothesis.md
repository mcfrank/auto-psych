Attenuated solitary perceptual oddity listener with twin contrast, evaluative valence framing, visual color contrast, and contextual familiarization base rates. Listeners interpret referring expressions by integrating contextual familiarization base rates with visual color contrast, evaluative valence framing, and a solitary perceptual oddity heuristic that operates exclusively when a unique solitary singleton contrasts against an identical twin pair. When candidate referents have been encountered during prior familiarization or appear in full chromatic coloration, listeners favor more frequent and chromatic exemplars in both spontaneous expectations and semantic comprehension, while selective attention to an informative word attenuates solitary oddity pop-out among matching referents and evaluative attitude modulates feature richness expectations.

### Refinement details relative to twin_contrast_valence_color_listener
- **Single stated change**: Extended twin_contrast_valence_color_listener by incorporating contextual familiarization base-rate weighting into candidate referent evaluation, adopted from twin_contrast_valence_base_rate_listener.
- **Why it should fit better**: In visual reference games featuring pre-test familiarization phases (such as experiment E5_baserate), listeners form empirical base-rate expectations favoring candidate referents they observed more frequently during prior exposure. The source model (twin_contrast_valence_color_listener) integrated visual color contrast, evaluative valence framing, and twin-contrast solitary oddity, but treated familiarized and novel objects as equally appealing a priori regardless of exposure frequency; incorporating familiarization base-rate weighting allows listeners to favor frequently seen exemplars in both ungrounded prior expectations and semantic utterance comprehension, while preserving visual color contrast weighting on chromatic displays, evaluative valence modulation under positive and negative framing, and twin-contrast solitary oddity pop-out when identical twin pairs are present.
- **Recursion depth**: Unchanged at depth 0 (`choice_probs` evaluates `L_heur` directly without recursive speaker simulation).
