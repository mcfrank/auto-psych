### Refinement details relative to twin_contrast_valence_base_rate_listener
- **Single stated change**: Decoupled the evaluative valence framing parameter in twin_contrast_valence_base_rate_listener into separate ungrounded prior (`w_valence_prior`) and linguistic utterance comprehension (`w_valence_utt`) weights.
- **Why it should fit better**: In visual reference games featuring evaluative framing (such as experiment E6_valence), speakers use positive framing ("my favorite") or negative framing ("my least favorite") to express an emotional attitude toward candidate referents. On ungrounded prior trials without an informative word, listeners follow this evaluative attitude unconstrained, strongly expecting speakers to target feature-rich exemplars under positive framing and sparse exemplars under negative framing; however, when an informative referring expression is produced, selective attention shifts primarily toward semantic truth conditions, dampening the behavioral influence of the speaker's emotional attitude among matching referents. The source model (twin_contrast_valence_base_rate_listener) enforced an identical valence weighting across both prior expectations and utterance comprehension, systematically overestimating valence distortion during linguistic comprehension and underestimating it in ungrounded prior choices; decoupling the valence weights allows listeners to attend strongly to speaker attitude a priori while attenuating emotional bias during utterance interpretation, while preserving twin-contrast solitary oddity pop-out and empirical familiarization base-rate weighting.
