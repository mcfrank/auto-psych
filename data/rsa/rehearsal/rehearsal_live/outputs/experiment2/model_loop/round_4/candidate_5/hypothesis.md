Attenuated solitary perceptual oddity listener with twin contrast, visual color contrast, attenuated evaluative valence framing, and contextual familiarization base rates. Listeners interpret referring expressions by integrating contextual familiarization base rates with visual color contrast, attenuated evaluative valence framing, and a solitary perceptual oddity heuristic that operates exclusively when a unique solitary singleton contrasts against an identical twin pair. When candidate referents have been encountered during prior familiarization or appear in full chromatic coloration, listeners favor more frequent and chromatic exemplars, while ungrounded expectations follow speaker evaluative attitudes that become attenuated during semantic comprehension and solitary oddity pop-out directs attention to singleton-twin contrasts.
