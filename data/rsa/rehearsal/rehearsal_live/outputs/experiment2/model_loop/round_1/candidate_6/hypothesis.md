Attenuated solitary perceptual oddity listener with twin contrast and evaluative valence framing. Listeners interpret referring expressions by combining evaluative valence framing with a solitary perceptual oddity heuristic that operates exclusively when a unique singleton contrasts against an identical twin pair, rather than against majority triplets or larger clusters. When no informative expression is heard, listeners spontaneously select the solitary singleton contrasting with twins; when an informative expression is heard, selective attention to semantic applicability attenuates oddity pop-out among matching referents; and under evaluative framing, expectations modulate toward feature-rich exemplars for positive framing and sparse exemplars for negative framing.
