Attenuated solitary perceptual oddity listener with twin contrast and familiarization base rates. Listeners interpret referring expressions by integrating contextual familiarization base rates with a solitary perceptual oddity heuristic that operates exclusively when a unique solitary singleton contrasts against an identical twin pair, rather than against majority triplets or larger clusters. When no informative expression is heard, listeners choose candidate referents by joint solitary oddity pop-out and prior familiarization frequency; when an informative expression is heard, selective attention to semantic applicability attenuates oddity pop-out among matching referents while continuing to weight candidate referents by their familiarization base rates.

### Refinement details relative to twin_contrast_solitary_oddity_listener
- **Single stated change**: Extended twin_contrast_solitary_oddity_listener by incorporating contextual familiarization base-rate weighting from base_rate_solitary_oddity_listener.
- **Why it should fit better**: In reference games with prior exposure or familiarization phases (such as E5_baserate), listeners develop empirical base-rate expectations favoring objects encountered more frequently. The source model treated all referents as having equal a priori base rates regardless of familiarization exposure; incorporating familiarization base-rate weighting allows listeners to favor frequently seen exemplars in both ungrounded prior expectations and semantic utterance comprehension, while preserving twin-contrast solitary oddity pop-out when identical duplicate objects are present.
- **Recursion depth**: Unchanged at depth 0 (`choice_probs` evaluates `L_heur` directly without recursive speaker simulation).
