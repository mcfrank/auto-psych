Attenuated solitary perceptual oddity listener with twin contrast, attenuated visual color contrast, and attenuated evaluative valence framing. Listeners interpret referring expressions by integrating visual color contrast and twin-contrast solitary oddity pop-out with evaluative valence framing, where bottom-up perceptual oddity, evaluative framing, and visual chromatic contrast are strong during spontaneous ungrounded expectations but attenuated during linguistic utterance comprehension. When no informative expression is heard, listeners strongly favor feature-rich exemplars under positive framing and sparse exemplars under negative framing, alongside visual preferences for solitary singletons contrasting with twins and fully colored objects; when an informative expression is heard, selective attention to semantic applicability attenuates solitary oddity pop-out, evaluative valence attitude, and chromatic color bias among matching referents.

### Refinement details relative to twin_color_attenuated_valence_listener
- **Single stated change**: Decoupled visual color contrast weighting into separate ungrounded prior (`w_color_prior`) and linguistic utterance comprehension (`w_color_utt`) weights.
- **Why it should fit better**: In visual reference games where candidate referents vary in chromatic coloration alongside desaturated grayscale distractors (such as experiment E7_color), listeners exhibit an intrinsic bottom-up visual preference toward fully colored items during spontaneous ungrounded visual inspection. However, when an informative referring expression is produced, selective top-down attention shifts toward semantic applicability and referential truth conditions, dampening bottom-up chromatic pop-out among semantically matching referents. The source model (twin_color_attenuated_valence_listener) enforced an identical color weighting across both prior expectations and utterance comprehension, overestimating chromatic bias during linguistic comprehension and underestimating it in ungrounded prior choices; decoupling the color weights allows listeners to attend strongly to visual coloration a priori while attenuating chromatic bias during utterance interpretation, complementing the attenuated oddity and attenuated valence mechanisms while preserving twin-contrast solitary oddity pop-out.
- **Recursion depth**: Unchanged at depth 0 (`choice_probs` evaluates `L_heur` directly without recursive speaker simulation).
- **Parameters added or removed**: Removed parameter `w_color`; added parameters `w_color_prior` (prior Normal(0.0, 2.0)) and `w_color_utt` (prior Normal(0.0, 2.0)); retained parameter `beta_prior` (prior Normal(0.0, 2.0)), parameter `beta_utt` (prior Normal(0.0, 2.0)), parameter `w_valence_prior` (prior Normal(0.0, 2.0)), parameter `w_valence_utt` (prior Normal(0.0, 2.0)), and parameter `lapse` (prior Beta(1.0, 9.0)).
- **Code differences**: In `PARAMS`, replaced `w_color` with `w_color_prior` and `w_color_utt`. In `choice_probs`, computed `color_prior_bias = params["w_color_prior"] * (1.0 - ctx.grayscale)` and `color_utt_bias = params["w_color_utt"] * (1.0 - ctx.grayscale)`, updated `prior = softmax_prior(params["beta_prior"] * is_singleton + val_prior_bias + color_prior_bias)`, and passed `color_utt_bias` into `L_heur` instead of the shared `color_bias`.
