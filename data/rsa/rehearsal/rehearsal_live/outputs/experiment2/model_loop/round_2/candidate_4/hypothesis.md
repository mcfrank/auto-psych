Attenuated solitary perceptual oddity listener with twin contrast, evaluative valence framing, and familiarization base rates. Listeners interpret referring expressions by integrating contextual familiarization base rates with evaluative valence framing and a solitary perceptual oddity heuristic that operates exclusively when a unique solitary singleton contrasts against an identical twin pair, rather than against majority triplets or larger clusters. When no informative expression is heard, listeners choose candidate referents by joint solitary oddity pop-out, prior familiarization frequency, and evaluative valence attitude; when an informative referring expression is heard, selective attention to semantic applicability attenuates oddity pop-out among matching referents while continuing to weight candidate referents by both their familiarization base rates and evaluative valence bias.

### Refinement details relative to twin_contrast_solitary_valence_listener

The source model lacked sensitivity to familiarization exposure, treating all referents as equally frequent a priori regardless of base-rate experience; incorporating familiarization base-rate weighting allows listeners to favor frequently seen exemplars in both ungrounded prior expectations and semantic utterance comprehension, while preserving twin-contrast solitary oddity pop-out when identical duplicate objects are present and evaluative valence sensitivity under positive and negative framing.
