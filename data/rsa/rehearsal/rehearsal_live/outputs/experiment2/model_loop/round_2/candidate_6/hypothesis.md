Attenuated solitary perceptual oddity listener with twin contrast, familiarization base rates, and evaluative valence framing. Listeners interpret referring expressions by integrating contextual familiarization base rates and evaluative valence framing with a solitary perceptual oddity heuristic that operates exclusively when a unique solitary singleton contrasts against an identical twin pair. When no informative expression is heard, listeners choose candidate referents by joint twin-contrast solitary oddity pop-out, prior familiarization frequency, and valence-modulated descriptive feature expectations; when an informative expression is heard, selective attention to semantic applicability attenuates oddity pop-out among matching referents while continuing to weight candidate referents by their familiarization base rates and valence framing.

### Refinement details relative to twin_contrast_base_rate_listener
- **Single stated change**: Extended twin_contrast_base_rate_listener by incorporating evaluative valence framing from twin_contrast_solitary_valence_listener.
- **Why it should fit better**: In visual reference games with evaluative framing (such as E6_valence), speakers use positive framing ("my favorite") to refer to complex, feature-rich objects and negative framing ("my least favorite") to refer to minimal, sparse exemplars. The source model lacked sensitivity to speaker evaluative framing attitude, treating all contexts as emotionally neutral and predicting equal choice splits across matching referents regardless of speaker attitude; incorporating evaluative valence framing allows listeners to modulate expectations over referent feature complexity under positive and negative framing while preserving twin-contrast solitary oddity pop-out and empirical familiarization base-rate weighting.
- **Recursion depth**: Unchanged at depth 0 (`choice_probs` evaluates `L_heur` directly without recursive speaker simulation).
