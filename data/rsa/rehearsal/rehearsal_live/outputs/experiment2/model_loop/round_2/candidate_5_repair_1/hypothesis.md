Attenuated solitary perceptual oddity listener with twin contrast, evaluative valence framing, and visual color contrast. Listeners interpret referring expressions by integrating visual color contrast and evaluative valence framing with a solitary perceptual oddity heuristic that operates exclusively when a unique solitary singleton contrasts against an identical twin pair, rather than against majority triplets or larger clusters. When candidate referents are displayed in full chromatic coloration alongside desaturated grayscale distractors, listeners exhibit an intrinsic perceptual bias toward fully colored objects; when an informative expression is heard, selective attention to semantic applicability attenuates oddity pop-out among matching referents while continuing to weight candidate referents by their chromatic coloration and evaluative valence attitude.

### Refinement details relative to twin_contrast_solitary_valence_listener
- **Single stated change**: Extended twin_contrast_solitary_valence_listener by incorporating visual color contrast weighting into candidate referent evaluation, adopted from rsa_l2_singleton_feat_color_valence_l0.
- **Why it should fit better**: In visual reference games where candidate referents appear in full chromatic coloration alongside desaturated grayscale distractors (such as experiment E7_color), listeners exhibit an intrinsic perceptual preference toward fully colored items in both ungrounded prior expectations and semantic utterance comprehension. The source model (twin_contrast_solitary_valence_listener) captured twin-contrast solitary oddity and evaluative valence framing but treated colored and grayscale objects as equally appealing a priori; incorporating visual color contrast weighting allows listeners to favor fully colored exemplars across visual displays, while preserving twin-contrast solitary oddity pop-out when identical twin pairs are present and evaluative valence modulation under positive and negative framing.
- **Recursion depth**: Unchanged at depth 0 (`choice_probs` evaluates `L_heur` directly without recursive speaker simulation).
- **Parameters added or removed**: Added parameter `w_color` (prior Normal(0.0, 2.0)); retained parameter `beta_prior` (prior Normal(0.0, 2.0)), parameter `beta_utt` (prior Normal(0.0, 2.0)), parameter `w_valence` (prior Normal(0.0, 2.0)), and parameter `lapse` (prior Beta(1.0, 9.0)).
- **Code differences**: In `PARAMS`, added `"w_color": dist.Normal(0.0, 2.0)`. In `L_heur`, added array parameter `color_bias: ...` and updated the referent choice weighting expression to `at(lex, u, r) * exp(beta_utt * vec(is_singleton, r) + vec(valence_bias, r) + vec(color_bias, r))`. In `choice_probs`, computed `color_bias = params["w_color"] * (1.0 - ctx.grayscale)`, updated `prior = softmax_prior(params["beta_prior"] * is_singleton + valence_bias + color_bias)`, and passed `color_bias` to `L_heur`.
