Attenuated solitary perceptual oddity listener with twin contrast, evaluative valence framing, and familiarization base rates. Listeners interpret referring expressions by integrating contextual familiarization base rates with evaluative valence framing and a solitary perceptual oddity heuristic that operates exclusively when a unique solitary singleton contrasts against an identical twin pair. When no informative expression is heard, listeners choose candidate referents by joint solitary oddity pop-out, familiarization frequency, and evaluative feature preferences; when an informative expression is heard, selective attention to semantic applicability attenuates oddity pop-out among matching referents while continuing to weight candidate referents by their familiarization base rates and valence framing.

The incumbent model (twin_contrast_solitary_valence_listener) captured evaluative valence framing and twin-contrast solitary oddity, but treated all referents as having equal a priori base rates regardless of familiarization exposure; incorporating familiarization base-rate weighting allows listeners to favor frequently seen exemplars in both ungrounded prior expectations and semantic utterance comprehension, while preserving twin-contrast solitary oddity pop-out when identical twin pairs are present and evaluative valence modulation under positive and negative framing.
