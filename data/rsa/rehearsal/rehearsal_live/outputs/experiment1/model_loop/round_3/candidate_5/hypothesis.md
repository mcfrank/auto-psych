Attenuated solitary perceptual oddity listener with evaluative valence framing. Listeners interpret referring expressions using a solitary perceptual oddity heuristic whose bottom-up pop-out salience is strong during spontaneous ungrounded expectations but attenuated during linguistic utterance comprehension, while modulating their expectations according to the speaker's evaluative framing attitude. Under positive framing ("favorite"), listeners expect speakers to target richer, feature-laden exemplars; under negative framing ("least favorite"), this expectation inverts to favor minimal, sparse exemplars; and under neutral framing, choices follow the solitary oddity heuristic alone.

### Refinement details relative to attenuated_solitary_oddity_listener
- **Single stated change**: Extended attenuated_solitary_oddity_listener by incorporating the evaluative valence framing component from rsa_l2_singleton_feat_color_valence_l0. The source model treated all trials as emotionally neutral and predicted an equal choice split across referents regardless of speaker attitude; incorporating evaluative valence framing allows listeners to modulate their expectations over referent feature complexity under positive ("favorite") and negative ("least favorite") frames while preserving solitary oddity pop-out under neutral framing.
- **Recursion depth**: Unchanged at depth 0 (`choice_probs` evaluates `L_heur` directly without recursive speaker simulation).
- **Parameters added or removed**: Added parameter `w_valence` (prior Normal(0.0, 2.0)); retained parameters `beta_prior` (prior Normal(0.0, 2.0)), `beta_utt` (prior Normal(0.0, 2.0)), and `lapse` (prior Beta(1.0, 9.0)).
- **Code differences**: In `PARAMS`, added `w_valence`. In `L_heur`, added parameter `valence_bias: ...` and updated the referent choice weighting expression to include `vec(valence_bias, r)`. In `choice_probs`, computed `valence_bias = params["w_valence"] * ctx.valence * ctx.feature_count`, updated `prior` to include `valence_bias`, and passed `valence_bias` to `L_heur`.
