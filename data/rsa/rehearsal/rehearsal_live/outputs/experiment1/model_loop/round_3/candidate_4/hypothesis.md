Attenuated solitary perceptual oddity listener with twin contrast. Listeners interpret referring expressions using a solitary perceptual oddity heuristic where an odd-one-out pop-out bias operates exclusively when a solitary singleton contrasts against an identical twin pair, rather than against majority triplets or larger background groups. When no informative expression is heard, listeners spontaneously select the solitary singleton contrasting with twins; when an informative referring expression is heard, selective attention to semantic applicability competes with and attenuates oddity pop-out among matching referents, while defaulting to uniform baseline expectations on scenes with majority triplets or no twin contrast.

### Refinement details relative to attenuated_solitary_oddity_listener
- **Single stated change**: Restricted the solitary oddity heuristic indicator from triggering against any duplicate background to triggering exclusively when the background consists of an identical twin pair (maximum copy count of 2), eliminating false pop-out on scenes containing majority triplets or larger clusters.
- **Why it should fit better**: In triad reference games, a singleton contrasting against an identical twin pair produces salient perceptual oddity pop-out; however, in displays containing a majority triplet of three identical objects, the triplet establishes a dominant visual majority category rather than a pair contrast, leading participants to distribute their prior expectations across the display rather than concentrating choices on the singleton. Restricting oddity to twin-contrast contexts eliminates spurious singleton biases on majority-triplet scenes while preserving solitary oddity on twin displays.
- **Recursion depth**: Unchanged at depth 0 (`choice_probs` evaluates `L_heur` directly without recursive speaker simulation).
- **Parameters added or removed**: None. Retains parameter `beta_prior` (prior Normal(0.0, 2.0)), parameter `beta_utt` (prior Normal(0.0, 2.0)), and parameter `lapse` (prior Beta(1.0, 9.0)).
- **Code differences**: In `compute_singleton_indicator`, added condition `& (max_copy > 1.5) & (max_copy < 2.5)` to require that the maximum copy count among objects in the visual display equals 2 (`max_copy = jnp.max(copy_count)`), restricting solitary singleton pop-out exclusively to twin-contrast visual scenes.
