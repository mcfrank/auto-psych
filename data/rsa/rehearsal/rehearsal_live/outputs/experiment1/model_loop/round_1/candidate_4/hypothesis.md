Solitary perceptual oddity heuristic listener. Listeners interpret referring expressions using a perceptual oddity heuristic where an item receives an oddity pop-out boost only when it constitutes the sole, solitary singleton contrasting against identical background duplicates in the visual context. When multiple distinct singletons exist alongside duplicates, or when all items are distinct, no solitary oddity contrast exists and listeners choose uniformly among referents satisfying the literal semantics. On uninformative trials without an informative word, listeners spontaneously select the solitary singleton, defaulting to uniform choice when no solitary contrast exists.

### Refinement details relative to oddity_heuristic_listener
- **Single stated change**: Restricted the oddity heuristic indicator from tagging any object without an identical twin to triggering exclusively when exactly one solitary singleton exists in the visual scene. In displays containing multiple singletons (such as two distinct items and one twin pair), the original model erroneously favored both singletons over the duplicates; the revised mechanism correctly treats them as lacking a solitary oddity contrast, defaulting to uniform choice over matching items.
- **Recursion depth**: Unchanged at depth 0 (`choice_probs` evaluates `L_heur` directly without recursive speaker simulation).
- **Parameters added or removed**: None. The model retains exactly two parameters: `beta` (prior Normal(0.0, 2.0)) for solitary singleton weighting, and `lapse` (prior Beta(1.0, 9.0)) for random clicking.
- **Code differences**: In `compute_singleton_indicator`, `is_singleton` is calculated by checking both that an object has a copy count of 1 and that the sum of singleton items across the entire visual display equals 1 (`(is_item_singleton > 0.0) & (singleton_count > 0.5) & (singleton_count < 1.5)`), replacing the unconstrained `(copy_count <= 1.0)`.
