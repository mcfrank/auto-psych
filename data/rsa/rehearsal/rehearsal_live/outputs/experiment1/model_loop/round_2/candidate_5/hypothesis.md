Listeners interpret referring expressions by integrating solitary perceptual oddity with the mutual exclusivity principle. When an expression matches multiple referents in a visual scene containing duplicate items, listeners favor a solitary visual singleton that contrasts against identical background duplicates; on scenes where referents are distinct, listeners resolve ambiguity by penalizing candidate referents that possess an exclusive, contextually unique feature that could have identified them unambiguously. On uninformative trials without an informative word, choices follow visual solitary oddity pop-out, defaulting to uniform choice when no solitary contrast exists.

### Refinement details relative to solitary_oddity_heuristic_listener
- **Single stated change**: Extended solitary_oddity_heuristic_listener by incorporating the mutual exclusivity feature-penalty component from mutual_exclusivity_listener. The source model lacked any pragmatic mechanism to resolve ambiguous expressions on displays where all items are distinct, predicting uniform choice and lagging hundreds of nats behind on standard scalar implicature tasks; incorporating mutual exclusivity enables listeners to penalize competitors with diagnostic exclusive features on distinct displays while preserving solitary oddity pop-out on scenes with identical duplicates.
- **Recursion depth**: Unchanged at depth 0 (`choice_probs` evaluates `L_heur` directly without recursive speaker simulation).
- **Parameters added or removed**: Replaced parameter `beta` with `beta_oddity` (prior Normal(0.0, 2.0)) for solitary singleton weighting; added parameter `beta_me` (prior Normal(0.0, 2.0)) for penalizing unmentioned exclusive features. Retained parameter `lapse` (prior Beta(1.0, 9.0)) for random clicking.
- **Code differences**: Added `compute_exclusive_count(ctx)` to count unmentioned features that apply to exactly one referent in the context; updated `L_heur` to weight matching referents by `exp(beta_oddity * vec(is_singleton, r) - beta_me * vec(exclusive_count, r))`; in `choice_probs`, passed `exclusive_count` and `beta_me` to `L_heur`.
