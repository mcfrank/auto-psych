Solitary attenuated perceptual oddity listener. Listeners interpret referring expressions using a perceptual oddity heuristic where an oddity pop-out bias operates exclusively when exactly one solitary singleton contrasts against identical background duplicates in the visual context, but this pop-out salience is strong in ungrounded prior expectations and attenuated during linguistic comprehension. When hearing no informative expression, listeners spontaneously target the solitary singleton with full pop-out strength; when hearing an informative word, top-down semantic constraints compete with bottom-up pop-out, dampening the singleton preference among matching referents, and defaulting to uniform choice among matching items when multiple singletons or no duplicates exist.

Refining attenuated_oddity_listener: The source model tagged any item lacking an identical duplicate as a singleton, incorrectly penalizing duplicate items in displays with multiple distinct singletons. We restrict the oddity heuristic indicator to trigger exclusively when exactly one solitary singleton exists in the visual scene, eliminating spurious oddity biases in multi-singleton contexts while preserving attenuated pop-out between ungrounded prior trials and grounded utterance comprehension.
