Attenuated solitary perceptual oddity listener with mutual exclusivity. Listeners interpret referring expressions by integrating a solitary perceptual oddity heuristic with the mutual exclusivity principle, where bottom-up visual pop-out is strong during spontaneous ungrounded expectations but attenuated during linguistic utterance comprehension. When no informative word is heard, listeners spontaneously select the solitary singleton contrasting against background duplicates with full pop-out salience; when an informative expression is heard, selective attention to semantic truth conditions dampens oddity pop-out while penalizing candidate referents that possess exclusive, identifying features that the speaker could have used to refer to them unambiguously.

Refining solitary_oddity_exclusivity_listener: The source model enforced an identical oddity bias across both ungrounded prior expectations and utterance comprehension. We decouple this mechanism into separate ungrounded prior (beta_prior) and linguistic utterance comprehension (beta_utt) weights, reflecting attentional attenuation of bottom-up visual pop-out when top-down semantic constraints are active.
