Twin-contrast solitary perceptual oddity listener with familiarization base rates. Listeners interpret referring expressions by integrating a solitary perceptual oddity heuristic with contextual familiarization base rates, where an odd-one-out pop-out bias operates exclusively when a solitary singleton contrasts against an identical twin pair rather than against majority triplets or larger background groups. When evaluating candidate referents, listeners favor solitary singletons contrasting with twins while additionally weighting candidates by their observed familiarization base rates from prior exposure, both during spontaneous ungrounded expectations and during semantic utterance comprehension.

### Refinement details relative to twin_contrast_solitary_oddity_listener
- **Single stated change**: Extended twin_contrast_solitary_oddity_listener by incorporating contextual familiarization base-rate weighting from base_rate_solitary_oddity_listener. The source model treated all referents as having equal a priori baseline rates whenever no twin contrast was present, ignoring empirical exposure frequencies established in familiarization phases; incorporating familiarization base rates allows listeners to weight candidate referents by their observed exposure frequency in both prior expectations and utterance comprehension, while preserving twin-contrast solitary oddity pop-out on duplicate displays.
- **Why it should fit better**: In reference games with pre-exposure familiarization trials, participants develop strong prior expectations favoring frequently encountered objects over rare ones. The source model lacked any familiarization mechanism, predicting uniform baseline distributions across distinct objects even under asymmetric exposure base rates; weighting referents by observed familiarization base rates captures these empirical exposure biases across both prior and utterance trials while retaining solitary oddity pop-out when a singleton contrasts with a twin pair.
- **Recursion depth**: Unchanged at depth 0 (`choice_probs` evaluates `L_heur` directly without recursive speaker simulation).
- **Parameters added or removed**: Added parameter `w_base` (prior `Normal(0.0, 2.0)`); retained parameter `beta_prior` (prior `Normal(0.0, 2.0)`), parameter `beta_utt` (prior `Normal(0.0, 2.0)`), and parameter `lapse` (prior `Beta(1.0, 9.0)`).
- **Code differences**: In `PARAMS`, added `w_base`. In `L_heur`, added parameter `base_rate: ...` and updated the referent choice weighting expression to `at(lex, u, r) * exp(beta_utt * vec(is_singleton, r) + w_base * vec(base_rate, r))`. In `choice_probs`, extracted `base_rate = ctx.familiarization`, computed `prior = softmax_prior(params["beta_prior"] * is_singleton + params["w_base"] * base_rate)`, and passed `params["w_base"]` and `base_rate` to `L_heur`.
