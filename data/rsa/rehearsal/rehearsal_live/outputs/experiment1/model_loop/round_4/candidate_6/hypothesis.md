Attenuated solitary perceptual oddity listener with twin contrast and evaluative valence framing. Listeners interpret referring expressions using an attenuated solitary perceptual oddity heuristic where bottom-up oddity pop-out is strong during spontaneous ungrounded expectations but attenuated during linguistic comprehension, operating exclusively when a solitary singleton contrasts against an identical twin pair rather than majority triplets or clusters. Furthermore, listeners modulate their expectations about referent feature complexity according to the speaker's evaluative framing attitude, favoring feature-rich exemplars under positive framing ("favorite") and sparse, minimal exemplars under negative framing ("least favorite").

### Refinement details relative to attenuated_solitary_valence_listener
- **Single stated change**: Restricted the solitary oddity heuristic indicator from triggering against any duplicate background to triggering exclusively when the background consists of an identical twin pair (maximum copy count of 2), eliminating false pop-out on scenes containing majority triplets or larger clusters.
- **Why it should fit better**: In visual reference games, a solitary singleton pops out as a salient odd-man-out when contrasted against an identical twin pair; however, in displays containing a majority triplet of three identical objects, the triplet establishes a dominant visual majority category rather than a pair contrast, leading participants to distribute their prior expectations rather than concentrating choices on the singleton. Restricting oddity pop-out to twin-contrast contexts eliminates spurious singleton biases on majority-triplet scenes while preserving both solitary oddity on twin displays and evaluative valence modulation across framed contexts.
- **Recursion depth**: Unchanged at depth 0 (`choice_probs` evaluates `L_heur` directly without recursive speaker simulation).
- **Parameters added or removed**: None. Retains parameter `beta_prior` (prior `Normal(0.0, 2.0)`), parameter `beta_utt` (prior `Normal(0.0, 2.0)`), parameter `w_valence` (prior `Normal(0.0, 2.0)`), and parameter `lapse` (prior `Beta(1.0, 9.0)`).
