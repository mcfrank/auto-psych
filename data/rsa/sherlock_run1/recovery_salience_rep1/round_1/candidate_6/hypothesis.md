We refine rsa_l1 by introducing a feature-count salience prior over referents, where listeners maintain a baseline prior that scales with an object's feature count rather than assuming all objects are equally likely a priori. Listeners penalize visually complex objects with more extraneous features, favoring simpler referents both when interpreting an utterance and on uninformative prior trials. Compared to rsa_l1, recursion depth is unchanged at depth 1 (calling L1), one parameter 'salience' is added with a normal prior, and the uniform object prior in the listener's mental model and on prior trials is replaced by a softmax distribution over feature counts.
