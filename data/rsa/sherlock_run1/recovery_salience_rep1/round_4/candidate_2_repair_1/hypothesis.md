Listeners in reference games differ in their cognitive processing architecture, forming a population mixture of structural exhaustification and recursive pragmatic mentalizing types. When interpreting an ambiguous description, exhaustivity listeners apply grammatical exhaustification to penalize candidate objects that possess extraneous unmentioned features, whereas pragmatic listeners invert a model of a communicative speaker to reason about informative alternatives. The aggregate distribution of referent choices reflects the population mixture proportion of pragmatic mentalizers versus structural exhaustification responders.
