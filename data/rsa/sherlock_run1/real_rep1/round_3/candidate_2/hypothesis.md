People interpret referential descriptions using a non-Bayesian feature parsimony heuristic: among objects matching the heard word, they prefer the object with the fewest total features, penalizing extraneous unmentioned properties. Rather than mentalizing an informative speaker or evaluating alternative utterances, listeners directly discount candidate referents as an exponential function of their unmentioned complexity. When no informative word is spoken, listeners choose uniformly at random.
