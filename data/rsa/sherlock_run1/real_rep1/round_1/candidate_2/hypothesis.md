People interpret referential descriptions by inverting a naive generative speaker who mentions any true feature of the intended object with equal probability, rather than simulating a speaker who mentalizes a listener. When hearing an ambiguous word, listeners favor objects with fewer total features because a naive speaker is less likely to have chosen that word for an object with many competing features. On displays where a multi-featured object has only uninformative alternative features, this model predicts listeners will disfavor it relative to an object with fewer features, whereas standard RSA predicts listeners will favor it because a strategic speaker avoids uninformative alternatives.
