We refine literal_pragmatic_mixture_listener by hypothesizing that pragmatic interpreters simulate a communicative speaker who operates with limited visual attention to unmentioned features, incorporating the attentional bottleneck mechanism from limited_attention_listener. When pragmatic listeners invert the speaker, candidate unmentioned utterances receive discounted attentional weight rather than full consideration, preventing distant non-matching alternatives from exerting undue pragmatic contrast. Relative to literal_pragmatic_mixture_listener, recursion depth remains unchanged at L1, one parameter att_unmentioned is added with prior Beta(1.0, 3.0), no parameters are removed, and the communicative speaker's utterance weights in L1 are scaled by unmentioned-utterance attention weights.
