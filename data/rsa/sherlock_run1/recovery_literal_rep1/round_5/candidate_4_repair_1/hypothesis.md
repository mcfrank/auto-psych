We refine softmax_belief_salience_listener by hypothesizing that listeners model a descriptive speaker who has limited visual attention to unmentioned features, incorporating the attentional bottleneck mechanism from limited_attention_listener. When evaluating how likely a descriptive speaker is to name the heard feature, alternative unmentioned features receive discounted attentional weight rather than equal consideration. Relative to softmax_belief_salience_listener, recursion depth remains unchanged at L1, one parameter att_unmentioned is added with prior Beta(1.0, 3.0), no parameters are removed, and the speaker's true-utterance weights are scaled by unmentioned-utterance attention weights.
