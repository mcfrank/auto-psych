We refine softmax_belief_salience_listener by hypothesizing that listeners view a naive descriptive speaker as choosing referents uniformly rather than sharing the listener's perceptual salience prior over objects, applying their object salience prior privately when selecting among candidate referents, incorporating the private salience prior mechanism from naive_speaker_salience_listener.
