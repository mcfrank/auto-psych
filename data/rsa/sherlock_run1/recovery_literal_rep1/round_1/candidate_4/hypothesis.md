Listeners assume that a speaker's communicative valence modulates object salience: feature complexity increases prior salience when referring to a favorite object, but this preference diminishes when referring to a least-favorite object. This valence-scaled salience prior is shared as common knowledge between speaker and listener in depth-1 pragmatic reasoning. Relative to rsa_l1_shared_prior, recursion depth remains at L1, one parameter w_valence is added to scale feature count by trial valence in the shared prior, and all other parameters and terms are retained unchanged.
