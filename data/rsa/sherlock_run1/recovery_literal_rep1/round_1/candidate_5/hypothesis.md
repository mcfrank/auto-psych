Depth-1 RSA where affective framing modulates feature salience in the common-knowledge prior over objects, such that positive speaker valence amplifies the expectation that described objects are feature-rich while negative valence dampens it. This improves fit because people assume a speaker describing a favorite item prefers richer, decorated objects, whereas a speaker describing a least favorite item lacks that preference. Relative to rsa_l1_shared_prior, recursion depth remains unchanged at L1, one parameter w_valence is added to the salience prior with a Normal(0, 1) prior, no parameters are removed, and the feature salience weight is modulated by the product of w_valence and the display valence.
