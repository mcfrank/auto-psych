We refine rsa_l1_salience by hypothesizing that listeners modulate object salience according to speaker valence: while people default to expecting speakers to refer to feature-rich objects, this feature preference is amplified for favored referents and reduced or inverted when referring to least-favored referents. Compared to rsa_l1_salience, recursion depth remains unchanged at L1, one parameter w_valence is added with prior Normal(0.0, 1.0) with none removed, and the only other difference is modulating feature count by valence in the salience prior logits.
