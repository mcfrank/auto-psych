We refine naive_speaker_salience_listener by hypothesizing that a naive descriptive speaker incurs an utterance production cost proportional to the number of non-referent distractors excluded by a feature, reflecting the cognitive effort of contrasting against distractors lacking that feature. A pragmatic listener inverts this distractor-cost-sensitive naive production speaker and scales the resulting descriptive evidence by their own perceptual salience prior over objects. Relative to naive_speaker_salience_listener, recursion depth remains unchanged at L1, one parameter c_feature is added with prior Normal(0.0, 1.0), no parameters are removed, and the speaker's true-utterance weights are discounted by an utterance cost proportional to the count of objects lacking the feature.
