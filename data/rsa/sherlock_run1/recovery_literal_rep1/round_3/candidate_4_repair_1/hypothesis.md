We refine limited_attention_listener by hypothesizing that when attention lapses during referent interpretation, choices default to the perceptual salience of objects rather than uniform random guessing, incorporating the salience-lapse mechanism from softmax_belief_salience_listener. Because unattended lapses fall back on bottom-up visual expectations shaped by feature complexity and familiarization rather than pure noise, this better captures human choice distributions across varied displays. Relative to limited_attention_listener, recursion depth remains unchanged at L1, no parameters are added or removed, and the response probability mixture replaces uniform guessing with the object salience prior.
