Listeners in a reference game population are cognitively heterogeneous, comprising a mixture of literal and pragmatic reasoning types. A subset of listeners act as literal interpreters who choose uniformly among referents that satisfy the literal truth conditions of the utterance, while other listeners act as pragmatic interpreters who mentally invert an informative communicative speaker. When cognitive attention lapses, choices across all listeners default to bottom-up perceptual salience shaped by feature complexity and familiarization base rates.
