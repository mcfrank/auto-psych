Listeners interpret referring expressions by modeling a speaker whose communicative goal addresses a specific aspect or property of the referent rather than its full identity. For an intended referent, the speaker selects an aspect from among the referent's true features and chooses an utterance to informatively communicate that aspect to a literal listener. The pragmatic listener jointly reasons about the speaker's intended referent and latent communicative goal, selecting referents by marginalizing over the question under discussion.
