We refine costly_naive_speaker_listener by hypothesizing that utterance production costs scale with the number of non-referent distractors excluded by a feature rather than inversely with feature extension, reflecting the cognitive demand of verifying visual contrast against distractors lacking that feature. A pragmatic listener inverts this distractor-cost-sensitive naive production speaker, which better accounts for reference across display sizes by penalizing discriminating labels proportionally to scene-level contrast. Relative to costly_naive_speaker_listener, recursion depth remains at L1, no parameters are added or removed, and the utterance production cost is modified from c_feature divided by feature extension to c_feature multiplied by the count of objects lacking the feature.
