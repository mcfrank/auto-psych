We refine costly_naive_speaker_listener by hypothesizing that a speaker's communicative valence modulates referent salience: listeners expect speakers describing a favorite referent to prefer feature-rich objects, but this preference diminishes or reverses when describing a least-favorite referent. Inverting a cost-sensitive naive production speaker under this valence-modulated prior allows pragmatic interpretation to flexibly adapt to emotional framing. Relative to costly_naive_speaker_listener, recursion depth remains at L1, one parameter w_valence is added with prior Normal(0.0, 1.0), no parameters are removed, and the feature count term in the prior logits is modulated by adding w_valence multiplied by display valence.
