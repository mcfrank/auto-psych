We refine naive_speaker_listener by hypothesizing that listeners view a naive speaker as choosing referents uniformly rather than sharing the listener's perceptual salience prior over objects, applying their object salience prior only when integrating the speaker's utterance. Because the naive speaker produces true features without factoring in object salience, the listener treats the speaker's utterance as pure descriptive evidence and scales it by their own prior expectations over objects. Relative to naive_speaker_listener, recursion depth remains unchanged at L1, no parameters are added or removed, the speaker's referent prior weight changes from the salience prior to uniform, and the listener weights the inverted speaker distribution by the salience prior.
