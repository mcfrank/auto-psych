Speakers incur a communicative effort cost when choosing an ambiguous, shared feature for a target that already possesses a uniquely distinguishing description, as the unique feature preempts ambiguous alternatives. In contrast, when an intended target possesses no uniquely distinguishing feature, all matching descriptions are considered without penalty. Pragmatic listeners invert this bounded preemption speaker at depth 2, inferring that a speaker who used a shared word lacked an unshared, distinguishing feature to identify their target.
