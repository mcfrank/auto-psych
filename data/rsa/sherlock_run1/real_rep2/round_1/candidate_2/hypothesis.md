People interpret a speaker not by recursively simulating a listener, but by inverting a direct production process in which the speaker samples uniformly among the true features of the intended referent. On complex displays containing an unambiguous feature alongside shared features, this mechanism disagrees sharply with recursive RSA: while recursive RSA predicts listeners will strongly favor the shared-feature object because the competitor possessed an alternative unambiguous feature, direct inversion predicts listeners remain indifferent between objects with equal feature counts. Listeners therefore evaluate candidate referents strictly by their feature specificity rather than by counterfactual word alternatives.
