      Cells

      Held-out is the exported model's held-out log predictive density minus the best starting model's, over the held-out conditions, with the SE clustered by held-out unit. Rank is among every model the cell scored on held-out data (live, pruned and starting models).

      Selection vs generalisation

      Each point is a model the cell had before its end-of-run prune. Across: ELPD-LOO on the training trials, relative to the cell's best (what the loop selects and prunes on). Up: held-out lpd relative to the best starting model (what we want). Models far to the left are drawn at the edge.

      Where the held-out gain comes from

      Real-data cells: held-out lpd by source, relative to the best starting model, for the exported model and the three models that did best on held-out conditions.

      What generalised

      The three best models on held-out conditions in each real-data cell, with the first sentence of their hypothesis.

      Recovery

      RMSE between each model's and the withheld ground truth's posterior-mean choice probabilities over the novelty pool (log scale). The dashed line is the verdict's 0.01 threshold.
