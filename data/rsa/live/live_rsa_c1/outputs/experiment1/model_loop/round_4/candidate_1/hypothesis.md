Listeners in reference games are cognitively heterogeneous, behaving as a population mixture of literal and depth-2 pragmatic listener types. Literal listeners select among truthful referents according to visual attention without counterfactual speaker reasoning, while pragmatic listeners perform recursive pragmatic inversion to resolve communicative intent. Across display sizes and feature overlaps, this mixture moderates extreme target inferences by providing a consistent literal choice baseline regardless of feature gap or display complexity.
