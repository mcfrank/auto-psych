Listeners interpret utterances under bounded cognitive capacity, performing conservative belief updating where communicative evidence from the speaker is tempered relative to prior scene expectations. Rather than executing unconstrained Bayesian belief revision that overwhelmingly eliminates complex competitor objects, a resource-limited listener dampens extreme likelihood ratios to maintain moderate expectations for multi-featured competitors. On uninformative trials where no communicative evidence is received, choices directly reflect prior perceptual and contextual scene expectations.
