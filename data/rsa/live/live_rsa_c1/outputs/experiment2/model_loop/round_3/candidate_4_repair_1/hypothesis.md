Pragmatic listeners reason at depth 1 about a similarity-contrast speaker under common knowledge of visual endpoint extremity and stochastic transmission noise, but recognize that speakers prioritize unambiguous distinguishing descriptors that pragmatically preempt using shared ambiguous features. Refining contrastive_extremity_noisy_speaker by incorporating the pragmatic preemption component from pragmatic_preemption_listener: when an intended referent possesses an unambiguous distinguishing descriptor, using an ambiguous shared feature is discounted by a preemption penalty. This preemption mechanism directly resolves the model's severe underprediction of minimal-feature targets on ambiguous utterance trials with redundant features and its overprediction of multi-featured competitors across large feature gaps, while preserving similarity-contrastive reasoning and endpoint salience.
