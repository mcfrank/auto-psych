Communicative speakers operate over graded semantic applicability where a descriptor's truth value weakens exponentially with extraneous unmentioned visual features, while listeners' referential expectations anchor asymmetrically on the maximally elaborated focal exemplar. Refining contrastive_focal_extremity_speaker by incorporating graded semantic applicability from graded_applicability_listener, a pragmatic listener inverts a similarity-contrast speaker who discounts over-complex matching referents and accounts for stochastic articulation noise. This graded focal-contrast mechanism resolves the severe underprediction of minimal-feature targets on redundant-feature trials and intermediate competitor overselection, while preserving focal exemplar salience on uninformative mumble trials.
