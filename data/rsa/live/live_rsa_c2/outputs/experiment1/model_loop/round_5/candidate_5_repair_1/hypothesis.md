Refining bounded_capacity_redundancy_l2, we incorporate perceptual load-constrained inferential capacity from perceptual_load_listener_l2 into pragmatic reference resolution. Visual attention is a strictly finite cognitive resource: in simple displays with few items, low perceptual load leaves ample attentional bandwidth for counterfactual reasoning, producing sharp pragmatic preemption; in visually crowded scenes with more items and feature conjunctions, high perceptual load divides visual attention, systematically attenuating pragmatic inferential precision and softening preemption penalties against competitor referents. Bounded pragmatic listeners invert redundancy-discounting speakers under load-dependent inferential precision, reconciling sharp preemption in sparse scalar displays with elevated competitor choices in complex, feature-rich scenes.

Differences from bounded_capacity_redundancy_l2:
- Recursion depth: Unchanged; choice_probs calls L2.
- Parameters added: kappa_base (prior: dist.LogNormal(0.0, 1.0)), w_load (prior: dist.Normal(0.0, 1.0)).
- Parameters removed: kappa.
- Pragmatic listener inferential precision: Replaces fixed inferential capacity kappa with scene-dependent perceptual load scaling (kappa = params["kappa_base"] * jnp.exp(-params["w_load"] * load)), where load scales with total display items and visual features (jnp.log(n_obj * (total_features + 1.0) / 18.0)) from perceptual_load_listener_l2.
- All other terms: Speaker contextual feature redundancy discounting (w_redundancy via pairwise Jaccard overlap) in S1 and S2, shared perceptual referent prior (w_singleton, w_distinct, w_features, w_empty, w_color), truth-conditional literal semantics at L0, depth-2 RSA recursion (L1, L2), and uniform lapse mixing remain identical to bounded_capacity_redundancy_l2.
