We refine salience_lapse_prev_redundancy_l2 by incorporating contrast-gated inferential precision from contrast_gated_listener_l2 into pragmatic listener inference. Listeners dynamically gate their pragmatic inferential capacity by the contextual discriminative contrast of the heard expression: words that rule out context distractors license strong counterfactual preemption, whereas ambiguous words that are true of all items in the display convey zero discriminative contrast, prompting listeners to attenuate pragmatic precision and rely on default perceptual expectations. This contrast-gated precision directly resolves the overprediction of pragmatic targets in two-object subset displays and ambiguous-word contexts while salience-captured lapses sustain competitor and false-feature choices.

Differences from salience_lapse_prev_redundancy_l2:
- Recursion depth: Unchanged; choice_probs calls L2.
- Parameters added: kappa_base (prior: dist.LogNormal(0.0, 1.0)), w_contrast (prior: dist.Normal(0.0, 1.0)).
- Parameters removed: kappa.
- Pragmatic listener inferential precision: Replaces fixed inferential capacity kappa with contrast-gated inferential precision (kappa = params["kappa_base"] * jnp.exp(params["w_contrast"] * contrast)) from contrast_gated_listener_l2, where contrast scales with the proportion of context objects ruled out by the heard utterance ((n_obj - freq) / n_obj, with 0.0 for the sink utterance).
- All other terms: Capacity-limited updating via vec(kappa, u) in L1 and L2, contextual feature prevalence accessibility (w_prevalence) and contextual feature redundancy discounting (w_redundancy via pairwise Jaccard overlap) in S1 and S2, literal truth-conditional semantics at L0, baseline perceptual prior components (w_singleton, w_distinct, w_features, w_empty, w_color), depth-2 RSA recursion, and salience-captured attentional lapse mixing ((1.0 - lapse) * heard + lapse * prior) remain identical to salience_lapse_prev_redundancy_l2.
