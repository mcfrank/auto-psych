We refine feature_binding_uncertainty_lapse_l2 by incorporating literal comprehension grounded in the Size Principle of generative communication from size_principle_listener_l2 into foundational semantic interpretation. Under the Size Principle, the probability that a speaker produces any specific feature of an object scales inversely with that object's total visual feature complexity, establishing a generative semantic baseline where simpler matching objects are naturally more probable referents than feature-dense competitors. Pragmatic listeners and speakers reason at depth 2 with common knowledge of this generative foundation alongside perceptual feature-binding noise and contextual lexical uncertainty, directly resolving the model's significant underprediction of choices for simpler candidates in cross-cutting non-nested displays and softening preemption in subset contexts.
