We refine competition_endpoint_binding_lapse_l2 by replacing display-normalized illusory conjunctions with complexity-scaled perceptual feature binding from Feature Integration Theory. When resolving referring expressions, features active in working memory are prone to illusory conjunctions on non-matching distractors, with an object's susceptibility scaling directly with its visual feature complexity. Pragmatic listeners invert competition-bounded speakers while factoring in this distractor-specific perceptual vulnerability, directly resolving the incumbent model's severe underprediction of literal semantic violations on visually complex non-matching objects (complex_false_object_rate).
