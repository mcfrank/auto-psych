We refine competition_distractor_endpoint_l2 by incorporating feature conjunction binding into perceptual reference resolution. Under Feature Integration Theory, illusory feature conjunctions occur specifically when candidate objects require attentional binding across multiple visual attributes, whereas objects with zero or single features are processed pre-attentively and do not suffer illusory conjunction errors. Susceptibility to illusory feature binding scales directly with an object's feature conjunction load, directly resolving the model's severe underprediction of choices for complex non-matching objects on word trials (complex_false_object_rate) while preserving distractor-interference reasoning, lexical competition, and contextual endpoint salience.

Differences from competition_distractor_endpoint_l2:
- Recursion depth: Unchanged; choice_probs calls L2.
- Parameters added: None.
- Parameters removed: None.
- Perceptual feature-binding mechanism: In choice_probs on word trials, illusory feature binding scales with candidate distractor feature conjunction complexity (conjunction_load = jnp.maximum(ctx.feature_count - 1.0, 0.0) / max_fc) rather than uniform non-matching presence, gating effective binding by the maximum conjunction complexity among context distractors (effective_bind = params["w_binding"] * jnp.max(is_false * conjunction_load)), so that single-feature and empty distractors are immune to illusory binding while multi-featured distractors attract illusory binding in proportion to their conjunction complexity.
- All other terms: Perceptual distractor interference in speaker utterance production (w_threat), lexical competition across candidate utterances (w_competition), contextual end-point salience (w_endpoint) in the shared visual referent prior (alongside w_singleton, w_distinct, w_features, w_empty, and w_color), capacity-limited inferential updating (kappa) at L1 and L2, contextual feature prevalence accessibility (w_prevalence) and referential equivalence class size redundancy discounting (w_redundancy via compute_equivalence_class) on speaker alternatives in S1 and S2, literal truth-conditional semantics at L0, depth-2 RSA recursion, and salience-captured attentional lapse mixing remain identical to competition_distractor_endpoint_l2.
