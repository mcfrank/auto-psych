We refine competition_endpoint_binding_lapse_l2 by incorporating similarity-based perceptual confusion from perceptual_confusion_listener_l2 into listener response choice. When choosing a referent in a visual scene, motor responses and perceptual clicks are subject to confusability with visually similar items: under Shepard's Universal Law of Generalization, response slips decay exponentially with feature distance from the intended referent rather than dispersing according to global salience. Pragmatic listeners integrate this similarity-governed confusion kernel at the response interface, directly resolving the incumbent model's underprediction of clicks on visually complex non-matching distractors and overprediction of minimal targets in subset displays while preserving lexical competition, contextual endpoint salience, and feature binding.

Differences from competition_endpoint_binding_lapse_l2:
- Recursion depth: Unchanged; choice_probs calls L2.
- Parameters added: gamma_confusion (prior: dist.LogNormal(0.0, 1.0)).
- Parameters removed: None.
- Response choice / perceptual confusion mechanism: On word trials in choice_probs, replaces salience-captured attentional lapse mixing toward the prior with similarity-governed perceptual confusion slips from perceptual_confusion_listener_l2 (p_choice = (1.0 - params["lapse"]) * p_word + params["lapse"] * slip, where slip = jnp.matmul(p_word, T) and T is a row-normalized Shepard confusion kernel with similarity S = jnp.exp(-params["gamma_confusion"] * total_dist)). On unprompted prior trials (ctx.is_prior > 0), listener choices remain governed by the visual referent prior.
- All other terms: Contextual end-point salience (w_endpoint) in the shared visual referent prior (alongside w_singleton, w_distinct, w_features, w_empty, and w_color), perceptual feature-binding noise on non-matching distractors (w_binding), capacity-limited inferential updating (kappa) at L1 and L2, lexical competition in speaker utterance production (w_competition), contextual feature prevalence accessibility (w_prevalence) and referential equivalence class size redundancy discounting (w_redundancy via compute_equivalence_class) on speaker alternatives in S1 and S2, literal truth-conditional semantics at L0, and depth-2 RSA recursion remain identical to competition_endpoint_binding_lapse_l2.
