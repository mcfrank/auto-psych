We refine feature_binding_salience_lapse_l2 by incorporating contextual end-point salience from endpoint_salience_l2 into the shared visual referent prior. In visual reference games, human perceptual attention spontaneously anchors at the structural boundaries of the scene's feature complexity distribution (the minimal baseline object and the maximal exemplar), while intermediate objects that fall strictly between these poles are disfavored as unremarkable non-targets. Pragmatic listeners invert informative speakers under this extremity prior alongside perceptual feature-binding noise and salience-captured attentional lapses, directly resolving the incumbent model's severe overprediction of strictly intermediate-complexity objects on both word trials (word_intermediate_match_rate) and unprompted mumble trials (mumble_intermediate_rate).
