We refine competition_endpoint_binding_l2 by incorporating partially egocentric speaker production from egocentric_endpoint_binding_l2 into communicative inference. When communicating about an object, speakers balance partner-directed informativeness against egocentric accessibility, frequently producing an applicable visual feature based on intrinsic availability without exhaustively verifying whether it uniquely distinguishes the referent from context distractors. Pragmatic listeners invert this competition-bounded, partially egocentric speaker via recursive Theory of Mind at depth 2, which softens recursive preemption in two-object subset displays and ambiguous-word contexts while favoring candidates with fewer total features in cross-cutting contexts because egocentric production probability scales inversely with each candidate's total feature count.
