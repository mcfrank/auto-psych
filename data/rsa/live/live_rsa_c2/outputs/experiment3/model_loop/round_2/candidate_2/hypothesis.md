Listeners in visual reference games are resource-limited strategic reasoners whose depth of recursive Theory of Mind is bounded by a cognitive stopping probability at each tier of recursion. Advancing from literal comprehension to first-order pragmatics and second-order strategic reasoning incurs cumulative executive control effort, such that listeners halt at lower depths with a constant stopping probability. Across display sizes from 2-object to 4-object scenes and complex feature overlaps, this bounded depth hierarchy naturally dampens extreme recursive preemption against competitor referents in subset contexts while robustly favoring simpler matching candidates in cross-cutting displays.
