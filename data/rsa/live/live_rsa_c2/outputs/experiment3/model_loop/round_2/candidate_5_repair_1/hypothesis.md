We refine endpoint_binding_salience_lapse_l2 by incorporating base-rate neglect from base_rate_neglect_listener_l2 into communicative inference. When interpreting referring expressions in visual reference games, listeners exhibit base-rate neglect upon receiving diagnostic linguistic evidence, attenuating default perceptual base rates in favor of the speaker's communicative likelihood while unprompted expectations remain governed by the full visual referent prior. This communicative discounting of perceptual base rates naturally favors simpler referents in cross-cutting displays (non_nested_fewer_features_rate) and sharpens pragmatic preemption in two-object subset displays (two_object_subset_target_rate) while preserving contextual endpoint salience, perceptual feature binding, and salience-captured attentional lapses.
