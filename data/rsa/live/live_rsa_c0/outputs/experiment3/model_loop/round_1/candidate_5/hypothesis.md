Pragmatic listener at depth 2 under capacity-limited executive inhibitory control with contextual category typicality, bounded speaker consideration, contrastive outlier pop-out, and speaker utterance ambiguity avoidance costs. Refining bounded_typicality_outlier_listener by incorporating utterance ambiguity costs from speaker_ambiguity_cost_listener: speakers in reference games penalize referring expressions in proportion to the number of unintended contextual objects they describe, strongly preferring unambiguous expressions when communicating referents that possess distinguishing alternatives. Pragmatic listeners invert this ambiguity-averse speaker, inferring that hearing an ambiguous word provides stronger evidence for referents that lacked distinguishing alternatives, which directly elevates choices of multi-feature pragmatic targets over all-feature competitors in four-object displays (four_object_multifeature_target_vs_all_feature_rate and four_object_two_feature_target_vs_three_feature_competitor_rate) while preserving bounded speaker consideration, contextual category typicality, and contrastive outlier pop-out.
