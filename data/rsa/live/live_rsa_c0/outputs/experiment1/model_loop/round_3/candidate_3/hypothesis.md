Listeners interpret referring expressions by reasoning about a speaker with uncertain communicative goals: either referential identification (singling out the target object among the display alternatives) or descriptive aspect attribution (communicating an informative visual feature of the referent). Under the aspect goal, speakers select expressions to inform the listener about the referent's descriptive features rather than its identity, moderating scalar exclusion and explaining why feature-rich referents retain substantial choice probability on ubiquitous and scalar displays.
