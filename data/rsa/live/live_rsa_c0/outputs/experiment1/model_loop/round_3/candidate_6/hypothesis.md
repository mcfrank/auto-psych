Pragmatic listener at depth 2 with bounded attentional capture, expected communicative success, competitor confusion avoidance, and visual salience prior. Refining expected_success_confusion_speaker by incorporating the capacity-limited attentional capture component from limited_attention_listener: listeners interpret referring expressions under capacity-limited visual attention, balancing top-down pragmatic reasoning against bottom-up visual attentional capture. Under higher visual load in larger displays, attentional dispersion occasionally causes bottom-up attentional capture by salient objects without full communicative verification, whereas unambiguous pop-out displays ensure near-perfect target identification; in the attentive mode, listeners invert a depth-2 speaker maximizing linear expected communicative success while penalizing competitor confusion. This resolves excessive scalar exclusion and elevates choices of feature-rich objects on ubiquitous and scalar displays while naturally capturing foil selections under visual load.
