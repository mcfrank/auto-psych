Refining candidate_load_attention_listener by incorporating the contrastive outlier pop-out component from contrastive_outlier_listener: when a referring expression is true of all objects in a scene except exactly one, the mentioned property establishes a shared perceptual background that isolates the solitary non-matching object as a contrastive pop-out outlier, occasionally drawing listener selections despite the lack of semantic fit. This contrastive pop-out mechanism directly rectifies the severe under-prediction of solitary foil selections on multi-object displays while preserving capacity-limited attentional reasoning and literal precision elsewhere.
