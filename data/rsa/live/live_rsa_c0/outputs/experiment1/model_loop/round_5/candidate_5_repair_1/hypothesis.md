Pragmatic listener at depth 2 with referential candidate load attentional capture, referential codability prior, expected communicative success, competitor confusion avoidance, and visual salience prior. Refining candidate_load_attention_listener by incorporating the referential codability component from referential_codability_listener: listeners expect speakers to refer to objects with high referential codability—objects possessing distinguishing descriptive features that make them viable communicative targets in the scene context. Before hearing an informative word, listeners evaluate each referent by its communicative affordance, defined as the total discriminative capacity of the available expressions that describe it, elevating a priori expectations for distinguishable entities. This communicative affordance prior directly moderates overly aggressive scalar implicatures on two-object displays, suppresses choices of intermediate feature-count competitors on multi-match displays, and elevates choices of feature-rich objects on ubiquitous utterance and mumble displays.
