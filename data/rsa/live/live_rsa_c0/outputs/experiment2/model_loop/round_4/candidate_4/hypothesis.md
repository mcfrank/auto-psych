Pragmatic listener at depth 2 under capacity-limited executive inhibitory control with centroid prototype distance typicality and bounded speaker consideration, refining prototype_typicality_bounded_listener. Before hearing an informative word and during referent selection, listeners evaluate each referent by its psychological distance to the scene's category prototype (the central tendency feature vector), penalizing both sparse outliers that lack characteristic scene features and overly complex referents with idiosyncratic features. This prototype distance prior tempers excessive singleton preferences on uninformative trials, elevates choices of typical duplicated candidates, and prevents all-feature competitors from gaining an artificial prior advantage over multi-feature pragmatic targets in multi-object displays.

Differences from prototype_typicality_bounded_listener:
- Recursion depth remains depth 2 (choice_probs calls L_typical, which inverts a speaker simulating depth-1 listener L1).
- Parameters added: None.
- Parameters removed: None (retains alpha, logit_p_consider, logit_p_base, w_load, w_popout, w_candidate, w_inhibit_leak, w_typical, w_singleton, w_distinct, w_features, w_valence, w_color, w_confusion, and lapse with identical priors).
- In choice_probs, replaced compute_typicality(ctx) with prototype distance: computed the scene prototype as the mean real feature vector across context objects, and defined an object's typicality as its negative squared Euclidean distance to this category centroid rather than the unnormalized sum of shared feature fractions.
