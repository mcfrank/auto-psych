Listeners interpret referring expressions under capacity-limited executive inhibitory control with a referential codability prior, balancing goal-directed pragmatic inference against distractor interference while expecting speakers to favor easily discriminable referents. Refining inhibitory_load_listener by incorporating the referential codability component from candidate_load_codability_listener: listeners evaluate candidate referents by their communicative affordance—the cumulative discriminative capacity of the descriptive expressions true of each object—elevating prior expectations for distinguishable entities with rich descriptive options. This communicative affordance prior directly tempers overly aggressive scalar implicatures on two-object displays and elevates choices of feature-rich objects on mumble and multi-match displays, while load-dependent inhibitory control maintains distractor suppression.

- Added parameter: w_codable (prior dist.Normal(0.0, 1.0)).
