Pragmatic listener at depth 2 under capacity-limited executive inhibitory control with contextual category typicality, bounded speaker consideration, and contrastive outlier pop-out. Refining prototype_typicality_bounded_listener by incorporating the contrastive outlier pop-out component from prototype_typicality_outlier_listener: when a referring expression is true of all objects in a visual scene except exactly one, the shared feature creates a contrastive perceptual background that isolates the solitary non-matching object as a visual outlier, occasionally capturing listener choices despite the absence of semantic fit. This contrastive pop-out mechanism directly rectifies the observed distractor intrusion rate where solitary non-matching foils in three-object displays are chosen significantly more often than standard pragmatic suppression predicts (three_object_distractor_intrusion_rate), while fully preserving bounded speaker consideration and contextual category typicality reasoning.
