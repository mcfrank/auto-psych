Pragmatic listener at depth 2 under capacity-limited executive inhibitory control with a referential codability prior and contextual category typicality prior. Refining inhibitory_codability_listener by incorporating the category typicality component from prototype_typicality_listener: listeners evaluate each referent by its contextual category typicality—the average feature overlap with other context objects—scaling smoothly across display sizes and redundant feature overlaps. This category typicality prior directly tempers excessive singleton preferences on uninformative mumble trials, elevates choices of duplicated candidates on multi-match displays, and increases selections of feature-rich objects under ubiquitous expressions, resolving the primary discrepancies identified in the posterior-predictive critique.
