Pragmatic listener at depth 2 under capacity-limited executive inhibitory control with a referential codability prior and contextual contrastive selectivity. Refining inhibitory_codability_listener by incorporating the contrastive selectivity component from contrastive_selectivity_listener: listeners engage in top-down pragmatic scalar reasoning only to the extent that a referring expression isolates a contrast set against non-matching distractors in the visual context. When an utterance is contextually unselective and applies to all or most scene objects, top-down scalar exclusion is suspended, allowing choices to be guided by referential communicative affordances and perceptual feature complexity. This contrastive selectivity mechanism directly moderates overly aggressive scalar implicatures on two-object displays, elevates selections of maximal-feature candidates on all-match and multi-match displays, and increases pragmatic target choices on selective four-object displays, resolving the primary discrepancies identified in the posterior-predictive critique.
