Pragmatic listener at depth 2 under capacity-limited executive inhibitory control with a referential codability prior, utterance specificity costs, and contextual contrastive selectivity attentional engagement. Refining inhibitory_codability_cost_listener by incorporating contextual contrastive selectivity into attentional engagement: listeners selectively recruit top-down pragmatic reasoning to the extent that an utterance isolates a contrast set against non-matching distractors in the visual context, whereas non-discriminating expressions that describe all scene objects suspend scalar exclusion in favor of visual salience and feature complexity. As visual scene size grows, selectivity naturally scales with the proportion of contextual objects ruled out, maintaining robust pragmatic scalar exclusion in large multi-object displays while moderating excessive scalar implicatures on two-object ambiguous displays and elevating maximal-feature object choices on ubiquitous utterance displays.
