\caption{Each run's best model against its seed models under ELPD, AIC and BIC. Each cell is the range, over the run's seeds, of the best model's advantage in nats: its ELPD minus the seed's, and the seed's AIC and BIC minus its own, halved to put them on the ELPD's scale. Positive values favor the best model. ELPD is the paper's: PSIS-LOO for subjective randomness, grouped 4-fold cross-validation by example set for number sequence generalization, and the held-out pre-registered experiment for reference games. AIC and BIC use the maximized log-likelihood (L-BFGS from several starts); for the reference games they are fitted to the pooled data (70{,}841 trials). \emph{All parameters} counts every free coordinate the likelihood depends on, person-level ones included. \emph{Person-level integrated} integrates the person-level coordinates out (importance sampling around a Laplace approximation, a lower bound in expectation) and counts only the population-level parameters. Number sequence generalization and reference games have no person-level parameters, so the two conventions coincide.}
