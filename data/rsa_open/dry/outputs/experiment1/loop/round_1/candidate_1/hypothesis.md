Speakers choose utterances to address one of two communicative goals (Questions Under Discussion): uniquely identifying the target referent, or truthfully attributing a property to it. Listeners perform joint Bayesian inference over the speaker's referent and communicative goal, expecting uniquely identifying descriptions when the speaker aims to identify, but retaining graded credibility for competitors that share the named property when the speaker merely describes an attribute. Prior expectations favor contextually unique singletons and feature-distinctive items.
