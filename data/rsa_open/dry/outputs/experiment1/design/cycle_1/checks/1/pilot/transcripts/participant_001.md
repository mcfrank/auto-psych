## Screen

Welcome. Welcome! In this short study, you will see groups of simple drawings and judge which object a speaker is talking about.
It takes about 5.1 minutes.

Instructions:
On each page, you will see between 2 and 4 objects (numbered from left to right) that can have different extra parts. A speaker who sees the exact same screen is talking about one specific object. You have 10 points to split among the objects on each page: give more points to objects you think the speaker is more likely referring to, and 0 points to objects you think the speaker could not mean. Your points must be whole numbers (0 to 10) that add up to 10. If the speaker says a word and only one object has that part, give most or all of your 10 points (such as all 10 points) to that object. When several objects have the named part, consider what other parts each object has and what other words the speaker could have chosen, and split your 10 points to reflect how likely each object is. On a few pages, you will not hear the speaker's word; on those pages, split your 10 points according to which object you think the speaker is most likely to talk about.

How do you answer on each page?
1) Split 10 whole-number points across the objects so that the points add up to 10.
2) Give 10 points to every object that has the named part.
3) Click on a single object without assigning points.
Reply ANSWER: <the option's number>.
