Refining rsa_l2_singleton_feat_color_valence_l0 by incorporating the speaker competitor-confusion aversion component from competitor_confusion_speaker: speakers at both depth-1 and depth-2 reasoning tiers actively avoid utterances that are shared with visually similar distractors in the display, penalizing each candidate word by the summed exponentially decaying feature similarity between the target referent and all competing objects that also satisfy that word. This improves fit because listeners inverting a confusion-averse speaker allocate more belief to perceptually isolated referents and multi-feature referents whose shared words suffer less neighbor crowding, while penalizing intermediate referents that closely resemble other literal matches of the heard word. Compared to rsa_l2_singleton_feat_color_valence_l0, the recursion depth remains depth 2 (listener calls L2, which inverts a speaker calling L1, which inverts a speaker calling literal L0), all six original parameters and the object prior remain unchanged, and one new parameter w_confusion is added to weight the utterance-by-object competitor confusion matrix inside the speaker utility at both L1 and L2.
