## Screen

Welcome. Welcome! In this study you will look at groups of friendly robots and judge how likely each robot is to be the one a speaker means.
It takes about 6.0 minutes.

Instructions:
On each screen you will see 3 or 4 robots. A speaker who sees the exact same robots says a single word that names a part of the robot they mean (or on some screens, you will judge which robot they are most likely to talk about before hearing any word). You have 10 points to split across the robots: put more points on robots that are more likely, give 0 points to any robot that does not have the named part, and if only one robot has that part, put all 10 points on it. Your points on each screen must add up to 10.

How do you answer on each screen?
1) Split all 10 points across the robots based on how likely each robot is to be the one the speaker means.
2) Give 10 points to every robot on the screen.
3) Type a description of the robots.
Reply ANSWER: <the option's number>.
