## Screen

Welcome. In this short study we are interested in how people figure out what others are talking about.
It takes about 4.0 minutes.

Instructions:
Meet Bob. Bob wants to tell you about some of his things: his friends, his snowmen, the sundaes and pizzas he makes, his boats and his Christmas trees. On each screen you will see a few pictures. Bob can only say one word to tell you which one he means. Click on the picture you think Bob is talking about. Sometimes Bob mumbles and you won't be able to hear his word. Then just click on the one you think he is talking about. There are no right or wrong answers; go with your first impression. After each click, one of the pictures is marked: say how likely it is that Bob means that one, from 1 (not at all) to 5 (certainly).

What do you do on each screen?
1) Click on the picture you think Bob is talking about
2) Type the word Bob said
3) Rate how much you like each picture
Reply ANSWER: <the option's number>.
