Refining rsa_l2_singleton_feat_color_valence_l0 by replacing scene-wide contextual distinctiveness on utterance trials with utterance-extension distinctiveness, so listeners and simulated depth-1 listeners evaluate how visually distinct a referent is only relative to the candidate objects that actually satisfy the word rather than against false distractors already ruled out by literal semantics. This prevents false featureless distractors from artificially inflating the salience of feature-rich competitors when a word applies to a subset of objects, while preserving the strong distinctiveness advantage of a feature-rich singleton when a word applies to every object in the display. Compared to rsa_l2_singleton_feat_color_valence_l0, recursion depth remains at depth two with listener calling L2 (which calls L1, which calls literal L0), no parameters are added or removed (retaining alpha, w_singleton, w_distinct, w_features, w_valence, and w_color), unused vec and with_lapse imports are removed, L1 and L2 add listener knows u and weight referent choice by the utterance-indexed prior at(prior, u, r) rather than vec(prior, r), and utterance trials compute distinctiveness across the extension of each real utterance while prior trials continue to use scene-wide distinctiveness.
