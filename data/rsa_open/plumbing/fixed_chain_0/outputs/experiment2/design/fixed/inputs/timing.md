# How long experiments took people

Completion times of finished experiments (aggregates only: how long, never what people answered). Use them to judge how many trials fit in your minutes. The pilot's time check uses the same records: its estimate is a simple model (reading at 200 words a minute, a look at each object, an answer per trial) scaled by people's real times on experiments of the same response type, or of every type when none has run yet.
