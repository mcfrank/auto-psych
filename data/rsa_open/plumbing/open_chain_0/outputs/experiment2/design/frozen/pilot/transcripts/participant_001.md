## Screen

Welcome. Welcome to our study on how people understand descriptions of objects.
It takes about 3.6 minutes.

Instructions:
In each round, you will see a row of objects that differ in their parts. A speaker sees the same objects and wants to point out one of them. On most rounds, the speaker says a single word that is true of the object they mean (when the speaker says a word, the object they mean always has that part). On some rounds, you have not heard the speaker's word yet.

Each round has two steps:
1. First, click the object you think the speaker means.
2. Second, one object will be highlighted: rate from 1 (not at all likely) to 7 (extremely likely) how likely it is that the speaker meant that highlighted object.

What two answers do you give on each round?
1) First click the object you think the speaker meant, then rate from 1 to 7 how likely a highlighted object is to be the one the speaker meant.
2) Click every object that has the named part, then type a word for the speaker.
3) Only rate one object without clicking any object first.
Reply ANSWER: <the option's number>.
