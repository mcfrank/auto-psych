In both the communicative speaker's referent expectations and on uninformative displays without a referring expression, visual salience integrates discrete singleton pop-out with continuous contextual distinctiveness based on total feature distance to all other display items. This addresses the under-allocation of points to isolated empty singletons in multi-singleton scenes and reduces the over-penalization of distinct competitors when hearing widely applicable features.
