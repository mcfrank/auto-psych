### rsa_l2_uncompounded_prior_l0 — rejected (round 1 candidate 5 refine incumbent rsa_l2_singleton_feat_color_valence_l0)

**Outcome:** not novel: its listener and speaker predictions are within RMSE 0.0000 of rsa_l2_uncompounded_l1's (threshold 0.002) over the novelty pool of 1597 scenes. A re-parameterisation of an existing mechanism is not a new hypothesis; change what the listener or speaker computes.

**Hypothesis:** # rsa_l2_uncompounded_prior_l0 Pragmatic listener at depth 2 with uncompounded perceptual prior and literal L0. Listeners invert a pragmatic speaker who simulates a purely semantic, uncompounded pragmatic listener, integrating the visual perceptual prior solely at the top decision level rather than redundantly compounding perceptual biases across recursive reasoning tiers. On uninformative prior trials without an informative word, listener choices follow the perceptual prior directly. ## Refinement of rsa_l2_singleton_feat_color_valence_l0 This model refines `rsa_l2_singleton_feat_color_valence_l0` with ONE stated change: removing the perceptual prior from the intermediate simulated pragmatic listener L1 so that L1 reasons with a uniform object prior. In the source model, L1 used the perceptual prior, which was then exponentiated in S2's communicative utility and multiplied again by the perceptual prior at L2, redundantly compounding perceptual biases across tiers. This compounding swamped semantic informativeness, leading to severe discrepancies where listeners were incorrectly predicted to prefer singleton competitors over duplicate scalar targets (e.g., in `singleton_competitor_inversion_unit25` and `universal_feature_simplicity_advantage_unit35`). Uncompounding the prior ensures that the visual prior (singleton salience, distinctiveness, complexity, and color contrast) is integrated exactly once at the top-level listener, preserving pragmatic scalar implicatures while maintaining perceptual salience. ## Differences from source 1. **Recursion depth**: Unchanged (both call depth-2 listener `L2`). 2. **Parameters**: None added or removed (`PARAMS` is identical: `alpha`, `w_singleton`, `w_distinct`, `w_features`, `w_valence`, `w_color`). 3. **Definition of L1**: In `L1`, the object prior `speaker: given(r in OBJ, wpp=vec(prior, r))` is replaced with a uniform prior `speaker: given(r in OBJ, uniformly)`, and the `prior` parameter is removed from `L1`'s signature (`L1[u: UTT, r: OBJ](alpha, lex: ...)`). 4. **Call to L1 in L2**: The call inside `L2`'s speaker utility is updated from `L1[u, r](alpha, lex, prior)` to `L1[u, r](alpha, lex)`. 5. **Other terms**: Unchanged. `L0`, `L2`, the perceptual prior feature computation in `listener`, and the prior trial handling `jnp.where(ctx.is_prior > 0, prior, heard)` are identical.

### isolated_graded_costly_l2 — rejected (round 1 candidate 6 refine chosen)

### rsa_l2_avoidable_ambiguity_l0 — rejected (round 1 candidate 5 refine incumbent rsa_l2_singleton_feat_color_valence_l0 repair 1)

**Outcome:** fitting failed: rsa_l2_avoidable_ambiguity_l0: the fit did not finish within 59 minutes (warmup took 42 minutes and it was killed while sampling. NUTS took 8.1 leapfrog steps per draw by the end of warmup, at step size 0.559)

**Hypothesis:** Refining rsa_l2_singleton_feat_color_valence_l0 by incorporating the avoidable ambiguity penalty from avoidable_ambiguity_speaker: when a target object possesses a uniquely identifying expression that distinguishes it from all context distractors, a cooperative speaker incurs an ambiguity penalty for choosing a shared, non-identifying descriptor instead. When an object lacks any uniquely identifying expression, it incurs no penalty and is forced to rely on shared descriptors. Pragmatic listeners invert this speaker at depth 2, correctly inferring that ambiguous words signal referents that could not be uniquely named and resolving the discrepancy on universal feature simplicity. Differences from source: recursion depth is unchanged (calls L2); no parameters added or removed; speakers in L1 and L2 subtract an ambiguity penalty of 1 unit of communicative utility when choosing an ambiguous word for a referent that admits a unique descriptor; all other prior and likelihood terms are unchanged.
