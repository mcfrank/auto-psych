We refine isolated_graded_costly_l3 by reducing the recursion depth from depth 3 to depth 2. Pragmatic listeners and speakers reason at depth 2 (inverting a speaker who simulates a depth-1 pragmatic listener) with perceptual isolation, graded semantics, and utterance extension costs, avoiding the excessive cognitive depth and computational cost of three-tier recursion. Differences from isolated_graded_costly_l3: recursion depth is reduced from L3 to L2 (the listener calls L2 instead of L3, and L3 is removed); all parameters and prior terms are unchanged.
