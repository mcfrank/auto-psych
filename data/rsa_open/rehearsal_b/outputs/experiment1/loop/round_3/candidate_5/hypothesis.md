Listeners interpret referring expressions with a strictly bounded reasoning depth (depth 1), inverting an informative speaker who evaluates literal semantics, while visual attention weights candidate referents by combining discrete singleton salience with continuous contextual distinctiveness. This refinement of bounded_depth_listener incorporates contextual feature contrast relative to all display competitors, resolving empirical under-allocation to empty singletons in full-duplicate displays and providing graded perceptual salience across distinct objects. Differences from bounded_depth_listener: recursion depth remains depth 1 (listener calls L1); parameter w_distinct is added with prior dist.Normal(0.0, 1.0); no parameters are removed (retaining alpha and w_singleton); and the object prior is extended by adding w_distinct times the total contextual feature contrast (summed Hamming distance across display objects).
