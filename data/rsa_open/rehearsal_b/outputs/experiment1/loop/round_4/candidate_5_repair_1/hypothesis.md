Refining bounded_depth_listener by incorporating visual feature parsimony into the referent prior: listeners interpret referring expressions with strictly bounded reasoning depth (depth 1), inverting an informative speaker who evaluates literal semantics, while visual attention weights candidate referents by combining discrete singleton salience with a parsimony penalty on total feature count. This directly penalizes multi-feature competitors in scalar implicature displays and favors simpler or empty referents on prior displays, preventing spurious singleton bonuses from overriding communicative informativeness when words match duplicate minimal targets. Differences from bounded_depth_listener: recursion depth remains unchanged (depth 1, listener calls L1); parameter w_features is added with prior dist.Normal(0.0, 1.0); no parameters are removed (retaining alpha and w_singleton); and the referent prior is extended by adding w_features times the object's feature count (ctx.feature_count).
