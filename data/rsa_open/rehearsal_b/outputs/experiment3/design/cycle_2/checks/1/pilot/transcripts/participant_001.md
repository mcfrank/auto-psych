## Screen

Welcome. Welcome! In this short study, you will play a communication game where a speaker describes one of the objects on the screen.
It takes about 3.0 minutes.

Instructions:
On each turn, you and the speaker see the same row of objects. The speaker has one target object in mind and says a single word that names a part on their target object. Your job is to click the object you think is the speaker's target. An object that does not have the named part cannot be the target. If only one object has the named part, it is definitely the target. If more than one object has the named part, click the one you think the speaker is referring to. On some turns, the speaker's word was cut off; on those turns, click the object you think the speaker is most likely to talk about.

What is your job on each turn when you hear the speaker's word?
1) Click the object you think is the speaker's target
2) Click an object that does not have the named part
3) Count how many total parts all the objects have
Reply ANSWER: <the option's number>.
