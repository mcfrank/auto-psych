Refining bounded_depth_feature_parsimony by incorporating continuous contextual distinctiveness into the visual salience prior: candidate referents are weighted by combining discrete visual singleton salience with continuous feature contrast (contextual distinctiveness) relative to all context competitors. On informative trials, listeners interpret referring expressions with bounded depth (depth 1) by inverting an informative speaker over literal semantics, penalizing candidate referents possessing redundant visual features through communicative feature parsimony while contextual distinctiveness captures the salience of unmentioned contextually unique features. On uninformative displays, visual attention defaults jointly to discrete singleton salience and continuous contextual distinctiveness. Differences from bounded_depth_feature_parsimony: recursion depth remains depth 1 (listener calls L1); parameter w_distinct is added with prior dist.Normal(0.0, 1.0); no parameters are removed (retaining alpha, w_singleton, and beta); the referent prior is extended by adding w_distinct times the total contextual feature contrast (summed Hamming distance across display objects); and L1 memo signature and listener choice weights remain unchanged.
