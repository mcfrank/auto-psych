Listeners interpret referring expressions under referential feature uncertainty, weighting literal semantics by the probability that the heard feature was selected from an object's feature bundle, but additionally discount candidate referents possessing redundant visual features through communicative feature parsimony taken from bounded_depth_feature_parsimony. On informative trials, this parsimony penalty sharpens rejection of intermediate competitors in multi-level scalar hierarchies and prevents visual singleton salience from spuriously elevating cluttered candidates, while spontaneous visual attention on uninformative displays defaults to contextually unique singletons. Differences from feature_uncertainty_listener_2: recursion depth remains depth 1 (listener calls L1); parameter beta is added with prior dist.LogNormal(0.0, 1.0); no parameters are removed (retaining alpha and w_singleton); L1 adds beta and feature_count to its signature and scales referent choice weights by exp(-beta * vec(feature_count, r)); and listener passes beta and ctx.feature_count into L1 on informative trials.
