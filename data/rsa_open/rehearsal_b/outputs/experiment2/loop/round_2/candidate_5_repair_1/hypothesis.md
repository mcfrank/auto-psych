RSA pragmatic listener at depth 1 with Gestalt perceptual grouping over identical objects, singleton salience, contextual distinctiveness, visual feature complexity, evaluative valence framing, and visual color contrast. Refining rsa_l1_singleton_feat_color_valence_l0 by incorporating Gestalt perceptual grouping into the prior over referents: human observers group identical display objects into a single perceptual type, so that duplicate tokens partition the prior probability mass of their visual equivalence class inversely proportional to their duplicate count, preventing identical distractors from artificially diluting unique visual singletons. This directly increases predicted ratings for probed visual singletons under universal words and widens pragmatic contrast in large asymmetric scenes. Differences from rsa_l1_singleton_feat_color_valence_l0: recursion depth remains depth 1 (listener calls L1); no parameters are added or removed (retaining alpha, w_singleton, w_distinct, w_features, w_valence, and w_color); and in listener the prior logits decrement each object's utility by the logarithm of its duplicate count (subtracting log(duplicate_count) inside softmax_prior).
