Listeners interpret referring expressions with bounded reasoning depth, inverting an informative speaker who balances literal informativeness against utterance extension costs. Refining bounded_depth_listener by incorporating utterance extension costs (from isolated_graded_costly_l3) into the communicative speaker: speakers penalize words with broad contextual extensions, favoring more specific descriptions when available. This addresses severe empirical over-allocation to multi-feature singleton competitors and under-allocation to minimal targets when words are shared across objects. Differences from bounded_depth_listener: recursion depth remains unchanged (depth 1, listener calls L1); parameter cost_weight is added with prior dist.Normal(0.0, 1.0); no parameters are removed (retaining alpha and w_singleton); and the speaker choice utility subtracts cost_weight times the utterance extension cost (fraction of display objects satisfying each real utterance).
